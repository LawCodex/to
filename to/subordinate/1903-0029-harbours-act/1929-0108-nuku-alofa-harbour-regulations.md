# Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Taulanga Nuku'alofa

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Taulanga Nuku'alofa VAHE 22.04.01 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 1. to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 3 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE TAULANGA NUKU'ALOFA Na‘e fa‘u ‘i he mafai ‘oku foaki ‘e he Lao ki he Ngaahi Taulanga 1 Kamata'anga [1 ‘o Sanuali 1929]

1. 'E ui 'a e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ko ‘eni ko e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Taulanga Nuku'alofa.

2. Ka ‘i ai ha vaka kuo lesisita ke uta kakai holo ‘i he ‘otu motu ni ‘oku’ikai ke lahi hake hono lesisita ke uta ‘i he toni ‘e 20, pe ko ha taha pe ‘i Tongatapu´ni ‘oku ‘i ai hano vaka ‘oku ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he fute ‘e 45 hono loloa, ‘e ‘ikai ke tutu maama ‘a e vaka ko ia, neongo ‘oku´ne lītaula pe no‘o ki ha poe, ‘i hetaimi tō ‘ae la‘ā´‘o a‘u ki he‘ene hopo ‘a e la‘ā´, ‘o kapau ‘oku tau ‘a e vaka ko ia ‘i he konga ‘o e feitu‘u ‘o Nuku‘alofa kuo ‘osi fakangatangata ‘e he tu‘utu‘uni hono 3, pea ‘e kamata ‘i heni hono ui ‘o e feitu‘u ko ia koe tau‘anga vaka ta‘e tutu ha maama.

3. Ko e tau‘anga vaka ko‘eni ‘oku lau ki ai, ‘oku tu‘u ia ‘i he taulanga ‘o Nuku‘alofa ‘i he feitu‘u fakatonga ‘i he laini 105 tikili, ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ongo faka‘ilonga ukamea

‘oku vali hina ‘i he tumutumu‘i faka‘ilonga pea ‘oku tu‘u ‘a e taha ‘i he matātahi ‘o fehangahangai mo e mākoni, pea tu‘u ‘a e taha ‘i he matātahi ‘i Ma‘ufanga ‘o fehangahangai mo e ‘api Siasi Katolika.
