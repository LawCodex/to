# Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Taulanga Vava’u

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Taulanga Vava'u VAHE 22.04.03 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 1 to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 3 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE TAULANGA VAVA'U Na‘e fa‘u ‘i he mafai ‘o e Lao ki he Ngaahi Taulanga1 Kamata'anga [1 ‘o Sanuali 1936]

#### 1 'E ui 'a e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ko ‘eni ko e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Taulanga Vava'u.

#### 2 Ka ai ha vaka kuo lesisita´ ke uta kakai holo ‘i he ‘otu motu´ni ‘oku ‘ikai ke lahi hake

hono lesisita ke uta ‘i he toni ‘e 20, pe ko ha taha pe ‘oku ‘i ai hano vaka ‘oku ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he fute ‘e 45 hono loloa, ‘e ‘ikai ke tutu maama ‘a e vaka ko ia, neongo

‘oku´ne lī taula oe no‘o ki ha poe, ‘i he taimi tō ‘a e la‘ā´ ‘o a‘u ki he‘ene hopo ‘a e la‘ā´, ‘o kapau ‘oku tau ‘a e vaka ko ia ‘i he konga ‘o e feitu‘u ‘o Vava‘u kuo ‘osi fakangatangata ‘e he tu‘utu‘uni 3, pea ‘e kamata ‘i heni hono ui ‘o e feitu‘u ko ia ko e tau‘anga vaka iiki, ta‘e tutu ha maama´.

#### 3 Koetau‘angavakaiikikohonokotoa‘oekonga‘oeTaulanga‘oNeiafukiheHahake

‘o fai ki he faka‘ilonga fuopotopoto tumu‘aki masila ki he matātahi ki he Hahake ‘o e taulanga.
