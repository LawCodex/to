## TEPILE 8

FAKATOKANGA ‘O E TOHI KOLE KE TAMATE‘I 8 FAKAHINOHINO KI HONO FAI1 Na‘e fa‘u ‘e he ‘Eiki Fakamaau Lahi (Le‘ole‘o) Kamata'anga [31 ‘o ‘Okatopa, 1986] FAKAHINOHINO KI HONO FAI Ko e fakahinohino founga eni na‘e fai ‘e he ‘Eiki Fakamaau Lahi Le‘ole‘o, Tevita Tupou, ke ne tataki ‘a kinautolu ‘oku nau faka‘aonga‘i ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘i he Lao ki he ngaahi kautaha felāve‘i mo hano tamate‘i ‘o ha kautaha:

#### 1 Tufa ‘o e Fanongonongo ‘o e ‘eke ki he tokotaha mo‘ua

‘O kapau kuo ‘osi ‘ave ha fakamatala fakaikiiki ‘o e mo‘ua ki he tokotaha ‘oku mo‘ua mai, ‘oku ngofua ke fokotu‘u ‘i he Fanongonongo ‘Eke ko e mo‘ua koe‘uhi

‘o fakatatau mo e:

“ngaahi koloa na‘e ‘oatu ko hono fakaikiiki na‘e ‘osi ‘oatu kiate koe” Kae kehe ‘o kapau leva ‘e ‘eke mai ‘e he tokotaha mo‘ua ‘a e ‘uhinga ‘o e kupu‘i lea ko eni, kuo pau leva ke ‘oange kiate ia ‘i he taimi pe ko ia ha fakamatala fakaikiiki

‘o e ngaahi koloa na‘e ‘oatu hili hono to‘o ‘a e ngaahi totongi na‘e fai. Kuo pau ke fai eni ki mu‘a pea toe fai ha fa‘ahinga ngaue kau ki hono tamate‘i ‘a e kautaha mo‘ua mai.

‘E ala fakamo‘oni ‘i he fakahā ‘eke ma‘a e tokotaha ‘eke mo‘ua hano fakafofonga kuo fakamafai‘i pe fakafofonga Lao.

#### 2 Tohi kole ke tamate‘i

‘I ha hili ‘a e uike ‘e 3 mei hono tufa ‘o e fakahā ‘eke, kuo te‘eki fai mai ha totongi pe ma‘u ha malu‘i fakapotopoto ke fiemālie ki ai ‘a e tokotaha ‘eke mo‘ua, ‘e ala fakahū leva ha tohi kole ke tamate‘i ‘a e kautaha ki he Fakamaau‘anga. Ko e ngaahi tohi kole kau ki he ngaahi kautaha ‘i he tukui motu ka ‘ikai ko Tongatapu ‘e lava pe ke fakahu ‘i Nuku‘alofa, kae kehe ‘e fa‘iteliha pe ‘a e fakamaau ia te ne fai pe ko e fe ‘a e feitu‘u ‘e fai ai ‘a e hopo. Ki mu‘a pea fai ha hopo koe‘uhi ko e tohi kole kuo pau ke poupou‘aki ‘a e-

(a) fuakava tufa

(b) fuakava fakamā‘ala‘ala

‘E lava ke tali ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ke fakahū mai ‘a e tohi kole a ia ‘e poupou‘aki

‘a e fuakava tufa ‘ata‘atā pe ka he ‘ikai ke ne lava ‘o fai ha hopo ni kae ‘oua leva ke toe fakahū mai ki he Fakamaau‘anga mo e fuakava fakamā‘ala‘ala.

#### 3 ‘Aho ke fai ‘i he ‘ofisi ‘o e fakamaau (chambers) mo e fakahu mai ‘o e

ngaahi fuakava

‘Oku totonu ke fokotu‘u ha ‘aho ke fai ai ‘a e ‘uluaki hopo koe‘uhi ko e tohi kole ‘i he ‘Ofisi ‘o e Fakamaau ‘e he Failesisita pea toki tufa ki he faka‘iloa. Kae kehe ko e aho ko ia ke fai ai ‘a e hopo kuo pau he ‘ikai ke toe nounou hifo he ‘aho ‘e 21 mei he ‘aho ‘e tufa ai ki he faka‘iloa. Ko e tohi kole ko ia ke tufa ki he faka‘iloa kuo pau ke fakaha ai ‘a e fiema‘u ke fakahū mai ha ngaahi fakamatala fuakava kuo ‘osi fuakava‘i ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 7 ki mu‘a ‘a e ‘aho hopo ‘o kapau ‘oku loto ke ne fakakikihi‘i

‘a e tohi kole. Ko ha fanongonongo ke ngāue‘aki ‘e ala ma‘u ia mei he Tepile. Kuo pau ke hā ‘i he ngaahi fakamatala fuakava ‘oku fakakikihi‘i ‘a e tohi kole ‘i he tu‘unga falala‘anga pea mo ha ngaahi tu‘unga lahi koe‘uhi ke ta‘ofi ‘aki hano fanongonongo ‘a e tohi kole mo hano fokotu‘u ha ‘aho ke fai ai ‘a e hopo.

#### 4 ‘Uluaki hopo ‘i he ‘ofisi ‘o e fakamaau (chambers)

‘I ha fai ‘o ha ‘uluaki hopo ‘eke ‘i he ‘ofisi ‘o e Fakamaau, ‘e lava ‘e he Fakamaau

‘o ma‘u ai pe ‘oku ‘i ai koā pe ‘ikai ha fakakikihi mo‘oni mo totonu. Pea ko ia ai te ne lava leva ke ne fakamo‘ui ai pe pē ‘e tamate‘i ‘a e tohi kole. ‘E lava ke ne fakamaau‘i ‘a e fakakikihi kau ki he mo‘ua ke fakasi‘isi‘i hifo ‘a e fakamole.Kae kehe ‘oku fa‘a toloi ‘a e tohi kole ke lava hano fakamaau‘i ‘a e fehu‘i ko ia ‘i hono fai ‘o e hopo. ‘Oku mafai ‘a e Fakamaau ke ne tu‘utu‘uni ke totongi ki he Fakamaau‘anga ‘a e lahi ‘o e mo‘ua kuo tukuaki‘i. ‘O kapau ‘e fai ‘a e ‘uluaki hopo pea ‘ikai ke fakahā ha ‘uhinga ‘e faka‘ali‘ali mai ai ‘oku fakakikihi‘i ‘a e tohi kole

‘aki ha tu‘unga falala‘anga pea mo ha ngaahi tu‘unga lahi, ‘e lava ‘e he fakamaau‘anga ke ne faka‘aho ‘a e tohi kole ke hopo pea mo fakangofua ke fanongonongo pe ‘a e tohi kole kuo tali.

#### 5 Fanongonongo ‘o e tohi kole

Kuo pau ke fanongonongo ‘i he Kalonikali Tonga ‘a e ‘aho ‘e fai ai ‘a e hopo koe‘uhi ko e tohi kole ke ‘ilo‘i ‘e he fa‘ahinga ‘oku mo‘ua ki ai.Ko e ‘aho ko ia ke fai ai ‘a e hopo he ‘ikai ke lava ia ki mu‘a ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 14 mei he ‘aho ‘o e fanongonongo. Kuo pau ke fai ‘a e fanongonongo ‘o ‘ikai toe laka hake ‘i he mahina

‘e 6 mei he ‘uluaki hopo.

#### 6 Ngaahi tu‘unga fo‘ou ‘oku fakakikihi‘i

‘E ‘atā ki he faka‘iloa ke ne kei fokotu‘u mai ‘i he hopo ha ngaahi tu‘unga fakakikihi na‘e ‘ikai ke kau ‘i he ‘uluaki hopo. Kapau leva ‘e malohi ‘a e faka‘iloa

‘o fakatatali ‘a e tohi kole koe‘uhi ko ha poini na‘e ‘ikai ke fokotu‘u mai ‘i he

‘uluaki hopo ‘eke, ‘oku ‘i ai ‘a e mafai ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga ke ne tu‘utu‘uni ko e fakatatali ‘e makatu‘unga ia mei hano totongi ‘a e ngaahi fakamole kuo hoko ki he talatalaaki ‘i he‘ene ‘omai ‘a e kau fakamo‘oni ki he hopo.

#### 7 Hopo koe‘uhi ko e tohi kole

‘I hono fai ‘o e hopo, pea ‘ikai ke ‘i ai ha fakakikihi felāve‘i mo e mo‘ua ‘i ha tu‘unga falala‘anga ‘o makatu‘unga ‘i ha ngaahi ‘uhinga lahi, ‘e ala fai ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ha tu‘utu‘uni ke tamate‘i ‘a e Kautaha ‘oku faka‘iloa.
