# Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni (Fakamaau'anga Tohi Tuku Mo e Pule'I 'o e Koloa 'a e Pekia) 'o e Fakamaau'anga Lahi

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni (Fakamaau'anga Tohi Tuku Mo e Pule'I 'o e Koloa 'a e Pekia) 'o e Fakamaau'anga Lahi VAHE 3.16.03 to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Page 3 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI (FAKAMAAU'ANGA TOHI TUKU MO E PULE'I 'O E KOLOA 'A E PEKIA) 'O E FAKAMAAU'ANGA LAHI1 Na‘e fa‘u‘e he Fakamaau ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi Kamata‘anga [26 ‘o Ma‘asi 1964] Ko kinautolu ‘oku nau ‘amanaki kole ki he Fakamo‘oni‘i ‘o ha Tohi Tuku pe Tohi Pule‘i ‘o e Koloa ‘a ha Pekia ‘oku pau ‘i he ‘ikai si‘i ange ‘i he mahina ‘e 2 ki mu‘a pea fai ‘a e kole ki he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi koe‘uhi ko e Lao ki he Fakamaau‘anga Tohi Tuku mo e pule‘i ‘a e koloa ‘a e pekia ke nau ‘ai ke fokotu‘u ‘i he “Kalonikali” ha fanongonongo ‘o ‘ikai si‘i hifo ‘i he mahina ‘e 2
