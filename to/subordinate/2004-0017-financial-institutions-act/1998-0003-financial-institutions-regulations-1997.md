# Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Ngaahi Kautaha Faka-pa'anga 1997

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Kautaha Faka-pa'anga VAHE 16.06.01 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 1 to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 5 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE KAUTAHA FAKA- PA'ANGA1 Na'e fa'u 'e he 'Ene 'Afio 'i he Fakataha Tokoni 'i he mafai 'o e kupu 21 'o e Lao ki he Ngaahi Kautaha Faka-Pa'anga Kamata'anga [9 ‘o Siulai 1998]

#### 1 Hingoa Nounou

‘E ui ‘a e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ko e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Ngaahi Kautaha Faka- pa‘anga.

#### 2 ‘Uhinga‘i Lea

‘I he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni, tukukehe kapau ‘oku hā mei hono tu‘unga ha ‘uhinga kehe —

“Lao” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he lao ki he, Ngaahi Kautaha Faka-Pa‘anga 1991.

#### 3 Ngaahi me‘a kuo fakapapau‘i ‘e he Pangike Pule ‘i he Kole laiseni.

Kuo pau ke kau ‘a e ngaahi me‘a ko ‘eni ‘i hono fakapapau’i ‘e he Pangike Pule ‘o fakatatau ki he ngaahi taumu‘a ‘o e kupu 7 ‘o e Lao —

(a) pe kuo ‘osi muimui pau ‘a e taha kole ki he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni kotoa pe kae lava ke ne fakahoko ‘ene pisinisi ‘i he founga kuo ne fokotu‘u mai;

(b) pe‘oku‘ihetu‘ungataau‘aetahakolekene ma‘uhalaiseni‘ofakatatau ki he Lao;

(c) fakatatau ki he sino‘i pa‘anga, ivi fakapa‘anga, fakakautaha, fakahoko ngaue mo e ‘ulungaanga totonu ‘a e taha kole, ‘e ‘ikai ai ke lava ‘o hoko Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 4 VAHE 16.06.01 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Kautaha Faka-pa'anga Peesi 6 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to ha me‘a ‘e ngali fakatu‘utamaki ki he kakai fakalukufua kapau ‘e foaki ange ha laiseni ‘a e taha kole ke ne fakahoko ha pisinisi fakapangike;

(d) ‘oku hanga ‘e he tu‘unga faka‘ekonomika mo e lelei fakalukufua ‘a e kakai ‘o fakatonuhia‘i hano foaki ha laiseni ‘a e taha kole.

#### 4 Ola ‘o e Fanongonongo ke Fakafoki ha Laiseni

‘I he taimi ko ia ‘oku ‘atu ai ha fanongonongo ‘o e ‘amanaki ke fakafoki ha laiseni pisinisi fakapangike ‘i he kupu 8 ‘o e Lao —

(a) kuo pau ke ‘atu ki he taha ma‘u iaiseni ‘a e ngaahi ‘uhinga ‘oku fokotu‘u atu ai ‘e he Pangike Pule ke fakafoki ‘ene laiseni;

(b) kuo pau ki he taha ma‘u laiseni ‘i loto ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 14 mei he ‘aho ‘o e fanongonongo ke ne fakahu atu ha ngaahi ‘uhinga ‘oku ‘ikai ke totonu ai ke fakafoki ‘a e laiseni.

#### 5 Ola ‘o e Fakafoki ‘o e Laiseni

Kapau ‘oku fakaloki ha laiseni i he kupu 8 ‘o e Lao —

(a) kuo pau ki he Pangike Pule ke ne fai ha fanongonongo ‘o pulusi ‘i he Kasete ‘o fekau‘aki mo e fakafoki ‘o e laiseni mo e ‘aho na‘e ngata ai ‘a e laiseni;

(b) ‘oku ‘ikai hanga ‘e he fakafoki ko ia ‘o e laiseni, ‘iate ia pe ‘o to‘o ‘a e totonu pe uesia ha fa‘ahinga totonu, ‘eke pe fatongia fekau‘aki mo e kautaha faka-pa‘anga ‘a ia na‘e fakafoki ‘ene laiseni;

(c) kuo pau ke fakafoki leva ‘e he kautaha faka-pa‘anga ki he Potungaue Pa‘anga ‘a ‘ene laiseni.

#### 6 Tokotaha Fale‘i ‘oku Fokotu‘u ‘e he Pangike Pule

‘E ngaue‘aki ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ko ‘eni kapau ‘e fokotu‘u ‘e he Pangike Pule ha taha ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu I5(2)(b) ‘o e Lao ke ne fale‘i ‘a e kautaha faka-pa‘anga kuo laiseni ‘o fekau‘aki mo e anga hono fakalele totonu ‘o ‘ene pisinisi —

(a) kuo pau ke fakaha tohi ‘a e fokotu‘u;

(b) kuo pau ke fakaha tohi ke ‘ilo ‘e he kautaha faka-pa‘anga ‘a e fokotu‘u;

(c) ko e tokotaha kuo fokotu‘u kuo pau ke ne fakahoko hono ngaahi fatongia

‘o fakatatau ki he ngaahi fakahinohino mo e pule‘i ‘a e Pangike Pule.
