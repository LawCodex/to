# Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he ‘Inivesi Muli

Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he 'Inivesi Muli 2021 Peesi 4 to C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE 'INIVESI MULI 2021 LAO KI HE 'INIVESI MULI 2020 'I HONO NGAUE'AKI 'a e ngaahi mafai kuo foaki 'i he kupu 45 'o e Lao ki he 'Inivesi Muli 2020, 'oku fa'u ai 'e he Minisita 'oku ne tokangaekina 'a e fefakatau'aki mo e fakalakalaka faka'ekonomika, 'i he loto ki ai 'a e Kapineti, 'a e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ko ‘eni -

#### 1 Hingoa nounou mo e kamata ngaue‘aki

(1) 'E ui 'a e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ko ‘eni ko e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he 'Inivesi Muli 2021.

(2) Kuo pau ke ngaue‘aki ‘a e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ko eni ‘i he ‘aho ‘o e kasete.

#### 2 ‘Uhinga‘i lea

‘I he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni, tukukehe ka fiema‘u ha ‘uhinga kehe –

“Lao” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Lao ki he ‘Inivesi Muli 2020;

“fakamo‘oni” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ha Fakamo‘oni Lesisita ‘Inivesi Muli kuo foaki

‘i he kupu 15(3)(a) ‘o e Lao;

“‘aho ngaue‘aki” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he ‘aho ‘oku foaki ai ‘a e fakamo‘oni;

“vaha‘ataimi fakahoko” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he mahina ‘e 12 mei he ‘aho ngaue‘aki;

“lesisita” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Lesisita ‘Inivesi Muli kuo fokotu‘u ‘i he kupu 30

‘o e Lao;

“Minisita” ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘a e ‘uhinga tatau ‘oku ‘oatu ‘i he kupu 2 ‘o e Lao;

“PACSIC” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Pacific Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities kuo pulusi ‘e he Secretariat of the Pacific Community, ‘i hono fakatonutonu mei he taimi ki he taimi.

#### 3 Kole ki ha Fakamo‘oni Lesisita ‘Inivesi Muli

(1) Kuo pau ko e kole ki ha fakamo‘oni ke ‘i he foomu kuo tali ‘e he Failesisita.

(2) Kapau ‘oku ‘ikai kakato ha kole, ‘e ngofua ki he Failesisita ‘i loto ‘i he ‘aho ngaue ‘e 5 ke tohi ki he taha kole ke fakakakato ‘a e kole.

#### 4 Fakahu atu ‘i he ‘initaneti

(1) ‘E ngofua ki ha taha ke ne fakahu atu ha ngaahi tohi ngaue ki he Failesisita ‘i he sevesi faka‘initaneti ki he ‘inivesi muli.

(2) ‘E ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ne fiema‘u ha toe tohi ngaue pe fakamo‘oni kehe mei he taha ‘oku ne fakataumu‘a ke ngaue‘aki ‘a e sevesi faka‘initaneti ke fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e faka‘ilonga (identity) ‘o e taha ko ia.

#### 5 Fokotu‘u ‘o ha taha fakatonutonu

(1) Kuo pau ki he Minisita ke ne fokotu‘u ha taha fakatonutonu ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni ni ke ne fakamahino ha tangi mei ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Failesisita ‘i he kupu 15(3)(b) pe kupu 18(3)(b) ‘o e Lao.

(2) Kuo pau ko e taha kuo fokotu‘u ‘e he Minisita ke hoko ko ha taha fakatonutonu ko ha –

(a) fakafofonga lao ma‘olunga ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘a e taukei ‘ikai si‘i hifo ‘i he ta‘u ‘e 10 ‘i he tu‘unga ko ia;

(b) taha ngaue pisinisi ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘a e taukei fe‘unga ‘i loto ‘i he sekitoa fakapisinisi; pe

(c) taha ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘a e ‘ilo mo e taukei makehe ‘i he ngaahi mala‘e ‘o e fefakatau‘aki mo e ‘ekonomika.

(3) Kuo pau ki he taha kuo fokotu‘u ko e taha fakatonutonu ke ne fakapapau‘i ‘oku

‘ikai ke ‘i ai ha‘ane kaunga fakafo‘ituitui ‘i he tangi.

(4) Kuo pau ke ngata ‘a e fokotu‘u ‘o e taha fakatonutonu ‘i he ngata ‘a e tangi.

#### 6 Lisi Tuku Makehe

‘Oku ‘oatu ‘a e Lisi Tuku Makehe ‘i he Tepile 1.

#### 7 Lisi Fakangatangata

‘Oku ‘oatu ‘a e Lisi Fakangatangata ‘i he Tepile 2.

#### 8 Fakaloloa ‘o e vaha‘ataimi fakahoko

(1) ‘I hono fakahoko ‘o ha tohi kole mei he taha ma‘u fakamo‘oni ke fakahoko ha ngaue fakangatangata, ‘e ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ne fakaloloa ‘a e vaha‘ataimi fakahoko.

(2) Kuo pau ko e kole ‘a e taha ma‘u fakamo‘oni ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni-si‘i (1) ke fakahoko ia ‘o ‘ikai si‘i hifo ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 30 kimu‘a ‘a e ngata ‘a e vaha‘ataimi fakahoko.

(3) ‘E ngofua ke fakaloloa ‘e he Failesisita ‘a e vaha‘ataimi fakahoko ki ha vaha‘ataimi ‘oku ‘ikai loloa ange ‘i he mahina ‘e 12 ‘i he taimi pe ‘e taha.

(4) ‘I hono ma‘u ‘a e kole, kuo pau ke ‘oatu ‘e he Failesisita ki he taha ma‘u fakamo‘oni ha tohi fanongonongo ‘o ‘ene tu‘utu‘uni ‘i loto ‘i he ‘aho ngaue ‘e 10.

(5) Kapau ‘oku tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Failesisita ke tali ha fakaloloa ‘o e vaha‘ataimi fakahoko, kuo pau ke ne fakatonutonu ‘a e lesisita ‘o fakatatau ki ai.

#### 9 Ngaahi totongi

‘Oku ‘oatu ‘a e ngaahi totongi ke totongi ‘i he Lao mo e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ko eni ‘i he Tepile 3.
