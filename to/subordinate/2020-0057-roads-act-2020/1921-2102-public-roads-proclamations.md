# Fanongonongo 'o e Fakaha 'a e Hala Pule'anga

FANONGONONGO 'O E FAKAHA 'A E HALA PULE'ANGA Kamata‘anga [26 ‘o Me, 1921]

(1) Fanongonongo ‘i he ‘aho 26 ‘o Me, 19211 TONGATAPU

1. Ko e hala ‘oku ofi ki he kolo Fatai ‘i Tongatapu ‘o kamata mei he hala lahi mei Nuku‘alofa ki Hihifo ‘o ‘alu ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ‘api ‘oku lisi ‘e he Pule‘anga kia A. Richelmann mo W. Cocker pea ‘alu fakatonga kae fakahahake ‘i he tafa‘aki ‘o e ‘api ‘oku lisi ki a A. Riechelmann, ‘api ‘o Valu, ‘api lisi ‘o J. F. Fuller ‘o tau ki he ngata‘anga fakatokelau ‘o e ‘api

‘oku lisi kia A. Cowley pea ‘oku ui ‘a e hala ni ko e hala mei Tapuvao ki Fualu.

2. Ko e hala ‘i Tongatapu ‘o kamata ‘i he hala lahi mei Nuku‘alofa ki Hihifo

‘o ‘alu ‘i he ngata‘anga fakahahake ‘o e ‘api ‘oku lisi ki a W. Cocker ‘o

‘alu fakatonga ‘i he ngata‘anga ko ia ‘o e ‘api ‘o W. Cocker kae ‘alu fakatongaahihifo ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o e ‘api ‘o e Siasi Uesiliana pea ‘oku ui

‘a e hala ni ko e hala mei Tapuvao Fualu.

‘alu fakatonga kae fakahahake ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o e ‘api ‘o ‘Isiieli Vea, Tonu, Tevita Tonga‘uli‘uli, Sione Manuao, Pelaki, Masau, Samiu Kavapalu, ‘o ‘alu ‘i he ngata‘anga fakahihifo ‘o e ‘api ‘o Sita Kavapalu ki Mataki‘eua pea ‘oku ui ‘a e hala ni ko e hala mei Houmalafo ki Mataki‘eua.
