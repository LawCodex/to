# Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ki he Ngaahi Laiseni Pisinisi 2025

“Lao” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he Lao ki he Ngaahi Laiseni Pisinisi [Vahe 17.01];

“fakaʻinitaneti” ʻoku ʻuhinga ʻi he –

(b) fekauʻaki mo hono faile ʻo ha tohi pe fanongonongo, ko hono faile ʻa e tohi pe fanongonongo ʻo fakafou ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti; pe

“ngaue fakaʻinitaneti” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki ha ngaue ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he, pe maʻae, Failesisita, ʻa ia ʻoku ne fakangofua ʻa e ngaahi tohi kole, ngaahi faile, ngaahi fanongonongo mo e ngaahi tohi ke lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ʻoatu ki he Failesisita ʻo fakafou ʻi he ʻinitaneti ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti, ki he totongi ʻo e ngaahi totongi mo e ngaahi moʻua, pea mo hono maʻu ʻo ha ngaahi ngaue kehe ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) Ko e ngaahi lea kotoa pe ʻoku fakamatalaʻi ʻi he kupu 2 ʻo e Lao kuo pau ke ngaueʻaki ia ʻi he ʻuhinga tatau mo ia ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni ʻi he Lao ʻi he taimi ʻoku lave ai ki ai ʻi he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ni.

#### 3 Ngaahi houa ngaue

(1) Kuo pau ke ‘ataa ‘a e ʻofisi ʻo e Failesisita ki he kakai ʻi he ʻaho ngaue kotoa pe, lolotonga ʻa e ngaahi houa kuo fokotuʻu ʻe he Failesisita mei he taimi ki he taimi, pe fakalukufua pe ʻi ha tuʻunga pau.

(2) Kuo pau ke ‘ataa houa ‘e 24 ‘a e lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ki he kakai.

#### 4 Lesisita Laiseni Pisinisi

(1) Kuo pau ki he Failesisita ke ne fokotuʻu, tauhi mo ngaueʻaki ha lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻi ha founga ʻoku tali ʻe he Failesisita ʻi he kupu 22(1A) ʻo e Lao.

(2) Kuo pau ke kau ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti, ʻi he tuʻunga maʻulalo taha, ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala ko ʻeni –

(a) ngaahi laiseni pisinisi kotoa pe kuo lesisita;

(b) ngaahi fika lesisita ki he ngaahi laiseni pisinisi kotoa pe kuo lesisita;

(c) ko e tuʻasila ʻo e tefitoʻi feituʻu pisinisi mo e ngaahi feituʻu kehe ʻoku fakahoko ai ʻa e pisinisi ʻi he laiseni pisinisi;

(d) ko e ʻaho ʻoku foaki ai ʻa e lesisita kotoa pe ʻo e laiseni pisinisi;

(e) ko ha hisitolia ʻo e ngaahi faile kotoa ʻi he Lao ʻoku fekauʻaki mo hono foaki ʻo ha laiseni pisinisi; mo

(f) ha fakamatala fakasitetisitika ʻokupehe ʻe he Failesisitaʻokutaaumei he taimi ki he taimi ke tanaki.

#### 5 Fakahu ʻo e ngaahi tohi ki he lesisita mo e ngaahi ngaue fakaʻinitaneti

(1) ʻE ngofua ke fekauʻi ʻe he Failesisita ʻa e fakahu ʻo ha ngaahi tohi kole, fanongonongo pe tohi ʻi he Lao pe ko e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni, ke fakahoko pe ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) ʻOku ʻikai ngaueʻaki ʻa e tuʻutuʻuni siʻi (1) ʻi he taimi ʻoku ʻave ai ki he Failesisita ha tohi faka-fakamaauʻanga.

(3) Kuo pau ke ngaueʻaki ʻa e founga ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ki he ngaahi tohi kotoa ʻoku fakahu mai ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(4) Kapau ʻoku lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ʻoatu ha tohi ki he Failesisita ʻo fakafou ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti, ko e taha ʻoku ne lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ʻoatu ʻa e tohi ki he Failesisita, kuo pau ke ne ʻoatu ha tuʻasila ʻimeili –

(a) ki he ngaahi fetuʻutaki fakalukufua; mo e

(b) fetuʻutaki mo e taha ʻoku ne maʻu ʻa e laiseni pisinisi.

(5) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ne ʻikai tali ke maʻu mai pe lesisita ha tohi kapau ʻoku pehe ʻe he Failesisita ko e tohi ʻoku fakahu ange –

(a) ʻoku kau ai ʻa e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku fepaki mo e lao;

(b) ʻi he ʻuhinga ko ha faʻahinga meʻa naʻe ʻikai ke fakahoko pe naʻe fakamatala halaʻi, kuo teʻeki ai ke fakakakato fakalelei;

(c) ʻoku ʻikai ke faipau ki he ngaahi fiemaʻu ʻa e Lao, ko e Tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni, pe ko e founga ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti; pe

(d) ʻokuʻiai ʻae ngaahi fakamatalaʻikai totonuʻoku ne fakatupuʻae ngaahi meʻa ʻi he tohi ke takihalaʻi, ʻe ngofua ki he Failesisita ke fekauʻi ke fakatonutonu pe fakakakato lelei ʻa e tohi pea toe fakahu mai, pe ke fakahu ha tohi foʻou ʻi hono tuʻunga.

(6) Ko ha tohi ʻoku fakahu mai ʻi he Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni ʻoku lau ia naʻe ʻikai ke fakahu tuku kehe kapau ʻoku tali ʻe he Failesisita.

(7) Kuo pau ke ʻikai tali ʻe he Failesisita ha tohi ʻoku ʻikai faipau ki he Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni tuku kehe ka ʻi ai ha ngofua makehe mei he Failesisita, kuo tali ʻi he ngaahi makatuʻunga ʻoku ne pehe ʻoku taau.

#### 6 Kau ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai mo e ngaahi ʻakauni ʻa e kau fakafofongaʻi

(1) ʻE ngofua ki ha taha, kau ai ha sino fakalao, sino fakalao muli, pe fakafofonga fakapuleʻanga, ke hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ke ne ngaueʻaki ʻa e lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ki he taumuʻa ke ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ke kole ha ngaahi tohi pe ha fakamoʻoni kehe mei ha taha pe ʻoku ne fie hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai koeʻuhi ke fakapapauʻi ʻa e fakaʻilongaʻi ʻo e taha pe sino ko ia ʻi he fiemalie ki ai ʻa e Failesisita.

(3) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita, ʻi he tohi kole, ke tali mo fokotuʻu ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ʻi he laiseni ʻo e pe maʻae taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ki he taumuʻa ke ne ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(4) Kuo pau ke ʻikai ʻi ai ha totongi ki he hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai pea mo hono fokotuʻu ʻo ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

(5) Kuo pau ki he tohi kole ki ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ke –

(a) ʻi he founga kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita; mo

(b) fakataha mo ha fakamoʻoni ʻoku fiemalie ki ai ʻa e Failesisita fekauʻaki mo e fakaʻilongaʻi ʻo e taha fakafofongaʻi.

(6) Koeʻuhi ke lava ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ʻo faʻu ha faile ʻi loto ʻi he lesisita, kuo pau ke ne fuofua hu ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻo ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala hu atu ʻoku tauhi fakafoʻituitui ʻo hange ko e lea maluʻi, fika fakapulipuli (PINs) pe hange ko ia. ʻOku ʻikai fiemaʻu ʻa e Failesisita ke ne fakapapauʻipekoetahafaile ʻiheʻinitanetikohatahangaueʻakikuofakamafai ʻoku ngofua ke ne ngaueʻaki ʻa e fakamatala hu atu ʻoku fakahu ki he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(7) Kuo pau ki he totongi ʻo e ngaahi totongi, moʻua, mo e ngaahi totongi tomui ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ke fakahoko mei he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

(8) ʻE ngofua ki he totongi ʻo e paʻanga ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ke ngaueʻaki ha founga kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita mei he taimi ki he taimi.

(9) ʻIhono kole,ʻe ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ʻoatu ha fakamatala ʻi he mahina taki taha ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi kotoa pe ʻo fakaha ai ʻa e ngaahi kuletiti mo e tepiti ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

#### 7 Ngaahi foomu lesisita fakaʻinitaneti

(1) Kuo pau ki ha tohi, fanongonongo pe foomu ʻoku fakahu mai ʻo fakafou ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ke fakakakato fakatatau ki he ngaahi fakamatala, ngaahi fakahinohino pe ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻoku fakamahinoʻi ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti, pe ʻe he Failesisita.

(2) Ko e ngaahi foomu ke ngaueʻaki ʻi he Lao ko e ngaahi foomu ia kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita ke fakahu ʻi he ʻinitaneti pea ʻoku kau ai ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala ʻoku maʻu ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻoku fekauʻaki mo e foomu.

(3) Kuo pau ki ha foomu kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita ki he lesisita ʻi he ʻinitianeti ke fakakakato ʻo fakatatau ki he ngaahi fakahinohino ʻoku ʻi loto ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(4) Kapau ko ha tohi, faile pe fanongonongo kuo fakahu ʻo fakafou ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ʻoku fiemaʻu ha toe fakamatala tanaki, kuo pau ki he fakamatala ko ia ke tuku hake ʻi he founga fakaʻelekitulonika ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

#### 8 Totongi fakalukufua

(1) Kuo pau ki he ngaahi totongi ʻoku ha ʻi he Tepile 1 ke totongi ki he Failesisita ʻo fekauʻaki mo e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku felaveʻi mo ia.

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he ngaahi totongi ʻoku ha ʻi he Tepile 1 ke kau ai ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi totongi tomui mo e ngaahi moʻua ʻe ala totongi ki hono fakahu ʻo ha tohi ki he Failesisita ʻi he hili pe ‘a e ‘osi ‘o e taimi kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he Lao pe Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni.

(3) Kuo pau ki he ngaahi totongi ke fakahoko ki hono ngaueʻaki ʻo e ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ke pulusi ʻe he Failesisita ʻi he uepisaiti ʻa e Potungaue mei he taimi ki he taimi.

#### 9 Taimi ki hono totongi ʻo e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua, pe ngaahi

totongi ki he faile tomui

(1) Kuo pau ke totongi ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua pe ngaahi totongi ki he faile tomui ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni ʻi he taimi ʻoku fakahoko ai ʻa e ngaue fakapaʻanga fekauʻaki mo ia.

(2) Kuo pau ke totongi ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua mo e ngaahi totongi tomui –

(a) ʻi ha founga totongi ʻoku tali ʻe he Failesisita mei he taimi ki he taimi; pe

(b) ʻaki ʻa e tepiti pe mei he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ʻoku kuletiti ʻo ʻikai siʻi hifo ʻi he mahuʻinga ʻo e totongi pe moʻua.

#### 10 Fakaʻataa ʻo e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua pe ngaahi totongi tomui

(1) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ʻi ha fanongonongo tohi ki he taha ʻe ala totongi ha totongi, moʻua pe totongi tomui ʻi he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni, ke ne fakaʻataa kakato pe ha konga ʻo ia.

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ne fakaʻataa ha totongi, moʻua pe totongi tomui kapau ʻoku ne pehe ko e fiemaʻu ke totongi kakato pe ha konga ʻo ia –

(a) ʻoku ngalingali te ne fakatupu ha faingataʻa lahi ki he taha ʻe ala moʻua ke ne totongi;

(b) ʻoku ʻikai ke tatau ia mo e meʻa ʻoku lolotonga hoko;

(c) ʻoku ngalingali ke hoko ha uesia ki he kau kuletitoa ʻo e laiseni pisinisi; pe Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 11 Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ki he Ngaahi Laiseni Pisinisi 2025 Peesi 10 to

(d) ko e ngaahi makatuʻunga ʻo e vahevahe taau ʻoku fakamahino pau ʻa e poupou ki ha fakaʻataa.

(3) ʻE ngofua ke fekauʻi ʻe he Failesisita ke totongi ha totongi, moʻua, pe totongi tomui ʻi he taimi ʻoku fakahu mai ai ha tohi fakataha mo ha kole ke fakaʻataa ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahimoʻua pe ngaahi totongi tomui, pea kapau ʻe taliʻa e fakaʻataa, ʻe ngofua ke fakafoki ʻe he Failesisita ʻa e totongi ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

#### 11 Tohi kole ke lesisita

(1) Kuo pau ke fakahu mai ha tohi kole ke lesisita ha laiseni pisinisi fakataha mo e totongi kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻoku felaveʻi ʻi he Tepile 1 pea ʻe fakahu ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻo fakafou ʻi ha ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) Kapau ʻe ʻikai tali ʻe he Failesisita ha tohi kole, kuo pau ke nau fakaha ki he taha kole ʻi ha tohi fekauʻaki mo e ngaahi ʻuhinga ʻo e ʻikai tali.

(3) Kapau ʻe ʻikai tali ha tohi kole makatuʻunga ʻi he ngaahi ʻuhinga fakalao pau ʻoku ha ʻi he kupu 5 ʻo e Lao, kuo pau ke ʻikai fakafoki ʻa e totongi kole.

(4) ʻE ngofua ke fakafoki ha tohi kole ʻe he taha kole ki he Failesisita, pea ʻi he tuʻunga ko ʻeni, kuo pau ke ʻikai fakafoki ʻa e totongi kole.

(5) ʻI he taimi ʻoku maʻu mai ai ha tohi kole ʻoku faipau ki he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni, kuo pau ke ʻoatu ʻe he Failesisita ki he taha kole ha tohi fakamoʻoni ʻo e lesisita laiseni pisinisi ʻoku ha ai ʻa e ʻaho ʻo e lesisita.

#### 12 Ngaue Fakapisinisi

(1) Kuo pau ki ha taha laiseni pisinisi ke ne maʻu ha laiseni pisinisi pe ʻe taha ki he kotoa ʻo ʻene ngaahi ngaue fakapisinisi ʻoku kau ki ai.

(2) Kuo pau ke fakamahinoʻi ʻe he tohi kole ʻa e ngaahi ngaue pisinisi pau ʻe kau ki ai ʻo hange ko ia ʻoku tuku atu ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(3) ʻOku feʻunga pe ʻa e tohi kole ʻe taha ke ne maʻu kotoa ʻa e ngaahi ngaue fakapisinisi kotoa pe ʻoku kau ki ai.

(4) Kapau ʻe liliu ʻa e ngaahi ngaue fakapisinisi ʻi ha vahaʻataimi, kuo pau ke fakahu ʻe he taha pisinisi ha fakatonutonu ki he Failesisita ʻa ia ʻoku ha mahino ai ʻa e ngaahi ngaue pisinisi lolotonga.

#### 13 Vahaʻataimi ʻo e lesisita

(1) Kuo pau ki ha laiseni pisinisi ke ʻaonga ʻo taʻengata ka kuo pau ki he kau maʻu laiseni pisinisi ke faipau ki he lao pea lesisita totonu ʻa e fanongonongo ʻo e hokohoko atu ʻo e ngaue fakapisinisi.

(2) Kuo pau ke fakahu atu ʻa e fanongonongo ʻo e hokohoko atu ʻo e ngaue fakapisinisi ʻi he taʻu kotoa pea ke fakahu fakataha mo e totongi totonu ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni ʻi he Tepile 1.

(3) ʻI hono maʻu ʻo ha fanongonongo ʻo e hokohoko atu ʻo e ngaue fakapisinisi fakataha mo e totongi totonu, kuo pau ke lesisita ʻe he Failesisita ʻa e fanongonongo pea foaki ki he taha kole ha laiseni pisinisi kuo fakafoʻou.

#### 14 Fakatonutonu ʻo e Lesisita mo e Laiseni

(1) Kapau ko e fakamatala ʻi loto ʻi he Tohi Fakamoʻoni ʻo e Laiseni Pisinisi pe ʻi he Lesisita Laiseni Pisinisi ʻoku ʻikai ke tonu koeʻuhi ko ha liliu ʻi he ngaahi makatuʻunga, kuo pau ki he taha pisinisi ʻi loto ʻi he ʻaho ngaue ʻe 10 ke ne faile ha fakatonutonu ki he lesisita laiseni pisinisi ʻo fakafou ʻi ha ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) Kuo pau ke kau ʻi he fakatonutonu ʻa e totongi kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻi he Tepile 1.

(3) Kapau ʻoku maʻu ʻe he Failesisita ha fakatonutonu ʻi he tuʻutuʻuni siʻi (1) ʻa ia ʻoku fakatonutonu ai ʻa e fakamatala ʻoku ha ʻi he Tohi Fakamoʻoni ʻo e Lesisita Laiseni Pisinisi, kuo pau ke foaki ʻe he Failesisita ha Tohi Fakamoʻoni ʻo e Lesisita Laiseni Pisinisi kuo fakatonutonu.

#### 15 Kaniseli ʻo e lesisita

Kapau ʻoku kaniseli ʻe he Failesisita ha laiseni pisinisi, kuo pau ke ne –

(a) fanongonongo ki hetaha pisinisi ʻi ha fetuʻutaki tohi ʻa e ʻuhinga ki hono kaniseli; pea

(b) kuo pau ke fakaha ʻe he fanongonongo ki he taha ʻoku laiseni ʻa ʻene totonu ke tangi.

#### 16 Tangi ki he Minisita

(1) ʻE ngofua ke fakahoko ha tangi ki ha tuʻutuʻuni kuo fakahoko ʻi he tuʻutuʻuni 11(2) pe 15 pea kuo pau ke fakahoko ʻi he tohi ki he Minisita ʻi loto ʻi he ʻaho ngaue ʻe 14 mei he ʻaho ʻoku ʻoatu ai ki he taha laiseni ʻa e fanongonongo ʻo e tuʻutuʻuni.

(2) Kuo pau ki he tangi ke fakaha ʻa e ngaahi makatuʻunga ʻo e tangi.

(3) Kuo pau ki he Minisita, ʻi loto ʻi he ʻaho ngaue ʻe 28 mei hono maʻu ʻo e tangi, ke fakakaukauʻi ʻa e kotoa ʻo e ngaahi fakamatala felaveʻi kuo fakahoko ʻe he taha ʻoku laiseni pea tuʻutuʻuni ki he tangi.

(4) Kuo pau ki he Minisita ʻi loto ʻi he ʻaho ngaue ʻe 3 mei he ʻaho ʻo e tuʻutuʻuni ki hetangi, kefakahokoki he tahalaiseni ʻihafetuʻutaki tohi ʻaʻenetuʻutuʻuni.

#### 17 Taimi ʻoku kamata ngaueʻaki ai ʻa hono kaniseli

Kapau ʻoku kaniseli ʻe he Failesisita ha laiseni pisinisi, kuo pau ke ʻikai kamata ngaueʻaki ʻa e kaniseli kae ‘oleva ke ʻosi ʻa e taimi ʻa ia ʻe malava ai ke fakahoko ha tangi pea, kapau kuo fakahoko ʻa e tangi ko ia, pea ‘i he taimi ‘e toki fakahoko ai ha tuʻutuʻuni fakaʻosi ki he tangi ko ia.

#### 18 Ngaahi tautea

(1) Kapau ʻoku ʻikai faipau ʻa e taha laiseni ki he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni, ʻe ngofua ki he Failesisita ke hilifaki ha tautea pe totongi faile tomui ʻi hono tuʻutuʻuni ʻi he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni.

(2) Kapau ʻe hilifaki ʻe he Failesisita ha tautea pe totongi faile tomui ʻi he tuʻutuʻuni siʻi (1), kuo pau ki he Failesisita ke fakahoko ki he taha laiseni ʻi ha fetuʻutaki tohi.

#### 19 Fakapekia

ʻOku fakapekia ʻi heni ʻa e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ki he Ngaahi Laiseni Pisinisi [Vahe 17.01.01].
