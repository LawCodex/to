(1) ‘E ui ‘a e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ko ‘eni ko e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni (Fakapekia) Fakaha ‘o ha Tu’unga Fakatu’utamaki ki he Mo’ui (COVID-19) 2022.

(2) ‘I he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni, kuo pau ke ui ‘a e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni Fakaha ‘o ha Tu’unga Fakatu’utamaki ki he Mo’ui (COVID-19) 2020, ‘i hono fakatonutonu, ko e “Tefito’i Tu’utu’uni”. 2 Fakapekia

‘Oku fakapekia heni ‘a e Tefito’i Tu’utu’uni.
