# Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he kelekele (Fetuku ‘o e ‘one‘one)

Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Kelekele (Fetuku ‘o e ‘One‘one) VAHE. 20.01.04 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni (Regulations) 1. to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 3 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE KELEKELE (FETUKU 'O E 'ONE'ONE)1 Na'e fa'u 'i he mafai 'oku foaki 'e he Lao ki he Kelekele Kamata'anga [27 ‘o Ma’asi, 1936]

1. Ko e hingoa nounou ‘o e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ko e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he (Fetuku

‘o e ‘One‘one) Kelekele. 2 . Ko e ‘ave pe fetuku ‘o e ‘one‘one mei he matatahi tuku kehe ‘a e matatahi ki loto ‘i he ngaahi fakangatangata ‘o ha taulanga, ta‘e ma‘u ha tohi fakangofua kuo fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai ‘a e Minisita, ‘oku fakatapui. 3 . Ko e ‘ave pe fetuku ha ‘one‘one mei he Kelekele ‘o e Pule‘anga pe ha ‘api kehe ta‘e ha tohi fakangofua kuo fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai ‘a e Minisita ‘oku fakatapui.2 4 . Ka ai taha te ne ‘ave pe fetuku, ta‘e ma‘u ha tohi fakangofua mei he Minisita, ha

‘one‘one mei ha matatahi pe mei ha potu kehe ‘a ia ‘oku fakatapui ‘a e ‘ave pe fetuku ‘one‘one mei ai, ta‘e ma‘u ‘a e tohi fakangofua kuo lau ki ai, pea ka ai taha te ne fakangofua ke ‘ave pe fetuku ha ‘one‘one mei ha potu pehe, tuku kehe ‘a e taha kuo ne ma‘u ha tohi fakangofua ‘o hange ko ia kuo lau ki ai, kuo pau te ne halaia ki he hia, pea ‘e fakamaau pea ka ‘ilo ‘oku mo‘oni ‘e tautea pa‘anga ia ‘o ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $100, pea ka ‘ikai totongi, te ne nofo popula ki ha taimi ‘o ‘ikai lahi hake
