# Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Mafai Faka-Fakamaau‘anga ‘a e Kau Tau ‘a ‘Ene ‘Afio

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he mafai faka-fakamaau'anga 'a e Kau Tau 'a 'Ene 'Afio VAHE 9.02.03 to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 3 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE MAFAI FAKA- FAKAMAAU'ANGA 'A E KAU TAU 'A 'ENE 'AFIO Fa‘u ‘e he ‘Ene ‘Afio ‘i he mafai oku foaki ‘e he kupu 95 ‘o e Lao ki he Kau Tau ‘a ‘Ene ‘Afio 1 Kamata'anga [1 ‘o Sanuali 1994]

1. ‘E ui ‘a e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ko e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Mafai Faka- Fakamaau‘anga ‘a e Kau Tau ‘a ‘Ene ‘Afio.2

2. (1) ‘Oku ‘uhinga ‘a e fakalea “‘o e Lao” ‘i he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Lao ki he Kau Tau ‘a ‘Ene ‘Afio.3

(2) Ko e ngaahi fakalea ‘i he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni tukukehe ‘o ka toki ‘i ai ha

‘uhinga kehe ‘e ha, ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘e ia ‘a e ngaahi faka‘uhinga tatau ‘o hange pe ko ha konga ‘o e Lao ‘a e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni.

3. Ko ha fa‘u ‘o ha tohi faka‘ilo, ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 78 o e Lao kuopau ke ‘i he sipinga ‘oku fokotu‘u atu ‘i he Sipinga 1 ‘o e Tepile 1 ki he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni.

4. (1) Ko ha ‘ofisa pule ‘oku ne vakai‘i ha faka‘ilo ‘o fakatatau ki he Tu‘utu‘uni -si‘i

(5), kuopau te ne —

(a) Lau pea fakamatala ‘a e fakamatala faka‘ilo ki he faka‘iloa; pea

(b) (i) fanongo ia ki he fakamatala fakamo‘oni ‘o fakatatau mo e Tu‘utu‘uni 5; pe

(ii) fakahoko ke liliu ‘o hiki ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni, ‘o fakatatau mo e Tu‘utu‘uni 7, pea fakakaukau ki ai.

(2) ‘E tatau ai pe pe kuo fanongo ‘a e ‘ofisa pule ki he kotoa pe konga ‘o e fakamatalafakamo‘oni,‘engofuakenefakahokokeliliu‘ohiki‘aefakamatala fakamo‘oni.

(3) ‘E ngofua ki he ‘ofisa pule ‘i he ‘osi ‘a hono liliu ‘o tohi ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni pea mo ‘ene fakakaukau ki ai, ke fanongo ki he fakamatala fakamo‘oni ‘o fakatatau mo e Tu‘utu‘uni 5.

(4) Kimu‘a pea tuku atu ‘e ha ‘ofisa pule ha faka‘ilo ki he Fakamaau Faka-kau tau

‘o fakatatau ki he Kupu 79 ‘o e Lao, kuopau te ne fakahoko ke liliu ke hiki ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni ‘i he sipinga ‘o ha fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni ‘o fakatatau ki he Tu‘utu‘uni 6.

(5) ‘O ka ‘i ai ha fakamatala fakamo‘oni na‘e fakahoko ‘o fakatatau mo e Tu‘utu‘uni -si‘i (1) kuo toe ‘ilo‘i ai ha hia kehe mei he hia ‘a ia ‘oku fai ki ai ‘a e vakai‘i, ‘e ngofua ki ha faka‘ilo fo‘ou ke ngaue‘aki ki he faka‘iloa ‘o tanaki atu, pe fetongi‘aki ki he ‘uluaki faka‘ilo pea ‘e lau ‘a e ngaahi vakai‘i ‘o e

‘uluaki faka‘ilo, ki he taumu‘a ‘o e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni, ko hano vakai‘i ‘o e faka‘ilo ‘oku tanaki atu pe ‘oku fetongi‘aki.

Ka ‘i hono tanaki atu pe fetongi‘aki ha faka‘ilo fo‘ou kuopau ke ‘oange ki he faka‘iloa ha taimi ke fakakaukau ki he faka‘ilo fo‘ou ki mu‘a pea toki hoko atu

‘a e ngaue ‘a e ‘ofisa pule ki hono vakai‘i ‘o fakatatau mo e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘unini.

5. ‘Ihe fanongo kihe fakamatala fakamo‘oni ha ‘ofisa pule‘oku ne vakai‘i hafaka‘ilo —

(a) kuopau ke fakamatala ngutu ‘e fakamatala fakamo‘oni ‘a e fakamo‘oni talatalaaki kotoa pe ‘i he ‘i ai ‘a e faka‘iloa, pe ko e ngofua ki he ‘ofisa pule ke ne lau ki he faka‘iloa ha fakamatala hiki na‘e fai ‘e ha taha fakamo‘oni, tuku kehe ‘o ka toki fiema‘u ‘e he faka‘iloa ke fakamatala ngutu pe ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni.

(b) kuopau ‘e ngofua ke fehu‘i fakafihi ‘e he faka‘iloa ha fakamo‘oni talatalaaki;

(c) kuopau ke fakaha ange ‘e he ‘ofisa pule ki he faka‘iloa, ‘i he hili ‘a hono fakahoko ‘o e ngaahi fakamatala fakamo‘oni, ‘o pehe —

“‘Oku ‘i ai ha me‘a ‘oku ke fie lea ki ai. ‘Oku ‘iate koe ke ke fai pehe pe

‘ikai, ka, ‘o kapau ‘oku ke loto ki ai, ‘e ngofua ke ke fai ha fakamo‘oni fuakava, pe ko ha‘o fai ha fakamatala ta‘efuakava. Ko e ngaahi fakamo‘oni pe fakamatala kotoa pe te ke fai ‘e hiki ia ‘o tohi pea ‘e ngofua ke ‘o hake ia ko e fakamo‘oni.”

(d) ‘e ngofua ki he faka‘iloa, ki he‘ene fakahoko ma‘ana, ke ‘oatu ha fakamo‘oni fuakava pe ko ha fakamatala ta‘e fakafuakava;

(e) ‘e ngofua ki he faka‘iloa ke ui ha‘ane kau fakamo‘oni ki he‘ene taukapo;

(f) kuopau ke fakahoko ‘e he kau fakamo‘oni ma‘ae taukapo ‘enau fakamo‘oni ‘i he ‘iai ‘a e faka‘iloa;

(g) kuopau ‘e ‘ikai fakahoko ha fakamo‘oni fuakava pe fakapapau mamafa, tukukehe ka kuo tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he ‘ofisa pule pe fiema‘u ‘e he faka‘iloa;

(h) ka ‘o kapau ‘e fai fakafuakava‘i pe fakapapau mamafa ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni, kuopau ke hanga ‘e he ‘ofisa pule, ka ‘e fakatatau ki he totonu ‘a e faka‘iloa ke fai ha fakamatala ta‘e fakafuakava, ke fekau ke fakafuakava‘i pe fai ha fakapapau mamafa ‘a e fakamo‘oni kotoa pea mo e fakatonu lea ‘o fakatatau mo e Tu‘utu‘uni 12, kimu‘a pea toki fai ‘a

‘ene fakamo‘oni

6. Kuopau ke ‘i he sipinga ‘oku fokotu‘u ‘i he Sipinga 2 ‘i he Tepile 1 ki he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ‘a e fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni, pea kuopau ke fakahoko ia ‘e he ‘ofisa pule pe ko ha ‘ofisa kehe ‘i ha Tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e ‘ofisa pule, ‘i he ‘i ai ‘a e faka‘iloa.

7. (1) Ko hono fakahoko ‘o e fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni kuopau ke fai

‘i he founga ‘oku fokotu‘u atu ‘i he Tu‘utu‘uni ni.

(2) Kuopau ke fakamatala ngutu ‘a e took taha fakamo‘oni talatalaaki kotoa pe pea kuopau ke fakangofua ‘a e faka‘iloa ke ne fehu‘i fakafihi ha taha fakamo‘oni talatalaaki.

(3) ‘O kapau ko e ‘ofisa ‘oku ne fakahoko ‘a e fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni ‘oku ne fakamo‘oni‘i ‘i he tohi ‘oku —

(a) ‘ikai ke lava ke fekau ‘a e toko taha fakamo‘oni talatalaaki ke ha‘u; pe

(b) tu‘unga ‘i he fiema‘u ‘a e fatongia pe ko ha fa‘ahinga ‘uhinga totonu kehe ‘e ‘ikai ai ke malava ke fakahoko ‘a e taliui mai ‘a e fakamo‘oni talatalaaki.

‘e ngofua ke lau pe ki he faka‘iloa, ha fakamatala tohi ‘oku lau ne fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai e toko taha fakamo‘oni, pea ‘e fakakau ia ‘i he fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni.

(4) Ka kuo ‘i ai ha toko taha fakamo‘oni kuo ne fai ha fakamatala tohi ‘oku lava ke fekau ke ha‘u, ‘oku ngofua ki he faka‘iloa ke ne kole ke ha‘u ‘a e toko taha fakamo‘oni koe‘uhi ke ne fehu‘i fakafihi ia ‘i he‘ene fakamatala.

(5) ‘I he hili hono ‘ohake kotoa ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni tukuaki‘i ‘o e faka‘iloa, kuopau ke fakaha ‘e he ‘ofisa pule ki he faka‘iloa, ‘o pehe —

“‘Oku ‘i ai ha me‘a ‘oku ke fie lea ki ai. ‘Oku ‘iate koe ke ke fai pehe pe ‘ikai, ka, ‘o kapau ‘oku ke loto ki ai, ‘e ngofua ke ke fai ha fakamo‘oni fuakava, pe ko ha‘o fai ha fakamatala ta‘efuakava. Ko e ngaahi fakamo‘oni pe fakamatala kotoa pe te ke fai ‘e hiki ia ‘o tohi pea ‘e ngofua ke ‘ohake ia ko e fakamo‘oni.”

(6) Kuopau ke hiki ‘o tohi ha fakamo‘oni na‘e ‘ohake pe fakamatala na‘e fai ‘e he faka‘iloa, pea ke lau ki he faka‘iloa pea fakatonutonu ‘o ka fiema‘u. ‘E kole leva ki he faka‘iloa ke fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai. ‘O kapau ‘e ‘ikai ke fie Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he mafai faka-fakamaau'anga 'a e Kau Tau 'a 'Ene 'Afio Peesi 6 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai, kuopau ke hiki e me‘a ko ia ‘i he pepa pea fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai e ‘ofisa na‘a ne fai ‘a e fakama‘opo‘opo.

(7) ‘E ngofua ki he faka‘iloa ke ui ha‘ane kau fakamo‘oni ki hono taukapo. Ko ha taha fakamo‘oni kuo ui ‘e he faka‘iloa kuopau ke fakahoko fakamatala ngutu

‘a ‘ene fakamatala fakamo‘oni.

(8) ‘O kapau ko e ‘ofisa ‘oku ne fakahoko ‘a e fakama‘opo‘opo ‘oku ne fakamo‘oni‘i tohi ‘oku —

(a) ‘ikai ke lava ke fekau ‘a e tokotaha fakamo‘oni taukapo ke ha‘u; pe

(b) tu‘unga ‘i he fiema‘u ‘a e fatongia pe ko ha fa‘ahinga ‘uhinga totonu kehe ‘e ‘ikai ai ke malava ke fakahoko ‘a e taliui mai ‘a e fakamo‘oni taukapo.

‘e ngofua ke lau pe ki he faka‘iloa, ha fakamatala tohi ‘oku lau ne fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai e toko taha fakamo‘oni, pea ‘e fakakau ia ‘i he fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni.

(9) Kuopau ‘e ‘ikai ngofua ke fai ha fehu‘i fakafihi ‘o e faka‘iloa pe ko e kau fakamo‘oni ki he taukapo.

(10) Kuopau ke hiki ‘o tohi ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni kuo fakamatala ngutu ‘e he tokotahafakamo‘oni kotoa‘aetalatalaakipetaukapo(tukukehe‘ae faka‘iloa), pea lau ki he tokotaha fakamo‘oni pea fakatonutonu ‘oka fiema‘u. ‘E kole leva ki he tokotaha fakamo‘oni ke fakamo‘oni ki ai.

(11) ‘E ngofua ke lekooti ‘a e fakamo‘oni ‘i he sipinga fakamatala, tukukehe ka ko hafehu‘i na‘efai‘ihefehu‘i fakafihi ‘ohatokotahafakamo‘oni ‘ehefaka‘iloa, pea mo e tali na‘e fai ‘e ha toko taha fakamo‘oni kuopau ke lekooti ia ‘i he lea totonu na‘e fai ‘o kapau na‘e kole ki ai ‘a e faka‘iloa.

(12) Ko e ngaahi fakamatala fakamo‘oni ke fai fakafuakava‘i pe fakapapau mamafa pea fakatatau ki he totonu ‘a e faka‘iloa ke fai ha fakamatala ta‘efakafuakava, kuo pau ke fiema‘u ‘e he ‘ofisa ‘oku ne hiki ‘a e fakama‘opo‘opo, mei he tokotaha fakamo‘oni kotoa pe ke ne fai ‘a e fuakava pe fakapapau mamafa ‘o fakatatau ki he Tu‘utu‘uni 12, kimu‘a pea toki fai ‘a ‘ene fakamatala fakamo‘oni.

(13) Ka kuo fakakaukau ‘a e ‘ofisa ‘oku ne fai ‘a e fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni, ‘oku ‘ikai mahino ki ha tamasi‘i ‘oku kei ta‘u si‘i na‘e ui mai ko ha tokotaha fakamo‘oni ‘a e ‘uhinga ‘o ha fuakava, ‘e ngofua ke tali pe ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni ‘a e tamasi‘i neongo na‘e ‘ikai ke ‘ohake ‘i he fakafuakava, ‘o kapau, ‘i he fakakaukau ‘a e ‘ofisa ‘oku ne fai ‘a e fakama‘opo‘opo, ‘oku mahino pe ki he tamasi‘i ‘a e ‘uhinga ‘o e lea‘aki ‘a e mo‘oni pea ‘oku ne ma‘u mo ha poto fe‘unga ke poupou‘i‘aki ‘a hono tali ‘o ha fakamatala fakamo‘oni ‘e lava ke ‘omai ‘e he tamasi‘i.

(14) ‘I he ‘osi ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni, kuo pau ke hanga ‘e he ‘ofisa ‘oku ne fai

‘a e fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o tohi ‘o fakakapapau‘i ‘i he lau‘i peesi fakamuimui na‘a ne fai fakatatau mo e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni‘o e Tu‘utu‘uni ni.

8. ‘O ka kuo liliu ‘o hiki ‘o fakatatau mo e Tu‘utu‘uni 7 ‘ae fakamatala fakamo‘oni ‘oku kau ki he faka‘ilo, kimu‘a pea fakamaau‘i ‘a e faka‘ilo ‘e ha ‘ofisa pule —

(a) ka ‘i ai ha taha fakamo‘oni talatalaaki na‘e ‘ikai ke fakamatala ngutu ‘a

‘ene fakamo‘oni kuo pau ke ne fai leva ia ‘o ka ‘oku fiema‘u ‘e he faka‘ilo; pea

(b) kuo pau ke toe ‘oange ‘e he ‘ofisa pule ha faingamalie ki he faka‘iloa ke ne fai ha fakamatala fakamo‘oni fuakava pe fai ha fakamatala ta‘efuakava pea ke ne ui ha kau fakamo‘oni ki hono taukapo.

9. ‘O ka ‘i ai ha faka‘ilo, ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 79 ‘o e Lao, kuo tukuatu ‘e ha ‘ofisa pule ki ha Fakamaau Faka-kau tau ke hopo, kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘e he ‘ofisa pule ke

‘oatu ki he Fakamaau Faka-kau tau ha tatau ‘o e faka‘ilo ‘a ia ‘oku puke ai ‘a e faka‘iloa pea mo ha toe faka‘ilo pe ngaahi faka‘ilo ‘a ia ‘oku fakakaukau ‘e he ‘ofisa pule ‘oku totonu ke hopo ai ‘a e faka‘iloa.

10. ‘O ka ‘i ai ha faka‘ilo, ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 79 ‘o e Lao, kuo tukuatu ‘e ha ‘ofisa pule ki ha Fakamaau Faka-kau tau ke hopo, kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘e he ‘ofisa pule ke

‘oatu ki he tokotaha talatalaaki mo e taukapo kuo fakanofo ‘e he Poate ‘o fakatatau mo e kupu 82 ‘o e Lao —

(a) ha tatau ‘o e faka‘ilo ‘a ia ‘oku puke ai ‘a e faka‘iloa pea mo ha toe faka‘ilo pe ngaahi faka‘ilo ‘a ia ‘oku fakakaukau ‘a e ‘ofisa pule ‘oku totonu ke hopo ai ‘a e faka‘iloa;

(b) ko e fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni; pea

(c) mo ha fakamatala ki he ‘ulungaanga mo e lekooti faka-kau tau ‘o e faka‘iloa.

11. (1) ‘E ngofua ke Tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he ‘ofisa pule ha tokotaha fakamo‘oni ‘oku fakaongoongo ki he lao faka-kau tau ke taliui hake ki hano fai ‘o ha fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o e ngaahi fakamo‘oni.

(2) ‘E ngofua ke fekau ke ha‘u ha tokotaha fakamo‘oni ‘oku ‘ikai ke fakaongoongo ki he lao faka-kau tau ke kau mai ki ha fakama‘opo‘opo ‘o ha fakamo‘oni ‘aki ha fekau ‘i he mafai ‘o e ‘ofisa pule ‘o e faka‘iloa

(3) Kuo pau ke ‘i he sipinga ‘oku fokotu‘u atu ‘i he Sipinga 3 ‘i he Tepile 1 ki he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ‘a e fekau ko ia ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ai ‘a e Tu‘utu‘uni -si‘i

(2) pea kuo pau ke tufa ki he tokotaha fakamo‘oni ‘aki ‘a hono —

(a) tufa kiate ia pe; pe

(b) tuku ma‘ana ‘i ha taha ‘oku mahino kuo laka hake ‘i he ta‘u motu‘a ko e ta‘u 16 ‘i he feitu‘u maheni ‘oku nofo ai ‘a e tokotaha fakamo‘oni.

12. (1) ‘O fakatatau ki he Tu‘utu‘uni ni, ka ‘oku ‘i ai ha taha ‘o fakatatau mo e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ‘oku fiema‘u ke ne fai ha fuakava, kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘a e fuakava ‘i Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he mafai faka-fakamaau'anga 'a e Kau Tau 'a 'Ene 'Afio Peesi 8 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to he sipinga totonu pea ‘i he founga ‘oku fokotu‘u atu ‘i he Tepile 2 ki he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni.

(2) ‘E ngofua ke liliu ‘a e ngaahi lea kamata‘anga ‘o e fuakava ki ha ngaahi lea pea mo e ngofua ke fakahoko ‘a e fuakava ‘i he founga ko ia ‘a ia ‘oku lau ‘e he tokotaha ‘oku fuakava ‘oku ongo ki hono konisenisi ‘o fakatatau ki he‘ene tui fakalotu.

(3) ‘O kapau ‘oku ‘ikai ke fai ha fuakava, pea fakangofua ai ha taha ke ne fai pe ha‘ane fakapapau mamafa, kuo pau ke ‘i he sipinga ‘oku fokotu‘u atu ‘i he Tepile 2 ki he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ‘a e fakapapau ko ia.

13. Ko ha faka‘iloa kuo hilifaki ki ai ha tautea ‘e he‘ene ‘ofisa pule pea ‘oku loto ke fai ha tohi tangi ki he Poate ki hano toe vakai‘i ‘o hono Tu‘utu‘uni ke ne mo‘ua pea mo e tautea, ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 86 ‘o e Lao, kuo pau ke ne ‘oatu ki he Poate ha tohi tangi ‘i he sipinga ‘oku fokotu‘u atu ‘i he Tepile 3 ki he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uniʹni.
