# Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni (Monu'ia Ngaue) ki he Kau Tau Malu'i 'a Tonga

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni (Monu'ia Ngaue) ki he Kau Tau 'a 'Ene 'Afioʹ VAHE 9.02.04 Kupu 1 to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 5 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI (MONU'IA NGAUE) KI HE KAU TAU 'A 'ENE 'AFIOʹ1 Na e fa'u 'i he mafai 'o e Kupu 141 'o e Lao ki he Kaumafai kuo foaki 'i he kupu 141 'o e Lao ki he Kau Tau a 'Ene Afioʹ Kamata'anga [1 ‘o Siulai 2013]

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ui ‘a e Laoʹni ko e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni (Monu'ia Ngaue) ki he Kau Tau Malu'i 'a Tonga .

#### 2 ‘Uhingaʻi lea

(1) ‘I he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuniʹni tukukehe ka toe ‘i ai ha ‘uhinga kehe ‘i hono tuʻunga —

“Lao” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Lao ki he Kau Tau ʻa ʻEne ʻAfioʹ;

“taha maʻu ‘inasi” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he taha kuo fokotuʻu ‘e ha memipa ‘o e Kau Tau ‘i ha tohi tuku pe tohi fakamoʻoni ke ne maʻu ha monūʻia pekia ‘i he ngaue ‘i he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuniʹni ‘i ha pekia ‘a e memipa;

“Poate” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Poate Malu’i ‘oku fokotuʻu ‘i he Konga III ‘o e Lao;

“tamasiʻi tauhi” ‘oku ‘uhingaʹ ki ha tamasiʻi tauhi ‘o ha memipa, kau ai ha fanau ‘oku ohi fakalao pe ha fanau ‘i ha tuʻutuʻuni tauhi fakalaoʹ;

“‘ofisafaitoʻo”ʻoku‘uhingakihatoketā‘okufeʻungapealesisitakuofokotuʻu

‘e he Poate ke ne sivi ‘a e kau memipa ‘o e Kau Tauʹ;

“memipa” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ha ‘ofisa pe sotia ‘o e Kau Tau, pea kau ki ai ha sotia kuo fokotuʻu ki he Kaate Faka-Tuʻi ‘a Tonga, Malini Faka-Tuʻi ‘a Tonga pe Tau Tahi Faka-Tuʻi ‘a Tonga;

“hoa” ‘i he‘ene felaveʻi ki ha memipa tangata ‘oku ‘uhinga ki hono uaifi, pea

‘i he ‘ene felaveʻi ki ha memipa fefine ‘oku ‘uhinga ki hono husepaniti;

“uitou” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he hoa ‘o ha memipa ‘a ia ‘oku kei moʻui ‘i he pekia

‘o ha memipa; pea

“ngaahi kafo” pe “ngaahi lavelavea” ‘oku kau ki ai (ka ‘oku ‘ikai fakangatangata ki ai) ‘a e nunuʻa ‘o e felaveʻi mo e ivi fakaʻatomi, kasa pe ngaahi kemikale kona kehe.

(2) Ko e ngaahi fakamatala kehe ‘i he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuniʹni ‘okuʹne maʻu ‘a e

‘uhinga tatau ‘o hangē pe ‘oku hoko ‘a e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuniʹni ko e konga ‘o e Lao.
