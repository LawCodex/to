# Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ki he Kakai ‘o e Fonua (Toe Liliu ke Tonga) 2007

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Kakai 'o e Fonua (To e Liliu ke Tonga) Vahe 10.02.03 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 1. to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 3 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE KAKAI 'O E FONUA (TO E LILIU KE TONGA) Na'e fa'u 'e he Palemia 'i he loto ki ai 'a e Fakataha Tokoni 'i he mafai 'o e Kupu 15 'o e Lao ki he Kakai 'o e Fonua1 Kamata'anga [4 ‘o Sanuali, 2008]

1. (1) ‘E ui ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni´ ni ko e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Kakai ‘o e Fonua (To e Liliu ke Tonga).

(2) ‘Oku ngaue‘aki ‘a e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ki he kakai ‘a ia na‘e mole honau tangata‘i fonua Tonga kimu‘a ‘i he ‘aho kamata‘anga ‘o e Lao (Fakatonutonu) ki he Kakai ‘o e Fonua 2007.

2. (1) Kuo pau ke fakahu ‘a e ngaahi tohi kole kotoa pe ‘i he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ´ni ki he Minisita ki Muli pe ko ha ‘Ofisa faka-Tipilometika pe Konisela Tonga.

(2) Ko e tohi kole kotoa pe kuo pau —

(a) ke fakahoko ‘i he Foomu 1 ‘o e Tepile 1 ‘o e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni; pea

(b) ke fakahu fakataha ia mo e —

(i) lau‘i ta paasipooti ‘e 2;

(ii) tohi ta‘u ‘o e taha kole;

(iii) fakamo‘oni ‘o e tangata‘i fonua muli;

(iv) lipooti fakafaito‘o ‘o e taha kole mei ha toketa kuo lesisita;

(v) lekooti faihia ‘o e taha kole;

(vi) totongi ‘oku tu‘utu‘uni ‘i he Tepile 2 ‘o e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni; mo ha

(vii) ngaahi tohi ngaue kehe ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Minisita ki Muli ke fakakakato ‘aki ‘a e tohi kole ko ia.

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 3. Vahe 10.02.03 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Kakai 'o e Fonua (To e Liliu ke Tonga) Peesi 4 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to

(3) Ko e taha kole kotoa pe ke to e liliu ke Tonga, hili ‘a e fakahu ‘ene tohi kole kuo pau ke faka‘eke‘eke ia ‘e he Minisita ki Muli pe ko ha ‘ofisa kuo ne fakamafai‘i ke ne fakahoko ‘a e faka‘eke‘eke ko ia.

3. Ko e ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni ‘o ha to e liliu ke Tonga ‘a ia kuo foaki ‘o fakatatau ki he Kupu 17 ‘o e Lao ki he Kakai ‘o e Fonua kuo pau ke ‘i he Foomu 2 ‘o e Tepile 1.

4. (1) Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai foaki ha tohi fakamo‘oni ‘o e to e liliu ke Tonga kae‘oua kuo fakahoko ‘e he taha kole ‘a e fuakava liliu kakai ‘a ia ‘oku tu‘utu‘uni ‘i he Lao ki he Kakai ‘o e Fonua.

(2) Kuo pau ke fakahoko mo fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘a e fuakava liliu kakai ‘i he ‘ao

‘o e Palemia, Minisita ki Muli pe ko ha ‘Ofisa faka-tipilometika pe ‘Ofisa Konisela pe ha ‘Ofisa Pule‘anga Ma‘olunga kuo fakamafai‘i ‘i he tohi ‘e he Palemia.
