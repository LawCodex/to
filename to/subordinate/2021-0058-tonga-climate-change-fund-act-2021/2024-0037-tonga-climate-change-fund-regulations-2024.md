## KONGA 1 - TALATEU

#### 1 Hingoa Nounou

'E ui 'a e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ko ‘eni ko e Tuʻutuʻuni ki he Sinoʻi Paʻanga ʻa Tonga ki he Feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻEa 2024. 2 ʻUhingaʻi lea

(1) Kuo pau ke maʻu ʻe he ngaahi lea kuo fakaʻuhingaʻi ʻi he Lao pea ngaueʻaki ʻi he ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ko eni ʻa e ʻuhinga tatau mo ia ʻoku ha ʻi he Lao.

(2) ʻIhe ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ni,tuku kehe kaʻi ai ha ʻuhinga kehe ʻi hono tuʻunga, —

“Lao” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he Lao ki he Sinoʻi Paʻanga ʻa Tonga ki he Feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻEa 2021;

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 2 Tuʻutuʻuni ki he Sinoʻi Paʻanga ʻa Tonga ki he Feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻEa 2024 Peesi 10 to

“sino kuo fakamoʻoniʻi” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki ha sino ʻa ia kuo fakamoʻoniʻi ʻo fakatatau mo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni;

“ngaue” koeʻuhi ko e ngaahi taumuʻa ʻo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ni ʻoku ʻuhinga ki ha palani pau ʻi loto ʻi ha poloseki ʻa ia ʻoku ne tuku atu ha ola pea ʻe ngofua ke ʻuhinga ki ha ngaue pau kuo aleapau ki ai ʻi loto ʻi he ngaahi taumuʻa ʻo ha poloseki;

“tohi kole ke fakamoʻoniʻi” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he tohi kole fakahoko ngaue fakamoʻoniʻi ʻa ia kuo tuku atu ʻi he Konga VI ʻo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ni;

“Palani Langa Fakalakalaka Fakakolo” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he palani ki hono matuʻuaki ʻaki ʻa e ʻea ʻa ia kuo faʻu ʻe he kolo pea lesisita ʻi he Potungaue ki he Ngaahi Ngaue Fakalotofonua;

“ʻaho” pe “ngaahi ʻaho” koeʻuhi ko e ngaahi taumuʻa ʻo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he ngaahi ʻaho ngaue;

“fanongonongo ʻo e ngaahi mahuʻinga ʻuluaki fakafonua” koeʻuhi ko e ngaahi taumuʻa ʻo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni ʻoku ʻuhinga ki hono fakaha ʻe he Minisita ʻi he kupu 7(1) ʻo e Lao ʻa e ngaahi mahuʻinga ʻuluaki fakafonua ki hono fakasiʻisiʻi mo e fakalelei ki he feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻea;

“Talekita” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he Talekita ʻo e Vaʻa ki he Feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻEa ʻa ia kuo fokotuʻu ʻe he Minisita ʻoku ne tokangaekina ʻa e feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻea;

“takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki” koeʻuhi ko e ngaahi taumuʻa ʻo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he kotoa ʻo e takai ʻo e Sinoʻi Paʻanga poloseki ʻa Tonga ki he Feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻEa ʻa ia ʻoku kamata ʻi hono fakaha ʻo e ngaahi mahuʻinga fakafonua pea hokohoko atu ʻo aʻu ki ha vahaʻa taimi ʻo aʻu ki he taʻu ʻe 2, ʻa ia ʻoku kau ki ai ʻa e –

(a) fakaafe ki he ngaahi poloseki;

(b) ngaue ki hono fili;

(c) foaki ʻo e ngaahi poloseki;

(d) fakahoko ʻo e poloseki; mo e

(e) siofi mo vakaiʻi ʻo e poloseki;

“Puleʻanga” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he Puleʻanga ʻo Tonga;

“kautaha ʻa e Puleʻanga” pe “ngaahi kautaha ʻa e Puleʻanga” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ha ‘ofisi, sino pe vaʻa ngāue ‘a e Puleʻanga ‘o kehe ange mei ha Potungaue pe pisinisi ‘a e puleʻanga;

“fakafuofua uesia ʻo e ʻatakai” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki ha fakafuofua uesia ʻo e ʻatakai ʻa ia kuo fakahoko ʻi he Lao ki he Fakafuofua Uesia ʻo e ʻAtakai (Vahe 21.04);

“Tohi Ngaue ki hono Fakakakato ʻo e Poloseki” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he tohi ngaue ʻi he tuʻutuʻuni 42 ʻo fakamoʻoniʻi kuo kakato hono fakahoko ʻo ha poloseki;

“Minisita” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he Minisita ʻoku ne tokangaekina ʻa e feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻea;

“Potungaue” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he Potungaue ʻoku ne tokangaekina ʻa e feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻea;

## KONGA II – NGAAHI MAHUʻINGA ʻULUAKI FAKAFONUA KI

HE FELIULIUAKI ʻO E ʻEA

#### 3 Pulusi ʻa e Fanongonongo

(1) Kuo pau ke pulusi ʻe he Sekelitali ʻa e fanongonongo ʻo e ngaahi mahuʻinga ʻuluaki fakafonua ki he feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻea ʻa ia kuo fakahoko ʻe he Minisita ʻo fakatatau mo e kupu 7 ʻo e Lao ʻi loto ʻi he ʻaho ʻe 7 hili hono fakahoko ʻa e fanongonongo.

(2) Kuo pau ke fakahoko ʻa hono pulusi ʻo e fanongonongo –

(a) ʻi ha letio ʻa ia ʻoku lahi fakaʻaongaʻi ʻe he kakai;

(b) ʻi ha nusipepa ʻoku lahi hono tufaki ʻi Tonga; pe

(c) ʻi he uepisaiti ʻa e Potungaue.

(3) Ki he tuʻunga ʻe ala lava, kuo pau ke pulusi ʻa e fanongonongo ʻi he lea faka- Tonga mo e Faka-Pilitania fakatouʻosi.

## KONGA III – PULEʻI ʻO E SINOʻI PAʻANGA

#### 4 Ngaahi fatongia ʻo e Komiti Pule

(1) Kuo pau ke tali ui ʻa e Komiti Pule ki he Kapineti koeʻuhi ko hono puleʻi ʻo e Sinoʻi Paʻanga pea kuo pau ke fakapapauʻi ʻoku faitatau ʻa e ngaahi founga ngaue ke puleʻi ʻaki ʻa e Sinoʻi Paʻanga mo e Lao mo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni.

(2) Kuo pau ke tokanga‘i ʻe he Komiti Pule –

(a) ʻa e ngaahi ngafa mo e ngaahi fatongia kuo fokotuʻu atu ʻi he kupu 9 ʻo e Lao;

(b) hono tali ʻo e poloseki ʻa ia kuo fokotuʻu atu ʻi he Konga VII ʻo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ni; mo

(c) hono siofi ʻo e ngaue ʻa e Sekelitali pe Sekelitali Fakatahataha mo e ngaahi komiti ngaue ʻo e Sinoʻi Paʻanga ke fakapapauʻi ʻa e faipau ki he Lao mo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ni.

(3) ʻI he ngataʻanga ʻo e takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki taki taha, kuo pau ke teuteuʻi pea ʻoatu ʻe he Komiti Pule ha lipooti ki he Kapineti ʻo fekauʻaki mo e ngaahi meʻa ko ʻeni –

(a) ko e lahi mo e natula ʻo e ngaahi poloseki ʻa ia naʻe tali ʻi he takai kuo toki ʻosi;

(b) ko e paʻanga kuo foaki ki he taha fakahoko poloseki taki taha; mo e

(c) ngaahi ola ‘oku ‘amanaki ke hoko mo e pe ngaahi ola ‘e hoko tonu, ‘i heʻenau felaveʻi mo e ngaahi ʻuluaki mahuʻinga fakafonua kuo fanongonongo ʻe he Minisita ʻi he kupu 7 ʻo e Lao.

#### 5 Ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni tataki

ʻI hono fakahoko hono ngaahi fatongia ʻi he Lao, kuo pau ke fakakaukauʻi ʻe he Komiti Pule ʻa e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni tataki ko ʻeni –

(a) fakapapauʻi ʻoku –

(i) ngaueʻaki ʻa e Sinoʻi Paʻanga ʻo fakatefito pe ki he ngaahi taumuʻa kuo fakataumuʻa ki ai ʻi he Lao;

(ii) ngaueʻaki ʻe he Sekelitali mo e ngaahi komiti ngaue ʻa e ngaahi tuʻunga maʻolunga taha ʻo e faitotonu mo ʻikai filifilimanako lolotonga ʻa e takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki taki taha;

(iii) ʻata ki tuʻa kakato ʻa e kotoa ʻo e ngaahi sino fekauʻaki ʻi he kotoa ʻo e takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki taki taha;

(iv) ʻi ai ʻa e taliui tatau ʻi he vahaʻa ʻo e Sekelitali, ngaahi komiti ngaue ʻo e Sinoʻi Paʻanga mo e kau fakahoko poloseki; pea Tuʻutuʻuni ki he Sinoʻi Paʻanga ʻa Tonga ki he Feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻEa 2024 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 6 to Peesi 13

(v) ʻi hono fakahoko ‘o e ngaahi mafai, ʻoku ʻikai ha fepakipaki ‘a e kaunga

‘i he kotoa ‘o e ngaahi sino ‘a ia kuo fokotuʻu ‘i he Lao, ‘aki hono fokotuʻu ‘o ha ngaahi founga ke fakaʻehiʻehi mei he hoko ʻeni;

(b) ko e fiemaʻu ke –

(i) ngaueʻaki ha ngaahi tuʻunga pule‘i totonu mo ha ngaahi fokotuʻutuʻu puleʻi fakapaʻanga ʻoku malohi ke fakapapauʻi ʻa e fakalakalaka mo e faʻu ʻo e ngaahi faʻunga pule fakalotofonua; pea

(ii) fakafaingofua ʻa e fengaueʻaki lelei mo e taha tokoni ‘i loto ‘i he tuʻunga

‘o e ngaahi faʻunga puleʻi ki ha tokoni lahi ange, koeʻuhi ke fakaʻehiʻehi mei hono hiki tatau ʻo e ngaahi fakamole; mo e

(c) mahuʻinga hono tanumaki ʻo e ivi malava ʻo e niʻihi fakahoko poloseki mo e ngaahi tukui kolo fakafeituʻu ke fakapapauʻi ʻa e kau mai ʻoku longomoʻui lolotonga ʻa e kotoa ʻo e takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki.

## KONGA IV – TAKAI ʻO E PAʻANGA POLOSEKI

#### 6 Vahaʻa taimi ʻo e takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki

(1) ʻI he kamataʻanga ʻo e takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki, kuo pau ke fokotuʻu atu ʻe he Sekelitali ha ngaahi ʻaho fakataha maʻae ngaahi komiti ngaue ʻo e Sinoʻi Paʻanga ki he taʻu fakatohimahina taki taha, ke faipau ki he ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻo e Lao.

(2) Neongo ʻa e ngofua ke kehekehe siʻi ʻa e fakahokohoko ʻo e ngaahi meʻa ʻi loto ʻi ha takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki ʻi he taʻu fakatohimahina taki taha, kuo pau ke fakapapauʻi ʻe he Sekelitali ʻoku muimui ʻa e vahaʻa taimi ʻo e takai ʻo e paʻanga poloseki ki he faʻunga kuo fokotuʻu atu ʻi he Tepile 1.

## KONGA V – TALI ʻA HONO FAʻU ʻO E PATISETI ʻO E

POLOKALAMA KI HE SINOʻI PAʻANGA

#### 7 Sekelitali ke ne faʻu ha patiseti ʻo e polokalama ki he sinoʻi paʻanga

(1) Ko e Sekelitali,ʻi hefealeaʻaki moe Talekita,kuopauke netokangaekina hono faʻu ʻo e patiseti ʻo e polokalama ki he sinoʻi paʻanga ʻa ia kuo pau ke ne aʻusia ʻa e ngaahi mahuʻinga ʻuluaki fakafonua ki he feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻea ʻa ia kuo fanongonongo ʻe he Minisita ʻi he kupu 7 ʻo e Lao.

(2) Kuopaukevaheʻi ‘ehepatisetikihepolokalama ‘oesinoʻi paʻangahapaʻanga ki he ngaahi mahuʻinga ʻuluaki ki he feliuliuaki ʻo e ʻea ʻa ia kuo fakamahino pau pe ngaahi ʻelia tefito ‘i he fotunga kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻi he Tepile 2.

(4) ʻE ngofua ke kau ha poloseki ‘e taha pe lahi ange pe ko ha ngaahi poloseki iiki ki ha polokalama.

(c) Palani Ngaue Fakalakalaka Vahaʻa Taimi Loloa ke Fakasiʻisiʻi ʻa e Mafola; mo

(d) ha tohi ngaue palani fakafonua kehe kuo tali ʻa ia ʻoku pehe ʻe he Minisita ʻoku taau.

(6) ʻI he fai pau ki hono faʻu, kuo pau ke fakahu atu ʻe he Talekita ʻa e patiseti ʻo e polokalama ki he sinoʻi paʻanga kuo fokotuʻu atu ki he Komiti Pule ke tali ʻo fakatatau mo e tuʻutuʻuni 8.

paʻanga

(2) ʻI hono fakakaukauʻi ke tali pe ʻikai tali ʻa hono faʻu ʻo ha patiseti ʻo e polokalama ki he sinoʻi paʻanga, kuo pau ke vakaiʻi ʻe he Komiti Pule ʻa e ngaahi tefitoʻi taumuʻa tatau ʻa ia kuo fokotuʻu atu ʻi he tuʻutuʻuni 5.

(3) Hili hono tali hono faʻu ʻo ha patiseti ʻo e polokalama ki he sinoʻi paʻanga kuo fokotuʻu atu, kuo pau ke tuku atu ʻe he Komiti Pule ha tuʻutuʻuni tohi ʻo fokotuʻu ai ʻa e ngaahi meʻa ko ʻeni –

(a) fakakatoa ʻo e mahuʻinga fakapaʻanga kuo vaheʻi ki he polokalama pe poloseki taki taha;

(b) fakahokohoko ʻo e ngaahi totongi mo e ngaahi ola kuo tuʻutuʻuni (kapau ʻoku ngaueʻaki ki ai); mo e

(c) vahaʻa taimi ki hono fakahoko ʻo e polokalama pe poloseki taki taha kuo faʻu.
