# Tu‘utu‘uni ki he (Ngaahi Totongi) ‘o e Kolonitini ki he ‘Akau 1997

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 2 VAHE 19.06.03 Tu'utu'uni ki he (Ngaahi Totongi) 'o e Ngaahi Kolonitini ki he 'Akau Peesi 2 TOHI LAO FAKATONUTONU 2020 to konga pe ‘o ia.

FAKA‘AUHA ‘O E VEVE Faka‘auha tutu ‘o ha koloa fakakolonitini e.g. ko e ngaahi vaka tau $0.40 ki he kilokalami.

FUFULU MAO Ki he fo‘i uta ‘e taha $15.00 ki he houa pe ha konga ‘o ia.

TAUHI FAKAMOKOMOKO Loki fakamokomoko/Puha ‘Aisi $0.02 ki he kilokalami ki he houa ‘e 24 kotoa pe, pe ha konga ‘o ia.

Filisa (Freezer) $0.04 ki he kilokalami ki he ‘uluaki houa ‘e 24 pea $0.02 ki he kilokalami ki he houa

‘e 24 pe ha konga ‘o ia

SIVI HONO HU MAI PE HU ATU

(a) Ngaahi Tohi Fakamo‘oni (Tohi Fakamo‘oni mo e Tohi Fakangofua) $4.00

(b) Sivi ‘o ha Koniteina $2.00

(c) Sivi ‘o e saliote misini $4.00

‘i he miniti ‘e 15) $2.00

#### 2C KOLONITINI HILI ‘A E TU‘UTA

(a) ‘Elia ‘o e Tepile ki he mahina (ki he 0.5m3 pe ha konga ‘o ia) $7.75

(b) Ngaahi me‘a ke tupu ai ‘a e ‘akau, kemikale pea mo ha ngaahi fakamole kehe fekau‘aki mo ia Fakakatoa ‘o e fakamole

#### 2D ‘OVA TAIMI

(a) Sivi mo hono faka‘ataa ‘o e vakapuna mo e kau pasese ‘i he ngaahi mala‘e vakapuna;

(i) ngaahi ‘aho ngofua $4.00 ki he houa

(ii) aho Tokonaki, Sapate pea mo e ngaahi ‘aho malolo $5.00 ki he houa

(b) Ngaahi ngaue kehe mei he Sivi mo hono faka‘ataa ‘o e vakapuna mo e kau pasese ‘i he ngaahi mala‘e vakapuna $2.00 ki he houa

#### 2E NGAAHI TOTONGI MAKEHE

(a) Noo misini hiki mo e faka‘uli $25.00 ki he houa

(b) Ko ha ngaue kehe ‘oku fai ka ‘oku ‘ikai fakamahino‘i hono totongi ‘i he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni $4.00 ki he ‘ofisa ki he haafe houa pe ha konga ‘o ia kau ai ‘a e taimi fefononga‘aki.
