# Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni (Tautea Mate) ki he Ngaahi Hia

NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI (TAUTEA MATE) KI HE NGAAHI HIA1 Na'e fa‘u 'e he 'Ene 'Afio 'i he Fakataha Tokoni 'i he 'aho 24 'o Sune 1955 Kamata‘anga [24 ‘o Sune, 1955]

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ui ‘a e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni´ni ko e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni (Tautea Mate) ki he Ngaahi Hia.

#### 2 Tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Minisitā Polisi

Ko e fakahoko ‘o e tautea mate kotoa pe ‘oku pau ke pule‘i ‘e he Minisitā Polisi ‘e ui ki mui ‘i heni ko e Minisitā ‘a ia te ne fai ha ngaahi fekau mo fai ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni te ne pehē ‘e fe‘unga.

#### 3 Ko e finangalo ‘a ‘ene ‘Afio ki he Tautea

Hili hano fakaha ‘e he Tu‘i kuo finangalo ‘i he loto ki ai ‘a e Fakataha Tokoni ki ha tautea mate ‘oku pau ke fakaha ia ‘e he Fakataha Tokoni ki he Minisita ‘i he tohinima pea ‘oku pau ke fai leva ‘e he Minisita ‘a e ngaahi me‘a kotoa pe ‘oku totonu ke fai koe‘uhi ke fakahoko ‘a e tautea ko ia ‘o ‘oua na‘a toe fakatuotuai.

#### 4 Feitu‘u ke fakahoko ai ‘a e tautea.

Ko e tautea mate kotoa pe ‘e fakahoko ia ‘i he pilisone ‘i he vahe fonua ‘a ia na‘e hilifaki ai ‘a e tautea ‘o e taha fai hia pe i ha pilisone kehe ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Minisita ‘i ha‘ane fakakaukau ke fai pehē.

#### 5 Taimi ‘o e fakahoko ‘o e tautea

Ko e tautea mate kotoa pe ‘oku pau ke fakahoko ia ‘i he taimi 8 ‘i he pongipongi ‘o ha ‘aho ngofua tuku kehe ‘a e Monite tuku kehe kapau koe‘uhi ko ha ‘uhinga makehe ‘e pehē ai ‘e he Minisita ‘oku totonu ai ke fai ‘a e tautau ‘i ha taimi kehe pe

‘i ha ‘aho kehe. Kapau ‘e pehē ‘oku pau ‘i he ‘uluaki faingamatie ‘e lava ai ia ‘i he hili ‘a e tautau ke fakaha ‘i he tohinima ‘e he Minisita ki he Fakataha Tokoni ‘a e

‘aho mo e taimi ‘o e tautau mo e ‘uhinga ‘o e faikehe mei he tu‘utu‘uni ‘oku ngaue‘aki.

#### 6 Misini

Ko e ‘atu mo e fokotu‘u ‘o e misini ki he fakahoko ‘o e tautau ‘oku pau ke fai ia ‘e he Potungaue Ngaue ‘i he feongoi mo e Minisita. ‘Oku pau ke tokanga‘i ke ‘oua na‘a lava ‘o sio ‘a e kau popula kehe pe ha ni‘ihi ‘o e kakai ki he tautau.

#### 7 Veteki mo hono Tauhi ‘o e Misini

‘I he hili hono fai ‘o e tautau ‘oku pau ke veteki ‘a e misini ko ia ‘e he Potungaue Ngaue pea tauhi ‘i he feitu‘u ‘a ia ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Minisita.

#### 8 Minisita ke Feongoi mo e Talekita Ngaue

‘I hono fa‘u, fokotu‘u, veteki mo e tauhi ‘o e misini ko ia ‘oku pau ke feongoi ‘a e Minisita mo e Talekita Ngaue.

#### 9 Minisita ke ne fakaha ki he sela

‘I he fiemalie leva pe ‘a e Minisita kuo kakato lelei ‘a e ngaahi teuteu kotoa pe ki he fakahoko ‘o e tautea ‘oku pau ke ne fakaha ki he Sela ‘oku pule ‘i he Pilisone ‘i he tohinima ‘a e ‘aho mo e taimi ‘e fai ai ‘a e tautau.

#### 10 Fatongia ‘o e sela

Ko e fatongia ‘o e Sela ko ia, fakatatau ki ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Minisita, ke vakai ke fai fakalelei ‘a e tautau.

#### 11 Minista ke fakatokanga

‘Oku pau ke ‘atu ‘e he Minisita ‘o ‘ikai si‘i ange ‘i he houa ‘e 24 ha fakatokanga ‘i he tohinima ‘a e feitu‘u, ‘aho mo e taimi kuo tu‘utu‘uni ki he tautau, ki he Fakamaau Polisi ‘o e vahefonua ko ia, ki he Talekita Mo‘ui pe ki he ‘Ulu ‘o e fa‘ahinga lotu

‘oku kau ki ai ‘a e taha fai hia, ‘o fakaha ki ai ko e toko taha fai hia ‘oku kau ki hono siasi pea kuo ne fakaha pe ‘ikai (hange ko ia ‘e hoko) ‘oku ne loto ke ‘i he fai ‘o e tautau ha Faifekau ‘o e Siasi ko ia.

#### 12 Ko kinautolu ‘oku ngofua ke ;i he fakahoko ‘o e tautea.

Ko e kakai pe ‘eni ‘oku pau ke nau ‘i he tautau mo mamata ai —

(a) ko e Minisita pe ha taha pe ni‘ihi te ne fakanofo ke fakafofonga ia;

(b) ko e Talekita Mo‘ui pe ha ‘ofisa faito‘o kehe te ne fakanofo;

(c) ko e Sela mo ha kau ‘ofisa kehe ‘o e pilisone ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Minisitā;

(d) ha faifekau ‘o e fa‘ahinga lotu ‘oku kau ki ai ‘a e taha fai hia ‘a ia ‘e fokotu‘u ‘e he ‘Ulu ‘o e lotu ko ia;

(e) ha ‘Ofisa Polisi ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Minisita.

#### 13 Ke maau pe ‘akinautolu ‘i he ‘api popula

‘I he ‘aho kuo tu‘utu‘uni ki he tautau ‘e ngofua ki he Sela ke ne fai ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ki he fe‘alu‘aki ‘a e kau popula ‘i he loto pilisone mo e faka‘atā ‘o e ni‘ihi

‘o e kakai ki loto pilisone ‘a ia te ne pehē ‘oku totonu mo fe‘unga.

#### 14 Fakamaau ‘eke

‘I hono ma‘u ‘o e fakatokanga ‘oku lau ki he Tu‘utu‘uni 11 ‘o ‘eni ‘oku pau ke fai ‘e ha Fakamaau Polisi ‘o e vahefonua ‘a e ngaahi me‘a kotoa pe ‘oku totonu ke fai ki he fai ‘o e fakamaau ‘eke ‘o fakatatau mo e kupu 37 ‘o e Lao ki he Ngaahi Hia.

#### 15 Ko e anga hono teuteu ‘o e fai hia

‘I he te‘eki fai ‘a e tautau ‘oku pau ke ha‘i ‘a e taha fai hia mo ‘ufi‘ufi hono mata ‘o fakatatau mo e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘e fai ‘e he Minisita.

#### 16 Ngaahi fakahāhā mo e ngaahi faka‘ilonga kuo lava ‘a e fakahoko ‘o e

tautea

‘I he kamata leva pe ke fai ‘a e tautau ‘oku pau ke ta ‘a e fafangu ‘o e fale lotu ‘o e pilisone ‘o hange ko e ta fafangu ki he fai ‘o ha putu pea ko e fuka ‘i he matapā lahi

‘o e pilisone ‘oku pau ke tuku hifo ia ke toumoloto pea ‘e toumoloto ai pe ia ‘o a‘u ki he to ‘a e la‘a he ‘aho ko ia.

#### 17 Tanu‘anga

‘Oku pau ke tanu ‘a e ‘anga‘anga ‘o e taha fai hia ‘i he loto pilisone ‘o e pilisone na‘e fai ai ‘a e tautau ‘i he feitu‘u ko ia ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Minisita. ‘Oku pau ke lau

‘a e Tu‘utu‘uni ni ko e fokotu‘u ia ‘o e feitu‘u tanu‘anga ‘o hange ko e kupu 38 ‘o e Lao ki he Ngaahi Hia.
