# Tu‘utu‘uni Fakataha Tokoni (Ngaahi Fonua kuo vahe‘i ‘o Fokotu‘u) ki he Fakafoki Faka-Pule‘anga ‘o e kau Fai Hia

Tu'utu'uni Fakataha Tokoni (Ngaahi Fonua kuo vahe'i 'o Fokotu'u) ki he Fakafoki Faka-Pule'anga 'o e kau Fai Hia VAHE 3.07.01 Tu‘utu‘uni 1 to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 3 C T TU'UTU'UNI FAKATAHA TOKONI (NGAAHI FONUA KUO VAHE'I 'O FOKOTU'U) KI HE FAKAFOKI FAKA-PULE'ANGA 'O E KAU FAI HIA Na'e fa'u 'e he'Ene 'Afio 'i he Fakataha Tokoni 'aki 'a e ngaahi mafai kuo foaki 'e he kupu 4(1) 'o e Lao ki he Fakafoki Fakapule'anga 'o e Kau Faihia1

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ala ui ‘a e Tu‘utu‘uni Fakataha Tokoni ko ‘eni ko e Tu‘utu‘uni Fakataha Tokoni (Ngaahi Fonua kuo vahe‘i ‘o Fokotu‘u) ki he Fakafoki Faka-Pule‘anga ‘o e kau Fai Hia.

#### 2 Fakafoki fakapule‘anga ‘o e kau fai hia

Ko e ngaahi fonua kuo fakamahino‘i ‘i he Tepile ki heni kuo vahe‘i ‘o fokotu‘u koe‘uhi ko e ngaahi kaveinga ‘o e Kupu 4 kupusi‘i (1) ‘o e Lao ki he Fakafoki Faka- Pule‘anga ‘o e Kau Fai Hia.‘E ngofua ‘a e kole mei he ngaahi fonua ko ia ke fakafoki fakapule‘anga ange ha taha faihia ‘aki ha lekooti ‘o e hopo ‘o hange ko e polovaisoa ki he kupu 7(2) ‘o e Lao ki he Fakafoki Fakapule‘anga ‘o e Kau Faihia.2

#### 3 ‘Iunaiti Siteiti ‘o ‘Amelika

Ko e ‘Iunaiti Siteiti ‘o ‘Amelika kuo vahe‘i ‘o fokotu‘u heni koe‘uhi ko e ngaahi kaveinga ‘o e kupu-si‘i (1) ‘o e Kupu 4 ‘o e Lao ki he Fakafoki Faka-Pule‘anga ‘o e Kau Fai Hia ki he loloa e taimi pea mo hono ngaue‘aki ‘o e Talite ki he Fakafoki faka-Pule‘anga ‘o e kau Fai Hia ‘a ia na‘e fai ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e Pule‘anga Pilitania mo e Pule‘anga ‘Iunaiti Sitete ‘o ‘Amelika pea na‘e Fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai ‘i Tu'utu'uni Fakataha Tokoni (Ngaahi Fonua kuo vahe'i 'o Fokotu'u) ki he Fakafoki Faka-Pule'anga 'o e kau Fai Hia Peesi 4 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to Lonitoni ‘i he ‘aho 22 ‘o Tisema, 1931 pea kuo kau ki ai ‘a Tonga ‘i he ‘aho 1 ‘o

‘Aokosi, 1966 pea mo e Aleapau Faka-Pule‘anga na‘e fakahoko ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e Pule‘anga Tonga mo e Pule‘anga ‘Iunaiti Sitete ‘o ‘Amelika felave‘i mo hono tuku atu ‘o e kakai ki he Fakamaau‘anga Fakavaha‘apule‘anga ki he Kau Faihia ‘a ia na‘e fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai ‘i Uasingatoni D.C. he ‘aho 21 ‘o Ma‘asi 2003.3

‘E ngofua ke ‘a e kole mei he ‘Iunaite Siteiti ‘o ‘Amelika ke fakafoki fakapule‘anga ange ha taha faihia ‘aki ha lekooti ‘o e hopo ‘o hange ko e polovaisoa ki he kupu 7(2) ‘o e Lao ki he Fakafoki Fakapule‘anga ‘o e Kau Faihia.4

#### 4 Fetalolo Lepupilika ‘o Siamane

‘Oku vahe‘i heni ‘a e Fetalolo Lepupilika ‘o Siamane koe‘uhi ko e ngaahi kaveinga

‘o e Kupu 4 kupusi‘i (1) ‘o e Lao ki he Fakafoki Fakapule‘anga ‘o e Kau Faihia 1972 ‘o a‘u ki he fuoloa mo hono ngaue‘aki ‘a e Talite ‘o e ‘aho 14 Me 1872 ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o Siamane pea mo Pilitania ke na fakatou Tukuatu ‘a e Kau Faihia Hola, ‘i hono fakatonutonu ‘e he Aleapau ‘o e ‘aho 23 Fepueli 1960 ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e Pule‘anga Fetalolo Lepupilika ‘o Siamane mo e Pule‘anga Pilitania mo e Pule‘anga

‘Ailani Tokelau ki he Fakafoki Fakapule‘anga ‘o e Kau Faihia Hola pea ke ngaue‘aki ‘e he Pule‘anga Tonga ‘a e ngaahi liliu fakafo‘ou ko ‘eni:

(a) Ko e ngaahi vahefonua eni kuo pau ke ngaue‘aki ki ai ‘a e Talite ‘o e

‘aho 14 Me 1872 ko e Pule‘anga Tonga ‘i he fa‘ahi ‘e taha, pea ko e Fetalolo Lepupiiika ‘o Siamane ‘i he fa‘ahi ‘e taha. Ko e ngaahi lave kotoa ki he ngaahi vahefonua ‘o e ongo Fa‘ahi Aleapau ‘o e Talite 1872 mo e Aleapau 1960 kuo pau ke mahino ‘o hange ko ‘eni.

(b) Ko e hokohoko ‘o e ngaahi hia ‘oku ala fakafoki fakapule‘anga ai ‘a e kau fai hia ‘o fakatatau ki he ‘Atikolo III ‘o e Aleapau ‘o e ‘aho 23 Fepueli 1960 kuo pau ke fakalahi ‘o kau ai ‘a e puke fakamalohi ‘o e mo e fakatu‘utamaki‘i ki he malu ‘o ha vakapuna, ngaahi hia fakatatau ki he Konivesio ‘o e ‘aho 14 Tisema 1973 ki hono Ta‘ofi mo hono Tautea ‘o e Ngaahi Hia ki he Taha ‘oku malu‘i Fakavaha‘apule‘anga, kau ai ‘a e kau fakafofonga Fakatipilometi, mo ha ngaahi hia kehe kotoa pe ‘a ia ‘oku ala fakafoki fakapule‘anga ai ‘a e kau Faihia ‘e ala foaki ‘e he lao ‘o e ongo Fa‘ahi Aleapau.

(c) ‘Atikolo IV ‘o e Aleapau ‘o e ‘aho 23 Fepueli 1960 kuo pau ke lau ‘o pehe ni:

Kuo pau he ‘ikai fiema‘u e ongo Fa‘ahi Aleapau ke na fakafoki la e kakai ‘o hona fonua. Ko e ma‘u mafai fe‘unga ‘o ha Puleanga na‘e kole kuo pau, ko ia ai, ‘oku ‘i ai ‘ene totonu ke foaki ha tautea fakafoki faka- pule‘anga ki ha kakai ‘o hono fonua pe ‘o‘ona kapau ‘oku ne pehe ‘oku totonu pea kapau ko e tautea fakafoki faka-pule‘anga ‘o e kau faihia

‘oku ‘ikai fepaki mo e konisitutone ‘o e Pule‘anga na‘e kole. Kuo pau he ‘ikai ha fa‘ahi ‘e fiema‘u ‘e he Talite ko ‘eni ke ne fakafoki ha taha kuo faka‘ilo ‘a ia ko e memipa ‘o e kau tau malu‘i fonua ‘o ha Pule‘anga kehe ‘oku nau nofo ‘i ha vahefonua ‘o e fonua na‘e kole. (d) Ka ‘i ai ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘o e lao ‘a e Pule‘anga na‘e kole ki hono fakatapui ‘o e fakafoki fakapule‘anga ‘o e kau faihia kuo pau ke vakai‘i.

(e) Kuo felotoi foki kuo pau ‘e likai hanga ‘e he fokotu‘utu‘u lolotonga ni

‘o ta‘ofi ha ngaahi sino‘i fakataha alea ‘a ha fa‘ahi mei hono fakahoko ha ngaahi Lao makehe ‘o ha Pule‘anga pea kuo pau ke fakaha ki he Pule‘anga ‘e taha ‘i he taimi ‘oku ‘amanaki ai ke ngaue‘aki ha Lao pea, kapau ‘oku fiema‘u, ke fakatonutonu ‘a e fokotu‘utu‘u lolotonga ni ‘e alea‘i leva.
