#### 1 Hingoa mo e Kamata‘anga

(1) ‘E ui ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ni ko e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni (Veteki) ki he Ngaahi Kautaha.

(2) Kuo pau ‘e kamata ngāue‘aki ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni´ ni ‘i ha ‘aho ‘e fakaha ‘e he ‘Ene ‘Afio ‘i he Fakataha Tokoni pea ‘e ngofua ke kamata ngaue‘aki ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni kehekehe ‘i ha ngaahi ‘aho kehekehe.

#### 2 ‘Uhinga‘i Lea

(1) ‘I he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni´ ni tuku kehe ‘oka ha atu ha toe ‘uhinga kehe ‘i hono tu‘unga —

“ko e Lao” ‘oku ‘uhinga ia ki he Lao ki he Ngaahi Kautaha;

“kautaha” ‘oku ‘uhinga ia ki he kautaha ‘oku lolotonga veteki;

“Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi” ‘oku ‘uhinga ia ki he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘oku fa‘u mei he taimi ki he taimi ‘o fakatatau ki he Lao ki he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi; pea ko e

“pangike” ‘oku ‘uhinga tatau mo ia ‘oku ha ‘i he Lao ki he Pangike Pule Fakafonua ‘a Tonga.

#### 8 Fakatonutonu ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e likuiteta fekau‘aki mo e ‘eke

Ka ‘i ai ha likuiteta,o fakatatau ki he kupu 313(3) pe kupu 314 ‘o e lao, te ne fakafoki pe fakatonutonu ‘ene tu‘utu‘uni ke tali-pe ta‘e tali fakakatoa pe fakakonga ha ‘eke, kuo pau ke lekooti ‘e he likuiteta ‘a e fakatonutonu ‘o ‘ene tu‘utu‘uni ko ia ‘i he tohi.

#### 9 Ngaahi tisikauni faka-fefakatau‘aki

Kuo pau ki he kuletitoa ‘oku ne fai ha ‘eke ke ne to‘o ‘a e ngaahi tisikauni faka- fefakatau‘aki ‘a ia na‘a´ ne mei ‘atu kapau na‘e ‘ikai veteki ‘a e kautaha´.

#### 10 Ngaahi totongi fakataimi pau

(1) Ka ‘i ai ha fa‘ahinga totongi (kau ai ‘a e totongi nofo) ‘oku totonu ke fai ‘i ha vaha‘a taimi pau, ka ‘oku kamata ‘a e veteki ia ‘i ha taimi kehe ‘o ‘ikai ‘i he kamata‘anga‘o hataha‘oengaahi vaha‘ataimi pau pea‘engofua kiakinautolu

‘oku totongi ki ai ke nau ‘eke ‘o a‘u ki he ‘aho na‘e kamata ai ‘a e veteki ‘e hangē ‘oku tanaki mai fakafo‘i ‘aho ‘a e totongi ‘oku te‘eki totongi ko ia.

(2) Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai ha me‘a ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni-si‘i (1) te ne uesia ‘a e totonu ‘a e lesoa ‘o e koloa ke ne ‘eke‘i ha totongi nofo ‘oku tānaki mai ‘i he pe hili ‘a e

‘aho ‘oku kamata ai ‘a e veteki.

#### 11 Ngaahi ‘eke ‘a e kau ngāue

(1) ‘E ngofua ke fai ‘e ha taha ha ‘eke ‘o fakafofonga‘i ‘a e kotoa pe ha ni‘ihi ‘o e kau ngāue ‘a e kautaha.

(2) Kuo pau ke ‘ai ha tepile ‘o e ngaahi hingoa ‘o e kau ngāue ko ia, mo e lahi ‘o e pa‘anga ‘oku totonu ke nau ma‘u takitaha ‘o fakapipiki ki he ‘eke.

(3) Ka fai ha ‘eke ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni´ ni kuo pau ke hangē pe ia na‘e fai ha ngaahi ‘eke kehekehe ‘e he tokotaha ngaue taki taha.

#### 12 Fanongonongo ki he kau kuletitoa ke nau ‘eke

(1) Fakatatau ki he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Lao pea tuku kehe ka fai ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ha tu‘utu‘uni kehe, ‘e ngofua ki he likuiteta ke ne fokotu‘u ha fo‘i ‘aho pau, ‘a ia kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai toe si‘i ange ‘i he ‘aho ngaue ‘e 10 mei he

‘aho ‘o e fanongonongo ke fai ai pe kimu‘a ai ‘e he kau kuletitoa ‘o e kautaha

‘enau ngaahi ‘eke, pea ke nau fokotu‘u ha‘anau ‘eke fakamu‘omu‘a ‘okapau

‘oku ala ma‘u ‘e he‘enau ‘eke ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 321 ‘o e Lao.

(2) Kuo pau ki helikuitela ke ne ‘atu hafanongonongo fakapule‘anga ‘o e ‘aho kuo fokotu‘u ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni-si‘i (1).

(2) Ka ‘i ai ha kuletitoa ‘oku ne fai ‘ene ‘eke kimui ‘i he ‘aho kuo fokotu‘u ‘o fakatatau he tu‘utu‘uni 12 pea kuo tali ‘ene ‘eke kuo pau ‘e ‘i ai ‘ene totonu ke ne ma‘u ‘a e monū‘ia ‘o ha fa‘ahtnga tufa na‘e ‘ikai kau ki ai ‘a e kuletitoa ki mu‘a kapau ‘oku kei toe ha koloa, pe ‘i he fakakaukau ‘a e likuiteta, ‘oku ngalingali ‘oku kei toe, ‘a ia ‘e lava ke tufa.

#### 14 ‘Ikai ke fokotu‘u ‘a e hokohoko ‘o e mahu‘inga ‘i he ‘aho kuo fokotu‘u ki

he ngaahi ‘eke

‘aho kuo fokotu‘u ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni 12 kuo pau ke ‘ikai kau ia ‘i he ta‘e loto ki ha fa‘ahinga tufa na‘e fai kimu‘a ‘i he taimi na‘e fokotu‘u ai ‘a e hokohoko ‘a e mahu‘inga ‘o ‘ene ‘eke.

(2) ‘E ngofua ki he likuiteta, ‘i ha‘ane fai ha fa‘ahinga tufa hili hono tali ‘o e ‘eke, ke ne fai pe ha fakafuofua ki he hokohoko ‘o e mahu‘inga ‘a ia ‘oku ala ma‘u

‘e he ‘eke pea ke ne ‘atu ki he kuletitoa ha monū‘ia mei he tufa ‘o fakatatau ki ai.

(3) Ka ‘i ai ha kuletitoa ‘oku´ ne fokotu‘u ‘a e hokohoko ‘a e mahu‘inga ‘o ‘ene

‘eke hili ‘a e ‘aho kuo fokotu‘u ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni 12 kuo pau ke ‘i ai

‘ene totonu ke ne ma‘u ha monū‘ia mei ha fa‘ahinga tufa na‘e ‘ikai kau ki ai ‘a e kuletitoa kimu‘a (kapau ‘oku ‘i ai) kapau ‘oku kei toe ha koloa, pe, ‘i he fakakaukau ‘a e likuiteta, ‘oku ngalingali ‘oku kei toe, ‘a ia ‘e lava ke tufa.

‘aho-na‘e ‘atu ai ‘e he likuiteta ki he kuletitoa ‘a e fanongonongo ‘o e ta‘e tali

ANGA FAI ‘O E NGAAHI FAKATAHA

#### 17 Feitu‘u fai‘anga fakataha

Kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘a e fakataha kotoa pe ‘a e kau kuletitoa pe kau ma‘u ‘inasi ‘a ia ‘oku ui ‘o fakatatau ki he Konga XVI ‘o e lao ‘i he feitu‘u, ‘oku pehe ‘e he taha

‘oku ne ui ‘a e fakataha, ‘oku faingamalie taha ki he tokolahi ‘o e kau kuletitoa pe kau ma‘u ‘inasi.

#### 18 Lisi ‘o e kau kuletitoa pe kau ma‘u ‘inasi

(1) Kuo pau ke fakapapau‘i ‘e he taha ‘oku sea ‘i ha fakataha ‘a e kau kuletitoa pe kau ma‘u ‘inasi, ‘o hange ko ia ‘e hoko, ‘oku tauhi ha lekooti tonu ‘o e kau kuletitoa pe kau ma‘u ‘inasi kotoa pe, ‘o hange ko ia ‘e hoko, ‘a ia ‘oku nau ma‘u fakataha pe fakafofonga‘i ‘i he fakataha, kau ai —

(a) ‘i he fakataha ‘a e kau kuletitoa —

(i) ‘a e hingoa ‘o e kuletitoa kotoa pe ‘oku ma‘u fakataha pe fakafofonga‘i ‘i he fakataha;

(ii) pe ‘oku fai ‘e he kuletitoa ha ‘eke, mo e mahu‘inga ‘o e ‘eke;

(iii) pe kuo faile ‘e he kuletitoa ha fakafofonga pe oku ne ma‘u fakataha pe ‘e ia; mo e

(iv) tokolahi fakakatoa ‘o e kau kuletitoa ‘oku ma‘u fakataha pe fakafofonga‘i ‘i he fakataha; pe Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 19 VAHE 17.03.01 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni (Veteki) ki he Ngaahi Kautaha Peesi 12 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to

(b) ‘i he fakataha ‘a e kau ma‘u ‘inasi —

(i) hingoa ‘o e taha ma‘u ‘inasi takitaha ‘oku ma‘u fakataha pe ‘oku fakafofonga‘i;

(ii) lahi ‘o e ngaahi ‘inasi kuo foaki ki he taha ma‘u ‘inasi ko ia;

(iii) lahi ‘a e ngaahi fakaha loto ‘oku lava ‘e he taha ma‘u ‘inasi ‘o ngāue‘aki ‘o fakatatau ki he konisitutone ‘o e kautaha;

(iv) pe kuo faile ‘e he taha ma‘u ‘inasi ha fakafofonga pe ‘oku ne ma‘u fakataha ‘e ia pe; mo e

(v) lahi fakakatoa ‘o e kau ma‘u ‘inasi ‘oku ma‘u fakataha pe ‘oku fakafofonga‘i.

(2) Ko ha lekooti ‘oku fiema‘u ‘e he tu‘utu‘uni-si‘i (1) ‘a ia kuo fakamo‘oni‘i ‘oku tonu ‘e he taha ‘oku ne sea pe ‘oku ne ui ‘a e fakataha ‘e hoko ia ko e ‘uluaki fakamo‘oni mahino ‘o e ngaahi fakaikiiki ‘oku ha ‘i he lekooti.

#### 19 Kau kuletitoa ‘oku totonu ke nau fakaha loto

Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai totonu ke fakaha loto ha taha ko ha kuletitoa ngata pe kapau ‘e a‘u ki he taimi ‘oku fai ai ‘a e fakahā loto, kuo fai ‘e he kuletitoa ha ‘eke ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 3l3(1) pe kupu 314(4) ‘o e lao, pe —

(a) kuo tali kakato pe fakakonga ‘e he likuiteta ‘a e kole ‘o tatau ai pe ki he totongi pe ki he fakaha loto; pe

(b) ‘oku fakangofua ‘e he sea ‘o e fakataha ‘a e kau kuletitoa ‘a e tokotaha ko ia ke ne fakaha loto ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni 20.

#### 20 Tali moe ta‘etali ‘o e ngaahi ‘eke ‘e he sea ‘o e fakataha ‘a e kau

kuletitoa fekau‘aki mo e fakaha loto

(1) Kuo pau ke ma‘u ‘e he sea ‘o e fakataha ‘a e kau kuletitoa ‘a e mafai ke tali pe ta‘e tali ha ‘eke fekau‘aki mo e fakaha loto ‘i he fakataha ko ia, ka kuo pau ‘e mo‘ulaloa ‘ene tu‘utu‘uni ki ha tangi ‘e fai ki he Fakamaau‘anga.

(2) Kapau ‘oku veiveiua ha taha sea pe ‘e tali pe ta‘e tali ha ‘eke, kuo pau ke ne fakangofua ‘a e kuletitoa ke ne fakaha loto ka ko e fakaha loto ko ia ‘e fakaha

‘oku ta‘e‘aonga ‘okapau ‘e ‘ikai tali ‘a e ‘eke fekau‘aki mo e fakaha loto.

#### 21 Ngaahi taimi ‘e ‘ikai fakaha loto ai ‘a e kau kuletitoa

Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai fakaha loto ha kuletitoa fekau‘aki mo —

(a) ha fa‘ahinga ‘eke ‘oku fakamahalo pe ‘oku fekau‘aki mo ha ngaahi maumau pe, ‘i ha fa‘ahinga ‘uhinga kehe, ‘oku ta‘epau hono mahu‘inga ngata pe kapau kuo faka‘esitimeti ‘e he likuiteta ‘pe kuo fakapapau‘i ‘e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni (Veteki) ki he Ngaahi Kautaha VAHE 17.03.01 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 22 to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 13 he Fakamaau‘anga ‘a e mahu‘inga ‘o e ‘eke ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 316

‘o e Lao; pe

(b) ha mo‘ua ‘i he pe malu‘i ‘aki ha lau‘itohi fetongi pa‘anga pe nouti palomesi pa‘anga lolotonga ‘oku ne ma‘u tuku kehe kapau ‘e hanga ‘e he kuletitoa ‘o lau ‘a e mo‘ua kiate ia ‘o e taha kotoa pe ko e ‘uluaki mo‘ua ia ki he kautaha pea ‘oku ‘ikai ha tu‘utu‘uni faka-fakamaau‘anga

‘oku ‘ikai ke toe lava ke totongi hono mo‘ua, ko ha malu‘i ia ‘oku ne ma‘u, pea ke ne fakamahu‘inga‘i ‘a hono mahu‘inga, pea ke ne to‘o ia mei he‘ene ‘eke, ‘o ‘ikai koe‘uhi ko e fakaha loto, ka koe‘uhi ko e tiviteni.

#### 22 Ngaahi fakaha loto ‘a e kau kuletitoa malu‘i

(1) Kuo pau kema‘u ‘e ha kuletitoa malu‘i ‘a e totonu ke fakaha loto —

(a) ‘i he kotoa ‘o e mo‘ua kapau ‘oku ne tukuange ha totonu malu‘i ki he likuiteta ma‘a e lelei fakalukufua ‘a e kau kuletitoa;

(b) fekau‘aki mo e toenga ‘o e mo‘ua kapau ‘oku ne fakamahu‘inga‘i ‘a e totonu malu‘i pea ne fai ‘a e ‘eke ‘a ha kuletitoa’’ikai malu‘i ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 314(4) ‘o e Lao ki he toenga ‘oku te‘eki ma‘u; pe

(c) fekau‘aki mo e toenga ‘o e mo‘ua kapau ‘oku ne liliu pa‘anga ‘a e koloa

‘oku fekau‘aki mo ha totonu malu‘i pea ne fai ‘a e ‘eke ha kuletitoa ‘ikai malu‘i ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 314(3)(a) ‘o e lao ki he toenga ‘oku te‘eki ma‘u hili ‘a e to‘o ‘a e lahi haohaoa kuo liliu pa‘anga.

(2) Fakatatau ki he Lao, kapau ‘oku fakaha loto ha kuletitoa malu‘i fekau‘aki mo e kotoa ‘o e mo‘ua ‘o e kuletitoa, kuo pau ke lau kuo tukuange ‘e he kuletitoa

‘ene totonu malu‘i

(3) ‘E ngofua ki ha kuletitoa ‘a ia ‘oku ‘ikai ha‘ane totonu ke fakaha loto ‘i hano fakangofua ia ‘e he likuiteta ke ne kau mo lea ‘i ha fakataha ‘a e kau kuletitoa.

NGAAHI FAKAFOFONGA

#### 23 Fokotu‘u ‘o e likuiteta, etc ke hoko ko e fakafofonga

‘E ngofua ki ha kuletitoa pe taha ma‘u ‘inasi ke ne fokotu‘u ha taha, kau ai ‘a e likuiteta pe, kapau ‘oku ‘ikai ha likuiteta, ‘a e sea ‘o ha fakataha, ke ne hoko ko hano fakafofonga.

(1) Fakatatau ki ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e fakataha ‘a e kau kulelitoa pe kau ma‘u ‘inasi, kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai kole‘i ‘e ha likuiteta ha kau fakafofonga.

(2) ‘O ‘ikai fakangatangata ai ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni kuo fai ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga, kapau ‘oku ‘i ai ha likuiteta ‘oku ‘ikai ke ne muimui ki he tu‘utu‘uni-si‘i (1) ‘e ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga —

(a) ke ne tu‘utu‘uni ‘oku ‘ikai totonu ke to e ma‘u ‘e he likuiteta hono vāhenga;

(b) ke ne fai ha tu‘utu‘uni ke to‘o ‘a e likuiteta mei hono lakanga; pea

(c) ke ne tu‘utu‘uni ‘o fakahā ai ‘oku ta‘e‘aonga ha fa‘ahinga fehū‘aki fakapa‘anga na‘e fai ‘e he likuiteta pe ke fakata‘e‘aonga‘i ha fa‘ahinga fakaha loto pea ke ne fai ha fa‘ahinga fakalelei ‘o hange ko ia ‘e pehe ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘oku fe‘unga.

#### 25 Fehalaaki ‘i he fanongonongo ‘o e fakafofonga

Ka ‘i ai ha fehalaaki ‘oku ‘ikai ke fu‘u mahu‘inga ‘i he fanongonongo ‘o e fakafofonga, ‘e ngofua ki he likuiteta pe sea ‘o e fakataha, ‘o hange ko ia ‘e hoko, ke ne tali ‘oku fakalao ‘a e fakafofonga ko ia ki he ngaahi taumu‘a fakaha loto, kapau

‘oku ne fiemalie ‘oku fakafofonga‘i ‘e he taha fakafofonga ‘a e kuletitoa pe taha ma‘u
