#### 1 Hingoa Nounou mo e kamata ngaueʻaki

(1) ‘E ui ‘a e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko ‘eni ko e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ki he Ngaahi Kautaha 2025.

(2) Kuo pau ke kamata ngaueʻaki ʻa e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni ʻi he ʻaho ʻoku kaseteʻi ai. 2 ʻUhingaʻi Lea

(1) ʻI he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ni, tukukehe ka fiemaʻu ha ʻuhinga kehe ʻi hono tuʻunga —

“ko e Lao” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he Lao ki he Ngaahi Kautaha [Vahe 17.03];

“fakaʻinitaneti” ʻoku ʻuhinga ‘i he —

(a) fekauʻaki mo e lesisita, ko ha lesisita fakaʻilekitulonika ʻe lava ke maʻu ʻi he ‘Initaneti;

“ngaue fakaʻinitaneti” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki ha ngaue ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he, pe maʻae, Failesisita, ʻa ia ʻoku ne fakangofua ʻa e ngaahi tohi kole, ngaahi faile, ngaahi fanongonongo mo e ngaahi tohi ke lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ʻoatu ki he Failesisita ʻo fakafou ʻi he ʻinitaneti ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti, ki he totongi ʻo e ngaahi totongi mo e ngaahi moʻua, pea mo hono maʻu ʻo ha ngaahi ngaue kehe ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) Tuku kehe ka fiemaʻu ha ʻuhinga kehe ʻi hono tuʻunga, ko e ngaahi lea pe fakafotungaʻi he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni ʻokutatau honau ʻuhinga ʻi he Lao.

#### 3 Ngaahi houa ngaue

(1) Kuo pau ke ‘ataa ‘a e ʻofisi ʻo e Failesisita ki he kakai ʻi he ʻaho ngaue kotoa pe, lolotonga ʻa e ngaahi houa kuo fokotuʻu ʻe he Failesisita mei he taimi ki he taimi, pe fakalukufua pe ʻi ha tuʻunga pau.

(2) ʻE ngofua ke ‘ataa ki he kakai ʻa e lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻi he ngaahi houa tuʻataimi mei he ‘ataa ʻa e ʻofisi ʻo e Failesisita.

#### 4 Fakahu atu ʻo e ngaahi tohi ngaue ki he lesisita mo e ngaahi ngaue

fakaʻinitaneti

(1) Kuo pau ki he Failesisita ke ne fokotuʻu, tauhi mo ngaueʻaki ha lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻi ha founga ʻoku tali ʻe he Failesisita ʻi he kupu 367 ʻo e Lao.

(2) ʻE ngofua ke fekauʻi ʻe he Failesisita ʻa e fakahu ʻo ha ngaahi tohi kole, fanongonongo pe tohi ʻi he Lao pe ko e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni, ke fakahoko pe ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(3) ʻOku ʻikai ngaueʻaki ʻa e tuʻutuʻuni siʻi (2) ʻi he taimi ʻoku ʻave ai ki he Failesisita ha tohi faka-fakamaauʻanga.

(4) Kuo pau ke ngaueʻaki ʻa e founga ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ki he ngaahi tohi kotoa ʻoku fakahu mai ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(a) ha tuʻasila ʻimeili ke fetuʻutaki mo e taha naʻa ne lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ʻoatu ha tohi ngaue ki he Failesisita ʻa ia kuo pau ke ʻoatu fakataha mo e tohi ngaue; mo Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ki he Ngaahi Kautaha 2025 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 5 to Peesi 7

(b) ha tuʻasila ʻimeili ke fetuʻutaki mo e kautaha ʻa ia kuo pau ke ʻoatu fakataha mo e tohi ngaue.

(6) Kapau ʻoku pehe ʻe he Failesisita ko ha tohi ngaue ‘oku fakahu ange –

(a) ʻoku kau ai ʻa e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku fepaki mo e lao;

(b) ʻi he ʻuhinga ko ha faʻahinga meʻa naʻe ʻikai ke fakahoko pe naʻe fakamatala halaʻi, kuo teʻeki ai ke fakakakato fakalelei;

(c) ʻoku ʻikai ke faipau ki he ngaahi fiemaʻu ʻa e Lao, ko e Tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni, pe ko e founga ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti; pe

(d) ʻokuʻiai ʻae ngaahi fakamatalaʻikai totonuʻoku ne fakatupuʻae ngaahi meʻa ʻi he tohi ke takihalaʻi, ʻe ngofua ke ʻikai tali ʻe he Failesisita ke lesisita pe maʻu ʻa e tohi ngaue, pea kuo pau ʻe ʻikai lau kuo fakahu atu ʻa e tohi ngaue, pea ʻe ngofua ke kole ʻe he Failesisita ke fakatonutonu totonu ʻa e tohi ngaue pe fakakakato pea toe fakahu atu, pe ko hono fakahu atu ha tohi ngaue foʻou ko hono fetongi.

(7) ʻOku ʻikai lau kuo fakahu atu ha tohi ngaue ʻa ia kuo fakahu atu ʻi he Lao pe ko e Tuʻutuʻuni ko ‘eni kae ʻoua kuo tali ia ʻe he Failesisita.

(8) Tuku kehe ʻi ha ngofua makehe ʻe he Failesisita, ʻaia ʻefoaki ʻofakatatau ki he ngaahi makatuʻunga ʻe pehe ʻe he Failesisita ʻoku feʻunga, kuo pau ʻe ʻikai ke tali ʻe he Failesisita ha tohi ngaue ʻoku ʻikai faipau ki he Tuʻutuʻuni ko ‘eni.

#### 5 Kau ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai mo e ngaahi ʻakauni ʻa e kau fakafofongaʻi

(1) ʻE ngofua ki ha taha, kau ai ha sino fakalao, sino fakalao muli, pe fakafofonga fakapuleʻanga, ke hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ke ne ngaueʻaki ʻa e lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ki he taumuʻa ke ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ke kole ha ngaahi tohi pe ha fakamoʻoni kehe mei ha taha pe ʻoku ne fie hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai koeʻuhi ke fakapapauʻi ʻa e fakaʻilongaʻi ʻo e taha pe sino ko ia ʻi he fiemalie ki ai ʻa e Failesisita.

(3) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita, ʻi he tohi kole, ke tali mo fokotuʻu ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ʻi he hingoa ʻo e pe maʻae taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ki he taumuʻa ke ne ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(4) Kuo pau ke ʻikai ʻi ai ha totongi ki he hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai pea mo hono fokotuʻu ʻo ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

(5) Kuo pau ki he tohi kole ki ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ke –

(a) ʻi he founga kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita; mo

(b) fakataha mo ha fakamoʻoni ʻoku fiemalie ki ai ʻa e Failesisita fekauʻaki mo e fakaʻilongaʻi ʻo e taha fakafofongaʻi pe niʻihi fakafofongaʻi.

(6) Koeʻuhi ke lava ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ʻo faʻu ha faile ʻi loto ʻi he lesisita, kuo pau ke ne fuofua hu ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻo ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala hu atu ʻoku tauhi fakafoʻituitui ʻo hange ko e lea maluʻi, fika fakapulipuli (PINs) pe hange ko ia. ʻOku ʻikai fiemaʻu ʻa e Failesisita ke ne fakapapauʻipekoetahafaileʻiheʻinitanetikohatahangaueʻakikuofakamafai ʻoku ngofua ke ne ngaueʻaki ʻa e fakamatala hu atu ʻoku fakahu ki he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(7) Kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘a e totongi ʻo e ngaahi totongi, moʻua, mo e ngaahi totongi tomui ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti mei he ʻakauni ‘o ha taha fakafofongaʻi.

(8) ʻE ngofua ki he totongi ʻo e paʻanga ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ke ngaueʻaki ha founga kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita mei he taimi ki he taimi.

(9) ‘E ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ʻoatu ha fakamatala ʻi he mahina takitaha ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi kotoa pe ʻo fakaha ai ʻa e ngaahi kuletiti mo e tepiti ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

#### 6 Ngaahi foomu lesisita fakaʻinitaneti

(1) Kuo pau ki ha tohi, fanongonongo pe foomu ʻoku fakahu mai ʻo fakafou ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ke fakakakato fakatatau ki he ngaahi fakamatala, ngaahi fakahinohino pe ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻoku fakamahinoʻi ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti, pe ʻe he Failesisita.

(2) Tukukeheʻaengaahifoomukuotuʻutuʻuniʻihetuʻutuʻuni-siʻi (4),koengaahi foomu ke ngaueʻaki ʻi he Lao ko e ngaahi foomu ia kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita ke fakahu ʻi he ʻinitaneti pea ʻoku kau ai ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala ʻoku maʻu ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻoku fekauʻaki mo e foomu.

(3) Kuo pau ki ha foomu kuo tuʻutuʻuni ‘i he tuʻutuʻuni-siʻi (4) mo ha foomu kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita ki he lesisita ʻi he ʻinitianeti ke fakakakato ʻo fakatatau ki he ngaahi fakahinohino, ngaahi fakamatala pe ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻoku ʻi loto ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(4) Ko e ngaahi foomu ʻoku fokotuʻu ʻi he Tepile 1 ko e ngaahi foomu pepa ia kuo tuʻutuʻuni ki he ngaahi tohi ngaue ko ‘eni –

(a) fakangofua ʻa e talekita ʻo ha kautaha ʻi he kupu 16A mo e 151;

(b) fakamatala fakataʻu tomui fekauʻaki mo ha kautaha Tonga ʻa ia kuo toʻo pea ʻoku kole ke toe fakafoki ki he lesisita ʻi he kupu 337; mo e

(c) fakamatala fakataʻu tomui ki ha kautaha muli ʻa ia kuo tamateʻi ʻene lesisita pe ʻoku kole ke toe fakafoki ʻene lesisita ki he lesisita.

#### 7 Totongi fakalukufua

(1) Koeʻuhi ko e ngaahi taumuʻa ʻo e kupu 376 ʻo e Lao, kuo pau ki he ngaahi totongi ʻoku ha ʻi he Tepile 2 ke totongi ki he Failesisita ʻo fekauʻaki mo e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku felaveʻi mo ia.

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he ngaahi totongi ʻoku ha ʻi he Tepile 2 ke kau ai ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi totongi tomui mo e ngaahi moʻua ʻe ala totongi ki hono fakahu ʻo ha tohi ki he Failesisita ʻi he hili pe ‘a e ‘osi ‘o e taimi kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he Lao pe Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni.

(3) ʻE ngofua ke fakahoko ʻa e ngaahi totongi mo e ngaahi founga totongi ki hono ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ʻo fakatatau ki he ngaahi founga mo e founga ngaue kuo fanongonongo mei he taimi ki he taimi ʻe he Failesisita.

#### 8 Taimi ki hono totongi ʻo e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua pe ngaahi

totongi ki he faile tomui

(1) Kuo pau ke totongi ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua pe ngaahi totongi ki he faile tomui ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni ʻi he taimi ʻoku fakahoko ai ʻa e ngaue fakapaʻanga fekauʻaki mo ia.

(2) Kuo pau ke totongi ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua mo e ngaahi totongi tomui –

(a) ʻi ha founga totongi ʻoku tali ʻe he Failesisita mei he taimi ki he taimi; pe

(b) ʻaki ʻa e tepiti pe mei he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ʻoku kuletiti ʻo ʻikai siʻi hifo ʻi he mahuʻinga ʻo e moʻua pe totongi.

#### 9 Fakaʻataa ʻo e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua pe ngaahi totongi tomui

(1) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita, ‘i he ‘ene tuʻutuʻuni, ʻi ha fanongonongo tohi ki he taha ʻe ala totongi ha totongi, moʻua pe totongi tomui kuo tuʻutuʻuni ‘e he Lao pe ʻe he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni, ke ne fakaʻataa kakato pe ha konga ʻo ia

‘i ha makatuʻunga ‘oku fakaha ‘i he tuʻutuʻuni siʻi (2).

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ne fakaʻataa ha totongi, moʻua pe totongi tomui kapau ʻoku ne pehe ko e fiemaʻu ke totongi kakato pe ha konga ʻo ia –

(a) ʻoku ngalingali te ne fakatupu ha faingataʻa lahi ki he taha ʻe ala moʻua ke ne totongi;

(b) ʻoku ʻikai ke tatau ia mo e meʻa ʻoku lolotonga hoko;

(c) ʻoku ngalingali ke hoko ha uesia ki he kau kuletitoaʻoe kautahafelaveʻi; pe

(d) ko e ngaahi makatuʻunga ʻo e vahevahe taau ʻoku fakamahino pau ʻa e poupou ki ha fakaʻataa.

(3) ʻE ngofua ke fekauʻi ʻe he Failesisita ke totongi ha totongi, moʻua, pe totongi tomui ʻi he taimi ʻoku fakahu mai ai ha tohi fakataha mo ha kole ke fakaʻataa ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahimoʻua pe ngaahi totongi tomui, pea kapau ʻe taliʻa e fakaʻataa, ʻe ngofua ke fakafoki ʻe he Failesisita ʻa e totongi ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

#### 10 Taʻofi ʻo e hingoa

Ko hono taʻofi ʻo e hingoa ʻo ha kautaha ʻa ia kuo fokotuʻu ke fakahu pe koeʻuhi ko ha fokotuʻu liliu hingoa ʻo e hingoa ʻo ha kautaha lolotonga, ʻoku ʻikai foaki ki he taha kole pe ko ha kautaha kuo fokotuʻu pe lolotonga ‘i ai, ʻa ia naʻe tuʻunga ai ʻa e taʻofi ʻo e hingoa, ha totonu ke maʻu pe ha kaunga ki he hingoa ko ia.

#### 11 Liliu lea ʻo e ngaahi tohi ngaue

(1) Ko hono liliu lea faka-Pilitania pe faka-Tonga ʻo ha tohi ngaue ʻoku ne fakamoʻoniʻi ʻa e lesisita ʻo ha kautaha pe ko ha kautaha muli pe ha tohi ʻoku ne fokotuʻu pe fakaʻuhingaʻi ha tohi ʻo ha kautaha pe o ha kautaha muli, pe ko ha tohi ngaue kehe ke ʻoatu ki he Failesisita ke lesisita ‘i he Lao, kuo pau ke fakamoʻoniʻi ʻoku tonu ʻa e liliu lea ʻe he taha naʻa ne fakahoko ʻa e liliu lea –

(a) kapau ko e liliu lea ʻoku fakahoko ia ʻi tuʻa mei Tonga, ʻi ha taha ʻoku ʻi ai hono mafai ke fakahoko fuakava ʻo fakatatau ki he lao ʻo e fonua ʻoku fakahoko ai ʻa e liliu lea; pe

(b) kapau ko e liliu lea ʻoku fakahoko ʻi Tonga, ʻi ha taha kuo fakamafai ke fakahoko fuakava ʻi Tonga.

(2) Koeʻuhi ko e ngaahi taumuʻa ʻo e tuʻutuʻuni ko ‘eni, ʻoku kau ki he

“fakamoʻoniʻi” ʻa e fakamoʻoni ʻa e taha naʻa ne fakahoko ʻa e liliu lea ʻi ha taha ʻo e kakai ʻoku fakamatalaʻi ʻi he palakałafi (1)(a) mo e (1)(b) ʻi he founga (kapau ʻoku ʻi ai) ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he lao ʻo e fonua ʻoku fakahoko ai ʻa e liliu lea ki hono fakamoʻoniʻi ʻo e ngaahi tohi ngaue.

(3) ‘E ngofua ke fakangofua ʻe he Failesisita ke ʻoatu kiate ia ha ngaahi liliu lea ʻa ia ʻoku ʻikai ke fakamoʻoniʻi ʻo fakatatau mo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻi ʻolunga ʻi ha ngaahi tuʻunga ʻoku ne fakakaukau ʻoku feʻunga.

(4) Kimuʻa pea ne tali ha liliu lea, ʻe ngofua ke tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he Failesisita ke ʻoatu kiate ia ʻe he taha ʻoku ne fakahu atu ʻa e liliu lea ha fakamoʻoni ʻa ia ʻe pehe ʻe he Failesisita ʻoku feʻunga, ʻo kau ki he malava ʻa e taha haʻana ʻa e liliu lea ke fakahoko ʻa e liliu lea.

#### 12 Fakamatala makehe ʻoku ʻoatu ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti

Kapau ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni ha tohi ngaue, faile pe fanongonongo ‘oku fakahu ‘i he ngaue

‘i he ‘initaneti ha toe fakamatala poupou, kuo pau ke tuku hake ‘i he fotunga fakaʻilekitulonika ‘i he ngaue ‘i he ‘initaneti.

#### 13 Tuʻasila ʻimeili

(1) Kapau ʻoku lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ʻoatu ha tohi ki he Failesisita ʻo ngaueʻaki ‘a e ngaue fakaʻinitaneti –

(a) ha tuʻasila ʻimeili ke fetuʻutaki mo e taha naʻa ne lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ʻoatu ha tohi ngaue ki he Failesisita ʻa ia kuo pau ke ʻoatu fakataha mo e tohi ngaue; mo

(b) ha tuʻasila ʻimeili ke fetuʻutaki mo e kautaha ʻa ia kuo pau ke ʻoatu fakataha mo e tohi ngaue.
