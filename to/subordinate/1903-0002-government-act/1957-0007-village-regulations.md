# Tu'utu'uni Faka-Kolo

TU'UTU'UNI FAKA-KOLO1 Na‘e fa‘u ‘i he mafai ‘o e kupu 26 ‘o e Lao ‘o e Pule‘anga

1. LEIMATU‘A, FELETOA MO MATAIKA Fakapekia ‘e he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni Fakavahefonua (Tuku ‘aa ‘o e Fanga Monumanu 2009) K.F 8/2009

2. TU‘ANUKU, LONGOMAPU, TEFISI, TAOA MO VAIMALO Na‘e fai ‘e he Pule Fakavahe ‘i he ‘aho 22 ‘o Tisema, 19582

1. 4. ‘OTEA, FALEVAI, TAUNGA, ‘OVAKA, MATAMAKA, LAPE, NUAPAPU, KAPA, HUNGA, FUA‘AMOTU, ‘EUAKAFA, ‘EUEIKI MU‘OMU‘A, TOTOKA’UTA MO VAKA‘EITU Na‘e fai ‘e he Pule Fakavahe ‘i he ‘aho 22 ‘o Tisema, 19584

2. Ko ia ‘e ‘ikai te ne fai ki he ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni ‘e tautea ia ‘o fakatatau ki he Kupu 26 ‘o e Lao ‘o e Pule‘anga.

5. NIUAFO‘OU MO ‘EUA Na e fai ‘e he Pule Fakavahe ‘i he ‘aho 28 ‘o Sanuali, 19595

1. Ko kinautolu kotoa pe ‘oku nau nofo‘i ‘a e ngaahi ‘api kuo pau ke nau to, ‘i honau ngaahi ‘api tukuhau pe ‘api kolo, ‘a e ngaahi ‘akau mo e ngaahi ngoue

‘a ia ‘oku ha ‘i he tepile ki heni.

2. Ko e teu‘i ko ia ke fai ia lolotonga ‘a e mahina ko Sanuali, 1959 pe ‘i he vave taha hili ange pe ia ‘o hangē ko ia ‘e fakapotopoto taha hono ala fai.

3. Ka ‘i ai ha taha ‘e ‘ikai te ne fai ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ni ‘e mo‘ua ia ki he ngaahi tautea kuo tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Kupu 26 ‘o e Lao Pule‘anga.

## TEPILE

NGAAHI TU‘UTU‘UNI TO NGOUE, NGAAHI ‘AKAU HU‘ANGA PA‘ANGA MO E NGAAHI ‘AKAU ‘ULUFONUA KE TO MO TAUHI ‘E HE KAINGA NIUAFO‘OU,

‘EUA, KE KAMATA ‘I HE TA‘U 1959 A. Ngaahi ‘akau ‘ulufonua, ke tō tu‘o taha —

1. Fu‘u Mei ‘e 5;

2. Fu‘u Mango ‘e 2;

3. Fu‘u Ifi ‘e 2;

4. Fu‘u Moli ‘e 2;

5. Fu‘u Kaho ‘e 5;

Ke ‘a‘ahi ‘i he ‘aho 15 ‘o Sepitema 1959, ke tō ‘i he ‘api kolo pe ‘api ‘uta.

B. Ngaahi ‘akau hū‘anga pa‘anga —

1. Fu‘u Kava ‘e 5 he mahina taki taha kamata tō ‘i Sanuali, 1959.

2. Fu‘u ‘akau taki hongofulu mei he kalasi ‘o e lō‘akau´ koe; Paongo, Tofua, Tutu‘ila.

C. Ngaahi ngoue me‘akai —

1. ‘Ufi fu‘u puke 50;

2. ‘Ufilei fu‘u puke 25;

3. Kape fu‘u Kape 50;

4. Hopa fu‘u Hopa 20;

5. Siaine fu‘u Siaine 50;

Ke ‘a‘ahi ‘i he ‘aho 30 ‘o Novema 1959 pea ‘i he ngaahi ta‘u taki taha kotoa pe ‘e hoko mai.

6. Talo Futuna ‘e 50;

7. Talo Tonga ‘e 50;

8. Manioke ‘e 80;

9. Kumala ‘e 50;

Ke tō ‘aki ‘a e vahavaha ko eni fute 6 ‘i he fute 6 ‘i he fute 5 pea ke ‘a‘ahi ‘i hono 30 ‘o Novema 1959 pea mo e ngaahi ta‘u taki taha kotoa pe ‘e hoko mai.
