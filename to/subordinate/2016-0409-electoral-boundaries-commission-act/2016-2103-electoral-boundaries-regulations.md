VAHENGA FILI VAVA‘U 16 FAKALUKUFUA KoVava‘u16‘okutu‘u ‘ihe konga faka-Tokelau ‘oe motukoVava‘u,pea‘okukapuiai ‘a eVahenga Leimatu‘u lolotonga pea mo e konga ‘i he motu lahi ‘o e Vahenga Ha‘alaufuli. ‘Oku kau ki ai ‘a Ha‘alaufuli,Ta‘anea,Ha‘akio,Houma,Mangia,Mataika,Feletoa,Leimatu‘a,Tu‘anekivale, Holonga, Koloa mo Holeva. ‘Oku toe kau ki ai ‘a e ngaahi motu ‘oku ‘ikai nofo‘i ko Matu‘anua, Motu Lekaleka and Pousini. Ko Vava‘u 16 ‘oku tu‘u ai ‘a e mala‘evakapuna.

KO E FAKAIIKIIKI ‘O E NGAAHI NGATANGATA‘ANGA Ko e kotoa ‘o e ‘elia ‘o e kelekele ‘oku ‘i loto ‘i he laine ‘oku muimui ‘i he Laine ‘Avalisi ‘o e Ma‘olunga Taha ‘o e Tahi ‘i he ‘otu motu ‘o Matu‘anua, Motu Lekaleka, Pousini mo Koloa; pea mo e kotoa ‘o e konga ‘o e Motu ko Vava‘u ‘oku ‘i loto ‘i he ngaahi laine ngatangata‘anga ko ‘eni – Kamata mei he poini ‘oku fetaulaki ai ‘a e hokohoko atu fakahihifo ‘a e ngatangata‘anga ‘i he vaha‘a

‘o e ongo tofi‘a ‘o Luani mo Fotu pea mo e Laine ‘Avalisi ‘o e Ma‘olunga Taha ‘o e Tahi ‘i he matatahi fakahihifo ‘o e Motu ko Vava‘u (18° 35’ 59”, 174° 00’ 35”), pea fou fakahahake pea fakatongahahake ‘i he ngatangata‘anga ‘o e ongo tofi‘a ko ia ki he‘ene fetaulaki‘anga mo e ngatangata‘anga ‘o e tofi‘a ‘o e Tu‘ipelehake (18° 36’ 06”, 174° 00’ 12”), pea fou fakatongahahake

‘i he ngatangata‘anga ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ongo tofi‘a ‘o e Tu‘ipelehake mo Luani pea ‘i he hokohoko atu ‘o e ngatangata‘anga ko ia ki he poini ‘oku fetaulaki ai mo e Laine ‘Avalisi ‘o e Ma‘olunga Taha

‘o e Tahi ‘i he matatahi (18° 36’ 21”, 173° 59’ 53”), pea fou fakatokelauhihifo ‘i he Laine ‘Avalisi ‘o e Ma‘olunga Taha ‘o e Tahi ‘o takai ‘i he matatahi ‘o e Motu ko Vava‘u ki he poini ‘oku fetaulaki ai

‘a e hokohoko atu fakahihifo ‘a e ngatangata‘anga ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ongo tofi‘a ‘o e Tu‘iha‘ateiho mo e Pule‘anga pea mo e Laine ‘Avalisi ‘o e Ma‘olunga Taha ‘o e Tahi ‘i he matatahi fakatokelau ‘o Neiafu (18° 27’ 15”, 173° 58’ 37”), pea fou fakahahake, fakatokelauhahake, fakatongahahake, fakatokelau pea fakatonga hahake ‘i he ngatangata‘anga ‘o e tofi‘a ko ia ki he‘ene fetaulaki‘anga mo e hala ‘oku ‘ikai fakahingoa (18° 37’ 18”, 173° 57’ 53”), pea hokohoko atu ‘o fou fakatongahahake

‘i he vaeua‘anga malie ‘o e hala ‘oku ‘ikai fakahingoa ko ia pea mo e hokohoko atu ‘o e hala ‘oku

‘ikai fakahingoa ko ia ki he poini ‘oku fetaulaki ai mo e Laine ‘Avalisi ‘o e Ma‘olunga Taha ‘o e Tahi

‘i he matatahi fakahahake ‘o e Motu ko Vava‘u, pea fou fakatokelau ‘i he Laine ‘Avalisi ‘o e Ma‘olunga Taha ‘o e Tahi ‘o takai fakahahake, fakatokelau mo e fakahihifo ‘i he matatahi ‘o e Motu ko Vava‘u ki he poini kamata‘anga.
