# Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Fili Fale Alea

NGAAHI TU‘UTU‘UNI KI HE FILI FALE ALEA1 Na‘e fa‘u ‘e he Komisoni Fili,‘i he loto ki ai ‘a e Kapineti, ‘i he mafai ‘o e Kupu 41

‘o e Lao ki he Fili Fale Alea Kamata‘anga [13 ‘o ‘Okatopa, 2010]

#### 1 Fuakava ‘o e Lakanga - Kau ‘Ofisa Fili, Kau ‘Ofisa Faifili mo e ni‘ihi ‘oku

ngāue ki he Komisoni Fili

(1) Kuo pau ki he kotoa ‘o e ni’ihi ‘oku nau ma’u lakanga ‘i he Komisoni Fili pe ngāue ma‘ae Komisoni Fili ke nau fakahoko ha fuakava ‘o e lakanga.

(2) Kuo pau ki he fuakava ke fakahoko ia ‘e ha Komisiona ‘o e Komisoni Fili, ‘i he ala fakahoko ia. Kapau ‘oku mama‘o ‘a e taha ko ia mei he Komisoni ‘e ngofua ke fakahoko ‘a e fuakava ‘e ha Fakamaau Polisi, ‘Ofisa Polisi pe ha Memipa ‘o e Kau Taki Lotu (Member of the Clergy).

(3) Kuo pau ko e ngaahi fuakava kuo fakakakato ke ‘oatu ia ki he Komisoni pea ke tauhi ‘e he Komisoni kae ‘oua kuo ‘ikai toe ‘i he pe ngāue ‘a e ‘ofisa pe taha ngāue ki he Komisoni.

(4) Kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘a e fuakava ‘o e lakanga ‘i he foomu ‘oku ha ‘i he Tepile.

#### 2 Tauhi ‘o e Tohi Fakakātoa ‘o e Kau Fili

(1) Kuo pau ki he tohi fakakātoa kuo ngāue‘aki ‘i ha fai‘anga fili ‘i he ‘aho fili ke fakafoki ki he Komisoni hili ‘a e tapuni ‘o e fili ‘o fakatatau ki he ngaahi fakahinohino kuo tuku atu ‘e he Supavaisa ‘o e Fili pe ko e Komisoni.

(2) Kuo pau ki he tohi fakakātoa ‘o e kau fili kuo ngāue‘aki ‘i he ngaahi fai‘anga fili ‘i he vāhenga fili takitaha ‘i he fili ‘o e Fale Alea ke faka‘auha ‘i he mahina ‘e 6 hili ‘a e ‘aho ‘o e fili tukukehe ka ‘i ai ha Kole ‘i he Konga V ‘o e Lao.

(3) ‘I ha ‘i ai ha kole ‘i he Konga V ‘o e Lao, ko e tohi fakakātoa (ngaahi tohi fakakātoa) ‘o e kau fili ki he vāhenga fili pe ngaahi vāhenga fili ko ia, ‘i he‘ene hoko, kuo pau ke tauhi kae‘oua kuo ‘ilo‘i ‘a e ola ‘o e Kole pea kuo‘osi kotoa ‘a e ngaahi founga ‘o e tangi hono fakahoko.

#### 3 Tauhi ‘o e Ngaahi Puha Paloti mo e Ngaahi Pepa Paloti

(1) Kuo pau ke tauhi ‘e he Komisoni ‘a e kotoa ‘o e Ngaahi Puha Paloti mei he fili ‘e taha ki he fili hoko ‘i he founga ‘oku maau lelei pea tauhi malu.

(2) Kuo pau ki he Komisoni, ‘i hono fiema‘u, ke fakalelei‘i pe liliu ‘a e Ngaahi Puha Paloti.

(3) Kuo pau ki he Komisoni ke tu‘utu‘uni ki he lahi, fōtunga mo e naunau ‘o e ngaahi puha paloti ke ngāue‘aki ‘i he ngaahi fili.

(4) Ko e ngaahi pepa paloti ki he vāhenga fili takitaha ‘i he fili ‘o e Fale Alea kuo pau ke faka‘auha ‘i he māhina ‘e 6 hili ‘a e ‘aho ‘o e fili tukukehe ka kuo ‘i ai ha Kole ‘i he Konga V ‘o e Lao.

(5) ‘I ha ‘i ai ha Kole ‘i he Konga V, ko e ngaahi pepa paloti ki he vāhenga fili pe ngaahi vāhenga fili ko ia, ‘i he‘ene hoko, kuo pau ke tauhi kae‘oua kuo ‘ilo‘i

‘a e ola ‘o e Kole pea kuo‘osi kotoa ‘a e ngaahi founga ‘o e tangi hono fakahoko.

#### 4 Tokoni makehe ki he Kau Fili ‘oku Kui pe Tonounou Fakaesino

(1) Ka fakafiemalie‘i ‘e ha taha fili ‘a e ‘ofisa faifili ‘oku tonounou ‘ene sio pe ko e taha fili ‘oku tonounou fakaesino pea ‘oku ‘ikai malava ai ke fili ta‘e ‘i ai ha tokoni, kuo pau ki he ‘ofisa faifili pe ko ha ‘ofisa tokoni fai fili ke tokoni ki he taha fili.

(2) Kuo pau ke hū ‘a e taha fili mo e ‘ofisa faifili ki he ngaahi fale fai‘anga fili

‘oku ‘ikai ngāue‘aki pea ‘i he fakahinohino ‘a e taha fili kuo pau ki he ‘ofisa faifili pe ‘ofisa tokoni fai fili ke ne faka‘ilonga‘i, pelu, pea fakahū atu ‘a e pepa paloti ‘a e taha fili ki he puha paloti.

(3) Kuo pau ke siofi ‘e he ‘ofisa polisi ‘oku ngāue ‘i he fai‘anga fili ‘a e tokoni‘i

‘o ha taha fili.

(4) ‘I ha ‘ikai ke ‘i he fai‘anga fili ha ‘ofisa polisi pe ‘oku ne mo‘ua kuo pau ko e tokoni ki he taha fili mo hono faka‘ilonga‘i ‘o ‘enau fili ke siofi ‘e ha taha kuo fili ‘e he taha fili, ‘a ia ko e taha ko ia he ‘ikai ko ha kaniteiti ki he fili.

#### 4A Ngaahi pepa paloti kuo maumau2

Fakatatau ki he kupu 12(6) ‘o e Lao, hili ‘a hono fakafoki ‘o e pepa paloti kuo maumau ki he ‘ofisa faifili, kuo pau ki he ‘ofisa faifili —

(a) ke kaniseli ‘a e pepa paloti kotoa kuo maumau ko ia ‘aki ‘i ha‘ane tohi

‘i he fofonga ‘o ia ‘a e ngaahi lea “Maumau‘i” e he taha fili, pea kuo

‘oatu ha pepa paloti fo‘ou pea tohi‘i ‘a e ‘uluaki mata‘itohi ‘o hono ngaahi hingoa kotoa ‘i ai;

(b) ka ‘i ai ha pepa paloti kuo maumau ‘i ha fehalaaki ‘a e ‘ofisa faifili pe ha taha ngaue faifili kehe, ke kaniseli ia ‘aki hono tohi‘i ‘i he fofonga ‘o ia ‘a e ngaahi lea “Maumau‘i” ‘e he ‘ofisa´ pea mo e ngaahi lea pea kuo oatu ha pepa paloti fo‘ou´ kapau ko ia kuo hoko, pea tohi‘i ‘a e ‘uluaki mata‘itohi ‘o hono ngaahi hingoa kotoa ‘i ai;

(c) tauhi ‘a e kotoa ‘o e ngaahi pepa paloti kuo maumau ‘oku´ne ma‘u kae

‘oua kuo tapuni ‘a e fili.

#### 5 Tuku atu ‘o e Kaati Faka‘ilonga‘i

(1) Kuo pau ki ha taha fili ‘i he ‘aho ‘o e fili, ‘i he taimi hono vakai‘i honau hingoa ‘i he tohi fakakātoa ‘i he fai‘anga fili, ke fakahā ki he ‘Ofisa Faifili ‘a

‘enau Kaati Faka‘ilonga‘i Fakafonua.

(2) Ko ha taha fili ‘oku ‘ikai malava ke ne fakahā ‘ene Kaati Faka‘ilonga‘i Fakafonua koe‘uhi kuo mole, puli pe ‘oku ‘ikai ma‘u ia pea ‘oku ‘ikai malava ke toe fakahā ha founga faka‘ilonga‘i kehe ‘oku fakafiemalie ki he ‘Ofisa Faifili ke ne muimui ki he ngaahi fiema‘u ‘o e kupu 14 ‘o e Lao ki he Fili Fale Alea.

#### 6 Paaki mo e pulusi ‘o e ngaahi tu‘uaki, ngaahi fanongonongo ki he fili

etc.

(1) Kuo pau ke ‘ikai paaki, pulusi pe tufaki pe fakatupu, fakangofua pe fakamafai ke paaki, pulusi pe tufaki, ha tu‘uaki, pepa tu‘uaki (handbill), pepa tu‘uaki (pamphlet), pepa tu‘uaki (poster) pe fanongonongo ki he fili tukukehe ka —

(a) ko e hingoa mo e tu‘asila ‘o e taha ‘oku ne fakamafai ‘a e tu‘uaki, pepa tu‘uaki (handbill), pepa tu‘uaki (pamphlet), pepa tu‘uaki (poster) pe fanongonongo ‘oku ha ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o ia; pea

(b) kapau ko ha tu‘uaki, pepa tu‘uaki (handbill), pepa tu‘uaki (pamphlet), pepa tu‘uaki (poster) pe fanongonongo ki he fili ‘a ia ‘oku paaki kehe ange mei ha nusipepa, ko e hingoa mo e feitu‘u ‘o e pisinisi ‘a e taha paaki ke ha ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o ia.

(2) Kuo pau ke ‘ikai fa‘u, pulusi pe tufaki pe fakatupu,fakangofua pe fakamafai ke fakaha , pulusi pe tufaki lolotonga ‘a e vaha‘a taimi fili ha lekooti letio pe vitio tukukehe ka ha ‘a e hingoa mo e tu‘asila ‘a e taha ‘oku ne fakamafai ‘a e lekooti letio pe vitio ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o ia.

(3) Ko ha taha ‘oku ‘ikai faipau ki he kupusi‘i (1) pe (2) ‘oku ne fakahoko ‘a e hia pea kuo pau ke mo‘ua ‘i ha‘ane halaia ki he —

(a) kapau ko e taha faihia ko ha taha taautaha, mo‘ua pa‘anga ‘oku ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $1,000;

(b) kapau ko e taha faihia ko ha sino‘i kautaha, ki ha mo‘ua pa‘anga ‘oku

‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $5,000.

(4) ‘Oku ‘ikai ngāue‘aki ‘a e kupusi‘i (1) ‘o fekau‘aki mo e —

(a) falani, fakama‘u, faka‘ilonga, pine faka‘ilonga, peni, peni vahevahe pe pula;

(b) ngaahi kaati pisinisi pe ‘a‘ahi ‘a ia ‘oku ne poupou ki he kanititeiti ‘o ha taha ‘i ha fili ki he Fale Alea; pe

(c) ngaahi tohi mo e ngaahi kaati: ‘oku hā ai ‘a e hingoa mo e tu‘asila ‘o e taha na‘a ne ‘oatu, pea ‘oku ‘ikai ma‘u ai ha fakafofonga pe fakangalingali ‘o e fakafofonga ‘o ha pepa paloti ke ngāue’aki ‘i ha fili

‘i he Fale Alea.

(5) ‘I he kupu ni —

“tu‘asila” ‘o ha taha ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ha tu‘asila, kau ki ai ha hala mo e Kolo pe feitu‘u, ‘a ia ‘e ala ngāue‘aki ke fetu‘utaki ki he taha ko ia lolotonga ‘a e

‘aho. ‘Oku ‘ikai kau ki ai ha puha positi ‘ofisi.

“tu‘uaki, pepa tu‘uaki (handbill), pepa tu‘uaki (pamphlet), pepa tu‘uaki

(poster) pe fanongonongo ki he fili” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ha tu‘uaki, pepa tu'uaki (handbill),pepa tu‘uaki (pamphlet), pepa tu‘uaki (poster), pe fanongonongo

‘oku ‘i ai ha me‘a ki he fili ka ‘oku ‘ikai kau ai ha tu‘uaki ‘i he nusipepa ‘o fanongonongo ai ‘a e fakahoko ‘o ha fakataha.

“me‘a ki he fili” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he me‘a ‘a ia ‘oku fakataumu‘a pe ngalingali ke ne uesia ha fili ‘i ha fili.

“lekooti letio pe vitio ki he fili” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ha lekooti letio pe vitio ‘a ia

‘oku ‘i ai ha me‘a ki he fili.

#### 7 Pulusi ‘o e ngaahi tu‘uaki fili ‘i he ‘initaneti

(1) ‘Oku fakahoko ‘e he taha ha hia kapau —

(a) ‘oku pulusi ‘e ha taha ha tu‘uaki fili ‘i he ‘initaneti pe ko e taha ko ia

‘oku ne fakatupu, fakangofua pe fakamafai ha tu‘uaki ki he fili ke pulusi ‘i he ‘initaneti;

(b) ‘oku fakataumu‘a ‘a e tu‘uaki ki he fili ke ne uesia ‘a e fili ‘i ha fili;

(c) ‘oku totongi ‘a e tu‘uaki ki he fili ‘e he taha pe ha taha kehe; pea

(d) ko e hingoa mo e tu‘asila ‘o e taha kuo ne fakamafai‘i ‘a e tu‘uaki ‘oku

‘ikai ke ha ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o e tu‘uaki.

(2) Kuo pau ki ha taha kuo halaia ki ha hia ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni ke ala mo‘ua ki he —

(a) kapau ko e taha faihia ko ha taha taautaha, totongi ‘a e mo‘ua pa‘anga

‘oku ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $1,000; pe

(b) kapau ko e taha faihia ko ha sino‘i kautaha, totongi ‘a e mo‘ua pa‘anga

‘oku ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $5,000.

(3) ‘I he kupu´ ni —

“tu‘asila” ‘o ha taha ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ha tu‘asila, kau ki ai ‘a e tu‘asila kakato

‘o e hala mo e Kolo pe feitu‘u, feitu‘u ‘i Tonga, ‘a ia ‘oku malava ke fakahoko ki ai ha fetu‘utaki ki he taha ko ia lolotonga ‘a e ‘aho. ‘Oku ‘ikai kau ki ai ha puha positi ‘ofisi.

#### 8 Taha fili ke fili ‘i ha fai‘anga fili kuo vahe‘i

(1) Kuo pau ke fili pe ha taha fili ‘i ha fili ‘i he fai‘anga fili kuo vahe‘i ki hono kolo.

(2) ‘E fakahā ‘e he Komisoni ‘i he fanongonongo fakapule‘anga ki he kau fili ‘a honau ngaahi fai‘anga fili kuo vahe‘i.

(3) ‘E ‘i ai ha fai‘anga fili kuo vahe‘i ‘i he Vahefonua takitaha ki he kau fili ‘oku nofo fakataimi pe ‘i ai pea ‘oku ‘i ha tohi fakakātoa ‘i ha Vahefonua kehe.

#### 9 Fili ‘a e kau ngāue ‘a e Komisoni

Ko ha taha fili ‘i ha fili ko ha ‘Ofisa Faifili pe ha ‘Ofisa Tokoni Fai Fili ‘i ha fai‘anga fili ‘e ngofua ke fili ‘i he taha ‘o ‘eni —

(a) ko e fai‘anga fili kuo vahe‘i ki hono kolo; pe

(b) koe fai‘anga fili ‘oku ngāue ki ai ‘i he ‘aho fili.

#### 10 Pekia ‘o ha Kanititeiti

(1) Kuo pau ke hoko atu ‘a e fili ki ha vāhenga fili, kapau ‘e pekia ha Kanititeiti ki ha fili ‘i ha vāhenga fili ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ‘aho ‘o e tapuni ‘o e ngaahi fokotu‘u mo e ‘aho fili.

(2) Kuo pau ki he Supavaisa ‘o e Fili, ‘i he‘ene ala lava, ke fokotu‘utu‘u ke toe pulusi ‘a e ngaahi pepa paloti ki he vāhenga fili. Kapau ‘oku ‘ikai malava ia kuo pau ki he Supavaisa ‘o e Fili ke fokotu‘utu‘u ke kohi‘i ‘a e hingoa ‘o e kanititeiti kuo pekia mo e siakale fili ‘oku fehangahangai mo e hingoa ‘i he ngaahi pepa paloti kotoa ki he vāhenga fili ko ia.

(3) Kuo pau ke fakapuliki ‘uli‘uli ‘i he ngaahi pepa paloti ‘oku toe pulusi ‘a e konga na‘e totonu ke hā ai ‘a e hingoa ‘a e kanititeiti kuo pekia mo e siakale fili.

(4) Kuo pau ke fanongonongo faka-pule‘anga ‘e he Supavaisa ‘o e Fili ‘a e pekia

‘o e kanititeiti pea fokotu‘utu‘u mo e Kau ‘Ofisa Faifili ‘i he fai‘anga fili takitaha ‘i he vāhenga fili ke fakahoko ke ‘ilo‘i ‘e he kau fili ‘a e pekia ‘a e kanititeiti.

#### 11 Tu‘utu‘uni ‘o e hokohoko ‘o e ngaahi hingoa ‘i he pepa paloti

(1) ‘I he kupu 9(5) ‘o e Lao ki he Fili Fale Alea kuo pau ki ha ‘Ofisa Faifili ke tu‘utu‘uni, kapau ‘oku lahi ange ‘a e tokolahi ‘o e kau kanititeiti ‘i he ngaahi sea ‘oku ‘atā, ‘a e hokohoko ‘a e hā ‘o e ngaahi hingoa ‘o e kau kanititeiti ‘i he ngaahi pepa paloti ke ngāue‘aki ‘i he fili.

(2) Kuo pau ki he hokohoko ‘i he pepa paloti ke tu’utu’uni ‘aki ha fili ‘i ha lulu,

‘aki hono fili ‘e he kanititeiti ha fika ‘a ia ko ‘ene fika ia ‘i he pepa paloti.

(3) Kuo pau ki he ‘Ofisa Faifili, ‘i he tapuni ‘o e ngaahi fokotu‘u ki he fili, ‘i he feitu‘u kuo ma‘u ki ai ‘a e ngaahi fokotu‘u ke fakahā fakapule‘anga pea ‘i he

‘ao ‘o kinautolu ‘oku ‘i he feitu‘u ko ia —

(a) ‘a e lisi ‘o e ngaahi hingoa ‘o e kau kanititeiti, ‘i he hokohoko ‘o hono ma‘u ‘o e ngaahi fokotu‘u; pea

(b) lau atu ‘a e lisi ko ia.

(4) Kuo pau ki he ‘Ofisa Faifili ke —

(a) maaka ‘i he ngaahi la‘i pepa takitaha ‘oku lalahi, lanu pe fa‘ahinga tatau ‘aki ha fika kehekehe, ‘o fakatatau ki he tokolahi ‘o e kau kanititeiti;

(b) peluki ‘a e ngaahi la‘i pepa takitaha ki he lalahi tatau, ‘a ia ‘oku maaka

‘aki ‘a e ngaahi fika kehekehe, pea fakahū ki ha kapa ‘oku lahi fe‘unga ke malava ai ‘a e ngaahi pepa ‘o fio tau‘ataina; pea

(c) kole ki he kanititeiti takitaha ‘i he hokohoko na‘a nau fili ke toho mei he kapa ‘a e la‘i pepa kuo peluki ‘e taha (1) pea ‘oatu ia ki he ‘Ofisa Faifili ‘a ia kuo pau ke ne folahi ‘a e la‘i pepa pea fakahā ‘a e fika, ‘a ia kuo pau ko e fika ia ‘i he fakahokohoko ‘e hā ai ‘a e kanititeiti ‘i he pepa paloti.

(5) Kapau ‘oku ‘ikai lava mai ha kanititeiti ‘e ngofua ke ne fakamafai‘i ha taha ke ne fakafofonga‘i ia. Kuo pau ki he fakamafai ko ia ke ‘i he tohi pea ‘oatu ia ki he ‘Ofisa Faifili kimu‘a pea fakahoko ‘a e toho.

(6) Kapau ‘oku ‘ikai lava mai ha kanititeiti pea ‘oku te‘eki ke ne fakamafai‘i ha taha ke ne fakafofonga‘i ia kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘e ha ‘Ofisa Polisi ‘a e toho mei he kapa ‘o fakafofonga‘i ‘a e kanititeiti.
