## KONGA I – NGAAHI KUPU TALATEU

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

(1) Kuo pau ke ui ‘a e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni ko e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ki he Kole Tu’unga ‘i he Fili 2016.

(2) Kuo pau ke kamata ngaue’aki ‘a e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni ‘i he ‘aho 17 ‘o Sune 2016.

#### 2 ‘Uhinga’i lea

Tukukehe ka toe fiema’u ‘i he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni ha ‘uhinga kehe ‘i hono tu’unga,

‘e ngaue’aki ‘a e ngaahi ‘uhinga’i lea ko eni:

“Lao” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Lao ki he Fili Fale Alea 1989, ‘i hono fakatonutonu;

“Tu’i Fakamaau Lahi” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he ‘Eiki Fakamaau Lahi ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi ‘o Tonga;

“Komisoni” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Komisoni Fili;

“Fakamaau’anga” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Fakamaau’anga Lahi ‘o Tonga;

“kole tu’unga ‘i he fili” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ha kole tu’unga ‘i he fili ‘i he kupu 25 ‘o e Lao ki he Fili Fale Alea 1989;

“Fakamaau” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Tu’i Fakamaau Lahi pea mo ha Fakamaau kehe ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi ‘o Tonga;

“Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni;

“Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi 2007 (‘i hono fakatonutonu mei he taimi ki he taimi).

#### 3 Taumu’a

‘Oku fa’u ‘a e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 29 ‘o e Lao ke tu’utu’uni ‘a e ngaahi founga fakahoko ngaue ke ngaue’aki ‘o fekau’aki mo e ngaahi kole tu’unga ‘i he fili ‘oku fakahoko ‘i he kupu 25 ‘o e Lao.

#### 4 Ngaue’aki

(1) Kuo pau ke lau fakataha ‘a e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni mo e Konga V ‘o e Lao pea kuo pau ke ngaue’aki ki he ngata’anga ‘a ia ‘oku na faitatau ai mo e ngaahi tu’utu’uni ‘o e Lao. Kuo pau ke ngaue’aki ‘a e Lao, kapau ‘oku ‘i ai ha faikehekehe.

(2) Kuo pau ke ngaue’aki ki he kotoa ‘o e ngaahi kole tu’unga ‘i he fili, kapau ‘oku

‘ikai ha tu’utu’uni ke ngaue’aki ‘i he Lao pe ‘i he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni, ‘a e ngaahi founga fakahoko ngaue ‘oku ha ‘i he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi, ki he ngata’anga ‘o e ‘ikai kenau faikehekehe mo e Lao pe ko e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni.

#### 5 Founga ‘o e fakamaau, fakataha mo e hopo

Ko e mafai ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi ‘i he’ene felave’i mo ha me’a fekau’aki mo e hopo’i ‘o ha kole tu’unga ‘i he fili kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘e ha Fakamaau pe ‘e taha ke fakamaau ta’e ‘i ai ha sula.

#### 6 Fai pau ki he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni

Kuo pau koe tu’utu’uni ‘ata’ata pe ‘i he mafai ‘o e Fakamaau’anga ki ha ‘ikai fai pau ki he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni ‘o fakatatau ki he ngaahi tu’utu’uni ‘o e kupu 35 mo e 36

‘o e Lao.

#### 7 Lave ki he Ngaahi Foomu

Tukukehe ‘o ka fakaha kehe ange, ko ha lave ki ha Foomu ‘aki ‘a e fika ‘i he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni ko e lave ia ki he Foomu ‘o e fika ko ia ‘i he Tepile ki he Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni.

#### 8 Kamata’anga ‘o e ngaahi hopo kole tu’unga ‘i he fili

(1) Kuo pau ke ‘i he Foomu 1 ha kole tu’unga ‘i hefili peakuo pau ke fakamo’oni hingoa ki ai ‘a e taha talatalaaki.

(2) Kuo pau ko e lahi ‘o e ngaahi tatau ‘o e kole tu’unga ‘i he fili ke faile, ke tatau mo e tokolahi ‘o e ni’ihi ke tufa ki ai, pea tanaki ki ai mo ha tatau ‘a e Fakamaau’anga.

#### 9 Ngaahi fa’ahi ki he kole tu’unga ‘i he fili

(1) Ko e ngaahi fa’ahi ki ha kole tu’unga ‘i he fili:

(a) ko e taha talatalaaki, ‘a ia kuo pau ke ‘i ai hano tu’unga ke fakahoko ‘a e kole ‘i he kupu 26 ‘o e Lao;

(b) ko e memipa ‘a ia ko hono fili pe ola ‘oku launga’i;

(c) kapau ‘oku launga ‘a e taha talatalaaki ‘i he ‘ulungaanga ‘o ha

‘ofisa, ko e Sea ‘o e Komisoni;

(d) ko ha taha kehe ‘e tu’utu’uni ‘e he Fakamaau’anga ke hoko ko ha fa’ahi kapau ko ‘ene ha ‘oku pehe ‘oku fiema’u pe taau ke fakapapau’i ha me’a

‘oku fehu’ia ‘i he kole tu’unga ‘i he fili.

(iii) ‘oku ne taukave’i na’e kanititeiti ‘i he fili; pea

(b) pe ‘oku tukuaki’i ‘e he taha talatalaaki:

(i) ko e fili na’e ‘ikai fakalao; pe

#### 11 Ngaahi totongi faile

Kuo pau ke tatau ‘a e totongi faile ‘o e faile ‘o ha kole tu’unga ‘i he fili pea mo e totongi faile kuo tu’utu’uni ki he ngaahi tohi fekau mo e fakamatala ‘o e ‘eke ‘i he Lao ki he Ngaahi Totongi Fakamaau’anga (Cap 12) ‘i hono fakatonutonu.

#### 12 Tufa ‘o e kole tu’unga ‘i he fili

(1) Tuku kehe ka tu’utu’uni kehe ‘e he Fakamaau’anga, kuo pau ke tufa fakafo’ituitui ‘a e kole tu’unga ‘i he fili kotoa ki he ngaahi fa’ahi kehe kotoa kuo fakahingoa ‘i loto ‘i he ‘aho ngaue ‘e nima (5) mei he ‘aho na’e faile ai ‘a e kole tu’unga ‘i he fili ko ia pe ‘i loto ‘i ha vaha’a taimi loloa ange ‘a ia ‘e ngofua ke faka’ataa ‘e he Fakamaau’anga ‘i he kole ‘a ha fa’ahi.

(2) Kuo pau ke fai ‘o fakatatau mo e Fekau 13 ‘o e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi ha kole ke fakaloloa‘ae taimi ki he tufa‘i heTu’utu’uni 12(1) ‘o e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ni pea ‘e ngofua ke fai ki mu’a pe hili ‘a e ‘osi ‘a e vaha’a taimi kuo fakamahino pau.

(3) Kuo pau ke fakapipiki ‘i mu’a ‘i he tohi kole tu’unga ‘i he fili kotoa ha tohi fanongonongo ‘o e tu’utu’uni fakahinohino ‘o fakatatau mo e Fekau 11, Tu’utu’uni 2 ‘o e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi.

(4) Kuo pau ke fakakakato pea faile ‘e he taha ‘oku ne tufa ‘a e kole tu’unga ‘i he fili ha tohi fakamo’oni ‘o e tufa ‘o fakatatau mo e Fekau 11, Tu’utu’uni 4 ‘o e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ‘o e Fakamaau’anga Lahi, ‘a ia kuo pau ko e fakamo’oni na’e tufa ‘a e tohi ‘o hange ko hono fakaha ‘i ai.
