# Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Fefolau‘aki Vakapuna Sivile (Fakangatangata ki hono Ngaue‘aki ‘o e Kelekele)

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Fefolau'aki Vakapuna Sivile (Fakangatangata ki hono Ngaue'aki 'o e Kelekele) VAHE 23.02.03 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 1. to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 3 C T NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE FEFOLAU'AKI VAKAPUNA SIVILE (FAKANGATANGATA KI HONO NGAUE'AKI 'O E KELEKELE)1 Na'e fa'u 'i he mafai 'o e Kupu 14(1) 'o e Lao ki he Fefolau'aki Vakapuna Sivile 1990 pea hoko atu 'i he mafai 'o e Kupu 237 'o e Lao ki he Fefolau'aki Vakapuna Sivile 2014 Kamata'anga [1 ‘o Sanuali, 1996]

1. (1) ‘E ui ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ko ‘eni ko e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Fefolau‘aki Vakapuna Sivile (Fakangatangata ki hono Ngaue‘aki ‘o e Kelekele).

(2) ‘E kamata hono ngaue‘aki ‘a e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ni hili ‘a e ‘aho ‘e 14 mei he

‘aho ‘oku pulusi ai ‘i he Kasete.

2. ‘Oku fakapekia ‘a e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Fefolau‘aki Vakapuna Sivile (Fakangatangata ki hono Ngaue‘aki ‘o e Kelekele) 1991.

3. ‘Oku fakatapui mo fakangata ‘a e ngaahi me‘a ko ‘eni ‘i he ngaahi ‘elia ‘oku fakamahino‘i ‘i he ngaahi Tepile ‘Uluaki, Ua, mo e Tolu mo e ngaahi fakatata ‘oku

‘uhinga ki ai —

(a) fokotu‘u ‘o e ngaahi fale, ngaahi langa pe ngaahi me‘a kehe;

(b) ko e to ‘o e ngaahi ‘akau;

(c) ko e to pe fakatupu ha fa‘ahinga ‘akau pe ngoue tukukehe ka loto ki ai ‘a e Minisita ‘i he ngaahi feitu‘u ‘e fakamahino‘i ‘e he Minisita ‘i ha tohi;

(d) ko hono ‘omai pe tau ha ngaahi vaka, me‘a ngaue pe saliote misini pe li taula pe no‘o ha fa‘ahinga vaka pe tau ai ha fa‘ahinga me‘a ngaue pe saliote misini.

## TEPILE I

FAKA‘ATA ‘O E MALA‘E VAKAPUNA FUA‘AMOTU Tafa‘aki Fakaloloa ‘o e Funga To‘anga:

‘Ihe ongo tafa‘aki ‘o e Mala‘e Vakapuna ‘oku lele fakahahake-tonga-hahake pea mo hihifo- tokelau-hihifo koe‘uhi ko ha laine ‘e kamata mei he poini pe ‘i he funga kelekele ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna, ‘a ia ‘e mita ‘e 150 mei he laine lotomalie ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna, ‘e ta fakatuliki hangatonu ki ai ‘i he tahakehake ko e 1 ‘i he 7 mei he laine fakahangatonu ki he tafa‘aki ‘i he fukahi kelekele ke ‘ata mei he ngaahi me‘a fakafe‘atungia kotoa pe.

‘I he ongo tafa‘aki ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘oku lele fakatokelau-tonga koe‘uhi ko ha laine ‘e kamata mei ha poini pe ‘i he funga kelekele ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna, ‘a ia ‘e mita ‘e 45 mei he laine to‘anga vakapuna ‘o ta fakatuliki hangatonu ki ai ‘i he tahakehake ko e 1 ‘i he 7 mei he laine fakahangatonu ki he tafa‘aki kuo pau ke ‘ata ia mei he ngaahi me‘a fakafe‘atungia kotoa pe.

Tafa‘aki ‘oku Fakatautauto/Puna hake ai ‘a e Vakapuna:

‘I he ongo tafa‘aki pe ‘i he ngata‘anga fakatou‘osi ‘o e mala‘e vakapuna ‘oku lele fakahahake-tonga-hahake mo hihifo-tokelau-hihifo ‘oku ‘i ai ha ngaahi konga ‘a ia ‘oku hange ha foneli honau fotunga ‘o hange ‘oku ha ‘i he Fakatata 1 ‘i he Tepile ni ‘a ia ‘e faka‘ata‘ata mei he ngaahi me‘a fakafe‘atungia kotoa pe. Ko e ngaahi tokalelei ko ‘eni kuo fakangata kinautolu ‘aki ha fo‘i ngata‘anga ki loto ‘a ia ‘oku mita ‘e 150 hono loloa pea ‘a ia ‘oku fakatuliki hangatonu ki he laine lotomalie ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘o tu‘u ia fakahangatonu ki he tafa‘aki ‘i he mita ‘e 300 ki he feitu‘u mahiki‘anga mei he konga puna‘anga ko ia ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna pea fakafalahi ‘i he ongo tafa‘aki ‘o fe‘unga mo e peseti ‘e 12.5. ‘E hakehake atu ‘a e ngaahi konga ko ‘eni ‘i ha tuliki ko e 1 ‘i he 62.5(1.6%) ki he fakalavalava mei he ngata‘anga ki loto, pea ko e ma‘olunga ‘o e hake ko ia ‘e fakatuliki hangatonuatuia ki he lainelotomalie ‘oeto‘anga vakapuna ‘a ia ko e 1 ‘i he7 mei he ngaahi ngata‘anga ‘o e konga ko ‘eni.

‘Ihe ongo ngata‘anga ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘oku lele fakatokelau-tonga ‘oku ‘i ai ha ngaahi konga ‘a ia ‘oku hange ha foneli honau fotunga ‘o hange kuo ha ‘i he Fakataataa 1 ‘i he tepile ni ‘e faka‘ata mei he ngaahi me‘a fakafe‘atungia kotoa pe. Ko e ngaahi konga ko ‘eni kuo fakangata kinautolu ‘aki ha fo‘i ngata‘anga ki loto ‘a ia ‘oku mita ‘e 80 hono loloa ‘a ia

‘oku fakatuliki hangatonu ki he laine lotomalie ‘o e feitu‘u mahiki‘anga mei he konga puna‘anga ko ia ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna pea fakafalahi ‘i he ongo tafa‘aki fakatou‘osi ‘e fe‘unga mo e peseti ‘e 10. ‘E hakehake atu ‘a e ngaahi tokalelei ko ‘eni ‘iha tuliki ko e 1 ‘i he 40 (2.5%) ki he laine fakahangatonu ki he tafa‘aki mei he ngata‘anga ki loto, pea ko e ma‘olunga ‘o e hake ko ia ‘e fakatuliki hangatonu atu ia ki he laine lotomalie ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘a ia ko e 1 ‘i he 7 mei he ngaahi ngata‘anga ‘o e ngaahi tokalelei ko ‘eni.

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Fefolau'aki Vakapuna Sivile (Fakangatangata ki hono Ngaue'aki 'o e Kelekele) Peesi 6 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to Konga ‘i loto ‘o e ‘Ata Fakatatau hifo ki he To‘anga:

Ko e konga ‘ata fakatatau hifo ki he to‘anga ‘oku ‘i loto ia ‘i ha tokalelei ‘oku lele fakahangatonu ki he tafa‘aki ‘oku tu‘u fute 150 (mita 45) ki ‘olunga mei he poini ‘oku fua mei ai ‘a e ma‘olunga ‘o e mala‘e vaka-puna. Ko hono fakangatangata ki tu‘a ‘oku ‘i he letiasi fakahangatonu ki he tafa‘aki ko e si‘isi‘i taha ko e mita ‘e 4,000 ‘o fua mei he ngata‘anga ‘o e to‘anga. Vakai ki he Fakataataa 2.

FAKA‘ATA ‘O E MALA‘E VAKAPUNA - KAUFANA, LAVINIA, MATA‘AHO Tafa‘aki Fakaloloa ‘o e Funga To‘anga:

‘I he ongo tafa‘aki ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘oku lele fakatokelau-tonga koe‘uhi ko ha laine ‘e kamata mei ha poini ‘i he fukahi kelekele ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna, ‘a ia ‘e mita ‘e 30 mei he laine lotomalie ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘o to fakatuliki hangatonu ki ai ‘i he tahakehake ko e 1 ‘i he 5 mei he laine fakalava ‘e faka‘ata‘ataa ia mei he ngaahi me‘a fakafe‘atungia kotoa pe.

Tafa‘aki ‘oku Fakatautauto/Puna hake ai ‘a e Vakapuna:

‘I he ongo tafa‘aki ‘o e ngaahi to‘anga vakapuna ‘oku lele fakatokelau-tonga ‘oku ‘i ai ha ngaahi konga ‘a ia ‘oku hange ha foneli honau fotunga kuo ha ‘i he Fakatata 1 ‘i he Tepile ni ‘a ia ‘e faka‘ata‘ata mei he ngaahi me‘a fakafe‘atungia kotoa pe. Ko e ngaahi konga ko ‘eni kuo fakangata kinautolu ‘aki ha fo‘i ngata‘anga ki loto ‘a ia ‘oku mita ‘e 80 hono loloa ‘a ia ‘oku fakatuliki hangatonu ki he laine lotomalie ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘o tu‘u ia fakahangatonu ki he tafa‘aki ‘i he mita ‘e 60 ki he feitu‘u mahiki‘anga mei he konga puna‘anga ko ia ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna pea fakafalahi atu

‘i he ongo tafa‘aki fakatou‘osi ‘e fe‘unga mo e peseti 10. ‘E hakehake atu ‘a e ngaahi konga ko ‘eni ‘i ha tuliki ko e 1 ‘i he 20 (5%) ki he fakahangatonu ki he tafa‘aki mei he ngata‘anga ki loto, pea ko e ma‘olunga ‘o e hake ko ia ‘e fakatuliki hangatonu atu ia ki he laine lotomaile ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘a ia ko e 1 ‘i he 5 mei he ngaahi ngata‘anga ‘o e konga ko ‘eni.

## TEPILE 3

FAKA‘ATAA ‘O E MALA‘E VAKAPUNA FAKAVAHA‘A PULE‘ANGA LUPEPAU‘U & SALOTE PILOLEVU Tafa‘aki Fakaloloa ‘o e Funga To‘anga:

‘I he ongo tafa‘aki ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ko ha laine ‘e kamata mei ha poini ‘i he fukahi kelekele ‘i he ngata‘anga ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘a ia ‘e mita ‘e 45 mei he ngaahi laine lotomalie ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘o to fakatuliki hangatonu ki ai ‘i he tahakehake ko e 1 ‘i he 7 mei he laine fakaloloa ki he tafa‘aki e faka‘ata‘ata ia mei he ngaahi me‘a fakafe‘atungia kotoa pe.

Tafa‘aki ‘oku Fakatautauto/Teuteu puna ai ‘a e Vakapuna:

‘I he ngata‘anga fakatokelau ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘oku ‘i ai ‘a e ngaahi tokalelei

‘oku fuo hange ha foneli ‘o hange ko ia ‘oku ha ‘i he Fakataataa 1 ‘i he Tepile ko ‘eni

‘e faka‘ata‘ata ia mei he ngaahi me‘a fakafe‘atungia kotoa pe. ‘Oku mita ‘e 90 ‘a e loloa ‘o e tapa ki loto ‘o e fo‘i tokalelei ‘o tuliki hangatonu ki he laine ‘i he lotomalie

‘o e to‘anga vakapuna ‘a ia ‘oku lele fakaloloa ki he tafa‘aki mita ‘e 90 mei he to‘anga ki he mahiki‘anga ‘o e to‘anga vakapuna pea faikehekehe‘aki ‘a e peseti 12.5 ‘i he ongo tafa‘aki. ‘Oku tahakehake ‘a e ngaahi tokalelei ‘aki ‘a e 1 ‘i he 40 (2.5%) mei he tapa ki loto ki he laine fakaloloa ki he tafa‘aki, pea ‘oku na tahakehake fakatuliki totonu ki he laine ‘i lotomalie, ‘i he to‘anga vakapuna ‘aki ‘a e 1 ‘i he 7 mei he ongo tapa ‘o e ngaahi tokalelei ko ‘eni.
