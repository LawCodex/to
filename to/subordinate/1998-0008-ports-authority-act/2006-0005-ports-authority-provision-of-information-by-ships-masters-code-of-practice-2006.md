# Tu‘utu‘uni Fakangaue 2006 'a e Ma'u Mafai ki he Ngaahi Taulanga (Fekau'aki mo e ngaahi Fakamatala/Lekooti ke 'omai 'e he 'Eikivaka)

Tu'utu'uni Fakangaue 'a e Ma'u Mafai ki he Ngaahi Taulanga (Fekau'aki mo e ngaahi Fakamatala/ Lekooti ke 'omai 'e he 'Eikivaka) VAHE 22.07.02 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 1 to Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 Peesi 3 C T TU'UTU'UNI FAKANGAUE 'A E MA'U MAFAI KI HE NGAAHI TAULANGA (FEKAU'AKI MO E NGAAHI FAKAMATALA/ LEKOOTI KE 'OMAI 'E HE 'EIKIVAKA) Na‘e fa‘u ‘i he mafai ‘o e Kupu 79 ‘o e Lao ki he Ma‘u Mafai ki he Ngaahi Taulanga1 Kamata'anga [6 ‘o ‘Epeleli, 2006]

1. Ko eTu‘utu‘uni fakangaue´ni ‘elau ko eTu‘utu‘uni Fakangāue 2006 ‘a e Ma‘uMafai ki he Ngaahi Taulanga (Fekau‘aki mo e ngaahi Fakamatala/ Lekooti ke ‘omai ‘e he

‘Eikivaka).

2. ‘I he tū‘uta ki he fa‘ahinga Taulanga, kuo pau ke ‘oange ‘e he ‘Eikivaka ‘a e tohi fakamo‘oni ki he Pule Taulanga pe fakafofonga ‘a e Pule Taulanga ‘oku´ne pule‘i ‘a e Taulanga ko ia —

(a) Tatau ‘o e tohi ‘o e uta kotoa pe ‘oku ‘i he vaka pea mo e natula mo e lahi ‘o e uta ‘oku ‘i he vaka pe ‘e fakahifo ‘i he Taulanga.

(b) Tatau ‘o e tohi ki he Pule Taulanga pe tokotaha Ma‘u Mafai pe fakafofonga ‘o e Pule Taulanga ‘o fekau‘aki mo e vaka, ngaahi koloa, kau pasese ‘i he vaka pe ‘amanaki ke nau heka ki he vaka.

3. Ka ‘ikai ke fai ‘a e ‘Eikivaka ki hono ‘oange ‘a e ngaahi Fakamatala/lekooti ‘oku

‘uhinga ki ai ‘a e Tu‘utu‘uni Fakangaue ‘oku ma‘u leva ‘e he Pule Taulanga pe tokotaha Ma‘u Mafai pe fakafofonga ‘o e Pule Taulanga ‘a e mafai ke ne to‘o ‘a e ngaahi tohi fakamatala mei he taha pe.

Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni 4 VAHE 22.07.02 Tu'utu'uni Fakangaue 'a e Ma'u Mafai ki he Ngaahi Taulanga (Fekau'aki mo e ngaahi Fakamatala/ Lekooti ke 'omai 'e he 'Eikivaka) Peesi 4 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to

4. ‘E ngofua ki he Pule Taulanga ke ‘oua te ne tali ke hū mai ha vaka kapau ‘e ‘ikai ke talangofua ‘a e ‘Eikivaka ke ne ‘oange ‘a e ngaahi fakamatala ‘i he Tu‘utu‘uni Fakangāue pe fakafaingata‘a‘ia ‘a e fengaue‘aki.

5. ‘E ‘ikai ngofua ki ha vaka ke mavahe mei he Taulanga tukukehe ka foaki ange ‘e he Pule Taulanga ‘a e Tohi Folau ‘a e vaka ko ia ‘a ia ‘oku fakaiikiiki ai ‘a e uta mo e kakai ‘i he vaka. Ko e ‘Eikivaka ‘e fakamo‘oni‘i ‘i he tatau ‘o e Tohi Folau ko hono fakapapau‘i ‘oku tonu ‘a e ngaahi fakamatala.

6. Ko e Tu‘utu‘uni Fakangaue ‘e fakatonutonu ‘i ha taimi pe ‘e he Poate Ma‘u Mafai ki he Ngaahi Taulanga.
