(1) Kuo pau ke fokotu’u atu ‘i he Foomu 1 ‘a e tohi kole ki hono foaki pe fakafo’ou

‘o ha laiseni vaka toutai muli ‘oku ngāue fakalotofonua ‘i he kupu 31 ‘o e Lao pea kuo pau ke ‘oatu fakataha mo e totongi kuo tu’utu’uni.

(2) Kapau ‘oku ma’u ha tohi kole ‘i he tu’utu’uni-si’i (1), kuo pau ke fakakakato
