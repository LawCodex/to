#### 4 Ngaahi tātānaki

‘A e Memipa

(1) Kuo pau ke tatanaki ‘e ha Memipa ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga ha peseti ‘e nima ‘o e Vahenga fakata‘u ‘o e Memipa ‘a ia ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘i he ‘Aho Sivi ‘o Siulai.

(2) ‘E ngofua ke tatanaki ‘e ha Memipa ha pa‘anga fakalahi ki he ngaahi pa‘anga

‘oku ne totongi ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni si‘i 4(1) ‘i he tu‘unga mo e ngaahi taimi ‘e fakapapau‘i mo fakaha ‘e he Memipa ki he ‘Ofisa Pule Fakahoko Ngaue.

(3) Ko e ngaahi tatanaki ‘a e Memipa ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni-si‘i 4(1) kuo pau ke to‘o ia mei hono Vahenga pea ‘e fakahu ia ‘e he Pule Ngaue‘anga ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga.

(4) Ko e ngaahi tatanaki ‘a e Memipa ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni-si‘i 4(2) ‘e lava —

(a) ke to‘o mei hono Vahenga pea ‘e fakahu ‘e he Pule Ngaue‘anga ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga; pe

(b) ke totongi fakahangatonu ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga ‘e he Memipa.

‘A e Pule Ngaue‘anga

(5) Kuo pau ke tatanaki ‘e he Pule Ngaue‘anga ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga ma‘ae Memipa takitaha ha peseti ‘o e Vahenga fakata‘u ‘o e Memipa, ‘a ia ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘i he ‘Aho Sivi ‘o Siulai, ‘o fakatatau ki he sikeili ko ‘eni —

(a) mei he ‘aho 1 ‘o Siulai 1999 ki he ‘aho 30 ‘o Sune 2004 - peseti ‘e nima;

(b) mei he ‘aho 1 ‘o Siulai 2004 ki he ‘aho 30 ‘o Sune 2009 - peseti ‘e fitu mo e konga; pe

(c) mei he ‘aho 1 ‘o Siulai 2009 - peseti ‘e hongofulu.
