# Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ki hono Lesisita ‘o e Hingoa ‘o e Ngaahi Pisinisi 2025

(1) ‘E ui ‘a e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ko ‘eni ko e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ki hono Lesisita

‘o e Hingoa ‘o e Ngaahi Pisinisi 2025.

(2) Kuo pau ke ngaue’aki ‘a e Ngaahi Tu’utu’uni ko ‘eni ‘i he ‘aho ‘e kasete’i ai.

#### 3 Ngaahi houa ngaue

(1) Kuo pauke‘ataa‘aeʻofisiʻo e Failesisitaki hekakai ʻihe ʻahongauekotoa pe.

(2) ‘E ngofua ke ‘ataa ‘a e lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ki he kakai ‘i tu’a mei he ngaahi houa ‘oku ‘ataa ai ‘a e ‘ofisi ‘o e Failesisita.

#### 4 Lesisita ‘o e Ngaahi Hingoa Pisinisi

(1) Kuo pau ki he Failesisita ke ne fokotuʻu, tauhi mo ngaueʻaki ha lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻi ha founga ʻoku tali ʻe he Failesisita ʻi he kupu 10 ʻo e Lao.

(2) Kuo pau ke kau ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti, ʻi he tuʻunga maʻulalo taha, ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala ko ʻeni –

(a) ngaahi hingoa pisinisi kotoa pe kuo lesisita;

(b) ngaahi fika lesisita ki he ngaahi hingoa pisinisi kotoa kuo lesisita;

(c) ko e tuʻasila ʻo e tefitoʻi feituʻu pisinisi mo e ngaahi feituʻu kehe ʻoku fakahoko ai ʻa e pisinisi ʻi he hingoa pisinisi;

(d) ko e ʻaho ʻoku foaki ai ʻa e lesisita kotoa pe ʻo e hingoa pisinisi;

(e) ko ha hisitolia ʻo e ngaahi faile kotoa ʻi he Lao ʻoku fekauʻaki mo hono foaki ʻo ha hingoa pisinisi; mo

(f) ha fakamatalafakasitetisitika ʻokupeheʻe he Failesisitaʻokutaaumei he taimi ki he taimi ke tanaki, ‘a ia kuo pau ko e ngaahi fakamatala ko ia ke Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki hono Lesisita 'o e Hingoa 'o e Ngaahi Pisinisi 2025 Kupu 5 to Peesi 7 mavahevahe ke ‘oua na’a ‘ilo’i ‘a e fakamatala mei ha lesisita hingoa pisinisi pau.

#### 5 Fakahu ‘o e ngaahi tohi ki he lesisita mo e ngaahi ngaue faka‘initaneti

(1) ʻE ngofua ke fekauʻi ʻe he Failesisita ʻa e fakahu ʻo ha ngaahi tohi kole, fanongonongo pe tohi ʻi he Lao pe ko e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni, ke fakahoko pe ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) ʻOku ʻikai ngaueʻaki ʻa e tuʻutuʻuni siʻi (1) ʻi he taimi ʻoku ʻave ai ki he Failesisita ha tohi faka-fakamaauʻanga.

(3) Kuo pau ke ngaueʻaki ʻa e founga ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ki he ngaahi tohi kotoa ʻoku fakahu mai ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(4) Kapau ʻoku lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ʻoatu ha tohi ki he Failesisita ʻo ngaueʻaki ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti —

(a) kuo pau ko e tu’asila ‘imeili ki he fetu’utaki mo e taha ‘oku ne lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ‘oatu ‘a e tohi ki he Failesisita ke ‘oatu fakataha mo e tohi; pea

(b) kuo pau ko e tu’asila ‘imeili ki he fetu’utaki mo e taha ‘oku ne ma’u ‘a e hingoa pisinisi ke ‘oatu fakataha mo e tohi.

(5) Kapau ʻoku pehe ʻe he Failesisita ko e tohi ʻoku fakahu ange –

(a) ʻoku kau ai ʻa e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku fepaki mo e lao;

(b) ʻi he ʻuhinga ko ha faʻahinga meʻa naʻe ʻikai ke fakahoko pe naʻe fakamatala halaʻi, kuo teʻeki ai ke fakakakato fakalelei;

(c) ʻoku ʻikai ke faipau ki he ngaahi fiemaʻu ʻa e Lao, ko e Tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni, pe ko e founga ʻoku ʻoatu ʻe he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti; pe

(d) ʻokuʻiai ʻae ngaahi fakamatalaʻikai totonuʻoku ne fakatupuʻae ngaahi meʻa ʻi he tohi ke matuʻaki takihalaʻi,

‘e ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ne ʻikai tali ke lesisita pe ma’u mai ‘a e tohi, pea kuo pau ke fakamahino na’e ‘ikai ke fakahu mai, pea ʻe ngofua ki he Failesisita ke fekauʻi ke fakatonutonu pe fakakakato lelei ʻa e tohi pea toe fakahu mai, pe ke fakahu ha tohi foʻou ʻi hono tuʻunga.

(6) Ko ha tohi ʻoku fakahu mai ‘i he Lao ni pe ʻi he Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni ʻoku lau ia naʻe ʻikai ke fakahu tuku kehe kapau ʻoku tali ʻe he Failesisita.

(7) Tuku kehe ka ʻi ai ha ngofua makehe mei he Failesisita, kuo tali ʻi he ngaahi makatuʻunga ʻoku pehe ‘e he Failesisita ʻoku taau, kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai tali ‘e he Failesisita ha tohi ‘oku ‘ikai ke faipau ki he Tu’utu’uni ko eni.

#### 6 Kau ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai mo e ngaahi ʻakauni ʻa e kau fakafofongaʻi

(1) ʻE ngofua ki ha taha, kau ai ha sino fakalao, sino fakalao muli, pe fakafofonga fakapuleʻanga, ke hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ke ne ngaueʻaki ʻa e lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ki he taumuʻa ke ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ke kole ha ngaahi tohi pe ha fakamoʻoni kehe mei ha taha pe ʻoku ne pe ‘oku ne fakataumuʻa ke hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai koeʻuhi ke fakapapauʻi ʻa e fakaʻilongaʻi ʻo e taha pe sino ko ia ʻi he fiemalie fakapotopoto ki ai ʻa e Failesisita.

(3) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita, ʻi he tohi kole, ke tali mo fokotuʻu ‘i ha‘ane tali ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ʻi he hingoa ʻo e pe maʻae taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ki he taumuʻa ke ne ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(4) Kuo pau ke ʻikai ʻi ai ha totongi ki he hoko ko ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai pea mo hono fokotuʻu ʻo ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

(5) Kuo pau ki he tohi kole ki ha ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ke –

(a) ʻi he founga kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita; pea

(b) fakataha mo ha fakamoʻoni ʻoku fiemalie ki ai ʻa e Failesisita fekauʻaki mo e fakaʻilongaʻi ʻo e taha pe niʻihi fakafofongaʻi.

(6) Koeʻuhi ke lava ha taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ʻo faʻu ha faile ʻi loto ʻi he lesisita, kuo pau ki he taha ngaueʻaki kuo fakamafai ke ne fuofua hu ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻo ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala hu atu ʻoku tauhi fakafoʻituitui ʻo hange ko e lea maluʻi, fika fakapulipuli (PINs) pe hange ko ia. ʻOku ʻikai fiemaʻu ʻa e Failesisita ke ne fakapapauʻi pe ko e taha faile ʻi he ʻinitanetikohatahangaueʻakikuofakamafaiʻoku‘iai‘enetotonukengaueʻaki ʻa e fakamatala hu atu ʻoku fakahu ki he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(7) Kuo pau ki he totongi ʻo e ngaahi totongi, moʻua, mo e ngaahi totongi tomui ki helesisitafakaʻinitanetikefakahokomeiheʻakauni ‘ohatahafakafofongaʻi.

(8) ʻE ngofua ki he totongi ʻo e paʻanga ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ke ngaueʻaki ha founga kuo tali ʻe he Failesisita mei he taimi ki he taimi.

(9) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ʻoatu ha fakamatala ʻi he mahina taki taha ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi kotoa pe ʻo fakaha ai ʻa e ngaahi kuletiti mo e tepiti ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

#### 7 Ngaahi foomu lesisita fakaʻinitaneti

(1) Kuo pau ki ha tohi, fanongonongo pe foomu ʻoku fakahu mai ʻo fakafou ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ke fakakakato fakatatau ki he ngaahi fakamatala, ngaahi fakahinohino pe ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻoku fakamahinoʻi ʻi he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti, pe ʻe he Failesisita.

(2) Kuo pau ki ha foomu kuo tali ‘e he Failesisita ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ke fakakakato‘ofakatataukihengaahifakahinohino,ngaahifakamatalapengaahi tuʻutuʻuni ‘oku ‘i loto ‘i he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti.

(3) Kapau ko ha tohi, faile pe fanongonongo kuo fakahu ʻo fakafou ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ʻoku fiemaʻu ha toe fakamatala tanaki, kuo pau ki he fakamatala ko ia ke tuku hake ʻi he founga fakaʻilekitulonika ʻi he ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

#### 8 Totongi fakalukufua

(1) Ki he ngaahi taumua ‘o e kupu 21 ‘o e Lao, kuo pau ki he ngaahi totongi ʻoku ha ʻi he Tepile 1 ke totongi ki he Failesisita ʻo fekauʻaki mo e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku felaveʻi mo ia.

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he ngaahi totongi ʻoku ha ʻi he Tepile 1 ke kau ai ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi totongi tomui mo e ngaahi moʻua ʻe ala totongi ki hono fakahu ʻo ha tohi ki he Failesisita ʻi he hili pe ‘a e ‘osi ‘o e taimi kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he Lao pe Tuʻutuʻuni ko eni.

(3) ‘E ngofua ki he ngaahi totongi mo e founga totongi ki hono ngaueʻaki ʻo e ngaue fakaʻinitaneti ke fakahoko ki he ngaahi founga mo e ngaahi tu’utu’uni kuo tuku atu mei he taimi ki he taimi ‘e he Failesisita.

#### 9 Taimi ki hono totongi ʻo e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua, pe ngaahi

totongi ki he faile tomui

(1) Kuo pau ke totongi ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua pe ngaahi totongi ki he faile tomui ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni ʻi he taimi ʻoku fakahoko ai ʻa e ngaue fakapaʻanga fekauʻaki mo ia.

(2) Kuo pau ke totongi ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua mo e ngaahi totongi tomui –

(a) ʻi ha founga totongi ʻoku tali ʻe he Failesisita mei he taimi ki he taimi; pe

(b) ʻaki ʻa e tepiti pe mei he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi ʻoku kuletiti ʻo ʻikai siʻi hifo ʻi he mahuʻinga ʻo e moʻua pe totongi.

#### 10 Fakaʻataa ʻo e ngaahi totongi, ngaahi moʻua pe ngaahi totongi tomui

(1) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita, ‘i he ‘ene tuʻutuʻuni, ʻi ha fanongonongo tohi ki he taha ʻe ala totongi ha totongi, moʻua pe totongi tomui ‘oku tu’utu’uni ‘e he Lao pe ‘e he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni, ke ne fakaʻataa kakato pe ha konga ʻo ia ‘i he makatu’unga ‘oku ha ‘i he tuʻutuʻuni siʻi (2).

(2) ʻE ngofua ki he Failesisita ke ne fakaʻataa ha totongi, moʻua pe totongi tomui kapau ʻoku ne pehe ko e fiemaʻu ke totongi kakato pe ha konga ʻo ia – Kupu 11 Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki hono Lesisita 'o e Hingoa 'o e Ngaahi Pisinisi 2025 Peesi 10 to

(a) ʻoku ngalingali te ne fakatupu ha faingataʻa lahi ki he taha ʻe ala moʻua ke ne totongi;

(b) ʻoku ʻikai ke tatau ia mo e meʻa ʻoku lolotonga hoko;

(c) ʻoku ngalingali ke hoko ha uesia ki he kau kuletitoa ʻo e taha ʻoʻona ‘o e hingoa pisinisi ‘oku fakafehu’i; pe

(d) ko e ngaahi makatuʻunga ʻo e vahevahe taau ʻoku fakamahino pau ʻa e poupou ki ha fakaʻataa.

(3) ʻE ngofua ke fekauʻi ʻe he Failesisita ke totongi ha totongi, moʻua, pe totongi tomui ʻi he taimi ʻoku fakahu mai ai ha tohi fakataha mo ha kole ke fakaʻataa ʻa e ngaahi totongi, ngaahimoʻua pe ngaahi totongi tomui, pea kapau ʻe taliʻa e fakaʻataa, ʻe ngofua ke fakafoki ʻe he Failesisita ʻa e totongi ki he ʻakauni ʻa e taha fakafofongaʻi.

#### 11 Tuʻasila ʻimeili

(1) ‘I he taimi ‘oku lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ‘oatu ‘a e tohi ki he Failesisita ‘i he ngaue faka‘initaneti —

(a) kuo pau ko e tu’asila ‘imeili ki he fetu’utaki mo e taha lesisita, tiliva, tuku atu pe ‘oatu ‘a e tohi ki he Failesisita ke ‘oatu fakataha mo e tohi; pea

(b) kuo pau ko e tu’asila ‘imeili ki he fetu’utaki mo e taha ʻoʻona ‘o e hingoa pisinisi ke ‘oatu fakataha mo e tohi.

(2) ‘E ngofua ki he Failesisita ke fetu’utaki mo e taha na’a ne lesisita ‘a e tohi ‘o fakafou ‘i he ‘imeili.

#### 12 Tohi kole ke lesisita

(1) Kuo pau ke fakahu mai ha tohi kole ke lesisita ha hingoa pisinisi fakataha mo e totongi totonu kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻi he Tepile 1 pea ʻe fakahu ki he lesisita fakaʻinitaneti ʻo fakafou ʻi ha ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) Kapau ʻe ʻikai tali ha tohi kole ‘e he Failesisita, kuo pau ki he Failesisita ke fakahoko ange ki he taha kole ‘i ha fetuʻutaki tohi ‘a e ngaahi ‘uhinga hono

‘ikai tali.

(3) ʻE ngofua ke fakafoki ha tohi kole ʻe he taha kole ki he Failesisita, pea ʻi he tuʻunga ko ʻeni, kuo pau ‘e ʻikai fakafoki ʻa e totongi kole.

(4) ʻI he taimi ʻoku maʻu mai ai ha tohi kole ʻoku faipau ki he Lao mo e Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni ko ʻeni, kuo pau ke ‘oatu ‘e he Failesisita ki he taha kole ha tohi fakamoʻoni ʻo e lesisita hingoa pisinisi ʻoku ha ai ʻa e ʻaho ʻo e lesisita.

#### 13 Vahaʻataimi ʻo e lesisita

(1) Kuo pau ki ha lesisita ‘o ha hingoa pisinisi ke ngaue’aki ‘i he ta’u ‘e taha ka ‘e ngofua ke fakafo’ou ‘i he ta’u kotoa ‘aki hano faile ki he Failesisita ha fakamatala fakafo’ou ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 15 ‘o e Lao.

(2) Kuo pau ke ‘oatu fakataha ha fakamatala fakafo’ou mo e fanongonongo totonu

‘o e totongi fakafo’ou ‘a e ngaue fakapisinisi ‘i hono tu’utu’uni ‘i he Tepile 1.

#### 14 Fakatonutonu ʻo e Lesisita

(1) Kapau ko e fakamatala ʻi loto ʻi he Tohi Fakamoʻoni ʻo e Hingoa Pisinisi pe ʻi he Lesisita Hingoa Pisinisi ʻoku ʻikai ke tonu koeʻuhi ko ha liliu ʻi he ngaahi makatuʻunga, kuo pau ki he Taha Lesisita ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 8 ‘o e Lao, ke ne faile ha fakatonutonu ki he lesisita ‘o e hingoa pisinisi ‘o fakafou ‘i ha ngaue fakaʻinitaneti.

(2) Kuo pau ke kau ʻi he fakatonutonu ʻa e totongi kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻi he Tepile 1.

(3) Kapau ʻoku maʻu ʻe he Failesisita ha fakatonutonu ʻi he tuʻutuʻuni siʻi (1) ʻa ia ʻoku fakatonutonu ai ʻa e fakamatala ʻoku ha ʻi he Tohi Fakamoʻoni ʻo e Lesisita Hingoa Pisinisi, kuo pau ke foaki ʻe he Failesisita ha Tohi Fakamoʻoni ʻo e Lesisita Hingoa Pisinisi kuo fakatonutonu.

#### 15 Kaniseli ʻo e lesisita

Kapau ʻoku kaniseli ʻe he Failesisita ha Lesisita Hingoa Pisinisi, kuo pau ki he Failesisita ke ne –

(a) fanongonongo ki he Taha Lesisita ʻi ha fetuʻutaki tohi ‘i he faipau ki he kupu 12 ‘o e Lao ʻa e ʻuhinga ki hono kaniseli; pea

(b) kuo pau ke fakaha ‘e he fanongonongo ki he Taha Lesisita.

#### 16 Tangi ki he Minisita

(1) ʻE ngofua ke fakahoko ha tangi ki ha tuʻutuʻuni ke kaniseli ha lesisita ‘o ha tu’utu’uni hingoa pisinisi pea kuo pau ke fakahoko ʻi he tohi ki he Minisita ʻi loto ʻi he ʻaho ngaue ʻe 14 mei he ʻaho ʻoku ʻoatu ai ki he Taha Lesisita ʻa e fanongonongo kaniseli.

(2) Kuo pau ki he tangi ke fakaha ʻa e ngaahi makatuʻunga ʻo e tangi.

(3) Kuo pau ki he Minisita, ʻi loto ʻi he ʻaho ngaue ʻe 28 mei hono maʻu ʻo e tangi, ke fakakaukauʻi ʻa e kotoa ʻo e ngaahi fakamatala felaveʻi kuo fakahoko ʻe he Taha Lesisita pea tuʻutuʻuni ki he tangi.

(4) Kuo pau ki he Minisita ʻi loto ʻi he ʻaho ngaue ʻe 3 mei he ʻaho ʻo e tuʻutuʻuni ki he tangi, ke fakahoko ki he Taha Lesisita ʻi ha fetuʻutaki tohi ʻa ʻene tuʻutuʻuni.
