‘o e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni Fakamaau‘anga. 2 Ngofua ki he Fakamaau Lahi mo e Failesisita ke ngaue‘aki ‘a e ngaahi Mafai Ko e Ngaahi Mafai kuo foaki ki he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ‘e he Lao ki hono Fakahoko

‘o e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni Fakamaau‘anga, (‘e lau ‘i he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni´ni ko e

“Lao”) ‘e lava ‘o ngāue‘aki ia ‘e he Fakamaau ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ‘i hono Loki pe ko e Failesisita ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi. 3 Tohi kole ke lesisita ‘o ha tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga Ko ha tohi kole ‘e fai ‘i he Lao ke lesisita ‘i he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ha tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga, ‘e lava ‘o fai ia ‘e ha fa‘ahi pe ‘e taha ka ‘e malava ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ke fai ha tu‘utu‘uni ki he tokotaha kole ke ne fakaha ki ha fa‘ahi ‘oku felave‘i mo e kole ‘e fai hano alea‘i ‘o e tohi kole, pea mo e ‘aho ‘e fai ai. 4 Ngaahi Tohi fakamatala fuakava poupou

(1) Ko ha tohi kole ke lesisita kuo pau ke poupou ‘aki ia ha Tohi Fuakava —

(a) fakaha ai ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga pe ko hano tatau kuo fakamo‘oni‘i pea kapau ko e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ‘oku ‘ikai fai ‘i he lea Faka-Tonga pe lea Faka-Pilitania, ko hono liliu ki he taha ‘o e ongo lea ko ia ‘e fakapapau‘i hono mo‘oni ‘aki ha Tohi Fuakava ne fuakava‘i ‘e he tokotaha liliu lea, ‘a ia te ne fakaha ai hono hingoa, tu‘asila pea mo hono poto taau ke liliu lea, pea kuo tonu ‘ene liliu ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ‘o fakatatau ki he lelei taha ‘o hono poto;

(b) fakaha ai mo e hingoa pea mo e nofo‘anga ma‘u pe tu‘asila fakamuimui taha ‘o e tokotaha malohi mo e tokotaha mo‘ua ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga pea mo hona tu‘asila ke fai ki ai ‘a e tufa, kapau ‘oku kehe.

(c) fakaha ai ki he lelei taha e fakamatala pe tui ‘a e tokotaha fuakava pea fakaha ai ‘a e ma‘u‘anga fakamatala pe ko e ngaahi tu‘unga ke tui —

(i) ko e tokotaha kole ‘oku ‘i ai ‘ene totonu ke fakahoko e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga, pea ko e fakahoko ‘a e kotoa ‘o e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga pe ko ha konga pe, pea kapau ko ha konga pe, ko e konga fe ia;

(ii) ‘e lava hano ngaue‘aki e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ‘aki hano fakahoko ‘i he fonua ‘o e fakamaau‘anga ‘uluaki;

(iii) ‘e ‘ikai ala tuku ‘a e lesisita ‘i he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘o e Kupu 6 ‘o e Lao;

(d) fakahā e lahi ‘o e tupu, kapau ‘oku ‘i ai ha tupu, ‘a ia ‘i he lao ‘o e fonua

‘o e fakamaau‘anga ‘uluaki, ‘oku totonu ke totongi atu ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ‘o a‘u ki he taimi ‘o e lesisita.

(e) fakahā, kapau ko e pa‘anga ‘oku totongi atu ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ‘oku ‘i he pa‘anga kehe mei he pa‘anga ‘a e Pule‘anga Tonga, ‘a hono lahi ‘i he pa‘anga Tonga, ‘i he tu‘unga fetongi pa‘anga na‘e ngaue‘aki ‘i he ‘aho na‘e fakahu pe fakakakato ai ‘i he fonua

‘uluaki;

(f) kuo ‘osi fai e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni kotoa ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga fekau‘aki mo e lesisita.

(2) ‘E toe fiema‘u ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ha tohi fuakava fakalahi ke faile, kapau te ne fiema‘u ha toe fakamatala ki he ngaahi me‘a ‘oku ha ‘i ‘olunga pe ko ha toe me‘a kehe fekau‘aki mo e lesisita. 5 Lesisita‘i ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga

(1) Kapau ‘e fakafiemalie‘i ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ‘e he tokotaha kole ko e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ‘oku tonu (‘i he fakamo‘oni ‘i he fakamaau‘anga) ke lesisita, ‘e lesisita leva ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ‘i ha tohi lesisita ‘a ia ‘e tauhi ‘e he Failesita ‘i he ‘Ulu‘i ‘Ofisi Fai‘anga lesisita ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi.

(2) ‘I hano lesisita ‘o e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga, kuo pau ke ma‘u ‘e he tokotaha kole ha fakamo‘oni ‘o e lesisita mei he ‘Ulu‘i ‘Ofisi Fai‘anga lesisita pea kuo pau ke fakaha ‘i he tohi fakamo‘oni —

(a) ‘a e fakalea totonu ‘o e lesisita;

(b) ko e taimi ‘oku faka‘atā ke fai ai ha kole ke tuku ‘a e lesisita;

(c) ko e founga ‘e fai‘aki hono tufa ‘o e tohi fakamo‘oni ki he tokotaha mo‘ua ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga.

(3) Ki mu‘a ke fakahoko ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga kuo pau ke faile ‘e he tokotaha kole ha tohi fuakava ‘o fakaha ai —

(а) kuo ‘osi tufa ‘a e tohi fakamo‘oni ki he tokotaha mo‘ua ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga, ‘i he founga na‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he fakamaau‘anga;

(b) ‘oku ‘ikai ha‘ane ‘ilo ki ha fa‘ahinga kole ke tuku e lesisita;

(c) kuo ‘osi ‘a e taimi na‘e faka‘ata ai ke fai ha kole ke tuku e lesisita.

(4) Hili hano faile ha tohi fuakava ne fiema‘u ‘e he tu‘utu‘unisi‘i (3) ‘e tohi‘i leva

‘e he Failesisita ‘a e fo‘i lea “fakahoko” ‘i he tohi lesisita ‘i he tafa‘aki e me‘a ne lesisita, taa‘i ai e Sitapa Fakamaau‘anga pea ke ne fakamo‘oni hingoa mo faka‘aho e lesisita.

(5) ‘E ‘ikai fakalao hano fakahoko e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga kae ‘oua leva kuo kakato hono fai e ngaue ‘oku ha ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni si‘i (4). 6 Tuku ‘o ha tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga

(1) Ko ha tohi kole ke tuku hano lesisita ‘o ha tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga kuo pau ke fai‘aki ia ‘a e tikite pea poupou‘i ‘aki ha tohi fuakava.

(2) ‘E malava ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga, ‘i ha‘ane‘ilohafa‘ahinga tohi kolepehe, kene tu‘utu‘uni ke fai ha hopo ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e tokotaha kole ke fai e lesisita mo e tokotaha kole ke tuku, ‘i ha fa‘ahinga me‘a ‘oku na fakakikihi‘i, pea ko e hopo ko ia ke fakamaau‘i ‘e he ‘Eiki Fakamaau Lahi pe ko e Fakamaau ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi.2

(3) ‘E malava ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ke tu‘utu‘uni ke fakamatala ngutu pe tohi fuakava ‘a e fakamatala fakamo‘oni.

(4) ‘E fakapapau‘i pe fakafoki e lesisita ‘e he fakamaau‘anga hili hano fai e hopo.

(5) Ko ha tangi koe‘uhi ko etu‘utu‘uni‘a eFakamaau‘anga, ‘efai ia ki he Fakataha Tokoni, ‘i hano fakangofua.

(6) Ko e kole ngofua ‘e fai ia ‘i he hopo pe ko e vaha‘a taimi ‘aho ‘e 14 mei he hopo. ‘E lava ‘e ha fa‘ahi loto mamahi, kapau ‘oku ‘ikai tali ‘a e kole ngofua ‘e he Fakamaau, ke fai ha tohi kole ngofua ki he Fakataha Tokoni ‘i he‘enau to‘u hopo hono hoko.

‘E ngaue‘aki e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ki mu‘a ‘o fakatatau ki he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘oku ha

‘i he Tu‘utu‘uni ‘i he Fakataha Tokoni ‘o fakahoko atu e Lao ki he fonua ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga ‘Uluaki. 8 Tohi kole ‘i he kupu 11 ‘o e Lao

(1) Ko ha tohi kole ‘i he Kupu 11 ‘o e Lao kuo pau ke fai ‘e ha fa‘ahi ‘e taha ki he Failesisita ‘i ha tohi fuakava.

Ko e Fuakava kuo pau ke —

(a) Fakaha ai e ngaahi fakaikiiki ‘o e hopo ne ma‘u ai e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga;

(b) Fakapipiki mo ha tatau ‘o e tikite pe ko e mela ne ne kamata e ngaahi ngauefakamaau‘anga,koefakamo‘onihonotufa,pena‘e‘iaiefaka‘iloa mo e ngaahi tatau ‘o e ngaahi tali, kapau ‘oku ‘i ai ha tali;

(c) Fakaha pe na‘e loto pe ta‘eloto ‘a e faka‘iloa ki he Mafai, pea kapau na‘e ta‘eloto, ko e ha e ngaahi tu‘unga ‘o ‘ene ta‘eloto;

(d) Fakahahe‘ikailavata‘ofihonofakahoko‘oetu‘utu‘unifakamaau‘anga;

(e) Fakaha kuo ‘osi e taimi ke fai ai e tangi pe ko e taimi ‘e ‘osi ai pea fakaha pe kuo fakahu ha tohi fakaha tangi; pea

(f) Fakaha ‘a e tu‘unga totongi ‘o e tupu ‘a ia ‘oku ha ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga.

(2) Ko e tatau kuo fakamo‘oni ‘o e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ‘e hoko ia ko e tatau

‘a e ‘Ofisi, sila‘i ‘aki e sila ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ‘o Tonga, fakamo‘oni‘i

‘aki ha tohi fakamo‘oni ne fakamo‘oni hingoa ai e Failesisita ‘o fakamo‘oni‘i ko e tatau mo‘oni ia ‘o ha tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga ne ma‘u mei he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ‘o Tonga pea ‘oku fakahu atu ia ki tu‘a ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 11 ‘o e Lao.

(3) ‘E ‘oatu fakataha mo e tatau kuo fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e tohi fakamo‘oni ‘a ia ‘oku ha ai ‘a e ngaahi fakamatala ko eni —

(а) Founga hono tufa ki he Faka‘iloa ‘a e tikite pe ko e founga ngaue fakamaau‘anga na‘a ne kamata, pe na‘e ‘i ai ‘a e tokotaha faka‘iloa;

(b) Ko e ha ha ngaahi ‘uhinga ta‘eloto, kapau na‘e ‘i ai, ne fai ki he mafai;

(c) Ko e ha ha ngaahi tali ne tufa, kapau na‘e ‘i ai;

(d) Pe kuo ngata e taimi ke fai ai e tangi pe ko e ‘aho ‘e ngata ai;

(e) Pe kuo fakahu ha tohi fakaha tangi koe‘uhi ko e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga;

(f) Ngaahi fakamatala kehe ‘e ngali fiema‘u ke ‘ave ki he fakamaau‘anga ‘i he fonua muli ‘oku fiema‘u ke fakahoko ai e tu‘utu‘uni fakamaau‘anga;
