# ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Fai ‘o e Mali

NGAAHI TU'UTU'UNI KI HE FAI 'O E MALI1 Na‘e fa‘u ‘i he mafai ‘o e Kupu 5 ‘o e Lao ki he Lesisita 'o e Fa‘ele, Pekia mo e Mali- Kamata'anga [31 ‘o ‘Okatopa, 1980]

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ui ‘a e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni´ni ko e ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ki he Fai ‘o e Mali.

#### 2 Ko e ngaahi kole ki ha tohi fakangofua ke mali

(1) Kuo pau ki he Tohi fuakava ‘oku tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Lao ki he Lesisita ‘o e Fa‘ele, Pekia mo e Mali ‘i hono fakatonutonu (hili eni ‘e ui “ko e Lao”) ke fakahaa‘i ai ‘a e ngaahi tohi ko eni —

(a) ko e tohi fā‘ele ‘o e ongo me‘a tohi kole fakangofua. Kapau leva he

‘ikai lava ‘e ha taha kole fakangofua ke ma‘u hono (tangata pe fefine) tohi fā‘ele pea, kae ‘oua ‘i he fanongo ‘a e Failesisita Tokoni ‘oku fiemālie ‘o ‘ikai ha toe tala‘a ki he tohi kole fakangofua ‘aki ‘a e ‘i ai tonu ‘a e taha kole fakangofua ‘oku (tangata pe fefine) laka hake hono ta‘u motu‘a he 18. Kuo pau te ne (tangata pe fefine) fai ‘ene fuakava ‘e ha taha ‘a ia ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘a e ‘ilo faka‘iateia ki he taimi na‘e fa‘ele‘i ai ‘a e taha kole fakangofua pea kuo pau ki he taha ko ia ke ne fakahaa‘i ‘a e founga ‘o ‘ene ma‘u ‘a e ‘ilo ko ia;

(b) ko e taha kole fakangofua ‘a ia ‘oku laka hake he ta‘u ‘e 15 ka ‘oku ‘i lalo he ta‘u ‘e 18 hono ta‘u motu‘a kuo pau ke ne fakahaa‘i ‘a e tohi

‘oku loto ki ai ‘ene ongo mātu‘a pe tauhi. Kapau ko ‘ene ongo matu‘a

‘oku na ‘osi vete pe māvae kuo pau ke fakahaa‘i ‘a e loto ‘o e tokotaha

‘o e mātu‘a ‘oku ne tauhi ia; pea ka ‘oku ‘ikai ha tu‘utu‘uni ki hono tauhi ‘o e taha ko ia pea ‘oku ‘ikai felotoi ‘a e ongo mātu‘a kuo pau leva ke fakapapau‘i ‘e he Tokoni Failesisita ko e fe ‘a e taha he ongo mātu‘a ke ne ma‘u ‘a e totonu ke fakahā hono loto pe ta‘e loto ki he me‘a ko ia;

(c) ‘i he taimi ‘oku kole ai ke fai ‘a e mali ‘e ha Faifekau ‘o e Lotu kehe mei he Faifekau ‘a ia ‘oku hā hono hingoa ‘i he ngaahi tohi ‘oku tauhi

‘i he fale, ko e loto fakahā ‘i he tohi ‘o e Faifekau ko ia ‘a ia ‘oku hā hono hingoa ‘i he tohi.

(2) Kuo pau ki he Tokoni Failesisita ‘oku fai ki ai ‘a e kole fakangofua ke ne —

(a) fakahā ki he ongo kole fakangofua ko e hia mamafa ‘aupito ke fai ha fakamatala ta‘e totonu ke ma‘u ha Tohi Fakangofua Mali;

(b) fiemālie ia ko e taha ‘o e ongo kole fakangofua mali kuo ne nofo ‘i he Vahefonua Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i ‘i he mahina ‘e 6 kimu‘a. Ko e ngaahi mama‘o ‘i hono fakakātoa ‘oku si‘i ange he mahina ‘e 1 ‘e ngofua ke

‘oua ‘e tali;

(c) ‘eke takitaha kiate kinaua ha taha pe kuo (tangata pe fefine) ne mali ki mu‘a;

(d) ‘eke takitaha kiate kinaua pe ‘oku na kainga;

(e) kapau ko e tali ki he fehu‘i (c) pe (d), ‘oku ‘io kuo pau ki he Tokoni Failesisita ke ne toe fai ‘a e ngaahi faka‘eke‘eke ‘a ia ‘oku ne pehē ‘oku taau ke ne fiemālie ai ko e ongo me’a kole fakangofua ke mali ‘oku ‘atā ke na mali. Kapau ‘oku ‘ikai ke ne fiemālie kuo pau ke ne fakafisi ke

‘oatu ha Tohi Fakangofua ke mali pea kuo pau ke ne fakahā ki he ongo me‘a kole fakangofua ke mali ‘oku ngofua pē kapau ‘oku na faka‘amu pehē ke na tangi ki he Failesisita Seniale;

(f) kapau ko e taha ‘o e ongo me‘a kole fakangofua ke mali kuo ne ‘osi mali kimu‘a pea ko e mali ko ia kuo vete ‘o ‘ikai ‘i he mafai ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ‘o Tonga, kuo pau ki he Failesisita Tokoni ke ne fakahā ‘a e me‘a ko ia ki he Failesisita Seniale kimu‘a ‘i hano ‘oatu ha tohi fakangofua ke mali;

(g) ‘i ha fiemālie ‘a e Lesisita Tokoni ko e ongo me‘a kole fakangofua ke mali ‘oku ‘atā ke na mali, kuo pau ke ne fai ‘a e fuakava kiate kinaua pea kole kiate kinaua takitaha ke na pehē “ ‘oku mo‘oni ‘a e me‘a kotoa pe ‘i he‘eku fuakava ni.” Kuo pau leva ke ne kole kiate kinaua takitaha

‘a e ongo me‘a kole fakangofua ke mali ke na fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘i he fuakava pea kuo pau ke ne fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a ‘ena fakamo‘oni;

(h) kuo pau ki he tohi fuakava ke ‘i he foomu kuo tu‘utu‘uni ‘i he Tepile ki he Lao ki he Lesisita ‘o e Fa‘ele, Pekia mo e Mali;

(i) kuo pau ki he Tokoni Failesisita ke ne toki ‘eke ki he ongo me‘a kole fakangofua mali ‘a e hingoa ‘o e Faifekau ‘o e Lotu ‘a ia ‘oku na loto ke ne fai ‘ena mali, pea kuo pau ‘i hono fakapapau‘i ko e hingoa ko ia

‘oku hā he Tohi Lesisita ‘o e kau Faifekau pea kuo pau ke fakatatau mo e tu‘utu‘uni 2(1)(c), ke ne ‘oatu ha tohi fakangofua ki he Faifekau ko ia ke ne fai ‘ena mali ‘i ha fale ‘a ia ‘oku tauhi ai ha tohi ‘o e ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni mali ‘i loto ‘i ha vaha‘a taimi ‘oku ‘ikai lahi hake he ‘aho

‘e 28 mei he ‘aho ‘o hono ‘oatu ‘o e tohi fakangofua;

(j) kuo pau ki he Tokoni Failesisita ke ne tauhi ha tatau ‘o e tohi fakangofua ‘i he‘ene tauhi‘anga lesisita;

(k) kuo pau ki ha Tokoni Failesisita ke ‘oua na‘a ne ‘oatu ha tohi fakangofua ki ha Faifekau ‘o e Lotu ke ne fai ha mali ‘i ha siasi ‘a ia

‘oku ‘ikai ‘i he feitu‘u Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i.

(3) (а) Kapau ‘oku loto ‘e ha taha ke mali ‘o ‘ikai ‘i he feitu‘u ‘o e Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i ‘a ia kuo ne nofo ai ‘i he mahina ‘e 6 ‘oku fiema‘u ke fakapapau‘i ‘a e makatu‘unga nofo ai hangē ko ia ‘oku tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Lao pe ‘i ha fale kehe mei he fale ‘oku tauhi ai ‘a e tohi fakamo‘oni mali kuo pau ke ne kole ki he Failesisita Seniale ki ha laiseni makehe.

(b) Ko e kole fakangofua ko ia kuo pau ke ‘ai fakataha mo e —

(i) tohi fuakava fakalao;

(ii) ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni ‘oku fiema‘u ‘e he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ko eni;

(iii) ha fakamatala ‘o e ngaahi ‘uhinga ‘o e tohi kole;

(iv) ha fakamo‘oni ‘o e ‘ilo ‘o e fale mo e Faifekau ‘o e Lotu ‘a ia ‘e fai ai mo ‘a ia te ne fai ‘a e mali.

(c) ‘E ngofua ki he Failesisita Seniale ke ne fakapipiki ‘a e ngaahi me‘a ko ia ki he tohi Fakangofua Makehe ko ia ‘a e me‘a ko ia ‘oku ne pehē

‘oku taau.

#### 3 Ko hono ‘oatu ‘o e ngaahi tohi ‘o e ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni mali

(1) Ko e ngaahi tohi ‘oku ‘i ai ‘a e ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni mali ‘e ‘oatu ke tauhi

‘i he ngaahi fale fe‘unga ‘e he Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i ‘a ia ‘oku tu‘u ai ‘a e fale.

(2) Kapau ‘e fakafisi ‘e ha Tokoni Failesisita ke ‘oatu ha tohi ki ha fale ko e siasi

‘oku nau ‘i he fale ‘e ngofua ke tangi ki he Failesisita Seniale.

(3) Kuo pau ki he Faifekau ‘o e Lotu ‘oku pule ki he fale ke ne tokanga‘i ‘a hono tauhi ke malu ‘a e tohi. Ko hono hingoa kuo pau ke fakahaa‘i ‘i he takafi ‘o ia.

(4) Kuo pau ki he kau Tokoni Failesisita ke nau ‘a‘ahi ki he fale kotoa pē ‘oku tauhi ai ha tohi ‘o ‘oua ‘e si‘i hifo he tu‘o taha he ta‘u mo fakapapau‘i ‘oku kei fe‘unga pē ‘a e potu ko ia ‘a ia ‘oku ‘ai ke fai ai ‘a e ngaahi mali pea ko e tohi ‘o e ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni mali ‘oku malu mo ‘i ha tu‘unga lelei.

#### 4 Ngaahi fatongia ‘o e kau faifekau ‘o e lotu

(1) ‘I Tongatapu — kuo pau ke fakapapau‘i ‘e he faifekau takitaha ‘o e lotu hili ‘ene fakahoko ha katoanga mali —

(a) ‘oku fakafonu ‘o tonu ha tatau ‘e 3 ‘o e tohi fakamo‘oni mali ‘i he tohi fakamo‘oni mali kuo ‘oatu;

(b) ‘oku tauhi ‘a e ‘uluaki tatau ‘i he tohi;

(c) ‘oku ma‘u ‘e he Failesisita ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi meiate ia ‘i loto ‘i he vaha‘a taimi ko e uike ‘e 3 ‘a e tatau hono ua; pea

(d) ‘oku ‘ave ‘a e tatau hono tolu ‘e ia ki he fefine mali.

(2) ‘I he kotoa ‘o e Ngaahi Fai‘anga Lesisita si‘i — kuo pau ke fakapapau‘i ‘e he faifekau takitaha ‘o e lotu hili ‘ene fakahoko ha katoanga mali —

(a) ‘oku fakafonu ‘o tonu ha tatau ‘e fa ‘o e tohi fakamo‘oni mali ‘i he tohi fakamo‘oni mali kuo ‘oatu;

(b) ‘oku tauhi ‘a e ‘uluaki tatau ‘i he tohi;

(c) ‘oku ma‘u ‘e he Failesisita si‘i ‘a ia te ne ‘oatu ‘a e laiseni ‘a e ongo tatau ua mo e tolu ‘i loto ‘i he vaha‘a taimi ko e uike ‘e 3; pea

(d) ‘oku ‘ave ‘a e tatau hono fa ‘e ia ki he fefine mali.

(3) ‘I Tongatapu mo e ngaahi fai‘anga lesisita si‘i kehe —

Kuo pau ke fakapapau‘i ‘e he faifekau takitaha ‘o e lotu ‘oku ne ‘oatu ha tohi talitotongi koe‘uhi ko e tohi fakamo‘oni mali kotoa pe kuo ma‘u meiate ia ‘o fakatatau ki he tu‘utu‘uni ni.

#### 5 Ko hono ‘oatu ‘o e ngaahi foomu ‘e he kau tokoni failesisita

(1) ‘I he ‘aho faka‘osi ‘o e mahina fakatohimahina kotoa pē ko e kau Tokoni Failesisita kotoa pē tuku kehe ‘a e Failesisita ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi kuo pau ke nau ‘oatu ki he Failesisita Seniale ‘a e tatau ‘e taha ‘o e ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni mali kotoa na‘e ma‘u mei he kau Faifekau ‘o e Lotu fakataha mo e ngaahi tohi fakangofua fekau‘aki mo ia.

(2) Kuo pau ki he kau Tokoni Failesisita kotoa pē ke nau tauhi ‘a e tatau ‘e taha

‘o e tohi fakamo‘oni mali kotoa pe na‘e fakafoki kiate kinautolu ‘e ha Faifekau.

(3) Kuo pau ki he kau Tokoni Failesisita tu‘o taha he mahina ‘e 3 kotoa pe ke nau vakai‘i ‘enau tafa‘aki tohi fakangofua ke fakapapau‘i pe na‘e ‘i ai ha tohi fakangofua na‘e ‘oatu ‘oku ‘ikai hā mai kuo ngāue‘aki. Kapau te ne ma‘u ha sipinga ‘o e me‘a ni, kuo pau ke ne faka‘eke‘eke ki he Faifekau ‘a ia na‘e

‘oatu ki ai pe na‘e fai mo‘oni ‘a e mali ko ia.

#### 6 Ko hono ‘oatu ‘o e ngaahi tohi

(1) ‘I he ‘osi hono ngāue‘aki kotoa ‘a e ngaahi foomu ‘i ha tohi ko e Faifekau ‘o e Lotu ‘oku pule ki he fale ‘a ia na‘e ‘oatu ki ai ‘a e tohi kuo pau ke ne fakafoki ia ki he Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i ‘a ia na‘e ‘oatu mei ai pea ma‘u ha tohi fakamo‘oni‘i ‘o ia.

(2) Ha tohi fetongi ke tauhi ‘e he Faifekau tatau ‘i he fale tatau kuo pau ke ‘oatu

‘i hono fiema‘u.

(3) ‘I he‘ene ngata ‘a e pule ‘a ha taha ‘oku pule ki ha fale ‘a ia na‘e ‘oatu ki ai ha ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni mali, ko e Siasi ‘oku ‘a‘ana ‘a e fale kuo pau ke ne fakahā ki he Tokoni Failesisita ‘o e vahefonua ‘a ia ‘oku tu‘u ai ‘a e fale. Kuo pau ‘i he taimi tatau ke fakahā ki he Tokoni Failesisita ‘a e hingoa ‘o e taha ko ia, kapau ‘oku ai ha taha kuo ne pule ki he fale.

(4) Kapau ‘oku ‘ikai ha taha ‘e fakanofo ke ne pule ‘i ha fale ‘a ia na‘e ‘oatu ki ai ha tohi fakamo‘oni mali ko e Siasi ‘oku ne ma‘u ‘a e fale kuo pau ke ne fakafoki ‘a e tohi ki he Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i ‘a ia na‘e ‘oatu mei ai.

(5) ‘I hono ma‘u ‘e ha Tokoni Failesisita ha tohi mei ha Siasi pe Faifekau ‘i he ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni Si‘i (1) — (4) ‘o e Tu‘utu‘uni ni kuo pau ke ne fai ha me‘a ke vakai‘i ke fakapapau‘i kuo ne ma‘u ha tohi fakamo‘oni ‘oku tatau mo e tohi fakamo‘oni kakato kotoa pē ‘i he tohi. Kapau ‘oku ‘i ai ha fehalaaki, kuo pau ke ne fakatotolo‘i ‘a e me‘a ko ia pea fakaha ki he Failesisita Seniale.

#### 7 Ngaahi liliu ‘o ha tohi lesisita mali pe ‘o ha tohi mali2

(a) Ko e ngaahi liliu ‘o ha tohi lesisita ‘o e mali pea/pe ‘o ha tohi mali ‘e toki fakahoko ia ‘i ha tohi fakangofua mei he Failesisita Seniale.

(b) Ko e ngaahi tohi kole kotoa pe ‘e fakahoko ia ‘o fakatatau mo e founga ‘oku ha ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni 8(2) mo e 8(3) ‘o e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Failesisita Seniale ‘o e Fa‘ele, Pekia mo e Mali. Ko e ngaahi liliu ‘e fakahoko ia ‘o fakatatau mo e tu‘utu‘uni 8(4) mo e 8(6)(b) ‘o e Tu‘utu‘uni ko ia.
