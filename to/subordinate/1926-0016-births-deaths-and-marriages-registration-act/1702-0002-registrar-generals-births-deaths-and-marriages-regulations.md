## KONGA I - FOKOTU‘UTU‘U

#### 2 Ngaahi fai‘anga lesisita si‘i

(1) Ko e Tokoni Failesisita kotoa pe ‘o hangē ko ia ‘oku fakamatala‘i ‘i he kupu 2

‘o e Lao ki he Lesisita ‘o e Fa‘ele, Pekia mo e Mali kuo pau ke ne fokotu‘u ha taha ‘o ‘ene kau kalake ko e kalake lesisita.

(2) ‘Oku pau ke ne fakahā ‘a e hingoa ‘o e Kalake ko ia ki he Failesisita Seniale.

(3) Kapau ‘oku ‘i ai ha fa‘ahinga ‘uhinga ‘oku ‘ikai ‘ata‘atā ai ‘a e kalake ko ia pea ke fakanofo ‘e he Tokoni Failesisita ha kalake ‘e taha ko hono fetongi.

Kapau ‘oku ngalingali ‘e laka hake ‘a e fetongi ko eni ‘i he māhina ‘e taha kuo pau ke fakahā ki he Failesisita Seniale.

(4) ‘I he ‘Ulu‘i Fai‘anga Lesisita, ‘a ia ko e Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i ia ma‘a Tongatapu, ‘e tokoni ai ki he Tokoni Failesisita ‘a e ‘Ofisa ki he Fā‘ele, Mali mo e Pekia pea mo ha kau kalake ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Failesisita Seniale.

(5) ‘Oku pau ko e fatongia ‘o e ‘Ofisa ki he Fā‘ele, Mali mo e Pekia ke ‘a‘ahi ki he Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i kotoa pē meimei tu‘o taha ‘i he ta‘u kotoa pē pea mo fakamatala‘i ‘ene ‘a‘ahi ki he Failesisita Seniale.

#### 3 Kau ‘ofisa fakavahefonua mo e kau ‘ofisa kolo

(1) ‘E fakamatala ‘e he kau ‘Ofisa Kolo ki honau kau ‘Ofisa faka-vahefonua ‘i he

‘uluaki ‘aho ‘o e māhina takitaha ‘a e fā‘ele mo e pekia kotoa pē ne nau faka- tokanga‘i lolotonga ‘a e māhina kuo hili. ‘E fai ‘a e fakamatala ‘i he Foomu V.S.3 hangē ko ia ‘oku hā ‘i he Tepile 3 ‘o e Lao ki he lesisita ‘o e Fa‘ele, Pekia mo e Mali.

Kapau na‘e ‘ikai hoko ha fā‘ele pe ha pekia pea ke ‘oatu pē ‘a e Foomu V.S.3 mo tohi‘i atu ai ‘a e “‘ikai”.

(2) (a) ‘E ‘ikai toe tōmui ange ‘i he ‘aho 15 ‘o e mahina takitaha hono ‘oatu mei he Kau ‘Ofisa Fakavahefonua ki he Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i ‘oku tu‘u

‘i hono Vahe ‘a e ngaahi Foomu V.S.3 fekau‘aki mo e māhina kuo hili.

(b) Kapau he ‘ikai ma‘u ha Foomu V.S.3 mei ha kolo, ‘e fai mai ‘e he

‘Ofisa Fakavahefonua ha‘ane fakamatala mo ne fakahā pe ko e hā ‘a e ngaahi me‘a kuo nau fai ke ma‘u ai ‘a e ngaahi fakamatala ‘oku fiema‘u.

(c) Kapau ‘e ‘ilo ‘e ha ‘Ofisa Fakavahefonua ki ha fā‘ele pe pekia ‘a ia

‘oku te‘eki fakahū ‘i ha Foomu V.S.3, ‘e teuteu leva ‘e ia ha Foomu V.S.3 ‘o fakahaa‘i ai ‘a e kolo ko ia na‘e hoko ai ‘a e fā‘ele pe pekia mo tohi‘i mahino mai ai “Ko e Fakamatala ‘a e ‘Ofisa Fakavahefonua”, pea

‘ave fakataha ia mo e ngaahi Foomu V.S.3 ka hoko mai, ki he Tokoni Failesisita.

(3) ‘E ‘a‘ahi ‘a e ‘Ofisa ki he Fā‘ele, Mali mo e Pekia ki he kau ‘Ofisa Fakavahefonua mo e kau ‘Ofisa Kolo meimei ki he tu‘o taha ‘i he ta‘u pea ke ne fakamatala ‘ene ‘a‘ahi ki he Failesisita Seniale.

(4) ‘Oku pau ke toutou fetu‘utaki ‘a e ‘Ofisa ki he Fa‘ele, Mali mo e Pekia ki he Potungaue Mo‘ui ke fakapapau‘i ko e fika ko ia ‘oku ma‘u mei ai (Potungaue Mo‘ui) ko ia ‘oku ngaue‘aki ki hono sivi ‘o e fakamatala ‘oku ma‘u mei he Ngaahi Fai‘anga Lesisita Si‘i.

## KONGA II - FOUNGA LOLOTONGA KI HE LESISITA ‘O E

NGAAHI FA‘ELE MO E PEKIA

#### 4 Ngaahi houa ngaue

(1) ‘Oku pau ke ava ‘a e ngaahi fai‘anga lesisita kotoa pē mei he 8:30 pongipongi ki he 4:00 efiafi Monite ki he Falaite tuku kehe ‘i he ngaahi ‘aho mālōlō fakapule‘anga.

(2) ‘Oku pau ke ‘atā ‘a e kalake na‘e vahe‘i ke ne fai ‘a e lesisita, ki hono lesisita

‘o e ngaahi fā‘ele mo e pekia kotoa pe kuo fakahā mai ‘i he ‘aho, he taimi

‘oku tonu ke fai ai kuo pau ke fai ‘a e feinga kotoa ke fakapapau‘i ke ‘oua na‘a foki atu ha taha na‘e ha‘u ke fai ha‘ane lesisita ne te‘eki fai ki ai ha tokanga.

#### 5 Angafai ‘o e lesisita

(1) ‘I ha ha‘u ha taha ‘o e kakai ki he Fai‘anga Lēsisita Si‘i ko e fakahā ‘o ha fā‘ele pe pekia ‘e fiema‘u ‘a e fefine pe tangata ko ia ke ne fakafonu, ‘e fai tokonia ia ‘e he kalake, mo fakamo‘oni hingoa ki he foomu lēsisita ‘i he Foomu V.S.10 ki he fa‘ele pe V.S.11 ki he pekia ‘o hangē ko e Tepile ki he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ni.

(2) Hili hono fakafonu ‘o e foomu ‘e he memipa ‘o e kakai pea kimu‘a ke fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai ‘e lau māmālie atu ‘e he kalake ‘a e foomu ki he tokotaha ko ia ‘oku ha‘u ‘o lipooti ‘a e me‘a na‘e hoko, mo ‘eke pe ‘oku tonu.

‘Oku pau ke fai ‘e he kalake ‘a e tokanga kotoa pē ‘oku lava fai ke fakapapau‘i ‘oku tonu ‘a e foomu.

(3) ‘E hanga leva ‘e he kalake ‘o teuteu ha tatau ‘o e foomu, fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai, sila‘i ‘aki ‘a e sila ‘a e Potungāue pea toki ‘oange ki he taha ko ia ‘o e kakai.

(4) ‘E fakahū ‘a e ‘uluaki foomu (original) ki he faile.

(5) ‘E hiki ‘a e ‘uluaki foomu (original) ki he Tohi Lēsisita pea ke fakamo‘oni hingoa ki ai ‘a e tokotaha na‘e ha‘u ke fai ‘a e lēsisita.

(6) ‘E hanga ‘e he kalake ko ia na‘a ne hiki ‘a e foomu ki he Lēsisita ‘o tohi‘i ai

‘a e ngaahi ‘uluaki mata‘itohi ‘o hono hingoa.

(7) ‘I ha ha‘u ‘a ha taha ‘o fakahā mai ha pekia, ‘oku pau ke ‘eke kiate ia pe na‘e

‘i ai ha Toketā na‘e sio ki he pekia pea kapau na‘e ‘i ai, ko hai hono hingoa mo hono tu‘unga fakangāue, ko e tali ki he ngaahi fehu‘i ko ‘eni ‘e fakahū ia ki he foomu.

(8) ‘I ha tu‘utu‘uni ke fai ha fakamaau ‘eke‘eke ki he tupunga ‘o e pekia pe ko ha fakahā ‘e he Minisitā Polisi ki he Tokoni Failesisita ‘oku ‘i ai ha hopo ‘oku te‘eki fai ‘a ia ‘oku tukuaki‘i ai ha taha ki he fakatupu ‘o e pekia ‘oku pau ke

‘oua ‘e lēsisita ‘a e pekia kae ‘oua leva kuo lava ‘a e Fakamaau ‘eke‘eke pe hopo. ‘E fakafonu pe ‘a e foomu mo tauhi kae ‘oua leva ke toki lava ‘o fai hano lesisita.

(9) ‘I hano lesisita ‘o ha pekia ‘a ha taha pea toki mahino mai ki he Tokoni Failesisita na‘e ‘ikai ko ha tangata Tonga ia, pea ‘e teuteu leva ha toe tatau ‘e taha ‘o e tohi fakamo‘oni pekia ‘o ‘ave ki he ‘Ulu‘i Fai‘anga Lēsisita fakataha mo ha tohi ‘o fakahaa‘i mai ai ‘a e pule‘anga ‘oku pehē na‘e kau ki ai ‘a e pekia ‘i he taimi na‘e mālōlō ai ‘a e tangata pe fefine ko ia.

#### 6 Ko ha fefine mali ‘oku ne lesisita ha tamasi‘i ta‘e mali

(1) He ‘ikai lava ‘e ha fefine mali ke lēsisita ta‘e mali ha‘ane tamasi‘i kae ‘oua leva kuo fai ki ai ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi. Ko e kole ‘o ha tu‘utu‘uni pehe ‘e fai ia ‘i ha tohi, poupou’i ‘aki ha ngaahi fakamatala Fuakava ‘a kinautolu ‘oku ‘atā mei he mali ‘o hangē ko e fiema‘u ‘a e kupu 44 ‘o e Lao ki he Fakamatala Fakamo‘oni.2

(2) Tuku kehe ‘o ka pehē ‘e he Fakamaau ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi he ‘ikai te ne fiema‘u ia ‘e ia, kuo pau ke tufa ‘a e tatau ‘o e tohi kole ni ki he husepāniti ‘o e tokotaha kole.

(3) ‘E ngofua ki he Fakamaau ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ‘oku ne fai ‘a e me‘a ni ke kole ki he ‘Ofisi ‘o e Fakahinohino Lao ke nau fakafofonga‘i ‘a e ngaahi me‘a felāve‘i pea mo e ki‘i tamasi‘i, pea mo tu‘utu‘uni ki he husepāniti, uaifi mo ha toe kau fakamo‘oni ke nau ‘ōmai ke fakafehu‘i.

#### 7 Ko hono lesisita ‘o e ngaahi fa‘ele mo e pekia ‘i muli

Ka ‘i ai ha taha ‘oku ne loto ke lesisita ha fā‘ele pe pekia ‘o ha tangata Tonga na‘e hoko ‘i tu‘a ‘i he Pule‘anga kuo pau ke ne fai ‘a e ngaahi me‘a ko eni ‘o pehē —

(1) Ke fakahā pe ‘omai ki he ‘Ulu‘i Fai‘anga Lesisita ‘a e tohi ta‘u pe tohi pekia mei he fonua ko ia na‘e hoko ai ‘a e fā‘ele pe pekia.

(2) Fakahū mai ha fuakava ‘o fakamatala‘i mai —

(a) hono kainga pe fekau‘aki mo e tokotaha ‘oku ‘ai ke lesisita;

(b) tu‘unga ‘oku ne pehē ko e tangata Tonga ‘a e tokotaha ko ia;

(c) ‘oku ne tui ‘oku totonu mo mo‘oni ‘a e ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni na‘e

‘omai.

(3) ‘I hono ‘omai ‘o e ngaahi tohi fakamo‘oni ko eni ‘oku pau ke fakangofua ‘e he Tokoni Failesisita ke hiki ‘a e fa‘ele pe pekia ‘i ha tohi lēsisita makehe, tuku kehe kapau ‘oku ne tala‘a ki he mo‘oni ‘o e fakamatala Fuakava.

#### 8 Ngaahi liliu ‘i he ngaahi lesisita fa‘ele

(1) Ko e ngaahi liliu ‘o ha tohi lēsisita fa‘ele ‘e toki fai pe ia ‘i ha tohi fakangofua mei he Failesisita Seniale.

(2) (a) Ka ‘i ai ha taha ‘oku ne loto ke fai ha liliu ‘i ha lēsisita fa‘ele tuku kehe

‘a e liliu ‘o e hingoa fakafāamili ‘o e tokotaha ko ia ‘oku lēsisita kuo pau ke ne fakahū mai ha fakamatala fuakava ‘o fakahā mai ai ‘a e tohi ta‘u ‘oku ne fiema‘u ke liliu.

(b) ‘Oku pau ke hā mai ‘i he fuakava ‘a e hingoa ko ia ‘oku fokotu‘u mai ke fetongi‘aki ‘a e hingoa na‘e fakahā mai ke liliu.

(c) ‘Oku pau ke fakahā mai ‘i he fuakava ‘a e ngaahi ‘uhinga ‘oku ‘iloa ai na‘e hala hono ‘uluaki lēsisita.

(3) ‘Oku pau ke feinga‘i ‘e he tokotaha kole ha toe ngaahi fakamatala kehe ‘e fiema‘u ‘e he Failesisita Seniale ke tanaki atu ki ai.

(4) Kapau ‘oku pehē ‘e he Failesisita Seniale ‘oku ma‘a e lelei ‘a e tokotaha kole mo e kakai foki pea ke ne tu‘utu‘uni ke lēsisita fo‘ou ‘a ia ‘oku pau ke fai ‘i he tohi lēsisita lolotonga ka ‘e fekau‘aki pe mo e lesisita motu‘a.

(5) Kapau ‘e fiema‘u ke fakahoko ha liliu koe‘uhi ko ha fehalaaki fakakalake ‘i he lesisita, ‘e ngofua ke fakahoko ‘a e liliu ‘aki hono tohi‘i ‘a e me‘a ‘oku tonu ‘i he funga ‘o e ‘uluaki tohi kuo fakahu, ‘aki ha vaitohi lanu kulokula.

Kuo pau ke ‘ikai maumau‘i ‘a e fofonga ‘o e ‘uluaki tohi ‘i ha fa‘ahinga founga pe. Kuo pau ke fakahoko ha femahino‘aki mo e Failesisita pe Tokoni Failesisita kimu‘a pea fakahoko ha fakatonutonu‘aki ha vaitohi lanu kulokula.3

(6) Kuo pau ki ha ngaahi liliu ‘o e ngaahi hingoa ‘i ha ngaahi ‘uhinga keheange mei he ohi, liliu mali pe fehalaaki fakakalake, ke fakahoko fakatatau ki he Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni (Liliu ‘o e Hingoa) ‘a e Failesisita Seniale 2011.4

#### 12 Ngaahi fakamatala ‘aki ‘a e peni kulokula

He ‘ikai hiki ‘a e ngaahi fakamatala na‘e fai ‘aki ‘a e peni kulokula ‘o hangē ko e tu‘utu‘uni 11 ki he ngaahi tatau ‘o e ngaahi tohi ta‘u.6

#### 13 Hiki ‘o ha lesisita fa‘ele7

(a) Ko e ngaahi tohi kole kotoa pe ki hano hiki ‘o ha lesisita fa‘ele mei he Lesisita

‘o e Kakai Muli ‘ikai ko ha Tonga (Non-Tongan Register) ki he Lesisita Tonga (Tongan Register) kuopau ke fai ia ki he Failesisita Seniale.

(b) Ko ha tohi kole ki hano lesisita ha fa‘ele na‘e hoko ‘i Tonga ni ‘a ia kuo ‘osi lesisita ‘a e tokotaha kole ia ko ha tangata‘i fonua ‘o ha fonua kehe mei Tonga kuopau ke ‘oatu ia ki he Failesisita Seniale.

(c) Ko e ngaahi hiki pehē ‘o e lesisita pe lesisita fo‘ou kuopau ke toki fakahoko pe ia ‘i ha tu‘utu‘uni tohi ‘a e Failesisita Seniale.

#### 14 Fakamatala fuakava ke poupou‘i ha tohi kole ke hiki ha lesisita fa‘ele8

(a) Ko e ngaahi tohi kole kotoa pe ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni kimu‘a kuopau ke ‘i ha tohi kuo fakatu‘asila ki he Failesisita Seniale.

(b) Ko e tohi kole pehe kuopau ke poupou‘aki ha fakamatala fuakava kuo ‘osi fuakava‘i ‘e he tokotaha kole ‘o fokotu‘u ai —

(i) ‘a e feitu‘u mo e ‘aho fa‘ele‘i ‘o e tokotaha kole;

(ii) ‘a e feitu‘u mo e ‘aho fa‘ele‘i ‘o e fa‘e mo e tamai ‘a e tokotaha kole ‘o ka ‘oku ‘ilo‘i;

(iii) ‘a e feitu‘u pe ngaahi feitu‘u na‘e lesisita ai ‘a e fa‘ele‘i ‘o e tokotaha kole mo e ngaahi ‘uhinga ‘o ia;

(iv) ‘a e ngaahi ‘uhinga ‘oku fakakaukau ai ‘a e tokotaha kole ‘oku totonu ke lesisita hono fa‘ele‘i ‘i he Lesisita Tonga.

(c) ‘O ka malava ko e ngaahi tohi ta‘u ‘o e tokotaha kole pea mo ‘ene ongo matu‘a pea mo ha ngaahi tohi ngaue poupou kehe kuopau ke fakaha fakataha ia mo e fakamatala fuakava.

#### 15 Ngaahi liliu ‘o ha tohi lesisita ‘o e mali pe tohi mali9

(a) Ko e ngaahi liliu ‘o ha tohi lesisita ‘o e mali pea/pe ‘o ha tohi mali ‘e toki fakahoko ia ‘i ha tohi fakangofua mei he Failesisita Seniale.

(b) Ko e ngaahi tohi kole kotoa pe ‘e fakahoko ia ‘o fakatatau mo e founga ‘oku ha ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni 8(2) mo e 8(3) ‘o e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Failesisita Seniale ‘o e Fa‘ele Pekia mo e Mali. Ko e ngaahi liliu ‘e fakahoko ia ‘o fakatatau mo e tu‘utu‘uni 8(4) mo e 8(6)(b) ‘o e Tu‘utu‘uni ko ia.

#### 16 Tauhia ‘o e fanau10

(1) Kapau ‘e fakahoko ha Tu‘utu‘uni Tauhia ‘o e Fanau fakatatau ki he Lao ki hono Tauhia ‘o e Fanau 2004, kuo pau ke ‘ikai liliu ‘a e ‘uluaki tohi ‘i he Fai‘anga Lesisita ‘i ha fa‘ahinga founga tukukehe pe ‘aki hono tanaki atu ‘a e ongo mata‘itohi lanu kulokula ko e “G.O” pea mo e fika mo e ‘aho ‘o e Tu‘utu‘uni.

(2) Kuo pau ki ha kole ke liliu ha hingoa ‘o ha tamasi‘i ‘a ia kuo fakahoko ki ai ha Tu‘utu‘uni Tauhia ‘o e Fanau ke fakahoko ia ‘e kinautolu kuo fokotu‘u ko e tauhi ‘i he ngaahi kupu ‘o e Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni (Liliu ‘o e Hingoa) ‘a e Failesisita Seniale 2011.
