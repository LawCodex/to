# Lao (Fakapekia) ki he Ngaahi Monu’ia Tukuhau ki he Sipoti ‘a e Pasifiki 2019 2018

Lao (Fakapekia) ki he Ngaahi Monu'ia Tukuhau ki he Sipoti 'a e Pasifiki 2019 2018 Kupu 1 to Lao Fika 5 ‘o e 2018 Peesi 5 C T LAO (FAKAPEKIA) KI HE NGAAHI MONU'IA TUKUHAU KI HE SIPOTI 'A E PASIFIKI 2019 2018 Lao Fika 5 ‘o e 2018 KO E LAO KE FAKAPEKIA 'A E LAO KI HE NGAAHI MONU'IA TUKUHAU KI HE SIPOTI 'A E PASIFIKI 2019 2017

‘Oku ou loto ki ai, TUPOU VI,

‘Aho 21 ‘o Sune 2018.

‘OKU TU’UTU’UNI ‘e he Tu’i mo e Fale Alea ‘o Tonga ‘i he Fakataha Alea ‘o e Pule’anga ‘o pehe: 1 Hingoa nounou

(1) ‘E ui ‘a e Lao ni ko e Lao (Fakapekia) ki he Ngaahi Monu’ia Tukuhau ki he Sipoti ‘a e Pasifiki 2019 2018.

(2) ‘I he Lao ni, kuo pau ke ui ‘a e Lao ki he Ngaahi Monu’ia Tukuhau ki he Sipoti

‘a e Pasifiki 2019 2017, ko e “Tefito’i Lao”. 2 Fakapekia

‘Oku fakapekia heni ‘a e Tefito’i Lao.

Na’e tali ‘e he Fale Alea ‘i he ‘aho 12 ni ‘o Ma’asi 2018.
