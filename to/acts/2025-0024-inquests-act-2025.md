#### 6 Lipooti ‘a e toketa, taha ‘analaiso pe taha feʻunga kehe

(1) Kuo pau ke fa‘u ‘e he toketa, taha ‘analaiso pe taha feʻunga kehe ‘oku ne fakahoko ‘a e tafa ‘o e sino ‘o e pekia ke sivi ‘i he malumalu ‘o e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ‘o e Lao ni, ha lipooti ‘i he foomu kuo tuʻutuʻuni ‘e he Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni.

(2) Kuo pau ke tuku atu ‘e he toketa ‘i he vave taha ‘e ala lava ‘a e tohi fakamoʻoni totonu ‘a e fakatupunga ‘o e pekia ki he Failesisita Seniale ‘i he hili ‘a hono fakahoko ‘a e tafa ‘o e sino ‘o e pekia ke sivi.

#### 7 Fatongia ‘o e Fakamaau Polisi ‘i hono maʻu ‘o e lipooti

(1) Kapau, ‘i hono maʻu ‘o e ngaahi lipooti ‘oku fiemaʻu, kuo pau ke fiemalie ‘a e Fakamaau Polisi, pea ‘i he ‘ikai fakahoko ‘o ha fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa, ki he fakatupunga ‘o e pekia, pea kuo pau ke ne lipooti ki he ‘Ateni Seniale ‘a e fakatupunga ‘o e pekia ‘i hono ma‘u ki he ‘ene fiemalie.

(2) Kuo pau ‘e ikai fakahoko ‘e ha Fakamaau Polisi ha fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ‘i he malumalu ‘o e Lao ni ‘o kapau ‘oku ‘i ai ha ‘uhinga ‘oku ne tui ai ko e ngaahi hopo hia fekauʻaki mo ha taha koeʻuhi ko ‘ene fakatupunga ‘a e pekia ‘o e pekia kuo, pe ‘oku teu ke, kamata.

(3) ‘I he ngaahi hopo kehe kotoa, kuo pau ke kamata ‘a e Fakamaau Polisi ‘i he vave taha ke fakahoko ‘a e fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa pea ‘e ngofua ke toloi ‘a e fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ‘o ‘ikai toe fanongonongo ‘o kapau kuo kamata ha hopo hia kuo ‘osi fakaha.

#### 8 Pekia ‘o ha taha ‘oku tauhi

Ka pekia ha taha lolotonga ‘oku tauhi ‘e he kau polisi, pe pilisone, kuo pau ke fakahoko ha lipooti ‘i he vave taha ‘e ala lava ‘a e pekia ki ha Fakamaau Polisi, pea kuo pau ki he Fakamaau Polisi ko ia pe ha taha kehe, ke ne fakahoko ha fakamaau

‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ki he fakatupunga ‘o e pekia pea, ki he taumuʻa ko ia, ‘e ngofua ke tuʻutuʻuni ha ‘ofisa polisi ke fakahoko ha fakatotolo mo e lipooti ‘oku lau ki ai ‘i he kupu 3.

#### 9 Ngaahi mafai ‘o e Fakamaau Polisi

(1) Kuo pau ki ha Fakamaau Polisi ‘oku ne fakahoko ha fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ke ne maʻu ‘a e ngaahi mafai kotoa ‘oku ngaueʻaki ‘e ha Fakamaau Polisi ‘oku ne fakahoko ha hopo hia ‘i he ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ‘o e Lao ki he Ngaahi Hia.

(2) ‘Engofuakiha FakamaauPolisi‘okunefakahokohafakamaau‘ekekihapekia fakafokifa, ‘o kapau ‘oku ne fakakaukau ‘oku faingamalie ange ki he sino ‘o e taha pekia ke sivi‘i ‘e ha toketa ke malava ‘o fekumi ki he fakatupunga ‘o e pekia, neongo pe naʻe fakahoko ha tafa ‘o e sino ‘o e pekia ke sivi ‘i he ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ‘o e kupu 5 pe ‘ikai, ke ne tuku atu ha tuʻutuʻuni ki ha toketa ke ne fakahoko ‘a e tafa ‘o e sino ‘o e pekia ke sivi, pea ‘e ngofua, ki he taumu‘a ko ia, ke tuʻutuʻuni ke hiki.

#### 10 ‘E ngofua ke vakai ‘a e Fakamaau Polisi ki he sino

‘E ngofua ki ha Fakamaau Polisi ke ne fakahoko ha fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa, kapau ‘oku ne fiemaʻu, ke vakai ki he sino ‘o e pekia pea ‘e ngofua, ki he taumuʻa ko ia, ke tuʻutuʻuni ‘a e sino ko ia ke hiki.

#### 11 Sivi ‘o e kau fakamo‘oni

Kuo pau ki ha taha pe ‘a ia ‘oku ‘i he fakakaukau ‘o e Fakamaau Polisi ko ha taha

‘oku kaunga tonu ke ‘i ai ‘ene totonu ke sivi‘i ha taha fakamoʻoni ‘i ha fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa pe fakahangatonu pe ‘i ha fakafofonga lao, ka kuo pau ke ‘ikai fakangofua ‘e he Fakamaau Polisi ha fehuʻi ‘a ia ‘oku ‘ikai felave‘i ‘ene fakakaukau mo ia, pe ‘oku fakalotokovi, fakaongoongo kovi pe fakamalohi.

#### 12 Hokohoko ‘o e fakafehu‘i

Tuku kehe ka ‘i ai ha tuʻutuʻuni kehe ʻa e Fakamaau Polisi, kuo pau ke ne ‘uluaki fakafehu‘i ʻe ia ha taha fakamoʻoni ki ha fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa pea kapau ʻoku fakafofongaʻi ʻa e taha fakamoʻoni ki he fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa, ke fakafehu‘i fakaʻosi ia ʻe hono fakafofonga.

#### 13 Ngaahi fehuʻi ngalingali ke mo‘ua

(1) Kuo pau ʻe ʻikai tuʻutuʻuni ha taha fakamoʻoni ʻi ha fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ke ne tali ha fehuʻi ʻa ia ʻe malava ke ngalingali ke mo‘ua ai.

(2) Kapau ʻoku ha ki he Fakamaau Polisi kuo ʻeke ha fehuʻi pehe ki ha taha fakamoʻoni ‘i he kupu si‘i (1), kuo pau ke ne fakaha ki he taha fakamoʻoni ko ia ʻoku ngofua ke ʻikai ke ne tali ʻa e fehuʻi.

#### 14 Niʻihi ʻa ia ʻe ngofua ke fakafehuʻi ʻenau toʻonga

(1) Ka ʻi ai ha taha ʻa ia ko ʻene toʻonga ʻoku ngalingali ʻi he vakai ʻa e Fakamaau Polisi ke ui ʻo fakafehuʻi ʻi ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa, kuo pau, kapaukuoʻikai keʻoatukiaihatohifekaukeʻoatuʻenefakamatalafakamoʻoni ʻi ai, ke ʻoatu ki ai ha fanongonongo ʻo ʻikai siʻi hifo ʻi he houa ʻe 24 ʻo fekauʻaki mo e ʻaho, houa mo e feituʻu ʻa ia ʻe fakahoko ai ʻa e fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa.

(2) Kapau ‘oku fehu‘ia ‘a e ‘ulungaanga ‘o ha taha ‘i he fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ‘i he ngaahi makatu‘unga ‘a ia ‘oku fakakaukau ‘e he Fakamaau Polisi ‘oku mahuʻinga pea ‘oku felaveʻi mo ha me‘a ‘oku lau ki ai ‘i he kupu 15 pea kapau ‘oku ‘ikai ‘i ai ‘a e taha ko ia ‘i he fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa pea te‘eki fekau ke ‘i ai pe tuku atu ha fanongonongo ‘o e fakahoko

‘o e fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa, kuo pau ke toloi ‘a e fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ke fakangofua ia ke ne ‘i ai, ‘o kapau ‘oku ne fiemaʻu.

#### 16 Fakakaukau ʻa e Fakamaau Polisi

Kuo pau ʻe ʻikai fakaha ʻe he Fakamaau Polisi haʻane fakakaukau ʻo kau ‘i ha ngaahi meʻa ʻo kehe mei he ngaahi meʻa kuo lave ki ai ʻi he kupu 15, ka kuo pau ʻe ʻikai ha meʻa ʻi he kupu ni ke ne taʻofi ʻa e Fakamaau Polisi mei hono fakahoko ha faleʻi ʻa ia kuo fakataumuʻa ke taʻofi ʻa e toe hoko ha ngaahi pekia ʻo tatau mo ia ʻi heʻene fekauʻaki mo e fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ʻoku fakahoko.

#### 17 Fakamatala fakamoʻoni

(1) Kuo pau ʻe ʻikai tali ʻi ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ha fakamatala fakamoʻoni ʻi he tohi ngaue ki he founga naʻe pekia ai ʻa e taha pekia tuku kehe ʻi ha fiemalie ʻa e Fakamaau Polisi ʻoku ʻi ai ha ʻuhinga lelei mo feʻunga ʻoku ʻikai totonu ke kau atu ʻa e taha naʻa ne faʻu ʻa e tohi ngaue ki he fakamau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa.

(2) Kapau ʻe tali ha tohi pehe ʻo e fakamatala fakamoʻoni ʻi ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa, kuo pau ke toloi ʻa e fakamau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa kae lava ʻa e taha naʻe ne faʻu ʻa e tohi ngaue ke ne ʻotau ha fakamatala fakamoʻoni ngutu ʻo kapau ʻe loto ki ai ʻa e Fakamaau Polisi.

(3) Kuo pau ʻe ʻikai haʻi ʻa e Fakamaau Polisi ʻe ha lao fakaefakamoʻoni ʻi hono fakahoko ʻo ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ka ʻe ngofua ke ne fakatokanga‘i ‘a e mafatukituki ‘o e fakamo‘oni ko ia ki ha fakamatala fakamoʻoni ʻa ia ʻoku ne pehe ʻoku taau.

#### 20 Ngaahi Mafai ʻo e ʻAteni Seniale

(1) Neongo ʻa e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻo e kupu 7, ʻe ngofua ke kole ʻe he ʻAteni Senialeʻi hataimipeki heFakamaauʻanga Lahi hatuʻutuʻuni ketuʻutuʻuni ʻaki ʻa hono fakahoko ʻe he Fakamaau Polisi ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ki he tupuʻanga mo e ngaahi tukunga ʻoku fekauʻaki, mo ha pekia kuo hoko ʻi ha ngaahi tukunga ʻa ia kuo fakamahino pau ʻi he kupu 3 pea ko e Fakamaau Polisi kuo ʻoatu ki ai hatuʻutuʻuni pehe kuopau ke ne fakahoko leva ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ʻo fakatatau mo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻo e tuʻutuʻuni ko ia mo e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻo e Lao ni.

(2) Kapau kuo ngata ʻa e ngaahi ngaue ʻi ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa pea ʻoku ha ki he ʻAteni Seniale ʻoku taau ke fakahoko hano toe fakatotoloʻi, ʻe ngofua ke ne kole ki he Fakamaauʻanga Lahi ha tuʻutuʻuni ke tuʻutuʻuni ʻaki ʻa hono toe vakai‘i ʻe he Fakamaau Polisi pe ha taha kehe ʻa e fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa pea fakahoko hano toe fakatotoloʻi, pea ko e Fakamaau Polisi kuo ʻoatu ki ai ʻa e tuʻutuʻuni ko ia, kuo pau ke ʻi ai ʻa e mafai kakato ke toe vakai‘i ʻa e fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa pea fakahoko ha toe fakatotolo, pea hili ia ke ne hokohoko atu ʻi he founga tatau ʻo hange pe naʻe ʻikai tapuni ʻa e ngaahi ngaue ʻi he fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa pehe.

Ka kuo pau ʻe ʻikai ngaueʻaki ʻa e kupu siʻi ni ki ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ʻa ia ko e ola ʻo ha fakapo, pe ha tamate ta‘efakalao ‘oku ‘ikai hoko ko ha fakapo, kuo fakafoki ‘o fekauʻaki mo ha taha.

(3) ʻE ngofua ke tuʻutuʻuni ʻe he Fakamaauʻanga Lahi ʻi hono faʻu ha tuʻutuʻuni ʻi he kupu ni, kapau kuo tanu ʻa e sino, pe kuo pau pe ʻikai ke hiki.

(4) Kapau ʻoku ha ki he Fakamaauʻanga Lahi ʻoku taau ke hoko atu ʻe ha fakmaau polisi kehe ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa kuo kamataʻi ʻe ha Fakamaau Polisi ʻe taha, ʻe ngofua ke tuʻutuʻuni pehe, ʻaki ha tuʻutuʻuni, ke fakahoko ia ʻe he ongo Fakamaau Polisi fakatouʻosi ʻa ia kuo pau kena faipau leva ki ai.

#### 21 Toe vakaiʻi mo e ngaahi tangi

(1) Kuo pau ke ‘i ai ‘a e totonu ha ngaahi faʻahi ʻoku kaunga ke kole hano toe vakaiʻi fakaefakamaauʻanga ʻa e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni kuo fakahoko ʻe he Fakamaau Polisi ʻi hono fakahoko ʻo ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa.

(2) Kuo pau ke fakamaauʻi ha ngaahi tangi kuo ʻohake mei he ngaahi ola mo e fokotuʻu atu mei ha fakamaau ʻeke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ʻo fakatatau mo e ngaahi lao ʻe ala fekauʻaki mo ia.

#### 22 Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni

ʻE ngofua ke faʻu ʻe he Minisita ʻi he loto ki ai ʻa e Kapineti ha ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ki he kotoa pe ha niʻihi ʻo e ngaahi taumuʻa ko eni –

(a) ki hono puleʻi ʻo e ngaahi founga ngaue mo e fakahoko ngaue ʻi he ngaahi fakamaau ‘eke ki ha pekia fakafokifa ʻoku felaveʻi mo e ngaahi meʻa ʻa ia kuo ʻikai tuʻutuʻuni ki ai ʻa e Lao ni;

(b) ki hono puleʻi ʻo e ngaahi foomu ke ngaueʻaki mo e kotoa ʻo e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku fekauʻaki mo ia;

(c) ki hono puleʻi ʻo e ngaahi lekooti ke tauhi, mo e ngaahi fakamatala ke faʻu, ʻe he kau Fakamaau Polisi ʻoku nau ngaueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi fatongia ʻi he Lao ni; pe

(d) ki hono fakahoko mo hono pule‘i lelei ʻa e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni mo e taumuʻa ʻo e Lao ni.

#### 23 Fakapekia

ʻOku fakapekia ʻe he Lao ni ʻa e Lao ki he Pekia Fakafokifa [Vahe 3.09].

Na‘e tali ʻe he Fale Alea ʻi he ʻaho 8 ni ʻo Me 2025.
