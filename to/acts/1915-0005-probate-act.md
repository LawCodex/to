# Lao ki he Fakamaau'anga Tohi Tuku

LAO KI HE FAKAMAAU'ANGA TOHI TUKUTOHI LAO FAKATONUTONU 2020 KO E LAO KE FAKATONUTONU 'A E LAO 'OKU KAU KI HE FAKAMAAU'ANGA TOHI TUKU1 Kamata'anga [13 ‘o Novema, 1915]

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ui ‘a e Lao ni ko e Lao ki he Fakamaau'anga Tohi Tuku.

#### 2 Fakahinohino

‘I he Lao ni —

“Fakamaau‘anga” ko hono ‘uhinga ko e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi.

#### 3 Mahu‘inga ‘o e koloa

‘E sivi ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘a e mahu‘inga totonu ‘o e ngaahi koloa ‘a ha pekia ‘o ka lava ke fai.

#### 4 Ko e fakaha ki he kau fai tohi tuku

‘Oku ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ‘i he ‘ene loto ki ai pe ‘i he kole ‘a ha taha ‘oku kau ki he tohi tuku ke fakaha ki he kau fai tohi tuku (executors)—(ka ‘i ai) kuo tu‘u

‘i he tohi tuku ko ia ke ha‘u ki he Fakamaau‘anga mo fakamo‘oni ‘a e tohi tuku ko ia pe ke fakafisi ke ngaue‘aki ki ai.

#### 5 Ngaahi fehu‘i

‘Oku ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke fakafisi ke fai atu ha tohi fakamo‘oni tohi tuku ki ha tohi tuku ‘i he te‘eki ai tali ‘i he ‘ene loto fiemalie ki he ngaahi fehu‘i kotoa pe

‘a ia ‘oku ha totonu mo ‘aonga ke fai.

#### 6 Tamate‘i pe fetongi

‘Oku ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke tamate‘i pefetongi ha tohi fakamo‘oni tohituku koe‘uhi ko e ngaahi ‘uhinga kuo pau ke tu‘u ‘i he tohi ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga.

#### 7 Tefito‘i tohi tuku

Kuo pau ke tauhi mo tuku ‘i he Fakamaau‘anga ‘a e ngaahi tohi tuku kotoa pe kuo fakamo‘oni‘i ‘i he Fakamaau‘anga.

#### 8 Ke tuku ki he fakamaau‘anga ‘a e ngaahi tohi

Ka ‘i ai ha taha ‘oku ne ma‘u pe tauhi ha tohi pe pepa ‘a ha taha pekia ‘a ia ‘oku ‘i ai ha tohi tuku kuo pau te ne ‘ave leva ‘a e tefito‘i tohi ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke tauhi ai.

Pea ka ‘i ai ha taha ‘e ‘ikai fai pehē ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 14 hili ‘a e ‘aho na‘e fai ai ‘a e pekia kapau te ne ‘ilo ki he pekia ko ia ‘e ngofua ke tautea ia ‘o ka ‘ilo ‘oku mo‘oni ‘o ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $500 ‘o hangē ko ia ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ki ai ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga.2

#### 9 Ka ‘ikai ha tohi tuku

‘Ihe me‘a ‘oku ‘ikai ha tohi tuku ‘e fai ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘i he anga tatau ‘o hangē na‘e ‘i ai ha tohi tuku ‘oka ne ka lava ki ai.

#### 10 Ka toko lahi ‘a e kainga ofi

Ka ‘i ai ha ni‘ihi kainga ‘o ha taha pekia pea kapau ‘e kole ‘e ha taha ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke tali ‘a e tohi fakamo‘oni tohi tuku pea kapau ‘oku ‘i ai ha taha pe ni‘ihi ‘oku kainga totonu ki he taha ko ia, kuo pekia ‘oku ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke tu‘utu‘uni ha tohi fakaha ki he kole ke ‘atu ki he ngaahi kainga kehe ko ia.

#### 11 ‘E pule‘i ‘e he fakamaau‘anga ‘a e koloa ‘a e pekia

Hili ha taimi kuo pekia ha taha ki he taimi kuo ‘osi fakamo‘oni ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ki he tohi tuku ‘e pule ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ki he ngaahi koloa ‘a e pekia ko ia.

#### 12 ‘E ma‘u ‘e he pule‘anga ‘a e koloa mole kotoa

‘O ka ‘osi ‘a e ta‘u ‘e 3 mei he ‘aho na‘e tuku ai ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke pule‘i ‘a e koloa ‘a e pekia, ‘o hangē ko ‘ene ha ‘i he kupu 11 ‘o e Lao ni, ‘oku ‘ikai ‘ilo ha taha ke ne ‘eke‘i pe taha pe ‘oku kainga ofi mo e pekia, pe ke fokotu‘u ha totonu ki he koloa, ko e pa‘anga ‘e ma‘u mei hono fakatau atu ‘o e koloa ‘a e pekia ko ia ‘e hoko ko e koloa ‘a e Pule‘anga, pea ke fakahu atu ki he Pa‘anga Hu Mai.3 Koe‘uhi ko ha ‘ū me‘a ‘o hangē ko ia ‘oku ha ‘i ‘olunga ‘e ngofua ke tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Minisita, ke fetongi hono fakahū atu ‘o e pa‘anga ‘e ma‘u mei hano fakatau atu ‘o e koloa ‘a e pekia ki he Pa‘anga Hū Mai, ‘aki ha tu‘utu‘uni ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke nau

‘oange ha tohi pule‘i ‘o e koloa ‘a e pekia ki ha taha ‘oku ‘i ai ha‘ane totonu ki he foaki pehē pe ki ha taha ko e hako tu‘utamaki ‘o e pekia.4

#### 13 Failesista ‘o e fakamaau‘anga lahi ke pule‘i

‘E ngofua ki he Failesisita ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ke pule‘i ‘a e ngaahi koloa ‘a ha taha kuo pekia pea kapau ‘e fakanofo ia ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘e ngaue ia ‘i he tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga.

#### 14 Koloa ‘ikai lahi hake hono mahu‘inga ‘i he $50005

Ka ha ki he Fakamaau‘anga ko e mahu‘inga ‘o e ngaahi koloa ‘a ha taha pekia ‘oku

‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $5000 ‘e ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ta‘e tohi fakamo‘oni tohi tuku ke totongi mei ha koloa hangaahi mo‘ua pe ngaahitotongi ‘okutotonu ketotongi

‘e he tokotaha pekia pea ke totongi ‘a e toenga (‘okapau ‘oku ‘i ai) ki ha taha ‘oku ‘i ai ha‘ane totonu ki ai.

#### 15 Fekau ki he kau fai tohi tuku pe pule tohi koloa

‘Oku ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ‘i he ‘ene loto ki ai pe ‘i ha kole ‘a ha taha ‘oku kau ki he pekia pe ha taha ‘oku mo‘ua ki ai ha taha ‘oku pekia ke fai ha tohi fekau ki he fa‘ahinga kuo tu‘u ko e kau fai tohi tuku ‘i he tohi tuku ke ha‘u ki he Fakamaau‘anga ‘o fakamatala pe ko e ha he ‘ikai fai ai ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘e Fakamaau‘anga ki he ngaahi koloa ‘a ha taha kuo pekia.

#### 16 Vahevahe a e koloa ‘a e pekia

‘E ma‘u ‘e he uitou ‘o ha taha pekia ‘a e fale nofo‘anga ‘i hono ‘api kolo (pea ka ‘oku lahi ange ‘a e ngaahi fale ‘i he ‘api ko ia ‘oku ‘i he Fakamaau‘anga ke tu‘utu‘uni ko e fale fe ke ‘atu ki he uitou), mo e ngaahi ngoue mo e fanga puaka mo e fanga moa mo e ngatu ‘e lau ia ko e koloa ‘a e uitou neongo ‘oku ‘i ai ha tohi tuku pe ‘ikai ka ko hono toe ‘o e koloa ‘e tufa ‘o hange ko e Tepile I ‘o e Lao ni.

#### 17 Tautea

Ka ‘i ai ha taha ‘e fakafisi pe ta‘e tokanga ke talangofua ki he tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ‘e ngofua ke tautea ia ki he pa‘anga ‘o ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $500 pe ngaue popula ‘o ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he mahina ‘e 6 ‘o hangē ko ia ‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga.6

#### 18 Koloa ‘oku ala tufa ‘e he tohi tuku

Fakatatau ki he Lao ni, ‘oku ngofua ki he taha kotoa pe ke ne tuku atu, ‘aki ha tohi tuku kuo fai ‘o hangē ko e Lao ni, ‘a e koloa kotoa pe na‘a ne ma‘u ‘i he taimi ‘o ‘ene pekia, pea ‘a ia kapau ‘oku ‘ikai ke tuku atu, ‘e kau ia ki he‘ene koloa ‘a ia ‘e tufa ‘o fakatatau ki he Lao ni.7

#### 19 Lava ke fai tohi tuku8

Koe‘uhi ke fakalao ha tohi tuku kuo pau ke fai ia ‘e ha taha ‘a ia —

(a) kuo a‘u ‘a hono motu‘a ki he ta‘u 16; pea

(b) ‘oku ne ma‘u ha ‘atamai lelei ‘oku lava ke tuku atu koloa.

#### 20 Ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ke muimui ki ai ‘i he fai ‘o ha tohi tuku9

(1) Fakatatau ki he Lao ki he Founga ‘o e Ngaahi Tukuange Fakatohi Tuku,10

‘oku

‘ikai ke fakalao ha tohi tuku kae‘oua ‘oku —

(a) fai tohi; pea

(b) fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘i lalo pe ngata‘anga ‘o ia ‘e he taha tohi tuku pe ha taha kehe ‘i hono ‘ao pea ‘i he‘ene tu‘utu‘uni, ‘o fakatatau ki he kupusi‘i

(2).

(2) Ko e fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘a e taha tohi tuku pe ‘a ha taha kehe ‘oku ha ‘i he kupusi‘i (1)(b) ‘oku fakalao —

(a) ‘o kapau pe na‘e fai pe fakamo‘oni‘i ‘e he taha tohi tuku ‘i he ‘ao ‘o e fakamo‘oni ‘e toko 2 pe lahi hake na‘a nau ‘i ai ‘i he taimi ko ia, ‘o fakamo‘oni‘i mo fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘e he kau fakamo‘oni pehē ki he tohi tuku ‘i he ‘ao ‘o e taha tohi tuku;

(b) ‘i he‘ene felave‘i mo hono tu‘u‘anga ‘o kapau ‘oku ne fai ki he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e kupusi‘i (4).

(3) ‘Oku ‘ikai ha sipinga makehe ia ‘o e fakamo‘oni‘i ‘oku fiema‘u ke fai, ka ‘oku fiema‘u ‘a e kau fakamo‘oni ke nau fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘i he ‘ao ‘o kinautolu takitaha, pea ‘oku ‘ikai fiema‘u ke toe fanongonongo ‘a e tohi tuku.

(4) Fekau‘aki mo e tu‘u‘anga ‘o e fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘a e taha tohi tuku, pe ‘o e taha ‘oku ne fakamo‘oni hingoa ma‘ana —

(a) ‘oku fakalao ha tohi tuku ‘o kapau ko e fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘oku tu‘u ‘i he, hili, hoko mai, lalo, tafa‘aki pe hanga ki he ngata‘anga ‘o e tohi tuku pea ‘oku ha mahino ‘i he fotunga ‘o e tohi tuku na‘e ‘uhinga ‘a e taha tohi tuku ke hanga ‘e he fakamo‘oni hingoa pehē ‘o fakahoko ‘a e tohi nima kuo fakamo‘oni‘i ko ‘ene tohi tuku;

(b) ‘oku ‘ikai uesia ha tohi tuku ‘e he ngaahi me‘a ‘oku hoko ‘o pehē —

(i) ‘oku ‘ikai hoko mai ‘a e fakamo‘oni hingoa, pe ‘oku ‘ikai hili pe,

‘i lalo pe ngata‘anga ‘o e tohi tuku;

(ii) ‘oku ‘i ai ha vaha‘a ‘oku ‘atā ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e lea faka‘osi ‘o e tohi tuku pea mo e fakamo‘oni hingoa;

(iii) ‘oku tu‘u ‘a e fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘i he lotolotonga ‘o e ngaahi lea

‘o ekupu fakamo‘oni pe‘oe kupu fakamo‘oni‘i,pe‘oku hoko mai pe ‘oku hili pe ‘i lalo ‘i he kupu fakamo‘oni‘i, ‘o ‘i ai pe ‘ikai ha vaha‘a ‘atā ‘i honau vaha‘a, pe ‘oku hoko mai pe ‘oku hili, lalo pe tafa‘aki ‘o e ngaahi hingoa pe taha ‘o e ngaahi hingoa ‘o e kau fakamo‘oni hingoa;

(iv) ko e fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘oku ‘i ha tafa‘aki, peesi pe konga kehe ‘o e pepa pe ‘ū pepa ‘oku tu‘u ai ‘a e tohi tuku pea ‘oku ‘ikai ke ‘i ai ha kupu pe palakalafi pe konga tuku atu koloa ‘o e tohi tuku ‘oku tohi‘i ‘i ‘olunga ‘i he fakamo‘oni hingoa; pe

(v) ‘oku ha mai ‘oku ‘i ai ‘a e ‘atā fe‘unga ke tu‘u ai ‘a e fakamo‘oni hingoa pe ‘i he ngata‘anga ki lalo ‘o e tafa‘aki kimu‘a, peesi pe konga kehe ‘o e pepa tatau ‘a ia ‘oku hiki ai ‘a e tohi tuku, pea ‘oku ‘ikai fakangatangata ‘e he ngaahi me‘a kuo fakalautelau ‘i

‘olunga ‘a hono ngaue fakalukufua ‘aki ‘o e kupu si‘i ni; ka ‘oku ‘ikai lava ke ngaue ha fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘i he kupu ni ke ne ‘oatu ha mafai ki ha tuku atu koloa pe tu‘utu‘uni ‘a ia ‘oku ‘i lalo pe hoko mai ai, pe

‘oku ne ‘oatu ha mafai ki ha tuku atu koloa pe tu‘utu‘uni ‘a ia kuo fakahu

‘i he hili ‘o e fai ‘o e fakamo‘oni hingoa.

(5) ‘Oku ‘ikai ko ha fakamo‘oni fe‘unga ha taha ki he fai ‘o ha tohi tuku ‘o kapau

‘oku ne fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e tohi tuku ‘i ha anga kehe mei he fakamo‘oni hono hingoa ‘i he ‘ene tohinima totonu pe ‘a‘ana.

#### 21 Fakahoko fakatohi tuku ‘o e mafai11

(1) ‘E ‘ikai fakalao ha fokotu‘u ‘oku fai ‘aki ha tohi tuku, ‘i hono ngaue‘aki ‘o ha mafai, kae ‘oua ‘oku fai ‘o hangē ko e kupu 20 pe ko e Lao ki he Founga ‘o e Ngaahi Tuku Fakatohi Tuku.12

(2) ‘Oku ugaue‘aki ‘a e kupusi‘i (1) neongo pe ko e ha ha me‘a ‘e kehe ‘i he tohi

‘oku ne ‘omai ‘a e mafai.

(3) Ko ha tohi tuku kuo fai ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 20 pe Lao ki he Founga ‘o e Ngaahi Tuku Fakatohi Tuku, ‘i he‘ene fekau‘aki mo hono fai pea mo hono fakamo‘oni‘i ‘o ia, ko ha fakahoko fakalao ia ‘o ha mafai ke fokotu‘u ‘aki ha tohi tuku, neongo ko e tohi ‘oku ne ‘omai ‘a e mafai ‘oku ne tu‘utu‘uni mahino

‘o pehē ko ha tohi tuku kuo fai ‘i hono ngaue‘aki ‘o ha mafai pehē ‘oku totonu ke fai ‘aki mo ha toe sipinga fai tohi tuku kehe pe founga ngaue kehe.

#### 22 Ta‘e fe‘unga ‘a e taha fakamo‘oni

Fakatatau ki he kupu 20(5), kapau ko ha taha ‘oku ne fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e fai ‘o e tohi tuku ‘oku ne hoko ‘i he taimi ‘o hono fai pe ‘i ha taimi kimui mei ai ‘o ta‘e fe‘unga ko ha tokotaha fakamo‘oni ke fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a hono fai, ‘e ‘ikai ta‘e fakalao ‘a e tohi tuku koe‘uhi ko e me‘a ko ia.13

#### 23 Foaki ki he fakamo‘oni14

(1) Fakatatau ki he kupusi‘i (2), kapau ko ha taha ‘oku ne fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e fai ‘o ha tohi tuku ko ha taha ia ‘oku ‘i ai ‘a hono kaunga ‘i he tohi tuku (pe ko ha foaki pe ko e ngaue‘aki ‘oha mafai ke fokotu‘u ka ‘oku ‘ikai ko e pea tuku kehe

‘a e ngaahi totongi mo e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ki he totongi ‘o e ngaahi mo‘ua),

‘oku ta‘e‘aonga ‘a e foaki pe fokotu‘u ‘i he‘ene fekau‘aki mo ha taha fakamo‘oni tohi tuku pehē pe ha taha ‘oku ne ‘eke ha‘ane totonu koe‘uhi ko e taha fakamo‘oni; ka ‘oku fe‘unga pe ‘a e taha fakamo‘oni tohi tuku ia ko ha taha fakamo‘oni ke ne fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e fai ‘o e tohi tuku, pe ke fakamo‘oni‘i

‘a e fakalao pe ta‘efakalao ‘o e tohi tuku, neongo ‘a e foaki pe fokotu‘u kuo fakaha ‘i he tohi tuku.

(2) Ko e fakamo‘oni‘i ‘o ha tohi tuku ‘o ha taha ‘oku foaki ki ai pe ko hono mali pe‘okufaikiai hatukuatukoloa‘ohangēkoia‘okufakamatala‘i‘ihekupusi‘i

(1) kuo pau ke fakata‘e‘aonga‘i ia kapau ‘oku totonu pe ‘a hono fai ‘o e tohi tuku ta‘e kau ai ‘a ‘ene fakamo‘oni pea ta‘e kau ai mo ia ‘a ha toko taha kehe pehe.

(3) ‘Oku ngaue‘aki ‘a e kupu ni ki he tohi tuku ‘a ha taha kuo pekia hili ‘a e fakahoko ‘o e Lao (Fakatonutonu) ki he Fakamaau‘anga Tohi Tuku 198715 , pe na‘e fai kimu‘a pe hili ‘a e fakahoko ‘o e Lao ko ia.

#### 24 Fakamo‘oni ‘a e taha ‘oku mo‘ua ki ai

Kapau ‘oku hilifaki ‘e ha tohi tuku ki ha koloa ke ne totongi ha mo‘ua pea —

(a) ko ha taha ko ia ‘oku mo‘ua ki ai ko hono mo‘ua kuo hilifaki ki ai ‘a e totongi; pe

(b) ko e uaifi pe husepaniti ‘o e taha ‘oku mo‘ua ki ai kuo lau ki ai ‘i he palakalafi (a),

‘oku ne fakamo‘oni ki he fai ‘o e tohi tuku, ‘oku fe‘unga ‘a e taha fakamo‘oni tohi tuku pehē, neongo ‘a ia kuo hilifaki ke totongi, ko ha tokotaha fakamo‘oni ke fakamo‘oni‘i‘ahonofaipekefakamo‘oni‘i‘aefakalaopeta‘efakalao‘oetohituku.16

#### 25 Fakamo‘oni ‘a ha fai tohi tuku

‘Oku ‘ikai ke ta‘efe‘unga ha taha, koe‘uhi ko ‘ene hoko ko e fai tohi tuku ‘o ha tohi tuku, ke hoko ko ha fakamo‘oni ke ne fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e fai ‘o e tohi tuku pe ke fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e fakalao pe ta‘efakalao ‘o e tohi tuku.17

#### 26 Mali ‘a e taha tohi tuku

Ko e mali ‘a e taha tohi tuku kuo pau ke ne tamate‘i ‘a ‘ene tohi tuku.18

#### 27 Liliu ‘i he ngaahi me‘a ‘oku hoko

‘Oku ‘ikai ke tamate‘i ha tohi tuku ‘aki hano lau ko e mo‘oni ha loto (intention) ‘oku makatu‘unga ‘i he liliu ‘i he anga ‘o e ngaahi me‘a ‘oku hoko.19

#### 28 Tamate‘i fakalukufua20

‘Oku ‘ikai ke lava tamate‘i ha tohi tuku, pe ko ha konga ‘o ia, tuku kehe —

(a) ‘aki ha tohi tuku kehe ‘e taha;

(b) ‘aki ha tohi, ‘o fakaha ai ha loto ke tamate‘i ‘a e tohi tuku, kuo fai ‘i he founga ko ia ‘a ia ‘oku fiema‘u ke fai ‘aki ha tohi tuku; pe

(c) ‘aki hano fai ‘e he taha tohi tuku, pe ha taha ‘i hono ‘ao pea ‘aki ‘ene tu‘utu‘uni,tutu, hae pefounga kehe ‘a honofaka‘auha‘o e tohituku, ‘aki hono loto ke tamate‘i ia.

#### 29 Liliu hili ‘a e fai ‘o e tohi tuku

‘Oku ‘ikai fakalao ha tamate‘i, tohi ‘i he vaha‘a laine pe liliu kehe kuo fai ‘i ha tohi tuku, hili ‘a hono fai, pe ‘i ai hano mafai koe‘uhi ‘oku ‘ikai mahino ‘a e ngaahi lea pe mafai ‘o e tohi tuku kimu‘a ‘i he liliu, kae‘oua ‘oku fai ‘a e liliu ‘i he founga ‘a ia ‘oku fiema‘u ke fai‘aki ha tohi tuku; ka ko e tohi tuku, mo e liliu ko e konga ‘o ia, ‘oku totonu pe ‘a hono fai ‘o kapau ko e fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘a e taha tohi tuku pea mo e fakamo‘oni hingoa ‘a e kau fakamo‘oni ‘oku fai ‘i he tapa ‘o e pepa, pe ‘i ha konga kehe ‘o e tohi tuku ‘o hanga pe ofi ki he liliu, pe ‘i lalo pe ngata‘anga ‘o e pe fehangahangai mo ha ki‘i fakamatala ‘o kau ki he liliu pea ‘oku tohi‘i ‘i he ngata‘anga pe ‘i ha konga kehe ‘o e tohi tuku.21

#### 30 To e mo‘ui ‘a e tohi tuku ne tamate‘i22

(1) ‘Oku‘ikai hatohituku,pehakonga ‘oia,‘aiakuotamate‘i,‘okutoefakamo‘ui mai tuku kehe ‘aki ‘a hono —

(a) toe fai ‘o e tohi tuku kuo tamate‘i; pe

(b) fai ha fakalahi ‘o e tohi tuku ‘o fakaha ai ha loto ke toe fakamo‘ui ‘a e tohi tuku kuo tamate‘i.

(2) Ka ‘i ai ha tohi tuku ‘a ia kuo ‘uluaki tamate‘i fakakonga, pea toki tamate‘i fakakatoa kimui, ‘oku toe fakamo‘ui mai, ‘oku ‘ikai kau ‘a e toe mo‘ui mai ko ia ki he konga kuo tamate‘i kimu‘a ‘i he tamate‘i fakakatoa ‘o e tohi tuku kae‘oua ‘oku fakaha ha loto ke toe fakamo‘ui ‘a e konga ko ia.

#### 31 Foaki (conveyance) pe ngaahi me‘a hili ‘a e tohi tuku

‘Oku ‘ikai ha foaki (conveyance) pe me‘a kehe, na‘e fai pe kuo fai hili ‘a e fai ha tohi tuku, ‘o e pe kau ki ha koloa kuo lau ki ai ‘i he tohi tuku (tuku kehe ha me‘a ‘oku ne tamate‘i ‘a e tohi tuku ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 28) ‘oku ne ta‘ofi ‘a e ngaue‘aki ‘o e tohi tuku ‘o kau ki he ngaahi kaunga ‘i he koloa ko ia ‘a ia ‘oku ma‘u mafai ki ai ‘a e taha tohi tuku ke ne tufa ‘aki ‘a e tohi tuku ‘i he taimi ‘o ‘ene pekia.23

#### 32 ‘Oku lea ‘a e tohi tuku mei he pekia

Kuo pau ke faka‘uhinga‘i ‘a e tohi tuku kotoa pe ke felave‘i mo e koloa oku lau ki ai

‘i he tohi tuku, ke lea mo ngaue‘aki ke hange na‘e toki fai kimu‘a pe ‘i he pekia ‘a e taha tohi tuku, kae ‘oua ‘oku ha mai ha ‘uhinga kehe mei he tohi tuku.24

#### 33 Foaki kuo ‘osi hono taimi mo ta‘e‘aonga

Tuku kehe ‘o ka ha mai ha ‘uhinga kehe mei he tohi tuku, kapau ‘oku ‘ikai lava ke fakahoko ha foaki fakatohi tuku pe ‘oku ta‘e‘aonga koe‘uhi ko e pekia ‘a e tokotaha

‘okufoaki ki ai ‘ihelolotonga ‘oe kei mo‘ui ‘aetahatohituku,koe‘uhi ‘okumaumau ai ‘a e lao pe ‘uhinga kehe, ko ha koloa ‘oku kau pe ‘oku ‘uhinga ke fakakau ‘i he foaki ko ia ‘oku lau ia ko e toenga koloa, kapau ‘oku ‘i ai, kuo tu‘u ‘i he tohi tuku.25

#### 34 ‘Osi ‘a e taimi ‘o e foaki ki ha hako

Ka ‘i ai ha koloa ‘oku tuku ki ha ki‘i tamasi‘i pe hako kehe ‘o e taha tohi tuku, ko ha koloa pe ha kaunga ‘a ia ‘oku ‘ikai lava ke fakapapau‘i ‘i he pe kimu‘a ‘i he pekia ‘a e taha ‘oku tuku ki ai ‘a e koloa, pea —

(a) ‘oku pekia ‘a e taha ‘oku tuku ki ai ‘a e koloa, ‘oku ‘i ai hano hako lolotonga ‘oku kei mo‘ui pe ‘a e taha tohi tuku; pe

(b) ko e hako pe ni‘ihi ‘o kinautolu ‘oku nau lolotonga mo‘ui ‘i he taimi o e pekia a e taha tohi tuku,

‘oku ‘ikai ‘osi ‘a e foaki, ka ‘oku mo‘ui mo ma‘u mafai ‘o hange na‘e hoko ‘a e pekia

‘a e taha ‘oku tuku ki ai ‘a e koloa hili pe ‘a e pekia ‘a e taha tohi tuku, tuku kehe ‘o ka ha mai ha ‘uhinga kehe mei he tohi tuku.26

#### 35 Tu‘utu‘uni fakalukufua ‘o e fa‘u27

(1) ‘Oku ngofua ke tali ha fakamatala fakamo‘oni mei tu‘a ke ne fakaha ‘a e loto

‘o e taha tohi tuku pea ke tokoni ‘i he fa‘u ‘o e pe ke fakamatala‘i ha fepakipaki

‘i ha tohi tuku.

(2) Kapau ‘oku lava ke laka hake ‘i he faka‘uhinga ‘e taha ‘a e ‘uhinga ‘o ha foaki fakatohi tuku, pea kapau ‘e ‘i ai ha veiveiua, kuo pau ke ngaue‘aki ‘a e faka‘uhinga ko ia ‘a ia ‘e lava ai ‘o fakahoko ‘a e foaki.

#### 36 Ko hono fakahoko28

#### 38 Koe pa‘anga ‘oku mo‘ua ai ‘a e pule‘anga ki ha pekia31

(1) Ka ‘i ai ha taha kuo pekia ‘oku mo‘ua ki ai ‘a e Pule‘anga pe na‘e ngaue ‘i he Pule‘anga pe ‘ikai ‘e ‘ikai ngofua ke totongi atu ‘a e pa‘anga kae ‘oua kuo fai tu‘utu‘uni ki ai ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga.

(2) (a) Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai ke totongi atu ki ha taha ma‘u monu‘ia ha pa‘anga kuo ma‘u ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘o fakatatau ki he Lao ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga MaloloMeiheNgaue1998,peNgaahiTu‘utu‘unikuofa‘u‘iai,kae‘oua kuo tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ki he koloa ‘a e taha pekia ‘oku felave‘i mo e pa‘anga ko ia.

(b) Kuo pau ke ma‘u ‘e he Failesisita ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga ‘a e mafai ke fakahu ki ha ‘akauni ‘oku ma‘u mei ai ha tupu, ha pa‘anga kuo ma‘u ‘i he palakalafi (a).

#### 39 Ngaahi tohi pule‘i ‘o e tohi tuku muli32

Ko ha tefito‘i tohi fakamo‘oni tohi tuku pe tohi pule‘i ‘o ha koloa ta‘e tohi tuku pe ko hono tatau, kuo ‘oatu ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga Ma‘olunga ‘o ha fonua muli pe Komoniueli, pe ko ha tatau loua kuo sila‘i pe ha tatau kuo fakamo‘oni‘i ‘o ia, ‘oku ngofua ke fai hano toe sila‘i ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘o kapau ‘oku pehe —

(a) Ko e tohi fakamo‘oni, ko ha tohi tuku ‘oku kau ki he tohi fakamo‘oni, pea mo ha toe ngaahi tohi ngaue poupou kehe kuo ‘omai pea kuo ‘i ai ha nau tatau loua kuo tuku ‘i he Fakamaau‘anga.

(b) Ko e katoa ‘o e ngaahi tohi ngaue kuo lau ki ai ‘i he kupusi‘i (1) —

(i) ‘oku ‘i he lea Faka-Pilitania pe ko ha liliu Faka-Pilitania kuo fakamo‘oni‘i totonu ‘oku fakapipiki ki ai;

(ii) ko e ngaahi tefito‘i tohi pe ko e ngaahi tatau mo‘oni kuo fakamo‘oni‘i totonu;

(iii) ‘oku sila‘i ‘aki ‘a e sila mo‘oni fakalao ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ‘oku ne ‘oatu ia;

(c) ‘Oku ‘ikai ha me‘a fakalao ‘i Tonga ke ne ta‘ofi ‘a e fai ‘o e tohi fakamo‘oni tohi tuku ki he tokotaha pe kakai kuo fokotu‘u ‘i ai;

(d) Ko e pule‘i totonu ‘o e tohi fakamo‘oni ‘e ‘ikai maumau ai ha lao ‘i he ngaahi lao ‘o Tonga;

(e) Ko ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ki he ma‘u mai ‘o ha kau malu‘i kuo lava hono fai;

(f) Tuku kehe ‘o ka fai ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ha tu‘utu‘uni kehe ‘e fai ‘a e tohi fakamo‘oni ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ma‘u mafai ‘i he feitu‘u ko ia na‘e pekia ai ‘a e pekia kuo ne nofo fonua ai (domicile);

(g) Ko e tohi kole ke fai ha toe sila‘i ‘oku fai ia ki he Fakamaau‘anga ‘e he pe ma‘a e tokotaha pe kakai ‘a ia na‘e fai ki ai ‘a e tohi fakamo‘oni;

(h) ‘I he fakakaukau ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ‘oku totonu mo taau ke fai pehe;

(i) Kuo totongi ‘a e ngaahi totongi ‘oku fiema‘u ke fai;

(j) Ko e katoa ‘a e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni kuo hilifaki ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga pea mo e Lao ni kuo lava hono fai.

#### 40 Kole ke to e sila‘i

Ko e tohi kole ke fai hano toe sila‘i kuo pau ke poupou‘i ‘aki ia ha fakamatala fuakava kuo fuakava‘i ‘e he tokotaha kole.33
