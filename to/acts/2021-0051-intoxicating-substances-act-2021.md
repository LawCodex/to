# Lao ki he Ngaahi Faito’o Kona 2021

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ui ‘a e Lao ni ko e Lao ki he Ngaahi Faito’o Kona 2021. 2 ʻUhingaʻi lea ʻI he Lao ni, tuku kehe ka ʻi ai ha ʻuhinga kehe ʻi hono tuʻunga –

“Minisita” ʻoku ʻuhinga ki he Minisita Polisí;

#### 3 Hia ke manavaʻaki pe maʻu ha ngaahi faitoʻo kona

Ka ‘i ai ha taha ʻi he ʻilopau ʻo ʻikai ha ʻuhinga fakalao, kuo pau ‘e ‘iate ia ‘a hono fakamoʻoniʻi, ʻoku ne manavaʻaki pe maʻu ha faitoʻo kona ki he taumuʻa ke fakatupunga ha konaa, heeheeʻia, fiefia, fakakeheʻi pe ha toe uesia fakaʻatamai kehe,ʻoku ne fakahoko ‘a e hia pea kuo pau ke ala moʻua ʻi haʻane halaia ki ha moʻua paʻanga ‘oku ʻikai lahi hake ʻi he $10,000 pe ke ngaue popula ki ha vaha’a taimi ‘oku ʻikai laka hake ʻi he taʻu ʻe 3 pe fakatouʻosi.

#### 6 Mafai ke puke ha faitoʻo kona mei ha taha

(1) ʻE ngofua ki ha ʻofisa polisi ke puke ha faitoʻo kona mei ha taha kapau –

(a) ko e taha ko ia ʻi he ʻilo pau ʻo ʻikai ha ʻuhinga fakalao kuo ne manavaʻaki pe ma’u pe ‘oku ne manava’aki pe ma’u ʻae faitoʻo kona; pe

(b) ko e ʻofisa polisí ʻoku ne mahamahalo fakapotopoto ko e taha ko ia ‘e malava ‘i he ‘ilo pau ‘o ‘ikai ha ‘uhinga fakalao ke ne manavaʻaki pe ma’u ʻa e faitoʻo kona kapau heʻikai puke ʻa e faitoʻo kona.

(2) Neongo ʻa e kupu siʻi (1), ʻe ngofua ke puke ha faitoʻo kona ʻo tatau aipe pe ʻoku ʻikai ke konaa ʻa e taha ko ia.

(3) Kuo pau ki ha ʻofisa polisi ke fakaʻauha ha faitoʻo kona kuo puke ʻi he kupu siʻi (1) ʻi ha founga ʻoku malu pea fakapotopoto –

(a) ʻi loto ʻi he mahina ʻe 1 hili ʻa e tuʻutuʻuni aofangatuku ʻo e hopo ki he hia pe ngaahi hia fekauʻaki mo e faitoʻo kona kuo puke; pe

(b) ʻi loto ʻi he mahina ʻe 1 hili hono fakapapauʻi ʻe he ʻofisa polisi ʻoku ʻikai toe fiemaʻu ia ke ngaueʻaki ko ha fakamoʻoni.

#### 7 Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni

ʻE ngofua ki he Minisitaá, ʻi ha loto ki ai ʻa e Kapinetí ke faʻu ha ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ki hono fakahoko totonu ʻo e Lao ni, kau ai ʻa e ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻa ia ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni ki he pe fekauʻaki mo e –

(a) puleʻi ʻo e tauhi mo e fakahingoa ʻo e ngaahi faitoʻo kona;

(b) tuʻutuʻuni ki he ngaahi hia mo e hilifaki ʻo e ngaahi tautea ʻa ia ko e moʻua paʻanga ‘oku ʻikai lahi hake ʻi he $10,000 pe ngaue popula ki ha vaha’a taimi ‘oku ʻikai laka hake ʻi he taʻu ʻe 3;

(c) tu’utu’uni ki he ngaahi founga ngaue, me’angaue sivi mo e naunau ki hono sivi mo hono ‘ilo’i ‘a e ‘i ai ha ngaahi faito’o kona ‘i ha sino ‘o ha taha; mo

(d) ha ngaahi meʻa kehe ʻa ia ʻoku fiemaʻu ki hono fakahoko totonu ʻa e ngaahi kupu ʻo e Lao ni.
