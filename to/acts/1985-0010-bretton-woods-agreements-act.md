# Lao ki he Ngaahi Aleapau Bretton Woods

LAO KI HE NGAAHI ALEAPAU BRETTON WOODS KO E LAO KE FAKAPAPAU‘I E KAUNGĀ MEMIPA ‘A E PULE‘ANGA TONGA ‘I HE SINO‘I PA‘ANGA (FUND) FAKAVAHA‘APULE‘ANGA MO E PANGIKĒ FAKAVAHA‘APULE‘ANGA KI HE LANGA FO‘OU MO E FAKALAKALAKA MO E KOPOLEISONI PA‘ANGA FAKAVAHA‘APULE‘ANGA MO E KAUTAHA (ASSOCIATION) FAKALAKALAKA FAKAVAHA‘APULE‘ANGA, PEA MO TU‘UTU‘UNI KI HE FAI ‘O HA KUMI PA‘ANGA TOKONI ‘I HE NGAAHI KAUTAHA (ORGANISATION) NI PEA MO E NGAAHI ME‘A ‘OKU FELAVE‘I MO IA1 Kamata'anga [1 ‘o ‘Aokosi, 1985]

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ui ‘a e Lao ni ko e Lao ki he Ngaahi Aleapau Bretton Woods.

## KONGA I - TALATEU

#### 2 ‘Uhinga‘i lea

‘I he Lao ni, kapau ‘oku ‘ikai ha ‘uhinga kehe mei he lau ‘a e ngaahi lea ‘i he Lao ni —

“Kautaha” (Association) ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Kautaha (Association) Fakalaka Fakavaha‘apule‘anga;

“‘Aleapau ‘a e Kautaha” (Association) ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Ngaahi ‘Atikolo ‘o e Aleapau ki he fokotu‘u mo e fakalele ‘o e Kautaha (Association);

“Pangikē” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Pangike Fakaevaha‘apule‘anga ki he Langa Fo‘ou mo e Fakalaka;

“Aleapau ‘a e Pangike” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Ngaahi ‘Atikolo ‘o e Aleapau ki he fokotu‘u mo e fakalele ‘o e Pangike, ‘o hange ko hono fakatonutonu mei he taimi ki he taimi;2

“Kopoleisoni” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Kopoleisoni Pa‘anga Fakaeva- ha‘apule‘anga;

“Aleapau ‘a e Kopoleisoni” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Ngaahi ‘Atikolo ‘o e Aleapau ki he fokotu‘u mo e fakalele ‘o e Kopoleisoni ‘o hangē ko hono fakatonutonu mei he taimi ki he taimi;3

“Sino‘i-Pa‘anga (Fund)” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Sino‘i-Pa‘anga (Fund) Fakaevaha‘apule‘anga;

“Aleapau ‘o e Sino‘i-Pa‘anga (Fund)” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he ‘Atikolo ‘o e Aleapau ki he fokotu‘u mo e fakalele ‘o e Sino‘i-Pa‘anga (Fund) ‘o hangē ko hono fakatonutonu mei he taimi ki he taimi;4

“Tu‘utu‘uni Fakapapau ‘o e Kaungā Memipa he Kautaha” (Membership Resolution) ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Tu‘utu‘uni Fakapapau ‘oku ngāue‘aki ‘e he Poate ‘o e Kau Kovana ‘o e Sino‘i-Pa‘anga (Fund) pe ‘o e Pangikē pe ‘o e Kopoleisoni pe ‘o e Kautaha (Association), ‘a ia pe ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ai, ‘o fakamahino‘iai engaahitu‘utu‘uni mo e ngaahitu‘unga ‘a ia kuo pau ke hū‘aki

‘e Tonga ‘o memipa ‘i he ngaahi kautaha (organisation) ‘oku felave‘i mo ia.

“Minisita” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Minisita ‘oku ne tokangaekina e Pa‘anga;5

“Ngaahi Kautaha” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Sino‘i-Pa‘anga (Fund) ki he Pangikē, ki he Kopoleisoni mo e Kautaha (Association).

KUPU 8 — Ngaahi Tau‘atāina mo e Ngaahi Monu‘ia ‘o e Kau ‘Ofisa mo e Kau Ngāue Kotoa e Kau Kovana, Kau Talekita Pule, Kau Fakafofonga, Kau ‘Ofisa, mo e Kau Ngaue ‘o e Kautaha (Association):

(i) kuopautenautau‘atainamei hengaahingauefakalao‘ihe‘enefekau‘aki mo e ngaahi ngaue ‘oku nau fai ‘i honau lakanga faka‘ofisi, tuku kehe ‘o ka tukuange ‘e he Kautaha (Association) e tau‘ataina ko ‘eni;

(ii) ‘o kapau ‘oku ‘ikai ko e kau tangata‘i fonua, kuo pau ke ‘oange e ngaahi tau‘ataina tatau mei he ngaahi fakangatangata fakafefolau‘aki, ngaahi fiema‘u ki he lesisita ‘o ha kau muli mo e ngaahi fatongia fakakautau fakafonua pea mo e ngaahi naunau tatau mo e ngaahi fakangatangata ‘o e fakafetongi, ‘o hange ‘oku foaki ‘e he kau memipa ki he kau fakafofonga, kau ‘ofisa pea mo e kau ngaue ‘a ia te nautu‘unga tatau mei he kau memipa kehe;

(iii) kuo pau ke foaki kiate kinautolu e tokanga tatau ‘i he fekau‘aki mo e ngaahi naunau folau hange kuo foaki ‘e he kau memipa ki he kau fakafofonga, kau ‘ofisa mo e kau ngaue ‘a ia te nau tu‘unga tatau mei he kau memipa kehe.

KUPU 9 — Ngaahi Tau‘atāina mei he Tukuhau

(a) Kuo pau ko hono ngaahi koloa mahu‘inga, koloa, pa‘anga hu mai pea mo hono fakalele ‘o e ngaue mo e ngaahi fehu‘aki ‘o e Kautaha (Association) ‘a ia ‘oku fakamafai‘i ‘e he Aleapau ni, ‘e tau‘ataina ia mei he tukuhau kotoa pe pea mei he tute kotoa pe.

Kuo pau foki ke toe tau‘ataina e Kautaha (Association) mei ha ‘eke ki ai (liability) ki ha tanaki pe totongi ‘o ha tukuhau pe tute.

(b) Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai ha tukuhau ‘e hilifaki ki he pe fekau‘aki mo e ngaahi vahenga mo e ngaahi totongi ngaue ‘oku totongi ‘e he Kautaha (Association) ki he Kau Talekita Pule, Kau Fakafofonga, kau ‘Ofisa pe kau ngaue ‘oe Kautaha (Association) ‘oku ‘ikai ko ha kakai tonu ‘o e fonua, kakai ‘oku mafai ki ai e lao ‘o e fonua, pe ha toe kau tangata‘i fonua kehe.

(c) Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai ha fa‘ahinga tukuhau ‘e hilifaki ki ha mo‘ua (obligation)’ pe malu‘i (security) kuo fokotu‘u ‘e he Kautaha (Association), (lau ki ai ha ‘inasi pe tupu ‘oku ‘i ai) ‘e he taha pe ‘oku ne ma‘u:

(i) ‘a ia ‘oku ne fakafaikehekehe‘i ha mo‘ua (obligation) pehe pe malu‘i (security) koe‘uhi pe ‘oku ‘omai ‘e he Kautaha (Association); pe

(ii) ‘o kapau ko e tefito‘i mafai fakalao ia ki he tukuhau pehe ko e feitu‘u pe pa‘anga ‘a ia na‘e fokotu‘u ki ai, na‘e ala totongi pe totongi, pe ko e tu‘u‘anga ‘o ha ‘ofisi pe feitu‘u ngaue‘anga na‘e kei tauhi ‘e he Kautaha (Association).

(d) Kuopauhe‘ikaihilifakihafa‘ahingatukuhau‘ihamo‘ua(obligation)pemalu‘i (security) ‘oku fakamo‘oni‘i (guaranteed) ‘e he Kautaha (Association) (kau ki ai ha ‘inasi pe tupu ‘oku ‘i ai) ‘e ha taha pe ‘oku ne ma‘u:

(i) ‘a ia ‘oku ne fakafaikehekehe‘i ha ngaahi mo‘ua (obligations) pehe pe malu‘i (security) koe‘uhi pe na‘e fakamo‘oni‘i (guaranteed) ‘e he Kautaha (Association); pe

(ii) ‘o kapau ko e tefito‘i mafai fakalao ia ki ha tukuhau pehe ko e feitu‘u

‘oku tu‘u ai e ‘ofisi pe feitu‘u ngaue‘anga ‘oku tauhi ‘e he Kautaha (Association).

## TEPILE V

(Kupu 6(3)) SINO‘I-PA‘ANGA (FUND) FAKAEVAHA‘APULE‘ANGA

‘ATIKOLO III KUPU 4 — Fetongi ‘o e pa‘anga ‘aki e Ngaahi Malu‘i (Securities) Kuo pau ke tali ‘e he Sino‘i-Pa‘anga (Fund) mei ha fa‘ahinga memipa ‘e fetongi ha konga ‘o e pa‘anga ‘a e memipa ‘i he ‘Akauni Fakalukufua ‘a e Ngaahi Koloa ‘aki e ngaahi nouti pe ngaahi fatongia tatau ‘oku ‘omai ‘e he memipa pe tauhi‘anga pa‘anga kuo fokotu‘u ‘e he memipa ‘i he ‘Atikolo XIII, Kupu 2, ‘a ia ‘i he ngaahi fehu‘aki, kuo pau ke ‘ikai lava ‘o fakafetongi, ‘ikai hano tupu pea ala totongi ‘i honau mahu‘inga ha mai ‘i hano fiema‘u ‘aki hano kuletiti ‘o e ‘akauni ‘a e Sino‘i-Pa‘anga

(Fund) ‘i he tauhi‘anga pa‘anga kuo fokotu‘u.

PANGIKE FAKAEVAHA‘APULE‘ANGA KI HE LANGA FO‘OU MO E FAKALAKALAKA

‘ATIKOLO V KUPU 12 — Anga hono tauhi ‘o e pa‘anga Kuo pau ke tali ‘e he Pangike mei ha fa‘ahinga memipa, ‘o fetongi ha konga ‘o e pa‘anga ‘a e memipa, kuo totongi ki ha Pangike ‘i he ‘Atikolo II, Kupu 7(i), ‘aki e ngaahi nouti pe ngaahi fatongia tatau kuo ‘omai ‘e he Pule‘anga ‘o e memipa pe ko e tauhi‘anga pa‘anga kuo fokotu‘u ‘e he memipa ko ia, pe ke fakaa‘u e ngaahi totongi fakafoki ‘o e no (amortiation) ‘i he ngaahi no kuo fai‘aki e pa‘anga ko ia, pea ‘ikai lava ‘o fakafetongi, ‘ikai hano tupu pea ala totongi atu ‘i honau ngaahi mahu‘inga fakafetongi pa‘anga lolotonga ‘i hano fiema‘u ke kuletiti ki he ‘akauni ‘o e Pangike ‘i he tauhi‘anga pa‘anga kuo fokotu‘u.
