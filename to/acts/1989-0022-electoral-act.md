# Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ki he Fili Fale Alea

KO E LAO KE TU'UTU'UNI KI HE FILI 'O E KAU FAKAFOFONGA KI HE FALE ALEA PEA KI HE NGAAHI ME'A 'OKU FEKAU'AKI MO IA1 Kamata'anga [vakai ki he fakamatala fakalahi2 ]

## KONGA I - TALATEU

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ui ‘a e Lao ni ko e Lao ki he Fili Fale Alea.

#### 2 ‘Uhinga‘i Lea

‘I he Lao ni, tuku kehe ‘o ka fiema‘u ‘e he kupu ha ‘uhinga kehe —

“kanititeiti” ‘oku ‘uhinga ko ha kanititeiti ki ha fili pe fili si‘i;

“fili” ‘oku kau ai ha fili-si‘i ‘oku ‘uhinga ko ha fili ‘o e kau fakafofonga ‘o e kau Nōpele pe ‘o e kakai ‘i he Fale Alea; pea ko e “fili-si‘i” ‘oku ‘i ai ‘uhinga tauhoa;

“Komisoni Fili” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he Komisoni kuo fokotu‘u ‘i he Lao ki he Komisoni Fili;3

“tohi fakakātoa fakataimi” ‘oku ‘uhinga koe tohi fakakātoa ki he fili ‘oku tauhi ‘i he kupu 4(l), ‘i hono fakatonutonu ‘o a‘u ki he ‘aho ‘o hono pulusi ‘i he kupu 5(1);

“ofisa faifili” ‘oku kau ai ‘ae ‘ofisa tokoni faifili mo e ‘ofisa faifili pule;

“Supavaisa” ‘oku ‘uhinga ko e Supavaisa ‘o e Fili kuo fokotu‘u ‘i he kupu 3(1), pea kau ai ‘a e Supavaisa Tokoni;

“Tangata‘i fonua Tonga” ‘oku ‘uhinga ki he faka‘uhinga ‘oku hā ‘i he kupu 2 ‘o e Lao ki he Kakai ‘o e Fonua.4

## KONGA II - KO HONO NGAUE‘I

#### 3 Supavaisa ‘o e Fili5

(1) Kuo pau ke fokotu‘u ‘e he Komisoni Fili ha Supavaisa ‘o e Fili pea kuo pau ke ne ma‘u ‘a e ngaahi mafai mo e ngaahi fatongia kuo foaki kiate ia ‘e he Lao ni mo ha toe Lao kehe.

(2) Ko e Supavaisa ‘o e Fili, kuo pau, ‘i he loto ki ai ‘a e Komisoni Fili, ke ne fokotu‘u ha ‘ofisa faifili ki he vahefonua fai‘anga fili mo e vāhenga fili taki taha pea kuo pau foki, ka fiema‘u, kene fokotu‘u ha ‘ofisa faifili tokoni pea mo ha toe kau tokoni ‘o hangē ko ia te ne fiema‘u.6

(3) Ko e Supavaisa ‘o e Fili kuo pau, fakatatau ki ha fakahinohino ‘a e Komisoni Fili, ke ne ma‘u ‘a e pule‘i fakalukufua ‘o e pea mo hono tokanga‘i ‘o e fili, ‘a hono teuteu ‘o e tohi fakakātoa ki he fili, ‘a hono tokanga‘i ‘o e ngaahi launga kau ki he tohi fakakātoa mo e ngaahi ngāue ‘a e kau kanititeiti pea mo honau kau poupou, ‘a hono teuteu mo hono tufaki ‘o e ngaahi pepa paloti, ngaahi puha paloti, ngaahi fale fai‘anga fili mo ha ngaahi me‘a kuo fokotu‘u ma‘u kehe ‘oku fiema‘u ki he fili, pea alea‘i foki ke fe‘unga ‘a e ngaahi ngāue ki he malu‘i.

#### 4 Ko e tohi fakakātoa

(1) Kuo pau ke tauhi ‘i he ‘Ofisi ‘o e Komisoni Fili ha tohi fakakātoa ‘o e fili

‘oku fakahā ai ‘a e kakai kotoa pe kuo ngofua ‘i he kupu 64 ‘o e Konisitūtone ke hoko ko e kakai fili ‘o e kau fakafofonga ‘o e kakai.7

(2) Kuo pau ke tauhi ‘a e tohi fakakātoa ‘e he Supavaisa ‘o e fili pea kuo pau —

(a) ke vahevahe ki he ngaahi vāhenga fili;8

(b) ke fakatefito ‘i he tohi fakakātoa faka‘osi na‘e ngaue‘aki ki he fili kimu‘a;

(c) ke fakatonutonu ke fakahā ‘a e ngaahi hala talu hono ‘ilo, kau fili fo‘ou kuo fakangofua, kau fili kuo pekia, kakai kuo ‘ikai ke nau kei hoko ko e kakai ‘o e Pule‘anga Tonga pe ko e kau fili kuo fakata‘e‘aonga‘i, pea mo e vāhenga fili fakamuimui taha ‘oku ‘i ai ha kakai ‘oku ‘i ai ‘enau totonu ke nau fili;9

(d) fakakau atu ki he vāhenga fili taki taha ha tupu kehe ‘o fakahā ai ‘a e kau fili ‘oku nofo muli mo honau tu‘asila.10

(3) Ko e Tonga kotoa pe ‘i he a‘u hono ta‘u motu‘a ki he ta‘u 21 kuo pau, tukukehe ‘o ka fakata‘e‘aonga‘i ‘i he kupu 64 ‘o e Konisitūtone ‘i he loto mahina ‘e tolu mei he‘ene a‘u ki he ta‘u motu‘a ko ia pe ‘i loto mahina ‘e 3 mei he‘ene foki mai ki he Pule‘anga ni kapau kuo te‘eki lēsisita pe ‘i he loto mahina ‘e 3 mei he‘ene hoko ko e tangata‘i fonua ‘o e Pule‘anga Tonga liliu kakai, ke ne kole ki he Supavaisa ke hiki ‘i he tohi fakakātoa ki he vāhenga fili na‘a ne nofo ai ‘i he vaha‘a taimi ko e mahina ‘e 3 kimu‘a ‘i he‘ene tohi ‘i he lesisita. Ko e tohi kole ‘i he Foomu 1 kuo pau ke kau ai ha laui tā ‘o e taha kole. ‘I hono lēsisita ‘o ha taha fili, kuo pau ki he Supavaisa ke ne tuku atu ‘a e sitifikeiti ‘oku hā ‘i he Foomu 2 ‘o e Tepile.11

(4) Neongo ‘a e kupu si‘i (3) —

(a) ‘o kapau kuo nofo ha taha ‘o laka hake ‘i he feitu‘u ‘e taha lolotonga ‘a e vaha‘a taimi ko e mahina ‘e 6 ‘oku ‘uhinga ki ai ‘i he kupu si‘i (3), kuo pau ke ne tohi kole ke lēsisita, ‘i ha‘a ne fili, ‘i ha taha pe ‘o e ngaahi vāhenga fili ko ‘eni —

(i) vāhenga fili ‘a ia na‘e nofo fakamuimui ai ki he vaha‘a taimi ko e mahina ‘e 3; pe

(ii) vāhenga fili ‘a ia kuo nofo ‘i ai ‘i he vaha‘a taimi ko e mahina ‘e 3 kimu‘a hono fakahoko ‘a ‘ene tohi kole;

(b) ki ha memipa ‘o e Fale Alea ‘a ia ‘oku ne fakafofonga‘i ha vāhenga fili

‘i tu‘a ‘i Tongatapu, ko e taimi ‘oku ne nofo ai ‘i Tongatapu ke fakakakato ‘a hono ngaahi fatongia ko e memipa pe Minisitā ‘o e Kapineti kuo pau ke ‘ikai ke lau ia ‘i he taumu‘a ki hono fakapapau‘i pe

‘oku ne nofo ‘i fē;

(c) ko ha tangata‘i fonua Tonga ‘a ia ‘oku ‘ikai ke nofo ‘i Tonga, ‘e ngofua ke ne tohi kole ‘i he founga ‘a ia kuo tu‘utu‘uni ‘i he kupu si‘i (3) ke fakahū atu ki he tohi fakakātoa ki he vāhenga fili ‘a ia na‘a ne nofo fakamuimui ‘i ai ki he vaha‘a taimi ko e mahina ‘e 3;

(d) ‘o kapau ‘oku fiemālie ‘a e Supavaisa ‘oku ‘i ai ha taha ‘oku ‘i ai hono fatongia ‘i he ngāue ‘oku fiema‘u ia pe ko hono hoa ke nofo ‘i ha feitu‘u ‘i tu‘a ‘i he vāhenga fili angamaheni, ‘e ngofua ke ne tohi kole ke lēsisita ia ‘i he vāhenga fili ‘a ia, kimu‘a ‘a ‘ene ngaahi fatongia ‘i he ngāue ‘a ia na‘e fiema‘u ke hiki ai, ‘a e feitu‘u na‘a ne nofo fakamuimui ‘i ai ki he vaha‘a taimi ko e mahina ‘e 3;12

(5) Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai ngofua ke lēsisita ha taha ko ha taha fili ‘i ha vāhenga fili ‘o lahi hake ‘i he taha ‘i he taimi tatau.13

(6) Ko e taha fili —

(a) ‘e ngofua ke ne tohi kole ki he Supavaisa ‘i he Foomu 3 ‘o e Tepile ke liliu ‘a e vāhenga fili ‘a ia kuo lēsisita ai;

(b) ‘oku ‘ikai ke toe tangata‘i fonua Tonga, kuo pau ke ne fakahā ki he Supavaisa.14

(7) Kuo pau ke hanga ‘e he Supavaisa, kimu‘a ‘i hono pulusi ‘o e tohi fakakātoa

‘o hangē ko ia ‘oku tu‘utu‘uni hili eni, ‘o kumi ‘a e lekooti ‘a e Failesisita ‘o e Fā‘ele, Pekia mo e Mali ki he ngaahi pekia mo e ngaahi mali pea ko e Fakamaau‘anga Lahi ki he ngaahi mo‘ua ‘i he ngaahi hiamatea pea fakatonutonu ‘a e tohi fakakātoa ‘o fakatatau ki ai.15

(8) Ko e ‘ikai ke fai ‘a e kole ‘o hangē ko ia ‘oku fiema‘u ‘e he kupusi‘i (3) pea ko e ‘ikai ke fai ‘a hono fakahā ‘o hangē ko ia ‘oku fiema‘u ‘e he kupusi’i (5) mo e (6) ‘oku lau ia ko e hia ‘oku pau ke tautea ‘aki ‘ae tautea pa’anga ‘o ‘ikai laka hake ‘i he $50 pea ko e Supavaisa, neongo ‘a e ta’e malava pehē, ‘e ngofua ke ne fakatonutonu ‘a e tohi fakakātoa ‘o kapau ‘oku ne fiemalie ko ha taha pehē ‘oku totonu ke hiki ‘i he tohi fakakātoa pe ‘oku pau ke tamate’i ia mei ai, pe hiki ‘i he tohi fakakātoa ki he vahenga fili kehe.16

(9) Ko ha taha kuo ‘ikai tali ‘ene kole ke lēsisita ko ha taha fili, pe kuo tamate‘i hono hingoa mei he lesisita, pe ‘oku ne fakakikihi‘i ‘a e vāhenga fili ‘a ia kuo lēsisita ai, ‘oku ngofua ke tangi ‘i he tohi nima ki he Komisoni Fili tu‘unga ‘i he Fili.17

(10) ‘Oku ngofua ki ha taha pe ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ngaahi houa ko e 10 pongipongi mo e 3 efiafi ‘i he ‘aho ngaue ke ne kumi ‘a e tohi fakakātoa.

#### 5 Pulusi ‘o e tohi fakakātoa

(1) ‘E ‘ikai si‘i hifo ‘i he mahina ‘e 6 mei he ‘aho ‘a ia kuo pau ke fai ai ha fili, ko e Supavaisa kuo pau ke ne pulusi ‘a e tohi fakakātoa fakataimi ‘i he Kāsete, pea kuo pau ke ne fakapapau‘i ‘oku tonu ‘a e tohi fakakātoa.

(2) ‘Oku ngofua ki ha taha, ‘i he loto mahina ‘e 2 mei he pulusi ‘o e tohi fakakātoa fakataimi, ke launga, ki he Supavaisa, ‘o ne pehē —

(a) ‘oku ‘i ai ha hingoa ‘oku hala hono fakakau;

(b) ‘oku ‘i ai ha hingoa ‘oku hala hono tamate‘i;

(c) ‘oku ‘i ai ha hingoa ‘oku fakakau ‘i ha vāhenga fili hala.18

(3) Kuo pau ke fakatotolo ‘e he Supavaisa ‘a e katoa ‘o e ngaahi launga pehē kuo fai ‘i he kupusi‘i (2) pea kuo pau ke ne fakatonutonu ‘a e tohi fakakātoa ‘i ha feitu‘u ‘oku ne fiemālie ‘oku ‘i ai ha hala ai. Kuo pau ke fakahā ‘e he Supavaisa ki he taha launga taki taha ‘o ‘ene Tu‘utu‘uni ki he launga pea mo ha taha ‘oku ‘ikai fiemālie ki he tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Supavaisa pe ‘oku ne pehē na‘e ‘ikai ke ‘i ai ha tu‘utu‘uni ki he launga totonu, ‘e ala tangi ‘i ha tohi ki he Komisoni Fili.19

(4) Hili hano fai ha tu‘utu‘uni ki he ngaahi launga pea hili hano fakatokanga‘i ‘o e ola ‘o ha ngaahi tangi kuo faka‘osi, kuo pau ke pulusi ‘e he Supavaisa, ‘o

‘ikai laka hake ‘i he mahina ‘e 4 pe si‘i hifo ‘i he mahina ‘e 2 mei he ‘aho kuo tu‘utu‘uni pau ki ha fili, ‘a e tohi fakakātoa faka‘osi ki he fili ko ia.

(5) Ko e tohi fakakātoa faka‘osi kuo pau ko e aofangatuku ia ‘o e kakai fili ‘i he vāhenga fili takitaha ‘a ia ‘oku malava ke nau fili ‘i he fili ko ia pea ‘oku pau

‘e ‘ikai ke fakatonutonu tukukehe —

(a) ke fakakau ha taha fili kuo fakangofua ke fili ‘i ha vāhenga fili ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ‘aho pulusi mo e ‘aho e 14 kimu‘a ‘a e ‘aho fili; pe20

(b) ke ‘oua ‘e fakakau ha taha fili kuo pekia pe kuo fakata‘e‘aonga‘i ke fili

‘i ha vāhenga fili ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ‘aho pulusi mo e ‘aho fili; pe

(c) ke fakahā ‘a e ola ‘o ha tu‘utu‘uni pe tangi ‘i he kupusi‘i (3) kuo te‘eki faka‘osi ‘i he taimi ‘o e tohi fakakātoa faka‘osi; pe

(d) ke fakatonutonu ha hala fakakalake.21

(6) ‘E ‘ikai ha launga ‘e ngofua ke fai ‘o pehē kuo fakakau ha taha fili ‘i he tohi fakakātoa faka‘osi na‘e ‘ikai totonu ke fakakau ‘i he tohi fakakātoa, pe ko e pehē kuo ‘i ai ha taha fili na‘e li‘aki mei he tohi fakakātoa faka‘osi.

(7) Neongo ‘a e kupusi‘i (6), ‘e ‘ikai ke hanga ‘e he fakakau ‘o ha taha fili ‘i he tohi fakakātoa ‘o uesia ‘a e mafai ‘o e fakamaau‘anga ke ne pehē ko ha kanititeiti na‘e ‘ikai totonu ia ke kanititeiti.

(8) Koe‘uhi ko e ngaahi taumu‘a ‘o e kupu ni, ko e fo‘i lea “fili” ‘oku ‘ikai ke kau ai ha fili-si‘i.22

#### 5A Tohi Fakakātoa ki ha Fili-si‘i23

Ko e tohi fakakātoa ‘e ngaue‘aki ki ha fili-si‘i ‘i ha vāhenga fili, kuo pau ko e tohi fakakātoa ko ia na‘e ngaue‘aki ‘i he fili fakamuimui taha ki he vāhenga fili kae fakakau ki ai ha taha fili kuo fakangofua ‘i ha vahenga fili ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e fili fakamuimui taha mo e ‘aho ‘e 14 kimu‘a ‘a e fili-si‘i ko ia.24

#### 6 Ngaahi Tangi ki he tohi fakakātoa ‘o e fili

Ngaahi tangi ki he tohi fakakātoa ‘o e fili ‘i hono fakahoko ha tangi pe ‘i he kupu 4(9) pe 5(3), ‘e ngofua ki he Komisoni Fili ke ne ‘oatu ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘i he ngaahi me‘a ko ia ‘oku nau pehē ‘oku totonu pea ko e tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e Komisoni Fili kuo pau ko e faka‘osi ia mo e aofangatuku pea ‘e ‘ikai ke lava ‘o toe tangi ki ha sino kehe.25

## KONGA III - FILI

#### 7 Ngaahi tohi fekau fili26

(1) Ki he taumu‘a ‘o e ngaahi fili fakalukufua ‘o e kau fakafofonga nōpele pe koe kakai ki he Fale Alea, kuo pau ki he Tu‘i, ‘i ha fale‘i ‘e he Palēmia, ke ne tuku atu ha ngaahi tohi fekau ‘o e fili, pea sila‘i, ‘o fakatu‘asila ki he Komisoni Fili, ‘o fakahā mahino ai ‘a e —

(a) ‘aho, ‘ikai toe tomui ‘i he uike ‘e 8 mei he ‘aho ko ia ‘e fakahoko ai ‘a e fili;

(b) ‘aho totonu pe kimu‘a ‘a ia ‘e ala fakafoki ai ‘a e tohi fekau takitaha ki he Tu‘i, ‘o fakahoko ki ai ‘a e ola ‘o e fili; mo e

(c) ngaahi feitu‘u kuo fokotu‘u ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 3(3) ‘o e Tefito‘i Lao, pea mo e taimi ‘i he vāhenga fili takitaha ‘a ia ‘e tali ai ‘e he Komisoni Fili ‘a e ngaahi fili ‘a e kau fili.

(2) Kuo pau ke pulusi ‘a e tohi fekau fili kotoa pe ‘i he Kāseté pea mo ha nusipepa ‘e ‘ikai toe si‘i hifo ‘i he taha ‘a ia ‘oku lahi hono tufaki takai pea kuo pau ko e fatongia ia ‘a e Komisoni Fili ke ne fale‘i ‘a e kau fili ‘i he ngaahi vāhenga fili takitaha ‘a e taimi mo e feitu‘u ‘e fakahoko ai ‘a e fili.

#### 8 Ngaahi fokotu‘u (nominations)

(1) Hili ‘a hono ‘oatu ‘o e ngaahi Tohi Tu‘utu‘uni fili, kuo pau ke pulusi ‘e he Supavaisa ‘i he Kāsete pea ‘i ha nusipepa ‘ikai toe si‘i hifo ‘i he taha ‘a ia

‘oku lahi hono tufaki takai ‘a e ‘aho pe ngaahi ‘aho kuo pau ke tali ai ‘a e ngaahi fokotu‘u ‘o e kau kanititeiti ki he ngaahi sea ke fakafonu ‘e he fili. Ko e tali ‘o e ngaahi fokotu‘u kuo pau ke fai ia ‘i he ‘Ofisi ‘o e Komisoni Fili kapau ko Tongatapu, pea ‘i he ‘Ofisi ‘o e Kōvana kapau ko Vava‘u mo Ha‘apai, pea ‘i he Ngaahi ‘Ofisi ‘o e Kau Fakafofonga Pule‘anga kapau ko

‘Eua, Niuatoputapu mo Niuafo‘ou.27

(2) Ko e ‘aho pe ngaahi ‘aho kuo fokotu‘u kuo pau ke ‘ikai na‘a si‘i hifo ‘i he

‘aho ‘e 21 pe lahi ange ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 28 hili ‘a e ‘aho na‘e ma‘u ai ‘a e tohi fekau. ‘E ngofua ke fokotu‘u ki ai ha ‘aho ‘e ua hokohoko ki he taumu‘a ‘o e kupu ni.28

#### 9 Kau Kanititeiti

(1) ‘I he ‘aho pe ngaahi ‘aho pea ‘i he feitu‘u kuo fokotu‘u kuo pau ke ‘i ai ‘a e kau ‘ofisa fai fili ‘i he vaha‘a ‘o e ngaahi houa ko e 10 pongipongi mo e 3 efiafi ‘o tali ‘a e fokotu‘u ‘o ha kanititeiti kuo ‘osi fakangofua totonu pe kau kanititeiti ki he sea pe ngaahi sea ke fakafonu.29

(2) Ko e kanititeiti kotoa pe kuo pau ke fokotu‘u mai ‘i he tohinima ‘o fakatatau mo e Foomu 4 ‘o e Tepile kuo fakamo‘oni ai ‘a e toko 50 ko e kau fili kuo fakangofua ki he vāhenga fili ko ia. Kuo pau ke loto ‘a e kanititeiti ki he fokotu‘u pea ke ne fai ‘a e fakahā ‘i ai ‘i he tohinima ‘aki ‘ene fokotu‘u ‘ene fakamo‘oni hingoa ki he pepa fokotu‘u. Fakatatau ki he kupu 65 ‘o e Konisitūtone, ‘e ngofua ke fili ha kanititeiti ko ha kanititeiti pe ‘i he vāhenga fili ‘a ia kuo ne lēsisita ko e taha fili ‘i ai.30

(3) ‘I he taimi ‘oe fokotu‘u kuo pau ke tuku ‘e he kanititeiti ‘i he ‘ofisa fai fili ‘a e pa‘anga ‘e $400 pea kuo pau ke ne ma‘u mei he ‘ofisa fai fili ha lau‘i talitotongi Pa‘anga hū mai ‘a e Pule‘anga ki ai kuo fakamo‘oni hingoa totonu ki ai. Ko e totongi ‘oku fakahoko ‘e he kanititeiti kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai lava ‘o totongi fakafoki ‘i ha fa‘ahinga makatu‘unga pe.31

(4) Kuo pau ki ha kanititeiti, kimu‘a pea fakakakato hono fokotu‘u, ke ne tuku atu ki he ‘ofisa fai fili ha tohi faka‘atā mei he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi mo e Fakamaau‘anga Polisi ke fakapapau‘i ‘oku ‘ikai ke ‘i ai ha lekooti ‘i he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi mo e Fakamaau‘anga Polisi ha tu‘utu‘uni kuo te‘eki ke fakahoko ‘o hangē ko ia kuo fakahā ‘i he kupu 65 ‘o e Konisitūtone felave‘i mo e kanititeiti.32

(5) Kapau ‘i he 3 efiafi ‘i he ‘aho fakamuimui ko e ngaahi fika tatau pe ‘o e kau kanititeiti kuo fokotu‘u ‘o hangē ko e ngaahi sea ‘oku ‘atā kuo pau ke fakahā

‘e he ‘Ofisa fai fili kuo fili totonu ‘a e kanititeiti pe kau kanititeiti ko ia.33

(6) ‘Oku ‘ikai ngaue‘aki ‘a e kupu ni ki he fili ‘o e kau fakafofonga ‘o e kau Nōpele.

#### 9A Fakafoki ‘o e fokotu‘u34

(1) ‘E ngofua ki ha kanititeiti ke ne fakafoki ‘a hono fokotu‘u ‘aki ‘ene ‘oatu ki he Supavaisa ha Fanongonongo ‘i he Foomu 8 ‘o e Tēpile, ‘o ‘oua ‘e tomui ange ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 7 hili hono ma‘u ‘o e fokotu‘u ‘o e kau kanititeiti. Kuo pau ke fai leva ‘e he Supavaisa ha fanongonongo fakapule‘anga ‘o ha fakafoki ‘o ha kanititeiti.35

(2) Ko ha fakafoki ‘o ha fokotu‘u kanititeiti ‘oku ‘ikai ke fakatatau ki he kupu si‘i

(1) ‘o e kupu ni kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai ‘aonga ia.

(3) Ka ‘i ai ha kanititeiti ‘oku ne fakafoki hono fokotu‘u ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu si‘i (1) ‘o e kupu ni, kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai lava ke fili ia ‘i ha fa‘ahinga fai‘anga fili pe.

(4) Ka ‘i ai ha kanititeiti te ne fakafoki hono fokotu‘u kuo pau ke mole ‘a ‘ene tipositi.

#### 9B Fili ‘o e kau fakafofonga ‘o e hou‘eiki nōpele36

Ko e fili ‘o e kau fakafofonga ‘o e hou‘eiki nopele kuo pau ke fakatatau ia ki ha Ngaahi Tu‘utu‘uni ‘e fa‘u ‘e he Palemia ‘i he finangalo ki ai ‘a ‘Ene ‘Afio ‘i he Fakataha Tokoni.

#### 10 Fili

‘I he ‘aho pe ngaahi ‘aho kuo fokotu‘u ‘e he Tohi Tu‘utu‘uni Fili ko e kau ‘Ofisa fai fili kuo pau ke nau takitaha ‘i he fai‘anga fili kehekehe kuo fakahingoa ‘i he vaha‘a

‘o e ngaahi houa ko e 9 pongipongi mo e 4 efiafi, ‘a ia kuo pau ko e taimi ia ki he fili tukukehe ka kuo fakahā kehe ‘i he Tohi Tu‘utu‘uni Fili.

#### 10A Toloi ‘o e Fili37

(1) Kapau ‘e ‘ikai lava ha fili ‘i ha fai‘anga fili ‘o kamata pe kuo ta‘ofi fakataimi koe‘uhi ko ha fakatamaki fakaenatula, ‘ikai lelei ‘a e tu‘unga ‘o e ‘ea pe ha

‘uhinga kehe, ‘e ngofua ki he Supavaisa ke toloi ‘a hono fakahoko ‘o e fili ‘i he fai‘anga fili ko ia ki he ‘aho hono hoko, pea ka fiema‘u mei he ‘aho ki he

‘aho kae ‘oua kuo lava ‘o fakahoko ‘a e fili, pea kuo pau, kapau kuo ne toloi

‘a e fakahoko ‘o e fili ke ‘oatu ha fanongonongo fakapule‘anga ‘o e toloi ‘i ha founga ‘oku ne fakakaukau ‘oku fe‘unga.

(2) Neongo ‘a e kupu si‘i (1), kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai fakaava ‘a e fili ‘o laka hake ‘i he houa ‘e 10 ‘i ha fai‘anga fili.

(3) Kapau ‘oku toloi ‘a e tapuni ‘o ha fili ‘i ha fai‘anga fili ‘i he kupu ni ‘i ha lau
