FALE ALEA 3 Fokotu‘u ‘o e Poate Kuo pau ke ‘i ai ha Poate ki he Ngaahi Monu‘ia Malolo ‘a e Fale Alea. 4 Fatongia ‘o e Poate

(1) Ko e fatongia ‘a e Poate ko hono fakahoko hono pule‘i ‘o e Lao ni.

(2) Kapau ‘e ‘ikai fakangatangata ‘a e ‘uhinga ‘o e kupu si‘i (1), ‘e fakahoko ‘e he Poate ‘a e ngaahi fatongia ko eni —

(d) fiema‘u ‘a e Kautaha ke ne lipooti mai ‘i he mahina kotoa ki he Poate ki he tu‘unga ‘o e ngaahi ‘inivesi kotoa pe na‘e fakahoko ‘i he Lao ni;

(e) vakai‘i ‘a e ngaahi founga ‘inivesimeni ‘a e Kautaha ‘i he ta‘u kotoa pe, pea ke fa‘u ha ngaahi fokotu‘u ke ‘ave ki he Fale Alea ‘o ka fiema‘u, pea ke liliu mei he Kautaha;

(f) fakapapau‘i ‘a e ngaahi monu‘ia ‘o fakatatau ki he Lao ni; mo e

(3) Fakatatau ki he Lao ni mo ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni kuo fakahoko, ‘e ngofua ki he Poate ke ne pule‘i ‘a ‘ene ngaahi founga ngaue pea kuo pau ke ne ma‘u ‘a e mafai ke fa‘u ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni (rules) ki hono fakahoko ‘o ‘ene founga ngaue. 6 Kakai ki he Poate

## KONGA IV - NGAAHI TĀTĀNAKI KI HE PALANI

17 Fili ke kau ‘i he Palani

(1) ‘E ngofua pe ki ha memipa pe ‘ofisa ke ne fili ke liliu ke ne hoko ko ha tokotaha tātānaki ‘a ia ‘oku ha atu ‘i he Lao ni pe ‘e nofo pe ‘i he palani motu‘a pea ‘e ‘ikai ke ‘i ai ha totonu ‘a e Poate ke ta‘ofi ha memipa pe ‘ofisa mei he ‘ene fili ke liliu ‘o hoko ko ha tokotaha tātānaki.

(2) ‘E fili ha memipa pe ‘ofisa ke hoko ko ha tokotaha tātānaki ‘i ha taha ‘o e ngaahi ‘aho ko eni —

(a) mei he ‘aho ‘oku kamata ai ‘a e Lao ni;

(b) mei he ‘aho na‘e kamata ai ‘i he Fale Alea, tuku kehe kapau ko e ‘aho

‘oku ‘ikai ke ‘i mu‘a ange ‘i he ‘aho na‘e kamata ai ‘a e Lao ni; pe

(c) mei ha fa‘ahinga ‘aho pe ‘oku ne fili.

(3) Kapau ‘oku fili ha memipa pe ‘ofisa ke liliu ki ha tokotaha tātānaki ‘a ia ‘oku ha ‘i he Lao ni, ‘e ‘ikai ke ne toe ma‘u ‘a e monu‘ia ‘oku fakaha atu ‘i he kupu 10 mo e 11(1) mo e (3) ‘o e Lao ki he Fale Alea, ‘o kamata mei he ‘aho na‘e fili ai.

#### 19 Tātānaki ‘o e kau Memipa mo e kau ‘Ofisa14

(1) Kuo pau ki ha memipa pe ‘ofisa, ke ne totongi fakamahina ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga, ‘a ia ‘e to‘o ‘a e peseti ‘e 10 mei he‘ene tefito‘i vahenga fakata‘u pea ko e tātānaki ko eni kuo pau ke fakahu ki he‘ene ‘akauni tātānaki tu‘upau.

(3) Kuo pau ke mafai ‘a e Fale Alea ke ne to‘o hangatonu ‘a e ‘inasi ‘o e tokotaha tātānaki mei he‘ene tefito‘i vahenga fakata‘u ko e totongi ‘o ‘ene tātānaki fakamahina´ ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga pea kuo pau ke ‘oatu ha fanongonongo ‘i ha tohi ‘o e to‘o ko ia ki he toko taha tātānaki.

#### 20 Tātānaki ‘a e Fale Alea

Kuo pau ki he Fale Alea, ke ne totongi fakamahina ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga ‘aki ‘a e peseti ‘oku ha atu ‘i he Tepile ki he tokotaha tātānaki ko eni.15

## KONGA V - MONŪ‘IA ‘O E KAU MEMIPA MO E KAU ‘OFISA

#### 21 Monū‘ia ‘i he malolo

(1) Ko ha tokotaha tātānaki ‘i he Lao´ ni, ‘oku ngofua ‘i he ‘ene malolo ke ne ma‘u ‘a e monū‘ia fakakatoa mei he Sino‘i Pa‘anga ki he taimi na‘e tātānaki ai ki he Sino‘i Pa‘anga.

(2) Neongo ‘a e kupu si‘i (1), ‘e malava ‘e ha tokotaha tātānaki ‘oku ‘ikai ko ha Memipa ‘i he Fale Alea, ‘i he taimi ‘e fakangata ai ‘a e Fale Alea ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 77(1) ‘o e Konisitutone, ‘i ha‘ane kole ki he pea loto ki ai ‘a e Poate, ke ne ma‘u ‘a ‘ene monū‘ia fakakātoa mei he Sino‘i Pa‘anga, ‘o fakatatau ki he taimi ko ia na‘a´ ne hoko ai ko e tokotaha tātānaki.16
