(1) Ko ha taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu ‘oku ne fakahoko ha fakaha ki he lelei ‘o e kakai ki ha maʻu mafai taau ‘i he loto lelei –

(2) ‘Oku ngaueʻaki ‘a e kupu siʻi (1) neongo pe pe ‘oku fakahoko ‘a e fakaha ki he lelei ‘o e kakai ‘o maumauʻi ha fatongia ki he fakapulipuli.

(3) Koeʻuhi ko e kupu siʻi (1), ‘oku fakahoko ‘e he taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu ha fakaha ki he lelei ‘a e kakai ‘i he loto lelei kapau pe ko e taha ko ia ‘oku ne –

(a) tui ‘i he ngaahi makatuʻunga fakapotopoto ko e fakamatala ‘i loto ‘i he fakaha ‘oku moʻoni; pe

(b) ‘oku ‘ikai ‘i ha tuʻunga ke ne maʻu ha tui ‘i he ngaahi makatuʻunga fakapotopoto ka ‘oku ne tui ‘i he ngaahi makatu’unga fakapotopoto ‘e ala lava ke mo’oni pea ‘oku ‘i he mahuʻinga fe’unga ke fakahoko hono fakaha koe’uhi ke fakatotolo’i ‘a e mo’oni.

#### 7 Ko e hia ‘o e Fakahoko ‘a e Fakamamahi

(1) ‘Oku fakahoko ‘e ha taha ha hia ‘o kapau –

(a) ‘oku ne fakatupu, pe fakamanamana ke fakatupu, ha maumau ki he taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu; pea

(b) ʻoku ne fakahoko pe fakamanamana ke fakahoko ha ngaue koeʻuhi ko e taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu –

(i) kuo ne fakahoko pe fakataumuʻa ke fakahoko ha fakaha ki he lelei ʻa e kakai ki ha maʻu mafai taau; pe

(ii) kuo ne faipau mo fengaueʻaki, pe fakataumuʻa ke faipau mo fengaueʻaki mo ha fiemaʻu ʻo ha ʻofisa maʻu mafai felaveʻi.

(2) Kuo pau ki ha taha ʻoku halaia ʻi he kupu ni ke ala moʻua ki ha tautea paʻanga ʻo ʻikai lahi hake ʻi he $100,000 pe nofo popula ʻi he taʻu ʻe 10, pe fakatouʻosi.

(3) ʻE ngofua ki ha fakamaauʻanga ʻoku ne faka’ilo ha taha ki ha hia ki he kupu siʻi (1), makehe mei hono hilifaki ha tautea ki he hia, ke tuʻutuʻuni ʻa e taha ko ia ke ne totongi huhuʻi ki he mamahi ʻo e hia ki he maumau naʻe hoko ki he mamahi.

(4) ʻOku ʻikai uesia ʻe he kupu siʻi (2) ʻa e totonu ʻa e mamahi ke kumi ha fakalelei sivile ki he maumau naʻe hoko kiate ia.

#### 10 Fakapulipuli ‘o e ‘iloʻi ‘o e taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu

(1) ‘Oku fakahoko ‘e ha taha ha hia ‘o kapau –

(a) ‘oku ne fakaha ‘a e ‘iloʻi ‘o e taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu ki ha taha pe mafai ‘ikai maʻu mafai; pe

(b) ‘oku ne fakahoko ha anga ‘oku hoko ai ha fakaha ‘o e ‘iloʻi ‘o e taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu.

(2) Kuo pau ki ha taha kuo halaia ‘i he kupu ni ke ne ala moʻua ki ha tautea paʻanga

‘oku ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $50,000 pe nofo popula ‘o ‘ikai toe laka hake ‘i he taʻu ‘e 5, pe fakatouʻosi.

(3) ‘Oku ‘ikai ngaueʻaki ‘a e kupu siʻi (1) ‘o kapau ‘oku fakaha ‘e he taha ko ia

‘a e ‘iloʻi ‘o e taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu –

(a) ki he ngataʻanga ‘oku fiemaʻu ke fakapapauʻi ‘a e ngaahi meʻa ‘a ia ‘oku felaveʻi mo ia ‘a e fakaha, ‘oku fakatotoloʻi fakalelei; pe

(b) ‘aki ‘a e loto ki ai ‘a e taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu.

#### 11 Ngaahi hia ki he fakaha ‘ikai moʻoni, taumuʻa kovi pe takihala

#### 12 Ngaahi Tuʻutuʻuni

#### 13 Vakai Fakataimi

(1) Kuo pau ke fakahoko ‘e he maʻu mafai taau ha vakai fakaʻauliliki ‘o e Lao ‘o

‘ikai siʻi hifo ‘i he tuʻo taha ‘i he taʻu e nima kotoa ke siviʻi ‘a hono ‘aonga mo e felaveʻi ki he ngaahi founga ngaue lelei taha ‘oku toutou liliu mo e ngaahi pole ‘oku ha mai ‘i hono maluʻi ‘o e taha fakaha ‘a e taʻefaitotonu.

(2) Kuo pau ke kau ‘i he founga siviʻi ‘a e fetalanoaʻaki mo e kakai ke maʻu mai ‘a e ngaahi fokotuʻu mei he niʻihi felaveʻi, ‘o kau ai mo e kau fakaha ‘a e Kupu 13 Lao ki hono Malu'i 'o e Taha 'oku ne Fakaha 'a e Ta'efaitotonu 2025 Peesi 12 Lao Fika 32 ‘o e 2025 to taʻefaitotonu, kau maʻu mafai fakapuleʻanga, ngaahi kautaha sosaieti sivile, mo e kakai ‘o e fonua.

(3) Kuo pau ke teuteuʻi ‘e he kau maʻu mafai taau ha lipooti ‘o fakaikiiki ai ‘a e ngaahi ola mo e ngaahi fokotuʻu ki ha ngaahi fakatonutonu ki he Lao ni. Kuo pau ke fakahu ‘a e lipooti ki he Fale Alea ki hono vakaiʻi.

(4) Kuo pau ke vakaiʻi ‘e he Fale Alea ‘a e ngaahi fokotuʻu pea, ‘i hono fiemaʻu, fokotuʻu ‘a e ngaahi fakatonutonu ki he Lao ke fakalelei ‘a hono ngaahi ‘aonga pea fakapapauʻi ‘oku fakatatau ki he ngaahi tuʻunga fakavahaʻapuleʻanga mo e ngaahi founga ngaue lelei taha.
