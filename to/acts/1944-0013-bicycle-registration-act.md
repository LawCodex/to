# Lao ki he Lesisita 'o e Pasikala

KO E LAO KI HE LESISITA 'O E NGAAHI PASIKALA1 Kamata'anga [3 'o 'Aokosi, 1944]

#### 1 Hingoa nounou

‘E ui ‘a e Lao ni ki he ngaahi me‘a kotoa pe ko e Lao ki he Lesisita ‘o e Pasikala.

#### 2 Fakahinohino

‘I he Lao ni tuku kehe ‘o ka toki fakaha hano ‘uhinga kehe ‘amui —

“ko e pasikala” ko hono ‘uhinga ko ha saliote ‘oku ai hano me‘a ‘aka ‘oku fakateka ‘e he tangata;

“koe ‘alu‘anga faka-Pule‘anga” ko hono ‘uhinga ko ha hala, feitu‘u, halafakakavakava, uafau pe ‘alu‘anga kehe ‘oku ngaue fakalao ‘aki ‘e he kakai.

#### 3 Ko e Lesisita ‘o e Pasikala

(1) Ko ia kotoa pe ‘oku ‘a‘ana ha pasikala ‘oku pau ‘i he te‘eki te ne ngaue‘aki ia

‘i ha ‘alu‘anga faka-Pule‘anga ke fai ha tohi kole ke lesisita ‘i he sipinga ko ia

‘oku fakaha atu ‘i he Tepile I ‘i heni pea ke ne lesisita ‘a e pasikala ko ia ‘i he

‘ofisa polisi ‘o e vahefonua ‘oku ne nofo ai mo ne totongi ‘a e lesisita fakata‘u koe‘uhi ko ia ‘o hange ko ia ‘oku fokotu‘u ‘i he Tepile 2 ki heni.

Ka ‘oku pau ma‘u ai pe he ‘ikai ha me‘a ‘i he kupusi‘i ni ‘e pehe ke ta‘ofi‘aki

‘a e tokotaha ‘oku ‘a‘ana, pe ko ha taha kuo ne fakamafai, ‘i hano ‘ave ‘o e pasikalakoia‘ihahalapule‘angakihe‘ofisipolisikoe‘uhikefaihonolesisita.2 Peleti ‘o e fika

(2) ‘I he fai ‘o ha kole ke lesisita ha pasikala ‘e ha taha ‘oku ‘a‘ana ‘oku pau ke

‘ave ‘a e pasikala ko ia ki he ‘Ofisa Polisi ke sivi pea ‘i hono tali ke lesisita ‘oku pau ke fakapipiki ki he pasikala ko ia ‘e he ‘Ofisa Polisi ha peleti fika ‘o hange ko ia ‘etu‘utu‘uni ‘ehe‘Ofisa Polisi ‘oku pule pea ‘oku pau ke ‘atu ‘e he‘Ofisa Polisi Lahi ‘o e Vahefonua kiate ia ‘oku ‘a‘ana ‘a e pasikala ha tohi fakamo‘oni lesisita ‘i he sipinga hange ko ia ‘oku tu‘u i he Tepile 3 ‘o e Lao ni.3

(3) Ka ‘i ai ha taha te ne ta‘e fai ki he ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ‘o e kupu ni ‘oku pau ke ne mo‘ua ‘i hano fakamaau pea ‘ilo ‘oku mo‘oni ki he tautea pa‘anga ‘o ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $4.4

(4) Ka ‘i ai ha taha te ne —

(a) fai ha fakamatala ta‘e totonu ‘i he‘ene tohi kole; pe

(b) liliu ta‘e fakalao ha fakamatala fakaikiiki ‘i ha tohi fakamo‘oni lesisita; pe

(c) ngaue‘aki ha peleti fika ‘i ha pasikala kehe ‘o ‘ikai ko e pasikala ia na‘e

‘oatu ki ai, kuo pau ‘e halaia ia ‘i he maumau lao pea ‘oku pau ke ne mo‘ua ‘i hano fakamaau pea ‘ilo‘oku mo‘oni ki hatautea pa‘anga ‘o ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $30.5

#### 4 Heka totongi ta‘e tomu‘a Lesisita

Koiakotoapetene ‘oatuhapasikalakefai ai hahekatotongita‘etenetomu‘alesisita

‘a e pasikala ko e pasikala ki he heka totongi ‘oku pau ke ne mo‘ua ‘i hano fakamaau pea ‘ilo ‘oku mo‘oni ki ha tautea pa‘anga ‘o ‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $20.
