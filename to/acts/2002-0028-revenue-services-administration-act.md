## KONGA II - NGAAHI FAKAMATALA TUKUHAU

3 ‘Oatu ‘o e ngaahi fakamatala tukuhau

(1) ‘E ngofua ki he Minisitā´, ke ne fakahā ‘i ha tohi´, kapau kuo ‘ikai ke ‘omai ‘e ha taha ha fakamatala tukuhau fakatatau ki he ﬁema‘u ‘a ha lao ki he pa‘anga Lao ki hono Pule'i 'o e Ngaahi Ngaue Tanaki Pa'anga Hu Mai Peesi 12 Tohi Lao Fakatonutonu 2020 to hu mai, ke ne tu‘utu‘uni ki ha taha tukuhau ke ne ‘omai ‘a e fakamatala kimu‘a ‘i he ‘aho ‘oku hā ‘i he tohi´.

(2) ‘E ngofua ki he Minisitā´, ke ne fakahā ‘i ha tohi, kapau kuo ‘ikai ke ﬁemālie ki ha fakamatala tukuhau kuo ‘omai ‘e ha taha tukuhau´ ke ne tu‘utu‘uni ki he taha tukuhau ke ne ‘omai ha —

(a) fakamatala ‘oku kehe´ pe ha fakamatala ‘oku to e kakato ange´; pe

(b) toe fakamatala kehe ‘oku fakahā ‘i he tohi, kimu‘a ‘i he ‘aho ‘oku hā ‘i he tohi. 4 Fakaloloa ‘o e taimi ke ‘omai ‘a e fakamatala tukuhau

(1) ‘E ngofua ki ha taha tukuhau kuo ﬁema‘u ke ne ‘omai ha fakamatala tukuhau´ ke ne kole ‘i ha tohi ki he Minisitā´ ki ha fakaloloa ‘o e taimi pea kuo pau ke fai ‘a e kole ko ia kimu‘a ‘i he ‘aho na‘e ‘uluaki ﬁema‘u ki ai ‘a e fakamatala tukuhau ‘oku fekau‘aki mo e kole ko ia.

(2) ‘E ngofua ki he Minisitā, ‘i he‘ene ﬁemālie ‘oku ‘i ai ha ‘uhinga lelei, ke ne tali ha kole ‘i he kupu´ ni pea kuo pau ke ne fakahā ‘a ‘ene tu‘utu‘uni ko ia ki he tokotaha kole.

(3) Kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai laka ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 30 ha fakaloloa taimi ‘i he kupu´ ni tukukehe ka ‘oku ngofua ki he Minisitā ke ne foaki ha fakaloloa taimi laka ‘i he ‘aho ‘e 30 ha ‘uhinga mahu‘inga.

(4) Kuo pau ko ha fakaloloa taimi kuo foaki ‘e he kupu´ ni ‘e ‘ikai ke ne liliu ‘a e

‘aho ke totongi ha tukuhau ‘oku fekau‘aki mo e fakamatala tukuhau ko ia´. 5 Fakamatala tukuhau kuo fakahoko totonu Kuo pau ko ha fakamatala tukuhau ‘oku lau kuo fakahoko ‘e ha pe ma‘a ha taha tukuhau ke lau ia kuo fakahoko ‘e he taha tukuhau ko ia, pe ‘i ha‘ane fakamafai tukukehe ka ‘i ai ha fakamo‘oni ‘oku kehe mei ai.

## KONGA IV - NGAAHI LAUNGA MO E TANGI TUKUHAU

8 Launga

(1) Ko ha taha tukuhau ‘oku ‘ikai ﬁemālie mo ha tu‘utu‘uni tukuhau ‘e ngofua ke ne ‘ohake ha launga´ ki he tu‘utu‘uni mo e Minisitā ‘i loto ‘i he ‘aho 30 mei hono fakahā ko ia ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni.

(2) Ko ha launga ki ha tu‘utu‘uni tukuhau kuo pau ke ‘i he foomu kuo tu‘utu‘uni.

(3) ‘E ngofua ki ha taha tukuhau´ ‘oku ‘ikai ﬁemālie ki he tu‘utu‘uni tukuhau´ ke kole, ‘i he tohi, ki he Minisitā´ ke fakaloloa ‘a e taimi pea ko e tohi kole ko ia kuo pau ke fai ia kimu‘a he ‘aho faka‘osi ‘o e taimi ke ‘o hake ai ha launga ‘i he kupu´ ni.
