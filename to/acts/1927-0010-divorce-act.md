#### 1 Hingoa nounou

Ko e hingoa nounou ‘o e Lao ni ko e Lao Vete Mali.

#### 2 ‘Uhinga‘i lea1

‘I he Lao ni, tuke kehe ‘o ka fiema‘u ‘e he kupu ha ‘uhinga kehe —

“fanau ‘o e famili” ‘oku kau ai ha fanau mali pe fanau houmotu‘a pe ha fanau ohi ‘a e ongo fa‘ahi ki he mali pea mo ha to e fanau kehe ‘oku nau maheni ‘i he fakafalala ki he ongo fa‘ahi ‘o e mali ‘a ia —

(a) ‘oku nau ta‘u 18 pe si‘i hifo ‘i he ta‘u ko ia pea ‘oku fakafalala honau tauhi ‘i he ongo fa‘ahi ki he mali; pe2

(b) ‘oku laka hake honau ta‘u motu‘a ‘i he ta‘u 18 pea si‘i hifo honau ta‘u motu‘a ‘i he ta‘u 25 pea ‘oku nau lolotonga ako taimi kakato

‘i ha ‘univesiti, kolisi, ako pe ako‘anga kehe, pe ‘oku nau lolotonga ngaue koe‘uhi ko e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni pe ngaahi aleapau mo ha fakakaukau ke nau lava ‘o fakahoko ‘i ha ngaue pe ngaue fakapoto, pea ‘oku nau fakafalala ki he ongo fa‘ahi ki he mali ki honau tauhi; pe

(c) ‘oku laka hake honau ta‘u motu‘a ‘i he ta‘u 18 pea ‘oku nau fakafalala ki he ongo fa‘ahi ki he mali ki honau tauhi koe‘uhi ko ha ta‘emalava fakasino pe faka‘atamai.

#### 3 Ngaahi tu‘unga kole ke vete mali

(1) ‘Oku ngofua ki ha husepaniti pe uaifi ‘a ia ‘oku ne nofo fonua i he Pule‘anga ni ‘i he taimi ‘oku fai ai ‘a e hiki tikite ‘o e hopo vete, ke fai ha tohi kole ki he Fakamaau‘anga Lahi (kamata heni hono ui “ko e fakamaau‘anga”) ‘o kole ki he fakamaau‘anga ke vete ha mali ‘o ka fakamo‘oni‘i ‘aki ha fakamatala fakamo‘oni —

(c) ko e faka‘iloa na‘a ne li‘aki mo ta‘e tauhi ‘a e talatalaaki ki ha taimi hokohoko ‘o ta‘u ‘e 2 pe lahi hake ‘i mu‘a pe ‘i he tuku atu ‘o e tohi kole vete;3 pe

(d) ‘oku mo‘ua ‘a e faka‘iloa ‘i ha mahaki ‘oku ‘ikai lava ke toe faito‘o pea

‘oku lava ke mafola ki he talatalaaki ‘i he feohi fakasino pe pipihi; pe

#### 4 Ko e kaungā faka‘iloa ‘a ia na‘a na tono

Ka ‘i ai ha tono ‘oku fakaha ‘i ha tohi kole ne fai ‘e he faka‘iloa ‘oku ‘i he talatalaaki pe faka‘iloa ‘o hange ko ia ‘e hoko ke ‘omi ‘a ia na‘a na tono ko e kaungā faka‘iloa tuku kehe ‘o ka fekau ‘e he fakamaau‘anga ‘i ha ‘uhinga ta‘e fa‘a fai ke ‘oua na‘a fai pehē.

#### 5 Ko e lakanga ‘o e fakamaau‘anga ke ‘eke pe na‘e fai ha tokoni fakafufū

(1) Ka fai ha tohi vete mali ko e fatongia ‘o e fakamaau‘anga ke ne fai ha me‘a

‘oku ‘uhinga totonu pea ke fiemalie ki ai ‘i he ongo me‘a ni ke hangē ko e ngaahi fo‘i mo‘oni kuo fakaha pea koe‘uhi foki pe ne fai ha tokoni fakafufū ki he talatalaaki ke ne fai pe ta‘e tokanga ‘o hoko ai ‘a e hia pe fai pe ta‘e fai hano fakamolemole‘i ‘o e tono, pea faka‘eke‘eke pe ‘oku ‘i ai ha launga fakafihi‘i ‘e

‘i ai ki he talatalaaki.

(2) ‘I hono fakakaukau‘i pe kuo fakamolemole‘i ‘a e tono pe ‘ikai, kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai lau ha fo‘i taimi ‘e taha (‘o ‘ikai laka hake ‘i he mahina ‘e 3) hili ‘a hono ‘ilo

‘o e tono ‘e he talatalaaki pea lolotonga ia kuo to e fakataha ‘a e ongo fa‘ahi mo e fakakaukau ke na to e fakalelei.9

(3) Ka ‘ikai loto fiemalie ‘a e fakamaau‘anga ‘i he fakamatala fakamo‘oni kuo fai ha tono ‘ohange ko ia ‘oku fakaha ‘i hetohi kole pe ‘ilona‘efai ha‘ane tokoni‘i fakafufū ‘e he talatalaaki ‘i he lolotonga ‘o e nofo mali pe ta‘e tokanga ‘o hoko ai pe kuo fakamolemole‘i ‘a e hia ‘oku fai ai ‘a e launga, pe ‘oku fai ‘a e tohi kole ni pe faka‘ilo ko ha alea fakafufū mo ha taha ‘o e ongo fa‘ahi faka‘iloa, ‘e

‘ikai ke tali ‘e he fakamaau‘anga ‘a e tohi kole.

(4) Ka loto fiemalie ‘a e fakamaau‘anga ki he fakamatala fakamo‘oni ‘oku ne fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e tohi kole pea ‘oku ‘ikai ke ‘ilo na‘e fai ‘e he talatalaaki ‘i ha founga ha tokoni fakafufū pe ta‘e tokanga ‘o hoko ai pe kuo fakamolemole‘i ‘a e hia pe ‘oku fai ‘a e tohi kole ni pe faka‘ilo ko ha alea fakafufū mo ha taha ‘o e ongo fa‘ahi faka‘iloa, ‘e tali ‘e he fakamaau‘anga ‘o fai ha tu‘utu‘uni vete mali:

Ka he ‘ikai pehē ko ha me‘a pau ke fai ‘e he fakamaau‘anga ha tu‘utu‘uni vete mali ‘o kapau ‘e ‘ilo ‘oku halaia ‘a e talatalaaki ‘i he lolotonga ‘a e nofo mali ‘i ha tono, pe ‘oku pehē ‘e he fakakaukau ‘a e fakamaau‘anga ‘oku ne halaia —

(a) ‘i he tuai ta‘e ha ‘uhinga totonu hono fai ‘o e tohi kole pe faka‘ilo; pe

(b) ‘i he ngaohi kovi‘i ‘o e fa‘ahi ‘e taha ki he mali; pe

(c) ‘i he li‘aki ta‘e ha ‘uhinga totonu ke tauhi, pe hola mei ai ta‘e ha ‘uhinga totonu ‘i hono loto pe, ‘a e fa‘ahi ‘e taha ‘i he te‘eki ke fai ‘a e tono ‘oku fai ai ‘a e launga; pe

(d) ‘i he ‘ene fai ‘ilo ha ta‘e tokanga pe anga kovi ‘o hangē ko ia kuo tokoni ki he tono.

#### 6 Ka ha mai ki he fakamaau‘anga ‘oku ‘ikai fe‘unga ‘a e fakamo‘oni kuo

fai ‘oku totonu ke fakahaofi ‘a e kaungā faka‘iloa Ka fai ha hopo, ‘a ia ko e tu‘unga ‘i he tohi kole ‘a e husepaniti ke fai ha vete mali, ko ia ‘oku pehē na‘a na tono ‘e ‘omi ia ko e kaunga faka‘iloa pe ka ko e tohi kole ‘a e uaifi ke fai ha vete mali, ko e toko taha ko ia ‘oku pehē na‘a na tono mo e husepaniti

‘e ‘omi ia ko e faka‘iloa, ‘oku ngofua ki he fakamaau‘anga, ‘i he ‘osi hono fai ‘o e fakamatala fakamo‘oni kotoa pe ma‘a e talatalaaki, ke fakahaofi ‘a e kaungā faka‘iloa pe faka‘iloa ‘o hangē ko ia na‘e hoko, mei he hopo ko ia ‘o kapau kuo ha mai ki he fakamaau‘anga ‘oku ‘ikai fe‘unga ‘a e fakamo‘oni kuo fai ke fakahalaia‘i ‘aki ‘a e tangata pe fefine ko ia.

#### 7 ‘Oku ngofua ki he faka‘iloa ta‘e kole ke ma‘u ‘a e me‘a ‘oku totonu ke

ne ma‘u Kapau ko e hopo vete mali ‘oku fai pea ‘oku tali tonuhia ‘a e faka‘iloa ki he me‘a ‘oku

‘eke, ‘oku ngofua ki he fakamaau‘anga ke ‘atu ki he faka‘iloa ‘a e me‘a ‘oku ‘eke ‘a ia ‘oku totonu ke ne ma‘u ‘o hange pe ko ia na‘a ne fai ‘a e tohi kole ke ‘eke ‘a e me‘a ko ia.

#### 8 Fuofua hopo

Ko e ‘uluaki tu‘utu‘uni vete mali kotoa pe ‘e ui ia ko e tu‘utu‘uni vete fakataimi pea he ‘ikai ke fai ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni vete faka‘osi kae ‘oua ke ‘osi ‘a e uike ‘e 6 mei he hopo ko ia.

#### 9 Ngafa ‘o e ‘Ateni Seniale

Ko e ‘Ateni Seniale pe ha taha kuo ne fakanofo kuo pau ke ne kau ‘i he hopo vete mali kotoa pe.10

#### 10 ‘Oku ngofua ke fakaha ha me‘a ‘oku ‘aonga ki he ‘Ateni Seniale ‘i he

te‘eki ke fai ‘a e Hopo Faka‘osi

‘Oku ngofua ki ha taha ‘i ha taimi pe ‘i he lolotonga fai hono fakamaau‘i ‘o e hopo vete pe ‘i he te‘eki ke fai ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni vete faka‘osi ke fakaha ki he ‘Ateni-Seniale, pe ko e ‘ofisa pule ‘o e kau polisi ‘o e vahe fonua ko ia ‘oku fai ai ‘a e hopo, ha me‘a

‘aonga ki he tu‘utu‘uni ‘oku ‘amanaki fai ki he hopo ko ia.11

#### 11 ‘Oku ngofua ki he ‘Ateni Seniale ke launga ‘i he te‘eki ke fai ‘a e hopo

faka‘osi vete mali12

(1) Ka kuo foaki ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ha tu‘utu‘uni vete fakataimi ‘i he tu‘unga pe ‘o e mavae ta‘u ‘e 2 ‘o fakatatau ki he kupu 2(1)(f), kuo pau ‘e ‘ikai fai ha tu‘utu‘uni vete faka‘osi ‘o e tu‘utu‘uni ko ia kae‘oua kuo fakamo‘oni‘i ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga —

(a) kuo fai ha tokonaki fakapa‘anga totonu ki he ongo fa‘ahi takitaha pea mo ha fanau ‘o e famili ;pe

(b) ‘oku ‘ikai totonu ke fai ha tokonaki pehē.

(2) Kapau ‘e tupu ‘i ha fa‘ahinga fakamatala pehē pe kehe ‘a e ma‘u ai ‘e he ‘Ateni Seniale ha ‘uhinga ke tui ai kuo ‘i ai ha fa‘ahi ‘i he ngaahi fa‘ahi ki he tohi kole vete ‘oku na pe kuo na ngaue ‘i he felotoi ki he taumu‘a ke ma‘u ha tu‘utu‘uni

‘o maumau ai ‘a e totonu ‘o e fo‘i hopo pe kuo ‘i ai ha ni‘ihi ‘o e ngaahi fa‘ahi ko ia kuo ‘ikai ke nau‘omai ki he ‘ao‘o eFakamaau‘anga ha ngaahifakamatala totonu (material facts), ‘e ngofua hili hono ma‘u ‘a e ngofua ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga ke ne launga ‘i ha taimi pe, kimu‘a ke fai ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni vete faka‘osi, pea fakaha ha ‘uhinga ‘e ‘ikai ai totonu ke fai ha tu‘utu‘uni vete faka‘osi ‘o ha tu‘utu‘uni vete fakataimi pehē ‘i ha hopovete mali, pea ‘e ngofua ke hiki ha tohi fekau fakamo‘oni ‘a ha kau fakamo‘oni ke fakamo‘oni‘i ‘a e felotoi ‘oku tukuaki‘i pe ko hono ta‘ofi ‘o e ngaahi fakamatala totonu (material facts); pea ‘i he hoko ‘a ha me‘a pehē ‘e ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga —

(a) ke ne fai ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni vete faka‘osi; pe

(b) tamate‘i ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni vete fakataimi; pe

(c) tu‘utu‘uni ke toe fai ha faka‘eke‘eke; pe

(d) fai ‘a ha toe tu‘utu‘uni ki he hopo ‘o hangē ko ia ‘oku pehee ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘oku fe‘unga.

(3) Ka kuo foaki ha tu‘utu‘uni vete fakataimi pea kuo ‘ikai fai ha tohi kole ke fai ki ai ha tu‘utu‘uni vete faka‘osi, ‘e he fa‘ahi ‘a ia na‘e foaki ki ai ‘i he loto mahina ‘e 3 mei he ‘aho na‘e foaki ai ‘a e tu‘utu‘uni, pea ‘e toki ‘atā leva ‘i ha taimi pe, hili ia ki ha fa‘ahi na‘e ‘ikai ke foaki ki ai, ke ne fai ha tohi kole ki he Fakamaau‘anga, pea ‘i he funga ‘o e kole ko ia ‘e ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke ne ngaue‘aki ha ni‘ihi ‘o e ngaahi mafai ‘oku fakaha ‘i he kupusi‘i (2).

#### 12 Ko e me‘a ‘oku hoko ka fakahu ki tu‘a ‘a e tohi vete mali

‘I he hili ange pe hano fai ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘i ha faka‘osi hopo ke vete mali, ‘oku ngofua ki he ongo fa‘ahi ‘o e mali ko ia ke toe mali ‘o hangē pe na‘e fakamavae ‘a e mali ko ia ‘e he mate:

Ka he ‘ikai ngofua ki ha tangata ke mali mo ha tokoua fefine tamai taha fa‘e taha pe tamai taha fa‘e kehekehe pe fa‘e taha tamai kehekehe ‘o hono uaifi vete pe ‘o e uaifi kuo ne vete‘i ia ‘i he lolotonga ‘a e kei mo‘ui ‘a e uaifi pe ko e uaifi vete ‘o hono tokoua tangata tamai taha fa‘e taha pe tamai taha fa‘e kehekehe pe fa‘e taha tamai kehekehe pe ko e uaifi na‘a ne vete‘i ‘a honotokoua tangata tamai tahafa‘e taha tamai taha fa‘e kehekehe pe fa‘e taha tamai kehekehe ‘i he lolotonga ‘a e kei mo‘ui ‘a e tokoua ko ia kuo lau ki ai.

#### 13 ‘Oku ngofua ki he talatalaaki ke ‘eke ha pa‘anga huhu‘i

(1) ‘Oku ngofua ki he talatalaaki ‘o ha tohi kole vete mali ke ‘eke ha pa‘anga ‘o

‘ikai lahi hake ‘i he $1000 ko e pa‘anga huhu‘i mei ha taha ko e tu‘unga ‘i ha tono ‘a e toko taha ko ia mo e uaifi pe husepaniti ‘o e talatalaaki pe faka‘iloa ‘o hangē ko ia na‘e hoko.13

(2) ‘Oku ngofua ki he fakamaau‘anga ke tu‘utu‘uni ha founga ke ma‘u pe lava ai hano totongi ‘o e pa‘anga huhu‘i.

#### 14 Ka ‘ikai ke totongi ‘a e pa‘anga huhu‘i ‘oku ngofua ki he fakamaau‘anga

ke tu‘utu‘uni ke nofo popula Ka ‘i ai ha taha kuo halaia ‘i ha pa‘anga huhu‘i ‘o hangē ko ia ‘oku ha ‘i he kupu kuo toki hili, te ne ta‘e totongi ‘i hono taimi pe ‘i he founga na‘e tu‘utu‘uni ‘e he fakamaau‘anga, ‘e tatau ai pe pe kuo fakahu atu ha tohi fakatautuki pe ‘ikai ka ‘oku ngofua ki he fakamaau‘anga ke tu‘utu‘uni ‘a e toko taha ko ia ke nofo popula ‘o ‘ikai ke lahi hake ‘i he mahina ‘e 6 hangē ko ia te ne pehē ‘oku fe‘unga.

#### 15 Taki taha ma‘u ‘ene koloa

Ka ‘i ai ha tu‘utu‘uni vete mali kuo fai, ‘oku ‘i he fa‘ahi taki taha ‘o e mali ko ia ke taki taha ma‘u ‘ene koloa.

#### 16 Totongi Fakamaau‘anga

‘Oku ngofua ki he fakamaau‘anga ke fai ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘oku kau ki he pa‘anga totongi fakamaau‘anga ‘o hange ko ia ‘e ha mai ‘oku totonu, ka ‘e totongi ma‘u ai pe ‘e he fa‘ahi ‘oku vaivai ‘ene totongi fakamaau‘anga.

#### 17 Ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni fakataimi ki he tauhi

‘I ha tohi kole vete mali ‘oku ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke ne fai ha ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni fakataimi ‘a ia te ne pehee ‘oku totonu ki he totongi ‘o e tauhi ki ha fa‘ahi pea ki ha fanau ‘o e famili.14

#### 18 Ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni tauhi15

(1) ‘I hano foaki ‘o ha tu‘utu‘uni vete mali pe ‘i ha taimi hili ia (pe kimu‘a pe hili

‘a hono fai ‘o e tu‘utu‘uni vete faka‘osi), ‘oku ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga, ‘o kapau ‘oku ne pehee ‘oku fe‘unga ke ne fai ha taha pe lahi ange ‘o e ngaahi tu‘utu‘uni ko eni —

(a) ha fekau ‘o tu‘utu‘uni ki ha taha pe ‘o e ongo fa‘ahi ke ne ‘omai ki he tahakoē,peakefiemaliekiai‘aeFakamaau‘anga,hafo‘ipa‘anga(lump sum) pe pa‘anga fakata‘u koe‘uhi ko ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘a ia ‘e pehē ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘oku fakapotopoto ‘i hano fakakaukau‘i fekau‘aki mo ha‘ane koloa lahi (kapau ‘oku ‘i ai), hono ivi pea mo e anga ‘o e ngaahi fa‘ahi;

(b) ha fekau ‘o tu‘utu‘uni ki ha taha pe ‘i he ongo faha‘i ke ne ‘omai ki he tokotaha ko ē, ‘o fiemalie ki ai ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga, ha fo‘i pa‘anga pe pa‘anga fakata‘u koe‘uhi ko ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘a ia ‘e pehē ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘oku fakapotopoto;

(c) ha fekau ‘o tu‘utu‘uni ha fa‘ahi pe ke ne totongi ki he fa‘ahi ‘e taha ha fo‘i pa‘anga ko ia ‘e pehē ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ‘oku fakapotopoto.

(2) Ko ha fekau ‘e fai ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga ko e tauhi ‘o ha fa‘ahi pe ‘o e fanau ‘o e famili, ‘e ngofua, ‘i ha taimi pe, ‘i he funga ha kole, ke liliu, toloi pe tamate‘i.

(3) Ko ha fekau na‘e fai ki he tauhi ‘o ha fa‘ahi kuo pau ke ngata ia ‘i he toe mali

‘a e fa‘ahi ko ia.

#### 19 Tu‘utu‘uni ki he fānau ‘o e famili

‘Oku ngofua ki he Fakamaau‘anga ke fai ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘i ha hopo vete mali ‘o hangē ko ia ‘oku ha mai ‘oku totonu ‘o kau ki hono pule‘i, tauhi mo ako‘i ‘a e fānau ‘o e famili.16

#### 20 Fakalahi ‘o e mafai ‘i he ngaahi faka‘ilo ‘a e uaifi17

(1) Tuku kehe pea ‘ikai kau kovi ki ha mafai ‘oku ngaue‘aki ‘e he Fakamaau‘anga kehe mei he kupu ni, ‘oku ‘i ai ‘a e mafai ‘o e Fakamaau‘anga ke ne tali ha faka‘ilo ‘oku fai ‘e ha uaifi, neongo ‘oku ‘ikai nofo fonua ‘a e husepaniti ‘i he Pule‘anga ni, ‘i he hoko ha faka‘ilo ‘i he Lao ni kapau —

(a) ko e uaifi kuo li‘aki ‘e hono husepaniti; pe

(b) kuo fakafoki ‘a e husepaniti mei he Pule‘anga koe‘uhi ko ha lao ‘oku lolotonga ngaue‘aki kau ki he fakafoki, pea na‘e nofo fonua ‘i he Pule‘anga ‘a e husepaniti kimu‘a pe ‘i he li‘aki pe fakafoki;

(c) ‘oku nofo fonua ‘a e uaifi ‘i he Pule‘anga pea kuo maheni nofo fonua aipe ia ai ‘o fe‘unga mo e ta‘u ‘e 2 kimu‘a pe ‘i he kamata ‘o e hopo, pea

‘oku ‘ikai nofo fonua ‘a e husepaniti ‘i ha konga ‘o e Pule‘anga ni.

(2) ‘I ha hopo ‘oku ma‘u mafai ai ‘a e Fakamaau‘anga koe ‘uhi ko e kupusi‘i (1) kuo pau ke fakakaukau‘i mo fai tu‘utu‘uni ki he ngaahi me‘a ‘i he hopo ‘o fakatatau ki he lao ‘a ia ‘oku ngaue‘aki ki ai ‘o hangē pe na‘e nofo fonua ‘a e ongo fa‘ahi ‘i he Pule‘anga ni ‘i he taimi ‘o e hopo.

#### 21 Mama‘o ‘a e faka‘iloa mei he pule‘anga ni

Ka hoko ‘o ha mai ki he Fakamaau‘anga pea loto fiemalie ki ai ko e faka‘iloa ‘oku ne mama‘o mei he Pule‘anga ni pea ‘oku ‘ikai ha tu‘utu‘uni ‘e fai ki hano tufa ‘o e tohi koleki hefaka‘iloa,‘oku‘iheFakamaau‘anga‘okakuofakamo‘oni‘i ‘ehetalatalaaki

#### 22 Fakamo‘oni kuo tufa ‘a e Tohi Kole ‘i ha mama‘o ‘a e faka‘iloa

Tuku kehe ‘a ia ‘oku ha ‘i he kupu kuo toki hili, he ‘ikai ke fie fakamaau‘i ‘e he fakamaau‘anga ha fakamatala fakamo‘oni ko e mo‘oni ‘a e ngaahi tu‘unga ‘o e tohi kole ‘i he mama‘o ‘a e faka‘iloa pe kaungā faka‘ilo kae ‘oua kuo tomu‘a fakamo‘oni‘i ki he fakamaau‘anga ‘a e tufa ‘o e tohi kole ki he kau faka‘iloa.
