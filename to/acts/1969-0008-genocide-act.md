# Lao ki he Senosaite

KO E LAO KE FAKAHOKO ‘A HONO NGAUE‘AKI ‘A E KONIVESIO KI HE TA‘OFI MO E TAUTEA ‘O E HIA KO E SENOSAITE1 Kamata'anga [31 ‘o Ma‘asi, 1970]

#### 1 Hingoa nounou mo e faka‘uhinga lea

(1) ‘E ui ‘a e Laó ni ko e Lao ki he Senosaite.

(2) ‘I he Laó ni ko e “Konivesio ki he Senosaite” ko hono ‘uhinga ko e Konivesio ki he Ta‘ofi mo e Tautea ‘o e Hia ko e Senosaite ‘a ia na‘e loto ki ai ‘a e Fakataha Alea Fakakātoa ‘o e Kautaha Fakatahataha ‘a e Ngaahi Pule‘anga ‘o Māmaní ‘i he ‘aho 9 ‘o Tisema ‘o e ta‘u 1948.

#### 2 Senosaite

(1) ‘Oku fai ‘e ha taha ‘a e hia ko e senosaite, kapau te ne fai ha me‘a ‘a ia ‘e tō ‘i loto ‘i he faka‘uhinga‘i ‘o e “senosaité” ‘i he ‘Atikolo II ‘o e Konivesio ki he Senosaité ‘a ia ‘oku hā atu ‘i he Tēpile ki he Laó ni.2

(2) Ko ha taha kuo halaia koe‘uhí ko e hia ko e senosaité kuo pau ‘i ha‘ane mo‘ua

‘i he funga ‘o e tohi fekau hiamatea —

(a) kapau ko e hiá ko ‘ene tāmate‘i ha taha, ke tautea ia ko e mate pe tautea ia ke nofo popula ki he ngata‘anga ‘o ‘ene mo‘ui;

(b) ‘i ha tūkunga kehe ‘o e hia ko iá, ke lava ‘o tautea ia ke nofo pōpula ki ha vaha‘a taimi ‘a ia ‘e ‘ikai ke fuoloa ange ‘i he ta‘u ‘e 14.

(3) Kuo pau ‘e toki ngofua pē ke fai ha ngāue faka-fakamaau‘anga koe‘uhí ko e hia ko e senosaite ‘i ha fokotu‘u ke fai ‘a e ngāue ko iá ‘e he pe ‘i he loto ki ai ‘a e Fakahinohino Lao Seniale ‘a e Pule‘angá.
