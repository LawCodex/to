# Tongan Internet Corporation Register (Fees) Regulations 2000

## SCHEDULE

1. For an application and registration of a Tongan Internet Corporation US$600.00

2. For a certificate of incorporation US$100.00

3. For annual renewal of registration US$300.00

4. For an application to register any charges affecting a Tongan Internet Corporation US$100.00

5. For certification of a copy of or extract from any document US$100.00

6. For an application for the restoration of a Tongan Internet Corporation to the register US$400.00

7. For an application to reserve a name for 12 months US$100.00
