# Passport Regulations

Made under section 34 of the Passport Act1 Commencement [1st July 2014]

#### 1 Short title

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Passport Regulations.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on the same date as the Passport (Amendment) Act 2003.2

#### 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires —

“Minister” means the Minister of Foreign Affairs.

#### 3 Applications

(1) An application for a passport or extension thereof or certificate of identity shall be made to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Kingdom’s Diplomatic Missions overseas or authorised agents of the Government, who have been so authorised in writing by the Minister to perform such functions.

(2) Every application for a passport shall be made in accordance with Form 1 in Schedule I of these Regulations.

(3) Every application for an extension for a passport shall be made in accordance with Form 2 in Schedule I of these Regulations.

(4) Every application for a certificate of identity shall be made in accordance with Form 3 or 4 in Schedule I of these Regulations.

(5) Any application for an endorsement or observation made in a passport shall be made in accordance with Form 5 in Schedule I of these Regulations.

(6) An application made for a person under the age of 16 years shall be made with the consent of a parent or a legal guardian.

#### 4 Application requirements

Application for a passport in Form 1 of Schedule I of these Regulations or certificate of identity in Form 3 of Schedule I of these Regulations shall include —

(a) a completed and signed application form as prescribed by regulation 3;

(b) original or certified copy of —

(i) birth certificate;

(ii) previous passport if not lost or stolen;

(iii) Certificate of Naturalisation if applicable to the applicant; or

(iv) Certificate of Re-admission if applicable to the applicant;

(c) two recent photographs of the applicant, endorsed with the words “I certify this is a true likeness of (applicant’s name)” on the reverse of the photograph and signed by any of the following who has known the applicant for more than 12 months —

(i) Minister of religion;

(ii) law practitioner;

(iii) Magistrate;

(iv) Commissioner for Oaths or equivalent in the country of application;

(v) public servant or public officer;

(vi) medical practitioner; or

(vii) a Tongan subject who is 18 years of age or over, is not related to the applicant or resident in the same address as the applicant and who currently holds a valid Tongan passport;

(d) the prescribed fee; and

(e) any other document that the Minister deems necessary.

#### 5 Certificate of identity

(1) Where any person who does not hold a passport needs to urgently travel into the Kingdom, he shall —

(a) make an application for a certificate of identity in accordance with Form 4 of Schedule I of these Regulations;

(b) include with the application the fees for certificate of identity in lieu of passport fees as prescribed in Schedule II of these Regulations; and

(c) only be allowed to use the certificate of identity issued under this regulation for one journey into the Kingdom.

(2) A certificate of identity shall only be issued to a person who previously held a passport.

#### 6 Endorsements or observation

An application for an endorsement or observation in any passport shall include —

(a) a completed and signed Form 5 as prescribed in Schedule I of these Regulations;

(b) the prescribed fee; and

(c) any other document that the Minister deems necessary.

#### 7 Processing time

(1) All applications for issuance of a passport, certificate of identity or endorsement or observation shall be dealt with as soon as may be practicable.

(2) An applicant who requests a passport, certificate of identity or endorsement or observation to be issued in less than 4 working days, shall pay the emergency fee in addition to any other applicable fee prescribed in Schedule II of these Regulations.

#### 8 Fees

(1) The fees shall be as prescribed in Schedule II of these Regulations.

(2) All fees prescribed in Schedule II of these Regulations are non-refundable.
