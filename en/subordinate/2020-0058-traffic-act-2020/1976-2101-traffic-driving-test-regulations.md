#### 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Traffic (Driving Test) Regulations.

#### 2 Interpretation

The definitions in the Traffic Act2 and the Traffic Regulations3 , shall apply equally to these Regulations and, unless the context otherwise requires: —

“Act” means the Traffic Act;

“Examining Officer” means a person appointed by the Principal Licensing Authority to be an Examining Officer.

#### 3 Forms – First Schedule

For the purposes of the Act and these Regulations, the forms set out in the First Schedule of forms to the like effect shall be used in cases to which they refer.

#### 4 Standard driving test

(i) The standard driving test will consist of two parts, namely—

(a) theoretical test; and

(b) practical test.

(ii) Both tests shall be conducted by an Examining Officer.

#### 5 Applications for tests

All applications for driving tests shall be made in duplicate, on Form I of the First Schedule to these Regulations, and shall be submitted to an Examining Officer who shall appoint a date, time and place for such test.

#### 6 Examiner to satisfy himself

The Examining Officer shall satisfy himself that the applicant qualifies for the issue of a driving licence in terms of Section 19 of the Act.

#### 7 Testing of sight

(a) The Examining Officer shall test the applicant's sight by requesting him to read (with spectacles, if normally worn) the number plate of another motor vehicle at a distance of not less than 25 metres. Failure to distinguish the letter and figures thereon immediately shall disqualify the applicant.

(b) No person who has lost the sight of either eye shall be permitted to undergo a road test for the duration of the disability.

#### 8 Testing of hearing

The Examining Officer shall satisfy himself that the applicant has normal hearing.

Where any doubt exists the applicant shall be requested to take a professional aural test to determine his fitness to drive.

#### 9 Testing of knowledge

The Examining Officer shall test the applicant on his knowledge of all aspects of the control of a motor vehicle, the rules of the road and the recognition of traffic signs and signals, all in accordance with the Questionnaire shown on the Second Schedule to these Regulations. Failure to answer the questions correctly shall disqualify the applicant.

#### 10 Vehicle for road test

The Examining Officer shall satisfy himself that the vehicle being used for the road test is currently licensed and in a roadworthy condition, particularly with regard to steering, brakes, horn and wiper and that the “L” plates are affixed as laid down by the Act:

Provided that where it is found to be deficient in any mechanical particular it shall be subject to the provisions of Regulation 10(2) of the Traffic Regulations.

#### 11 Ability to drive

The Examining Officer shall test the applicant's ability to drive a motor vehicle on a road in accordance with the Road Test Programme shown on the Third Schedule to these Regulations. Failure to execute any part of the programme in the approved manner shall disqualify the applicant.

#### 12 Retesting

Where an applicant is disqualified for any reason he may apply to be retested not earlier than one month from the date of his previous test.

#### 13 Practical test

Practical tests shall be carried out on a non automatic motor vehicle.

#### 14 Heavy goods vehicle

Any person applying for a licence to drive a heavy goods vehicle, a public service vehicle or heavy plant must previously have held a valid driving licence for another class of motor vehicle for a period of not less than 6 months.

#### 15 Public service vehicle

Any person who wishes to obtain a driving licence for a public service vehicle will be required to satisfy the Examining Officer that he has a thorough knowledge of the regulations governing public service vehicles as laid down in the Traffic Regulations and of the passenger bus and taxi tariffs as laid down by the Competent Authority.

#### 16 Completion of driving test

The successful completion of the driving test shall qualify the applicant to make application for a driving licence in accordance with the provision of the Act.

1. Q. When may a motor vehicle be driven on a road without registration plates or licence? A. When being taken to —

(a) an Examining Officer for examination;

(b) a Licensing Officer for Licensing;

(c) a place other than a road, for use there;

(d) a garage for repair.

2. Q. Is a person under the age of 18 years permitted to be the registered owner of a motor vehicle? A. No.

3. Q. What does the Law state regard to fixing of registration plates on motor vehicles? A.

(a) Except for motor-cycles which require only one plate all motor-vehicles must have one plate affixed to both front and rear.

(b) Plates must be easily distinguishable and not obscured in any way.

4. Q. Is a person permitted to drive a motor-vehicle on a road at any time without a valid driving licence? A. No.

#### 5 Q. What conditions apply to a learner-permit?

A.

(a) Experienced driver with, valid licence, to accompany learner — driver at all times.

(b) “L” plates to be displayed at both front and rear of the motor vehicle.

(c) No passengers to be carried at any time.

(d) Learner-driver must be 18 years of age or over.

6. Q. Is the experienced driver liable for any offences committed by the learner- driver? A. Yes.
