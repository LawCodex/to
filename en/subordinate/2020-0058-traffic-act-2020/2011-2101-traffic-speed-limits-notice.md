# Traffic (Speed Limits) Notice

Traffic (Speed Limits) Notice CAP 24.03.05 Notice 1 to 2020 Revised Edition Page 3 C T TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) NOTICE 1 Made under section 22 of the Traffic Act Commencement [7th December 2011] 1 The Minister of Police hereby designates that the speed limit for:

(a) non built up and non congested areas shall be 70 kilometres per hour;

(b) built up and congested areas shall be 50 kilometres per hour; and

(c) very congested areas at specific times shall be 30 kilometres per hour.:

Provided that the speed limit is not otherwise provided for by speed limit sign. 2 For the purposes of this notice:

“built up and congested areas” include but is not limited to within town boundaries and village boundaries; and

“very congested areas” include but is not limited to schools, central business districts and markets.
