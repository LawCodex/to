# Rent of Leases to Tongans Regulations

RENT OF LEASES TO TONGANS REGULATION1 Made by Her Majesty in Council on 19th September, 1930

1. This regulation may be cited as the Rent of Leases to Tongans Regulations.

2. The rent of any lease issued under the provisions of Part V of the Act shall not exceed 40 seniti per acre per annum.

3. The provisions of this regulation apply only to the leases of lands the property of the Crown, Nobles and Matapule ma'u Tofi'a.
