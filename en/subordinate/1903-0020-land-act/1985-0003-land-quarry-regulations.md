# Land (Quarry) Regulations

Made by His Majesty in Council1

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Land (Quarry) Regulations.

2. No person shall allow his tax allotment to be used as a quarry.

3. The quarrying on and removal from a tax allotment of stone of any description is hereby prohibited.

4. Any person who offends against these regulations shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding $100
