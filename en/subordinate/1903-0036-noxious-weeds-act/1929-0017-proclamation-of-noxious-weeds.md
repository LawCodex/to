# Proclamation of Noxious Weeds

Commencement[26th August 1926] Name of Plant Area to which the proclamation extends

1. (Cactus) (Cactaceae)1 The Kingdom of Tonga

2. Johnson Grass, commonly known as Hana or Kola2 All land and islands in the District of Ha‘apai and Vava‘u

3. Johnson Grass known also as Hana and Kola3 Niuatoputapu and Tafahi

4. Blue rat tail (Stachytarpheta Indica) or Fisi Lanumata.4 The whole of Tonga

5. Prickly Solanum (Solanum Torvum) commonly known as Tisaipale.5 The whole of Tonga
