# Cabinet (Restriction on Removal of Cash from the Kingdom) Order

Cabinet (Restriction on Removal of Cash from the Kingdom) Order CAP. 20.06.3 Regulation 1 to 2016 Revised Edition Page 5 C T CABINET (RESTRICTION ON REMOVAL OF CASH FROM THE KINGDOM) ORDER Made under Regulation 3 of the Emergency Powers (Maintenance of Public Order) Regulations 2006 by Cabinet1 Commencement [22nd November, 2006]

#### 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Cabinet (Restriction on Removal of Cash from the Kingdom) Order.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires —

“Customs officer” means any officer employed by the Customs Division of the Revenue Services Department;

“Governor” means the Governor of the National Reserve Bank of Tonga;

“Minister” means the Minister of Finance;

“remove” means any attempt to cross any controlled exit point, including Immigration Division and Customs Division posts, and airline entry control for persons departing on an aeroplane or other craft, whilst carrying restricted cash (or causing such cash to be removed in a suitcase or other baggage or storage that belongs to the traveller or any other person);

“restricted cash” means any cash exceeding the amount of $10,000 pa’anga, or cash in foreign currency of any country or countries in any combination that exceeds the value of $10,000 pa’anga.

#### 3 Purpose of Order

This Order is issued for the purposes of protecting the domestic economy from the threat of a severe cash shortage should substantial amounts of cash be removed from the country following the outbreak of public disorder on 16 November 2006.

#### 4 Permission required for removal of restricted cash

No person shall attempt to remove any restricted cash from the Kingdom, except with the written permission of the Governor.

#### 5 Minister to advise travellers

The Minister shall advise, in the appropriate manner, all persons intending to travel out of the Kingdom, of the restriction on the removal of cash prescribed under this Order.

#### 6 Declaration

Any person intending to travel out of the Kingdom shall complete and sign a declaration in the form prescribed in Schedule 1 to confirm whether he is carrying any restricted cash or not.

#### 7 Attempt to remove restricted cash

(1) Any person who attempts to remove restricted cash, or makes a false declaration, shall be temporarily held by a Customs officer for the purposes of this Order.

(2) A Customs officer shall perform the following actions in relation to any person temporarily held under subclause (1) —

(a) advise the person that the restricted cash will be confiscated and may be forfeited;

(b) seize and confiscate the restricted cash;

(c) note the name, address and other details of the person who was carrying the restricted cash;

(d) give the person a receipt and notice that he may apply in writing to the Minister for the return of the restricted cash, and shall supply evidence Cabinet (Restriction on Removal of Cash from the Kingdom) Order CAP. 20.06.3 Regulation 8 to 2016 Revised Edition Page 7 to the satisfaction of the Minister as to why the amount exceeded the stipulated maximum or why a false declaration was made;

(e) secure the restricted cash in a safe place; and

(f) as soon as possible, transfer the restricted cash to the custody of the Ministry of Finance.

#### 8 Forfeiture of restricted cash

The restricted cash may be forfeited to the Crown, unless the person who had such cash confiscated satisfies the Minister in writing within 4 weeks of the confiscation, that it is in the interests of fairness and justice that all or part of the restricted cash should be returned.

#### 9 Partial forfeiture

Notwithstanding any decision by the Minister to return restricted cash, the Minister may also decide that a portion of the restricted cash shall be forfeited to the Crown to cover the administrative cost of the confiscation and the application for return.
