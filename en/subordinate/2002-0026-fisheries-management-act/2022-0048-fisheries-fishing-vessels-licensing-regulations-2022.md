## PART II– LOCALLY BASED FOREIGN FISHING VESSELS

#### 4 Application for a locally based foreign fishing vessel licence

(1) An application for the issuance or renewal of a locally based foreign fishing vessel licence under section 31 of the Act shall be set out in Form 1 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(2) Where an application is received under sub-regulation (1), the Ministry shall process the application within 2 months of receiving the application.

(3) The operator of the locally based foreign fishing vessel shall present the vessel at a port so designated by the Chief Executive Officer for a full pre-fishing inspection and pre-fishing briefing of the terms and conditions for its fishing license, prior to being issued its license to fish in the fisheries waters.

(4) As part of the pre-fishing inspection required under sub-regulation (2), the operator shall be required to ensure that the vessel is seaworthy and complies with the safety standards applicable under the Shipping Act [Cap.22.09].

#### 5 Determination of application

The Chief Executive Officer shall have regard to the following matters in determining whether or not to grant a locally based foreign fishing vessel licence – Fisheries (Fishing Vessels) Licensing Regulations 2022 6 to Page 9

(a) the capacity of Tonga to ensure the effective implementation of international conservation and management measures relevant to that vessel;

(b) the previous offending history, if any, in relation to fishing or related activity of the vessel, operator and crew; and

(c) any other matter the Chief Executive Officer considers relevant.

#### 6 Licence

A locally based foreign fishing vessel licence shall be set out in Form 2 and accompanied by the prescribed fee.

## PART III – FOREIGN FISHING VESSELS

#### 7 Application for a foreign fishing vessel licence

(1) An application for the issuance or renewal of a foreign fishing vessel licence under section 38 of the Act shall be set out in Form 3 and accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(2) Where an application is received under sub-regulation (1), the Ministry shall process the application within 2 months of receiving the application.

(3) The operator of the foreign fishing vessel shall present the vessel at a port so designated by the Chief Executive Officer for a full pre-fishing inspection and pre-fishing briefing of the terms and conditions for its fishing license, prior to being issued its license to fish in the fisheries waters.

(4) As part of the pre-fishing inspection required under sub-regulation (3), the operator shall be required to ensure that the vessel is seaworthy and complies with the safety standards applicable under the Shipping Act [Cap.22.09].

#### 8 Determination of application

The Minister shall consider the following matters in determining whether or not to grant a foreign fishing vessel licence- Regulation 9 Fisheries (Fishing Vessels) Licensing Regulations 2022 Page 10 to

(b) the previous offending history, if any, in relation to fishing or related activity of the vessel, operator and crew; and

(c) any other matter the Minister considers relevant.

#### 9 Licence

A foreign fishing vessel licence shall be set out in Form 4 and accompanied by the prescribed fee.

#### 10 Conditions

In addition to any other applicable conditions that may be imposed from time to time, a locally based foreign fishing vessel licence or a foreign fishing vessel licence shall be subject to the following conditions –

(a) the vessel shall only be usedfor such fishing and duringsuch periods and in such areas as are specified in the licence;

(b) the vessel shall not fish within the territorial waters, special management areas or any other Closed Areas as notified by the Chief Executive Officer;

(c) no fish may be transhipped except with the written authorisation of the Chief Executive Officer;

(d) the operator shall ensure that the automatic location communicator is switched on and must be reporting normally, consistently and automatically to the Regional Vessel Monitoring System at all times;

(e) the vessel shall at all times in the fisheries waters -

(i) fly the flag of the State in which it is registered;

(ii) display identification markings in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule 3; and

(iii) display lights and shapes for the vessel and activity in which it is engaged in compliance with the requirements of the Convention on the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea 1972 (COLREG 1972) and the International Conference on Revision of the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea 1990 (1990 Edition);

## PART IV – HIGH SEAS PERMIT

#### 12 Application for a High Seas Permit

An application for the issuance or renewal of a high seas fishing vessel permit under section 48 of the Act shall be in the form set out in Form 5 and accompanied by the prescribed fee.

#### 13 Determination of application

In addition to the grounds set out in section 49 of the Act, the Chief Executive Officer shall consider the following matters in determining whether or not to grant a high seas fishing vessel permit –

(a) the capacity of Tonga to ensure the effective implementation of international conservation and management measures relevant to that vessel;

(b) the previous offending history, if any, in relation to fishing or related activity of the vessel, operator and crew; and

(c) any other matter the Chief Executive Officer considers relevant.

#### 14 Permit

A high seas fishing vessel permit shall be in the form set out in Form 6 and accompanied by the prescribed fee.

#### 15 Conditions

In addition to the requirements under section 50 of the Act, the Chief Executive Officer may attach such other conditions including, but not limited to, the following –

(a) the operator shall ensure that the automatic location communicator is switched on and must be reporting normally, consistently and automatically to the Regional Vessel Monitoring System at all times;

(b) Ensure that its vessels comply with the reporting requirements on its catch; and

(c) the master and each member of the crew of the vessel shall allow or provide all reasonable assistance in his power to enable a high seas fisheries inspector to exercise their duties in accordance with the Act.

## PART V – AUTHORISATIONS

#### 17 Application for authorisation

(1) An application for the issuance or renewal of an authorisation under section 32 of the Act to conduct fishery scientific research, test fishing or surveys shall -

(a) be set out in Form 7, accompanied by the prescribed fee and submitted to the Minister;

(b) be submitted with a satisfactory scientific research or test fishing operation or survey plan; and

(c) contain information or be submitted with such information as may be required under the Act and such other information as the Minister may require.

(2) The scientific research, test fishing operation or survey plan shall specify –

(a) theobjectiveofthescientificresearchortest fishingoperationorsurvey;

(b) the activities of the scientific research;

(c) in respect of a test fishing operation or survey, the fishing gear or fishing method that shall be used and the target species for such test fishing operation or survey;

(d) the area proposed for conducting scientific research or test fishing operation or survey;

(e) the period of the proposed scientific research or test fishing operation or survey;

(f) an impact assessment of the scientific research or test fishing operation or survey for which the application is made; and

(g) such other matters as the Minister may specify.

#### 20 Transhipment

The operator of a vessel licensed under these Regulations to fish in the fishery waters or a vessel in respect of which a high seas fishing permit has been issued shall -

(a) not conduct transhipment except with the written authorisation of the Chief Executive Officer;

(b) only tranship at the time and port authorised by the Chief Executive Officer for transhipment and under such terms and conditions as the Chief Executive Officer so stipulates;

(c) provide 72 hours’ notice of a request to tranship any or all of the fish on board and shall provide thename of the vessel, its international radio call sign, position, the catch on board by weight and by species, the time and place where such transhipment is requested to occur and an undertaking to pay all prescribed fees; and

(d) submit full transhipment reports to the Chief Executive Officer on any forms which may be prescribed or are otherwise required.

## PART VI - MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

#### 21 Notification of changes

Any change in the information set out in the application form shall be notified to the Chief Executive Officer no later than 5 working days from the date of the change.

#### 22 Period of validity of licences and authorisations

Unless sooner cancelled or suspended, all licences, permits or authorization issued under these regulations shall remain valid for the periods specified not more than 12 months.

#### 23 Renewal of licences

(1) Any holder of a license or authorisation issued by the Minister under these Regulations may apply to the Minister to renew the term of such licence or authorisation.

(2) Any holder of a license or permit issued by the Chief Executive Officer under these Regulations may apply to the Chief Executive Officer to renew the terms of such license or permit.

(3) Every application for renewal in both sub regulations (1) and (2) above shall be made within 28 days prior to the expiration of each license, authorisation or permit in the relevant forms in Schedule 2. Each application must be addressed to the Chief Executive Officer and shall contain such information required under the Act or these regulations or such other information as may be requested.

(4) In considering the renewal of licence, authorisation or permit the Minister or Chief Executive Officer where applicable, shall take into account any grounds set out in these Regulations.

#### 24 Transferability

No licence issued in respect of any fishing vessel under these Regulations shall be transferable to any other vessel except with the written permission of the Chief Executive Officer or, in the case of a foreign fishing licence, the Minister.

#### 25 Information to be true, complete and correct

(1) Any information required to be recorded, notified, communicated or reported pursuant to any requirement of these regulations shall be true, complete and correct.

(2) Any change in circumstances which has the effect of rendering any such informationfalse,incompleteormisleadingshallbenotifiedimmediatelytothe appropriate person, authority or body.

#### 26 General Penalty for breach of regulations

A person who contravenes any provision of these regulations or acts in contravention of any licence, permit or authorisation granted pursuant to these regulations commits an offence and, unless otherwise provided in these regulations or in the Act, shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $250,000.

#### 27 Savings

All licences, permits or authorisations issued under the Act, or regulations before the commencement of these regulations shall, except so far as they are inconsistent with the Act or these regulations, continue to have effect as though made or given pursuant to the Act or these regulations.

VESSEL IDENTIFICATION MARKINGS SUMMARY OF THE FAO STANDARD SPECIFICATIONS FOR THE MARKING AND IDENTIFICATION OF FISHING VESSELS Introduction The specifications below were endorsed by the Eighteenth Session of the FAO Committee on Fisheries, Rome April 1989, for adoption by States as a standard system to identify fishing vessels operating, or likely to operate in waters of States other than those of the flag State.

1. Basic System 1.1 Vessels shall be marked with their International Telecommunications Union

(ITU) Radio Call Signs (IRCS). 1.2 Vessels not provided with an IRCS shall be marked with the characters allocated by the ITU to the flag State and followed by the license or registration number assigned by the flag State. In such cases a hyphen shall separate the ITU characters and the assigned number. 1.3 Apart from the vessel's name or identification and port of registry the marking system specified shall be the only other vessel identification mark consisting of letters and numbers painted on the hull or superstructure.

2. Application 2.1 The markings shall be prominently displayed at all times:

(i) on the vessel's side or superstructure, port and starboard;

(ii) on a deck; Should an awning or other temporary cover obscure the mark, the awning or cover shall also be marked. Deck marks shall be placed athwart ships with the top of the numbers or letters towards the bow. 2.2 Marks shall be placed as high as possible above the water line. The flare of the bow and the stern shall be avoided. 2.3 The marks shall:

(i) be placed so that they are not obscured by the fishing gear whether stowed or in use;

(ii) be clear of the flow from scuppers and overboard discharges including areas which might be damaged or discoloured from the catch of certain species;

(iii) not extend below the waterline.
