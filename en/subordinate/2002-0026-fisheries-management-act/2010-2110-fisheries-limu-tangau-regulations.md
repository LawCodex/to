# Fisheries (Limu Tanga’u) Regulations

FISHERIES (LIMU TANGA'U) REGULATIONS Made under section 101 of the Fisheries Management Act1 Commencement [26 January 2010]

#### 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Fisheries (Limu Tanga’u) Regulations.

#### 2 Interpretation

In these regulations:

“Act” means the Fisheries Management Act;

“collectors” means persons who collect limu tanga’u from the sea for the purpose of sale;

“Committee” means the Limu tanga’u Management Committee;

“form” means a form in Schedule II;

“HACCP” means the internationally recognised process of Hazard Assessment at Critical Control Point;

“limu tanga’u” means the brown algae Cladosiphon sp;

“Plan” means the plan approved by the Minister under section 7 of the Act;

“Ministry” means the Ministry of Fisheries; and

“sea grass” includes all forms of herbaceous flowering plants that live in inter-tidal and sub-tidal waters.

#### 3 Establishment

The Limu tanga’u Management Committee is hereby established.

#### 4 Appointment of members

Members of the Committee shall be appointed for a two-year term and may be re- appointed for a second or subsequent term.

#### 5 Membership

(1) Membership of the Committee shall consist of:

(a) the Secretary or his delegate (as Chairman);

(b) a representative from the limu tanga’u processing sector;

(d) a representative from a designated coastal community that has involvement in the harvest of limu tanga’u;

(e) an officer of the Ministry of Labour, Commerce and Industries;

(f) an officer of the Department of Environment; and

(g) the secretariat shall be provided by the Ministry.

(2) The Chairman may co-opt any person to observe or advise.

(3) The Committee shall determine its own operating procedures.

#### 6 Quorum

The quorum shall be 4 members including the chairman.

#### 7 Responsibilities

The Committee shall be required to:

(a) review the Plan every year;

(b) make recommendations to improve management arrangements for the industry;

(c) set priorities for gathering information;

(d) review and comment on any application to process and export on the basis of the criteria provided in these regulations;

(e) review progress on codes of practice for processing and export;

(f) set standards for the annual training programme for collectors referred to in regulation 20 that is to be implemented by the Ministry;

(g) advise on inter-agency issues involving the industry; and

(h) undertake any other matters requested by the Ministry.

#### 8 Remuneration

Members of the Committee shall be remunerated and reasonable travel costs shall be met by the Ministry in accordance with current government policy.

#### 9 Application

(1) An application for a fish processing establishment licence under section 33 of the Act to process limu tanga’u shall be in the form set out in Form 1 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(2) The Secretary, in considering the application, shall take into account the following –

(a) the applicant’s capacity to:

(i) meet the objectives of the Plan;

(ii) develop and implement a HACCP programme for all phases of the proposal for collection, processing and export of limu tanga’u;

(iii) undertake secondary processing and value adding of limu tanga’u in the Kingdom;

(iv) help develop and work within a national code of conduct for national standards on quality control of limu tanga’u produced in, and exported from, the Kingdom; and

(v) assist in implementing an annual training programme.

(a) availability of a suitable processing plant, freezing plant and holding capacity to process all limu tanga’u supplied by collectors to a state fit for storage and export on the basis of the implemented HACCP programme;

(b) the applicant’s past history of compliance to the plan, fisheries legislation and licence conditions; and

(c) the potential impact on the national economic production and total employment levels of issuing further licences.

#### 10 Licence to process

A licence to process limu tanga’u shall be issued in the form set out in Form 2 upon payment of the prescribed fee.

#### 11 General conditions

Every licence to process limu tanga’u shall, in addition to conditions endorsed on the licence, be subject to the following:

(a) adherence to the procedures and process required under HACCP standards and any national code of practice required for quality standards of limu tanga’u;

(b) that the processor only purchase limu tanga’u from collectors that have been authorised by the Ministry;

(c) that the processor only purchase limu tanga’u during the harvesting times established by the Ministry for any of the locations noted in Form 4; and

(d) provision of monthly returns during the months of August to and including February, and in any months outside this period when they purchased limu tanga’u, giving details of quantities purchased, employment levels and harvest locations for all limu tanga’u purchased on Form 5.

#### 12 Licence to process not transferable

No person or company that holds a licence to process limu tanga’u may transfer that licence to another person or company.

#### 13 Renewal

An application to renew a fish processing establishment licence to process limu tanga’u issued under section 33 of the Act shall be in the form set out in Form 6 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

#### 14 Application for licence to export

An application for a licence to export limu tanga’u or renewal thereof under section 35 of the Act shall be in the form set out in Form 3 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

#### 15 Licence to export

A licence to export limu tanga’u shall be issued in the form set out in Form 4 upon payment of the prescribed fee.

#### 16 Licence to export not transferable

No person or company licensed to export limu tanga’u may transfer that licence to another person or company.

#### 17 Authorisation of collectors

(1) No person may collect and sell limu tanga’u except with a written authorisation from the Secretary and in accordance with such conditions as he may specify.

(2) Such authorisation shall be provided each year only after a person has completed the annual training programme that is implemented by the Ministry.

(3) Any person who contravenes sub-regulation (1) shall be liable upon conviction to the fine provided in section 102 of the Act.

#### 18 General conditions for collectors

(1) Any authorisation to collect and sell limu tanga’u shall be subject to the following conditions:

(a) prohibition on collection in any location until the Ministry has allowed harvesting;

(b) prohibition on sale of limu tanga’u to an unlicensed processor; and

(c) prohibition on the use of a rake to collect limu tanga’u or other collection methods that result in the destruction of seagrass.

(2) Any person who contravenes sub-regulation (1) shall be liable upon conviction to the fine provided in section 102 of the Act

#### 19 Annual review of seagrass distribution

The Ministry shall undertake an annual review of seagrass distribution in collection areas through the use of satellite imagery and supplementary field assessments, and report its findings to the Committee.

#### 20 Annual training programme

The Ministry shall:

(a) implement an annual training programme on habitat protection, appropriate collection procedures, quality control and limu tanga’u marketing issues for all collectors;

(b) include in training correct collection techniques, seagrass conservation, handling and cleaning techniques and marketing requirements; and

(c) develop and implement training to the standards required by the Committee.

#### 21 Appropriate times to harvest

The Ministry shall monitor the growth of limu tanga’u and inform collectors of the appropriate times to harvest limu tanga’u from any of the locations in Form 5.

## SCHEDULE I

FEES 1) Fee for application for a fish processing establishment licence to process limu tanga’u: $10.00. 2) Licence fee for a fish processing establishment licence to process limu tanga’u: $500.00 per year. 3) Fee for application for a fish export licence, or renewal thereof, to export limu tanga’u: $10.00. 4) Fee for application to renew a fish processing establishment licence to process limu tanga’u: $10.00. 5) Licence fee for a fish export licence to export limu tanga’u: $1000.00 per year.
