# Fisheries Snapper Notice

CAP18.02.06 Fisheries Snapper Notice Page 2 2020 Revised Edition to C T FISHERIES SNAPPER NOTICE1 Made under section 19(1) of the Fisheries Management Act 2002, the Minister responsible for Fisheries, with the consent of the Cabinet, hereby orders that:

Commencement [7th September 2007]

(1) The long term sustainability of the snapper and grouper fisheries needs to be addressed:

(2) Fishing for snapper or grouper is limited to vessels:

(a) holding a current licence to fish for snapper and grouper with droplines as at the date of this Order; or

(b) involved in the process of renewal of existing licences as at the date of this Order; or

(c) who lodged an application for a new licence to fish for snapper and grouper before this Notice comes into force; or

(d) entered on the Cyclone Waka Repair Register which apply for such licences to fish for snapper and grouper.

(3) Vessels referred to in clause (2) above shall:

(a) not be more than 15 metres in length overall (LOA); and

(b) fish from seamounts in depth deeper than 50 metres; and

(c) use the traditional Samoan style hand - reels (drop line) to target, non- target, associated or dependent species taken in the course of fishing.

(4) This Notice shall remain in effect until further Notice by the Minister.
