# Special Management Area Order

(1) Persons currently living in ‘O’ua shall have their —

(a) names listed on the Coastal Community Register of Fishers; and

(b) vessels listed on the Coastal Community Register of Fishing Vessel.

(2) Persons listed on the ‘O’ua Register of Fishers in accordance with sub- paragraph (1) may fish within the defined ‘O’ua Special Management Area but not within the Fish Habitat Reserve.

(3) Any other person not listed on the ‘O’ua Register of Fishers may apply for a subsistence fishing permit from the Coastal Community Management Committee for a single fishing trip for subsistence fishing purposes only within the Special Management Area but not within the Fish Habitat Reserve. 6.7

(1) The Coastal Community Management Committee may issue, for the ‘O’ua special management area, —

(a) up to 30 subsistence permits per annum, provided that it has consulted at least 5 of its members; or

(b) a recreational fishing permit to a person or vessel to undertake recreational fishing:

Provided that such permits shall be consistent with the Coastal Community Management Plan, the regulations and the policies of the Ministry of Fisheries, and shall consider current levels of exploitation.

(2) A permit issued under sub-paragraph (1) shall be cancelled if any person or vessel is found exceeding any maximum catch limit listed in their permit. 8.8 No person shall access or undertake fishing of any nature in the Fish Habitat Reserve except —

(a) the Coastal Community Management Committee must approve access to the Fish Habitat Reserve for observation, monitoring and restocking activities

(b) any other activities (including mariculture) shall require the endorsement of the Coastal Community Management Committee and the written authorisation of the Secretary of Fisheries and be in accordance with such conditions as may be specified; or

(c) in an emergency or circumstances involving the immediate safety of a vessel or its crew. 9.9 Illegal fishing methods prohibited under the Act, including poisons and explosives are prohibited in the special management area. 10.10 No person shall use destructive methods of fishing, including the use of metal bars or rods, to fish in the special management area.
