# Public Finance Administration (Accounts) Regulations

PUBLIC FINANCE ADMINISTRATION (ACCOUNTS) REGULATIONS1 Continues to be in force under section 48 of the Public Finance Management Act Commencement [5 March 1984]

#### 1 Short Title

This Regulation may be cited as the Public Finance Administration (Accounts) Regulations.

#### 2 Public accounts

(1) Subject to the provisions of any other enactment, in respect of Public each financial year and as soon as possible after the close of each financial year there shall be prepared and the Auditor General shall express an opinion on the following account —

(a) a statement of the asset of liabilities;

(b) a statement of loans to local statutory;

(c) a statement of investments;

(d) a statement of revenue for the 5 proceeding years;

(e) a statement of expenditure for the 5 proceeding years;

(f) such notes to the public accounts as to assist in their comprehension.

(2) The accounts and the report of the Auditor General shall be published in the Tongan Government Gazette.

#### 3 Interim accounts

In respect of each month and as soon as possible after the close of each month there shall be prepared and the Auditor General shall agree the amounts to the books of the Treasury a statement of the assets and liabilities.

#### 4 Format

The presentation and format of all accounts whether under the terms of this regulation or any other enactment will be the responsibility of the Accountant General in consultation with the Auditor General.
