# Public Finance Management (Treasury Bills Issue) Regulations

Public Finance Management (Treasury Bills Issue) Regulations Page 4 2020 Revised Edition to C T PUBLIC FINANCE MANAGEMENT (TREASURY BILLS ISSUE) REGULATIONS Made under section 29 of the Public Finance Management Act1 Commencement [28th June 2006]

#### 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Finance Management (Treasury Bills Issue) Regulations.

#### 2 Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations —

“Act” means the Public Finance Management Act;

“Minister” means the Minister of Finance;

“Reserve Bank” means the National Reserve Bank of Tonga established under the National Reserve Bank of Tonga Act; and

“Treasury Bills” means bills issued in accordance with section 29(1) of the Act and these Regulations.

(2) The terms and phrases defined in the Act shall be given the same meaning in these Regulations, unless the context requires otherwise.

#### 3 Issuing authority

Subject to the Act and these Regulations, the Minister, for and on behalf of the Government, may issue Treasury Bills on such terms, in such amounts, and at such time as the Minister may see fit.

#### 4 Currency

Treasury Bills shall be issued in the Tongan currency.

#### 5 Maturity

Treasury Bills shall mature on a day specified by the Minister prior to the issue of the Treasury Bills, but shall be a date that is not less than 7 days, and not more than 1 year, after the date of issue.

#### 6 Appointment of issuing agent

The Reserve Bank shall —

(a) act as the issuing agent for the Government in respect of the Treasury Bills;

(b) receive, process and determine applications for subscriptions of Treasury Bills;

(c) hold any funds received from the issue of Treasury Bills on account for the Government;

(d) maintain a register of Treasury Bills; and

(e) undertake all administrative matters in relation to Treasury Bills.

#### 7 Reserve Bank may conduct tender

The Reserve Bank may conduct a tender process for the issue of any amount of Treasury Bills.

#### 8 Interest

Interest on Treasury Bills shall —

(a) accrue at a fixed or variable rate to be determined by the Minister prior to the issue of the Treasury Bills; or

(b) in the event the Reserve Bank conducts a tender for the issue of any amount of Treasury Bills, at a rate agreed between the Reserve Bank, with the consent of the Minister and the successful bidder.

#### 9 Transferability and redemption

The Minister may issue Treasury Bills that are —

(a) freely transferable by the holder of the Treasury Bills;

(b) redeemable by the Government at the option of the Minister; and

(c) redeemable by the Government at the option of the holder of the Treasury Bills.
