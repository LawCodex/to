# Public Finance Administration (Public Stores) Regulations 1984

Public Finance Administration (Public Stores) Regulations CAP. 2.10.04 Regulation 1 to 2020 Revised Edition Page 5 C T PUBLIC FINANCE ADMINISTRATION (PUBLIC STORES) REGULATIONS1 Continues to be in force under section 48 of the Public Finance Management Act- Commencement [5 March 1984]

#### 1 Short Title

This Regulation may be cited as the Public Finance Administration (Public Stores) Regulations.

#### 2 Requisitions an order

All requisitions an order for supplies and stores from non-Tongan sources shall be approved by the Head of Department subject to the Accountant General confirming the availability of funds for the payment thereof.2

#### 3 Responsibilities for public stores

The Head of Department or officer in charge of a store or section of a store, should be specifically responsible for —

(1) Checking, handling and storage of public stores received.

(2) Care of stores, including proper and storage according to the nature of the stores and with due observation of safety precautions.

(3) Issue of stores supported by authorising vouchers ensuring such issues are checked and properly packet.

(4) Progression of stores issues show that items of all stocks are issued before new stock.

(5) Management of inventory stock levels taking into account the demand and time for replacement of the items from the supply source.

(6) Avoidance of waste of stores and irregular issues.

(7) Periodic verification of stock balances with cards reporting any discrepancies revealed, including damaged or deteriorated stores access stores or obsolete stores.3

#### 4 Responsibility for store - rooms

The Head of Department, or Officer, in charge of a store is responsible for —

(1) Existence and observation of security regulations with the approval of Cabinet with should include preventions of unauthorised access.

(2) Frequent inspection of store-room giving special attention to the prevention of the illegal entry.

(3) Ensuring that the store rooms are clean and properly ventilated.

(4) Reporting when the store rooms are not in good repair.4

#### 5 Security of store rooms

(1) The Head of Department or Officer in charge of a store, or section of a store is required to be present when the store-rooms are opened and in any case must visit the store-room daily. He is solely responsible for the keys and no delegation of the duties of the locking-up may be permitted, except in the special circumstances which may require more than one officer to be specially authorised or when more than one officer to be specially authorised or when more than of the lock is in use for security, all such officers being responsible in equal degree.5

(2) All store premises must have adequate fire-fighting appliances provided and maintained in a serviceable condition.

#### 6 Stores card

A separate store card must be kept for each item in the store. All receipts and issues must be recorded on the store cards which will be posted from the vouchers immediately when the stores are physically received or issued.

#### 7 Board of survey inspection

(1) The Board of Survey will carry out inspections at least yearly. A physical test check will be made between the physical stores and balances in the store cards. The number of items to be checked will be decided in consultation with the Auditor General.

(2) The Auditor General whether or not he is a member of the Board of Survey, shall be notified of every Survey to be held and shall be entitled to be present, either in person or by his officers, at any survey.

(3) At any survey the Board of Survey may accept any sealed package of Public Stores without counting the contents thereof, if either the seals are intact or the package appears not to have been tampered.

(4) A report of each survey, containing a list of each item of Public Stores counted together with the balances in the store cards shall be forwarded by the Board of Survey to Cabinet together with a copy to the Auditor General.

#### 8 Register of public stores6

The Head of Department shall maintain a proper register of all public stores belonging to the Department.

#### 9 List of public stores recommended for disposal7

(1) The Head of Department shall submit to the Standing Board of Survey a list of the public stores recommended for disposal.

(2) The submission from the Head of Departments shall include the following information —

(i) type of public stores;

(ii) date of purchase or acquisition;

(iii) estimated economic life;

(iv) cost price and relevant invoice;

(v) recommended disposal value; and

(vi) recommended method of disposal.

#### 10 Disposal and destruction of public stores8

(1) The Standing Board of Survey shall inspect deteriorating, obsolete and surplus public stores. A report by the Standing Board of Survey shall be made to the Minister of the Department concerned, on the disposal or destruction of such public stores.

(2) Such public stores shall be disposed of by public tender or auction or trade-in or if unsuitable for sale shall be destroyed in the presence of at least one member or nominee of the Board.
