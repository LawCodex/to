# Manufacture of Intoxicating Liquor (Licence Fees) Order

MANUFACTURE OF INTOXICATING LIQUOR (LICENCE FEES) ORDER1 IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred by section 11(1)) of the Manufacture of Intoxicating Liquor Act (CAP. 85), His Majesty in Council, makes the following Order:

Commencement [18th December, 2008]

#### 1 Short title

This Order may be cited as the Manufacture of Intoxicating Liquor (Licence Fees) Order.

#### 2 Principal Act

The Manufacture of Intoxicating Liquor Act is referred to in this Order as the Principal Act.

#### 3 Fee payable for licence

The fee payable for a manufacture of intoxicating liquor licence issued under the Principal Act is set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

LICENCE FEES Licence Fee Manufacture of Intoxicating Liquor Licence $1,00
