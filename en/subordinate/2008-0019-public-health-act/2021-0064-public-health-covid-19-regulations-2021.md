#### 3 Objectives

The objectives of these Regulations is to prevent and limit the risks of the spread of COVID-19 by –

(a) putting in place isolation and quarantine requirements for persons arriving in Tonga in a conveyance;

(b) prevent, protect, control and provide a public health response to the international and domestic spread of the COVID-19;

(c) manage and minimise the impacts of COVID-19; and

(d) restricting entry to any managed quarantine facility to persons who are authorised or required to enter.

#### 4 Application

These Regulations shall apply to all the land and sea areas of the Kingdom of Tonga.

## PART 2 – NOTIFIABLE CONDITION

#### 5 Notifiable disease

COVID-19 Novel Coronavirus is an infectious disease notifiable for the purposes of the Act.

## PART 3 – AUTHORISED OFFICERS AND MEDICAL

EMERGENCY OFFICERS

#### 6 Appointment of authorised officers

(1) Pursuant to section 6(1)(c) of the Act, the Chief Executive Officer may appoint as an authorised officer any person who holds necessary expertise and experience to assist during a declared Public Health Emergency period.

(2) Pursuant to section 177(1)(a) all authorised officers appointed under sub- regulation (1) are deemed as emergency officers during a Declaration period.

#### 7 Appointment of medical emergency officers

Pursuant to section 178 of the Act, the Chief Executive Officer may designate health practitioners as medical emergency officers or any person who holds necessary expertise and experience to assist during a declared Public Health Emergency period.

#### 11 Medical examinations, testing and quarantine on entry to Tonga

(1) A person who enters Tonga (other than excluded arrivals) shall –

(a) immediately report for and submit to, medical examination and testing, after their arrival at the security designated area at which they arrive;

(b) be quarantined for the required period by remaining throughout at a designated quarantined facility, unless permitted to leave the quarantine facility;

(c) maintain social distancing throughout that period from all other persons except for your fellow roommate;

(d) further report for and submit to, medical examination and testing, as directed by a medical emergency officer, at any time during the required period of quarantine; and Public Health (COVID-19) Regulations 2021 Regulation 12 to Page 9

(e) wear personal protective equipment as directed by a medical emergency officer.

(2) The required period of quarantine of a person under this section is 21 days, or as amended from time totime, after the date on which a person arrives in Tonga and was ordered into a quarantine facility and upon the medical emergency officer providing clearance to the person after being satisfied that the person is COVID-19 free.

(3) A quarantined person shall only leave a quarantine facility –

(a) on the person’s quarantine period ending;

(b) if the Chief Executive Officer authorises a person to leave due to exceptional circumstances; or

(c) to move to another place of isolation or quarantine.

(4) Before authorisinga personto leavethe quarantinefacility,theChief Executive Officer shall –

(a) take into account any advice from the medical emergency officer of any risk that may occur if the person is released; and

(b) be satisfied that the person will comply with any conditions of the authorisation.

#### 12 Restrictions to Quarantine Facilities

(1) A person shall not enter a quarantine facility unless –

(a) their entry has been approved by the quarantine supervisor; or

(b) they are authorised or required by law to enter the quarantine facility.

(2) A person who is not authorised of required by law to enter a quarantine facility may do so without approval if it is –

(a) necessary to preserve or protect a person’s life, health or safety; and

(b) not reasonably practicable to obtain approval.

#### 14 Importation of human remains to the Kingdom

(1) A permit shall be first issued by the Ministry for the importation of a human remain to the Kingdom before it is imported.

(2) All human remains imported to the Kingdom shall be prior to importation –

(a) embalmed;

(b) enclosed in a hermetically sealed casket with a clear glass directly over the head of the human remain to allow for ease of identification of the human remain;and

(c) accompanied with the following documentations –

(i) death certificate confirming cause of death; and

(ii) certificate of embalming.

(3) Upon the arrival of the human remains in the Kingdom –

(a) all relevant documentations shall be immediately submitted to the Ministry upon the arrival of the human remains;

(b) human remains shall be transported from the Fuaʻamotu Airport to Vaiola Hospital Mortuary under the Ministry’s supervision;

(c) human remains will be viewed on arrival only under the supervision of the Ministry; and

(d) human remains will remain under the close supervision of an authorised officer for 72 hours before permission for viewing by the family is granted.

(4) All funerals conducted for the human remains shall be in a closed casket and the human remains is to be viewed using a clear glass during the burial ceremony.

(5) The human remains shall be buried in accordance to the Ministry’s burial guidelines.

## PART 7 – MISCELLANEOUS

#### 17 Organisation of Committees

(1) The National Emergency COVID-19 Operations Committee is hereby established, which shall have the following functions –

(a) activate ministries and organisations in response to a public health emergency event that has happened, is happening or may happen;

(b) liaise with ministries, non government organisations and community groups in the execution of their emergency management roles and responsibilities;

(c) carry out initial assessment of the public health emergency situation;

(d) collate and prioritise immediate public health relief requirements; and

(e) manage the distribution of immediate relief supplies.

(2) The National Emergency COVID-19 Operations Committee shallconsist of the following members –

(a) the Minister who shall be the chairman;

(b) the Chief Executive Officer;

(c) Commissioner of Tonga Police;

(d) Chief of the Defence Staff of His Majesty’s Armed Forces;

#### 18 Offence relating to compliance with directions
