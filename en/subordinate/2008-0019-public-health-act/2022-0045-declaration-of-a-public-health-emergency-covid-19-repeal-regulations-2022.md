Declaration of a Public Health Emergency (COVID-19)

(Repeal) Regulations 2022 Regulation 1 to Page 5 C T DECLARATION OF A PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY (COVID-19) (REPEAL) REGULATIONS 2022 PUBLIC HEALTH ACT [CAP 12.15] IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred by section 167 of the Public Health Act [Cap 12.15], the Minister responsible for health, with the approval of Cabinet, makes the following Regulations - 1 Short Title and Interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Declaration of a Public Health Emergency (COVID-19) (Repeal) Regulations 2022.

(2) The Declaration of a Public Health Emergency (COVID-19) Regulations 2020, as amended, shall be referred to as the Principal Regulations. 2 Repeal The Principal Regulations is hereby repealed.

Made at Nuku’alofa this 17th day of February 2022.

Dr. The Hon. Saia Piukala Minister of Health
