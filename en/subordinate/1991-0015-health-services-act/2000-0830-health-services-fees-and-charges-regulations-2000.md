# Health Services (Fees and Charges) Regulations 2000

Health Services (Fees and Charges) Regulations CAP12.06.02Regulation 1 to 2020 Revised Edition Page 3 C T HEALTH SERVICES (FEES AND CHARGES) REGULATIONS Made under Section 11 of the Health Services Act1 Commencement [16th October 2000]

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Fees and Charges) Regulations.

2. The medical fees and charges set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the authorised fees and charges for services rendered by or on behalf of medical and dental officers of the government.

3. All fees and charges are to be paid to general revenue.

4. The Minister of Health may, by notice published in the Gazette, exempt any person or class of persons from the payment of any fees or charges listed in the Schedule and such exemptions may be made subject to such conditions as the Minister may specify.

5. The Minister of Health, on humanitarian grounds and upon the recommendation of the Chief Executive Officer for Health, may waive any person for the payment of fees or charges under these Regulations.2
