# Electoral (Elections of Representatives of the Nobles) Regulations 2017

Electoral (Elections of Representatives of the Nobles) Regulations CAP.2.03.02 Regulation 1 to 2020 Revised Edition Page 3 C T ELECTORAL (ELECTIONS OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE NOBLES) REGULATIONS1 Made under section 9B of the Electoral Act Commencement [8th September, 2017]

#### 1 Short Title

This Regulation may be cited as the Electoral (Elections of Representatives of the Nobles) Regulations.

#### 2 Electoral districts of Nobles

(1) A Noble shall only vote or stand for election as a representative in accordance with the Electoral Districts set out in the Schedule.

(2) Any person appointed by His Majesty as a Life Peer under clause 44 of the Constitution, and having the same rights and benefits of a Noble, may vote as a Noble for an Electoral District, but shall not stand as a candidate, and shall declare the Electoral District they will vote for to the Supervisor of Elections, and shall maintain it.
