# Pesticides Regulations 2025

Arrangement of Sections Pesticides Regulations 2025 Page 4 to FORM 2 15 FORM 3 17 FORM 4 20 FORM 5 21 FORM 6 23 FORM 7 26 FORM 8 27 FORM 9 30 Pesticides Regulations 2025 Regulation 1 to Page 5 C T PESTICIDES REGULATIONS 2025 PESTICIDES ACT 2002 IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred by section 26 of the Pesticides Act [Cap.12.13], the Minister, with the consent of Cabinet, makes the following Regulations —

#### 1 Short Title

This Regulation may be cited as the Pesticides Regulations 2025.

#### 2 Interpretation

For the purposes of these Regulations –

“the Act” means the Pesticides Act [Cap. 12.13];

“hazardous pesticides” means pesticides that are acknowledged to present particularly high levels of acute or chronic hazards to health or environment according to internationally accepted classification systems such as World Health Organization or Global Harmonized System or their listing in relevant bindinginternationalagreementsorconventions.Pesticidesthatappeartocause severe orirreversible harmto health or theenvironmentunder conditions of use in a country may be considered to be and treated as highly hazardous;

“full permit” means a permit registered for import, sales and use of pesticides in Tonga;

“restricted permit” means a permit pesticide restricted use for a particular purpose and a restricted time; and Regulation 3 Pesticides Regulations 2025 Page 6 to

“experimental permit” means pesticide use for a particular experiment only.

#### 3 Fees and forms

(1) The fees set out in the Schedule 1, are hereby prescribed for the purposes of the Act and these Regulations.

(2) The forms set out in Schedule 3, are hereby prescribed for the purposes of the Act and these Regulations.

#### 4 Application for registration of a pesticide

An application for registration of a pesticide shall be in Form 1, accompanied by the particulars set out in Schedule 2.

#### 5 Application for a licence

Every application for a pesticides licence shall be in in Form 2 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

#### 6 Issuance of a pesticides licence

(1) Every pesticide licence shall be issued in Form 3 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(2) The holder of a pesticide licence shall comply with the terms and conditions of the licence and general conditions set out in Regulation 16.

#### 7 Labelling of containers

(1) All containers containing pesticide imported into, sold or distributed to consumers in the Kingdom shall be clearly and durably marked.

(2) Every label shall, as a minimum, contain the following information –

(a) the word POISON;

(b) the appropriate toxicity and hazard symbols;

(c) the brand name and concentration of all active ingredients;

(d) the nature and formulation of the pesticide;

(e) date of formulation and batch number;

(f) any restrictions of use, or safety precautions;

(g) directions for use;

(h) minimum safety interval in days from last application until time of harvest or consumption;

Pesticides Regulations 2025 Regulation 8 to Page 7

(i) direction as to first aid in case of burns or poisoning, including details of appropriate antidote and its use;

(j) instructions regarding disposal of empty containers; and

(k) the name and address of the manufacturer.

(3) Licence holders shall use labels that –

(a) include appropriate symbols and pictograms, whenever possible, in addition to written instructions, warnings and precautions in the Tongan language; and

(b) include, in the Tongan language, a warning against the reuse of containers and instructions for the safe disposal or decontamination of used containers.

(4) Except as approved by the Registrar, no pesticide shall be sold or distributed otherwise than in its original container.

#### 8 Advertising

(1) No person shall advertise any –

(a) unregistered pesticide; or

(b) pesticide in a manner that –

(i) is false or misleading in any material particular or is intended to deceive;

(ii) employs any false or misleading comparisons with any other pesticide; and

(iii) is contrary to the conditions of registration of the pesticide.

#### 9 Pesticide Users

No person is allowed to use or handle pesticides without training and obtaining a Certificate on Safety and Proper use and handling of Pesticide from the Registrar.

#### 10 Returns

The holder of a pesticides licence shall, no later than 30th September in each year, submit to the Registrar a return in Form 4.

#### 11 Application for restricted use permit

(1) Every application for a restricted use permit shall be in Form 5.

(2) For registered use for the town allotment, every application must be trained by MAFF and certified his safety skills before filing.

#### 12 Restricted use permit

(1) Every restricted use permit shall be in Form 6 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(2) The holder ofa restricted use permitshallcomplywith the terms and conditions of the permit and the general conditions set out in Regulation 16.

#### 13 Application for Experimental use permit

Every application for an experimental use permit shall be in Form 7 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

#### 14 Experimental use permit

(1) Every experimental use permit shall be in Form 8 and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(2) The holder of an experimental use permit shall comply with the terms and conditions of the permit and the general conditions set out in Regulation 16.

#### 15 Conditions of use of permit

(1) Every pesticide for experimental and restricted purposes shall be used in accordance with the instruction specified on the manufacturer’s instructions.

(2) No person shall combine or mix for use two or more pesticides under sub- regulation (1) without the approval in writing of the Registrar.

#### 16 General conditions

(1) Every pesticide licence shall, in addition to the relevant provisions of the Act and these Regulations, have the following general conditions –

(a) in the case of a pesticides licence and restricted use permit, the holder of the licence or permit and their staff, for handling pesticides, shall have a sound knowledge of the latest “Guidelines on retail distribution of pesticides with particular reference to storage and handling at the point of supply to users in developing countries” published by the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations;

(b) in the case of experimental use permit, the permit holder and any other officer of the Ministry who will be handling the pesticides shall, to the satisfaction of the Registrar, have a sound knowledge of the use, storage andhandlingandsafetyprecautionsrequiredforsafeuseofthepesticide;

(c) appropriate safety and first aid equipment shall be available on the premises, including suitable dust masks or respirator, goggles or face Pesticides Regulations 2025 Regulation 16 to Page 9 shields, waterproof and impervious complete outer clothing, gloves, boots, hat and long-sleeved coat or suit;

(d) there shall be an emergency water supply adequate for washing off from the skin corrosive or toxic materials;

(e) absorbent materials such as sand, soil or sawdust shall be available on the premises to absorb spillage;

(f) where food, drink or medicines for human or animal consumption are to be kept in the same premises as pesticides –

(i) they shall not be permitted to come into contact with pesticides and shall not be stored or displayed within 3 meters of pesticides; or

(ii) the Registrar may give approval for such pesticides to be stored and displayed in a locked cupboard or rack;

(g) such fire-fighting equipment, in good order, as may be required by the Registrar shall be available on the premises;

(h) a notice maintained in good condition at all times and prominently displayed in close vicinity to the pesticides and containing the words

“DANGER PESTICIDES - NO SMOKING, EATING OR DRINKING PERMITTED IN THIS AREA - NOT TO BE SOLD TO PERSONS UNDER 18 YEARS OF AGE” together with the symbol of the skull and crossbones. For retail shops, the letters and numerals shall be at least 25 millimetres in height and painted in dark red on a white background. For all other premises where pesticides are stored the letters and numerals shall not be less than 50 millimetres in height and painted in dark red on a white background;

(i) at all times when not under official supervision, the premises shall be kept securely locked;

(j) all pesticides shall be stored and displayed out of the reach of children and shall at all times be so placed as to prevent access by unauthorised persons;

(k) the floor, wall and roof of the premises shall be constructed of material which is impervious to water;

(l) the floor of the storage or sale area shall be constructed of impervious material and designed in such a manner as to contain all spillage from the largest container of pesticide intended to be stored in the facility;

(m) there shall be adequate ventilation or other provision for air circulation to the satisfaction of the Registrar; and

(n) the stock of pesticides in the premises shall be rotated as directed by the Registrar.

(2) No pesticide shall be sold or distributed to any person under 18 years of age.

(3) The licenceholder and his staff andagentsshall takeallreasonableprecautions, particularly with regard to storage and transport of pesticides, to protect the Regulation 17 Pesticides Regulations 2025 Page 10 to health of human beings, creatures and plants, except target species, and to safeguard the environment.

#### 17 Record of restricted use pesticides

The holder of a restricted use permit shall complete Form 9 and submit to the Registrar, no later than 30th September in each year.

Pesticides Act 2002 PARTICULARS TO ACCOMPANY AN APPLICATION FOR THE REGISTRATION OF A PESTICIDE (Regulation 4) Each application for the registration of a pesticide shall be on Form 1 and accompanied by the following information:

1. An original or copy of the label which is affixed or is proposed to be affixed to containers of the pesticide which shall include the crops, vegetation or animals on which use is proposed; the pests to be controlled; instructions on use, including method of application, rate of application and time of application; instructions on mixing or diluting the pesticide and precautions to be taken in using the pesticide.

2. Where not provided on the label the following information is to be supplied to the Registrar by the applicant:

(a) the name of each active ingredient contained in the pesticide being the approved common name of the active constituent, or, if there is no common name of the active constituent, the chemical name of the active constituent.

(b) the form or forms in which each active constituent occurs in the pesticide and the proportions of each form of active constituent.

(c) the net weight or volume of the contents of each type of container of the pesticide the applicant proposes to market and which will bear the proposed label;

(d) if required by the Registrar, safety directions and directions for first aid;

(e) if required by the Registrar, details of any withholding period statements; and

(f) the name and complete address of the manufacturer of the pesticide.

3. A statement as to whether the pesticide is intended for domestic use, agricultural or horticultural use or for restricted use or experimental use.

4. Where the pesticide to which the application relates is intended for restricted use, the nature of that use, intended target organism, and the name and address of the person who holds the current restricted-use permit for the pesticide and who will be using the pesticide for the purposes for which the restricted-use permit was approved.

5. For imported pesticides, the name, company position and complete address of the proposed importer of the pesticide.

6. The name and complete address of the applicant for the registration of the pesticide.
