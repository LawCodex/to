# Charitable Trusts Regulations 2009

CHARITABLE TRUSTS REGULATIONS 1 Made under section 28 of the Charitable Trusts Act Commencement [8th December 2009]

#### 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Charitable Trusts Regulations 2009.

#### 2 Interpretation

For the purposes of these Regulations, “Act” means the Charitable Trusts Act 1993.

#### 3 Fees

The fees to be paid in respect of matters arising under the Act or these Regulations shall be those specified in Schedule I.

#### 4 Forms

(1) The forms referred to in these Regulations are those set out in Schedule II.

(2) The Registrar shall furnish copies of the printed forms free of charge.

#### 5 Vesting of property

A list of all property required under section 10 of the Act, shall be submitted to the Registrar in the form set out in Form 1.

#### 6 Change of name

An application for change of name under section 12 of the Act shall be made in the form set out in Form 2.

#### 7 Language of documents

Any document forming part of an application or submitted to the Registrar pursuant to the Act or these Regulations and which is in the Tongan language or any language other than English shall be accompanied by a translation into English.

#### 8 Body corporate

(1) A document purporting to be signed for or on behalf of a body corporate shall contain the names of the persons having the authority to do so, and shall affix the official stamp of the respective organisation.

(2) The Registrar may, whenever he deems it necessary, request evidence of authorisation to sign.

#### 9 Changes in the register

The Registrar shall enter in the register in respect of every Trust or Society, any change with respect to such information, in particular, the name, address, or address for service indicated therein.
