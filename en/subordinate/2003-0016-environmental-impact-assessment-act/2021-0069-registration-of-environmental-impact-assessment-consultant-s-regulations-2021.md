#### 1 Short Title and commencement

(1) This Regulation may be cited as the Registration of Environmental Impact Assessment Consultant's Regulations 2021.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on the date of gazettal.

#### 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires –

“Act” means the Environmental Impact Assessment Act [Cap. 21.04];

“CEO” means the Chief Executive Officer responsible for Environment;

“certificate” means the EIA Consultant Certificate issued under these Regulations;

“court” means the Supreme Court of Tonga;

“EAC” means the Environmental Assessment Committee established under section 13 of the Act;

“EIA” means environmental impact assessment;

“EIA Regulations” means the Environmental Impact Assessment Regulations [Cap. 21.04.01];

“local consultant” means any consulting firm or individual who is a locally recognized specialist or authority in a professional technical field. A Tongan citizen or resident for more than 5 years, and is physically in Tonga during the time of service provided;

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Environment;

“Ministry” means the Ministry responsible for Environment;

“overseas consultant” means any consulting firm or individual who is recognized abroad by a recognized institution as a specialist or authority in a professional technical field; and

“Register”meanstheRegisterofEIAConsultantsestablishedunderregulation 12 of these regulations.

#### 3 EIA Consultant Certificate

(1) Any person who conducts an environmental impact assessment under the Act and EIA Regulations shall hold a valid EIA Consultant Certificate.

(2) The Minister shall refuse any environmental impact assessment prepared by a person who does not hold a valid EIA Consultant Certificate.

#### 4 Application for an EIA Consultant Certificate

(1) An application for an EIA Consultant Certificate shall be –

(a) in Form 1;

(b) accompanied by –

(i) the prescribed fee provided in Schedule 2;

(ii) a police record issued within 6 months before the date of submission of the application, from every country that the applicant worked in during the past five years; and

(iii) information as to any corporation or partnership of which the consultant is a member or employee; and

(c) filed with the CEO.

(2) If an applicant’s supporting documents are not in the English language, the applicant shall provide a certified translation of those documents.

(3) Subject to regulation 17, registration shall be valid for 2 years and is renewable on application and payment of the renewal fee set out in Schedule 2.

#### 5 Review Panel

(1) The CEO shall convene a Review Panel to consider an application and make recommendation on the suitability of the applicant for registration.

(2) The Review Panel shall comprise of the following –

(a) CEO shall be the Chair ;

(b) one seniorofficer fromtheMinistrywiththerelevanttechnicalexpertise;

(c) one from the Ministry, agency or body whose mandate the major project to be assessed falls under; and

(d) a technical expert in the relevant area under assessment.

(3) The Review Panel –

(a) may request further documents from the applicant;

(b) may interview the applicant;

(c) may obtain advice from technically qualified persons as to the suitability of the application for registration; and

(d) shall , as soon as practicable, reach a decision on the application.

(4) The Review Panel shall upon reaching a decision, recommend to the CEO, to grant or decline the application for EIA Consultant Certificate.

(5) The Ministry shall provide secretariat services to the Review Panel.

#### 6 Grant of an EIA Consultant Certificate

(1) The CEO, shall grant a person an EIA Consultant Certificate, upon the recommendation of the Review Panel.

(2) The EIA Consultant Certificate shall be in Form 2.

#### 7 Categories of consultants

(1) The categories of consultants are as follows –

(a) principal consultant;

(b) senior consultant; and

(c) assistant consultant.

(2) Upon application for registration, an applicant shall select an area, or areas of expertise from the list provided in Schedule 1.

(3) The CEO shall issue and publish the criteria for registration in each category of consultants.

(4) A person can apply to be registered in only one category.

Registration of Environmental Impact Assessment Consultant's Regulations 2021 Page 8 to

(5) A person wishing to change the category of registration shall apply for fresh registration, paying the appropriate fee.

#### 8 EIA Study Team

An EIA study team shall consist of –

(a) a principal consultant as the team leader; and

(b) any number of senior consultants and assistant consultants.

#### 9 Responsibilities of the principal consultant

The principal consultant shall be responsible for the following matters –

(a) selection of team members who shall meet the relevant qualification and work experience for the project;

(b) ensure that every team member is registered under these Regulations;

(c) undertake the scoping exercise for the study;

(d) prepare the Terms of Reference to be approved by the CEO;

(e) coordinate and assign tasks to team members;

(f) ensure that all team members act appropriately according to the Code of Conduct in Schedule 3;

(g) quality control of the report; and

(h) submission of final report.

#### 10 Responsibilities of senior consultants

The senior consultant shall –

(a) assist the principal consultant in their obligations specified in Regulation 9;

(b) adhere to and perform the tasks allocated to them diligently; and

(c) act appropriately throughout the EIA study according to the Code of Conduct in Schedule 3.

#### 11 Responsibilities of the assistant consultants

The assistant consultants shall be responsible for the following matters –

(a) assist the principal and senior consultant in any of their obligations;

(b) adhere to and perform the tasks allocated to them diligently; and

(c) act appropriately throughout the EIA study according to the Code of Conduct in Schedule 3.

#### 12 Register of EIA Consultants

(1) There shall be a Register of EIA Consultants maintained by the CEO.

(2) The CEO may publish a list of registered consultants on the Ministry’s website.

(3) The Register may be kept in a form that the CEO thinks fit, including, partly or wholly, in electronic form, provided that the CEO shall have regard to facilitating the search or inspection of the register by members of the public.

(4) Any person may, on payment of the prescribed fee –

(a) search or inspect the Register; or

(b) on request, obtain a copy of an extract from the Register.

(5) A search or an inspection made at the office of the Ministry shall be made during the hours when the office is open to the public for business on a working day.

#### 13 Contents of Register

The Register shall contain the following information –

(a) the name of the consultant;

(b) the current holder’s contact details, including email address;

(c) the registration number and date of issue of the certificate;

(d) registration category;

(e) area(s) of expertise;

(f) if the certificate has been de-registered under regulation 17, the effective date of de-registration and the grounds for de-registration; and

(g) documents filed under this Regulations for registration on the Register.

#### 14 Amendments to Register

(1) The CEO may amend the Register as required, to –

(a) update information;

(b) correct or delete information wrongly entered in the Register; or

(c) include information wrongly omitted from the Register.

(2) The CEO shall give written notice to the relevant consultant of any amendment under sub-regulation (1).

#### 15 Online submission

A person may file a document with the CEO in any format permitted by the CEO, in accordance with the procedures established by the CEO from time to time.

#### 16 Code of Conduct

All EIA consultants shall observe the Code of Conduct in Schedule 3.

#### 17 De-registration and non-renewal of registration

(1) The CEO may de-register a consultant or refuse to renew an application for registration on the grounds that the consultant has –

(a) breached the Code of Conduct;

(b) been found guilty of an offence under these Regulations; or

(c) not performed his duties under the Act or these Regulations to a satisfactory level.

(2) A consultant who is likely to be de-registered or refused renewal shall be given an opportunity to be heard, orally or in writing to the EAC.

(3) A consultant who is not satisfied with a decision of the CEO under this regulation may appeal to the Minister.

#### 18 Offences

Any person who –

(a) conducts an environmental impact assessment without a valid EIA Consultant Certificate;

(b) provides false information in order to obtain an EIA Consultant Certificate; or

(c) provides false information in an EIA report which may have detrimental impact in the environment under study if the major project is allowed to proceed, commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $5000 or a term of imprisonment not exceeding 2 years or both.

CODE OF CONDUCT All EIA Consultants shall –  act professionally, accurately, fairlyand in an unbiased manner in undertaking his work;  take all due care and diligence to collect the relevant data to assess the environmental issues in the various stages of the environmental assessment;  not include in the report, without acknowledgement of the source, any data that is not the result of their findings;  consult widely with all relevant agencies, stakeholders, interested parties and the general public on all matters that are likely to affect them;  abide by all relevant laws and guidelines;  keep abreast of emerging trends and issues in the environmental field and shall conduct his profession in accordance with the best available practice taking into account advances in science and technology; and  declare any potential conflict of interest.
