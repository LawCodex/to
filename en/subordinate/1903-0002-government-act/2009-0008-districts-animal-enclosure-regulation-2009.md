# Districts (Animal Enclosure) Regulation 2009

Districts (Animal Enclosure) Regulations CAP 8.06.03 Regulation 1 to 2020 Revised Edition Page 5 C T DISTRICTS (ANIMAL ENCLOSURE) REGULATIONS1 Made under section 26 of the Government Act Commencement [6th July 2009]

#### 1 Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the Districts (Animal Enclosure) Regulations.

#### 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations —

“animal” means any pig, goat, horse or cow;

“Districts” means the Districts listed in the Schedule to these Regulations;

“public place” means anypublic wayand anybuildingplace or vessel towhich for the time being the public are entitled or permitted to have access either without condition or upon condition of making any payment; and

“public way” means any road or other way lawfully used by the public.

#### 3 Application

These Regulations shall apply to all residents of the Districts listed in the Schedule.

#### 4 Animal enclosure

(1) Every animal owner in the Districts shall —

(i) keep the animal in a secure enclosure;

(ii) secure such animal in an appropriate manner; and

(iii) ensure that the animal has sufficient food and water.

(2) Where an animal is secured in at enclosure, such enclosure shall be kept clean to the satisfaction of the District Officer.

#### 5 Offence

Any animal owner who —

(a) fails to provide a secure enclosure for such animal; and

(b) such animal is found on a public way or place, commits an offence.

#### 6 Occupier or owner of land

The owner or occupier of any cultivated land or of any enclosed land may shoot or kill any animal found trespassing and damaging such land provided that the owner or occupier shall —

(a) notify the owner of the animal, if known; or

(b) if the owner of the animal is not known after reasonable attempts to find himor does not claimthe same within one hour after the animal is killed, the carcass may be disposed in a manner determined by the owner or occupier.

#### 7 Offence

Any person who fails to comply with these Regulations shall upon conviction be subject to the penalties provided by section 26 of the Government Act.

#### 8 Repeal

(1) The Government (Hihifo District Villages) Regulations 2000 and the Leimatu‘a, Feletoa and Mataika District Regulations 1958 are hereby repealed.

(2) Regulations 3, 4 and 5 of the Niuatoputapu Villages Regulations 2000 are hereby repealed.
