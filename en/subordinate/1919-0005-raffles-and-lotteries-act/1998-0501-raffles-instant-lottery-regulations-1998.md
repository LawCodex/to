# Raffles (Instant Lottery) Regulations 1998

RAFFLES (INSTANT LOTTERY) REGULATIONS Made under section 7 of the Raffles Act1 Commencement [3rd August 1998]

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Raffles (Instant Lottery) Regulations.

2. In these Regulations —

“Instant Lottery” means a game available for entry by the public through the purchase of tickets whereby prizes and prize winners are determined by scratching or otherwise revealing details on the tickets.

3. Before a licence to conduct any instant lottery shall be granted, the applicant shall furnish to the Minister of Police the following information:

(a) a description of the instant lottery;

(b) the term during which the instant lottery is proposed (including whether there are to be any options for renewal);

(c) the matters described in section 3 of the Raffles Act.

4. Tickets shall not be sold at a price other than the price declared in the application.

5. The method and place whereby prize winners may collect prizes shall be made public through at least one local newspaper prior to the commencement of the lottery.

6. In case of any dispute or protest relating to any prize, the decision of the licence holder shall be final.

7. The Minister of Police shall issue a licence to conduct an instant lottery setting out any conditions that Cabinet may deem fit to impose.
