# Raffles Regulations

RAFFLES REGULATIONS1 Commencement [14th November 1930]

1. Before a licence to hold any raffle shall be granted the applicant shall furnish to the Minister of Police the information following, that is to say —

(a) a description and value of the article or articles to be raffled, and the place where such article or articles are situate;

(b) the amount to be charged for each ticket or chance;

(c) the number of tickets to be offered for sale;

(d) the date, time and place of holding the raffle.

2. No tickets shall be sold in any raffle after the time set down for the draw or throw.

3. Tickets shall not be sold or given to the father, mother, husband, wife, son or daughter of a licensee.

4. Tickets shall not be sold at a price less than the declared price.

5. If at the time appointed for the commencement of the raffle the number of tickets sold shall be insufficient to warrant the holding of a raffle, the raffle may be withdrawn by the licensee provided he refunds to ticket holders all monies received by him or any person on his behalf.

6. An extension of the time prescribed in the licence can only be granted by the Minister of Police.

7. Should any raffle take place without all the tickets having been sold the winner shall not be required or asked to pay for the unsold tickets.

8. If any ticket holders are not present at the time of the raffle the licensee or his appointed deputy may ask any one present to throw or draw for an absent ticket holder and he will be bound by such throw or draw.

9. If the Minister of Police shall be dissatisfied with the valuation of the articles as declared by the applicant for a licence he may place a value on such articles or he may request the Collector of Customs or any other competent person to value all or any such articles and such value shall be the amount for which the licence shall be issued.

10. No article the subject of any licence shall be used by any person so as to cause any deterioration in the value of such article or any part thereof.

11. If two or more ticket holders shall throw an equal number and if there is only one prize they shall throw off to ascertain who is the winner. If there is more than one prize they shall throw off to ascertain who is the winner of the first, second or other prize.

12. No raffle shall be held before 10 a.m. or after 10 p.m. on any day.

13. In case of any dispute or protest against any raffle on account of any fraud or breach of these regulations, the complaint shall be made at the time of the raffle and the licensee shall refuse to hand over the prize or prizes until the dispute has been settled. If not settled the party protesting shall commence action within 14 days on the Civil side of the Court and if he fails to do so the licensee shall deliver the prize or prizes to the winner or winners and all disputes shall be deemed to have lapsed.

14. Tickets shall be sold from blocks having a counterpart numbered similarly to the number on the butt and such counterpart shall be kept by the licensee and produced at the time of the raffle. The name of the purchaser shall be written on the butt and on the counterpart.

15. The licensee shall not reserve to himself any tickets in the raffle.

16. The Regulations shall not apply to (loteli vave) or pool betting.
