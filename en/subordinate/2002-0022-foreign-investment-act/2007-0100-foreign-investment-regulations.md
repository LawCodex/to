# Foreign Investment Regulations

Regulation Foreign Investment Regulations 2021 Page 4 to C T FOREIGN INVESTMENT REGULATIONS 2021 FOREIGN INVESTMENT ACT 2020 IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred by section 45 of the Foreign Investment Act 2020, the Minister responsible for trade and economic development with the consent of Cabinet makes the following regulations –

#### 1 Short Title and commencement

(1) This Regulation may be cited as the Foreign Investment Regulations 2021.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on the day it is gazetted.

#### 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires —

“Act” means the Foreign Investment Act 2020;

“certificate” means a Foreign Investment Registration Certificate issued under section 15(3)(a) of the Act;

“operational date” means the date that on which the certificate is issued;

“implementation period” means 12 months from the operational date;

“register” means the Foreign Investment Register established under section 30 of the Act;

“Minister” has the same meaning as provided for under section 2 of the Act;

“PACSIC” means the Pacific Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities published by the Secretariat of the Pacific Community, as amended from time to time.

#### 3 Application for a Foreign Investment Registration Certificate

(1) AnapplicationforacertificateshallbeintheformasapprovedbytheRegistrar.

(2) If an application is incomplete, the Registrar may within 5 working days write to the applicant to complete the application.

#### 4 Online submissions

(1) Any person may submit documents to the Registrar via the foreign investment online service.

(2) The Registrar may request further documentation or other evidence from a person who intends to use the online service to authenticate the identity of such a person.

#### 5 Appointment of an arbitrator

(1) The Minister shall appoint an arbitrator in accordance with this regulation to determine an appeal from the decision of the Registrar under section 15(3)(b) or section 18(3)(b) of the Act.

(2) A person appointed by the Minister to be an arbitrator shall be —

(a) a senior law practitioner with at least 10 years of experience in that level;

(b) a business person with well-known experience within the business sector; or

(c) a person with special knowledge and experience in the fields of trade and economics.

(3) A person appointed as an arbitrator shall guarantee that he has no personal interest in the appeal.

(4) An arbitrator’s appointment shall cease when the appeal is concluded.

#### 8 Extension of implementation period

(1) Upon written application by the holder of a certificate to carry on a restricted activity, the Registrar may extend the implementation period.

(2) An application by the holder of a certificate under sub regulation (1) shall be made at least 30 days before the end of the implementation period.

(3) The Registrar may extend the implementation period for a length of time no longer than 12 months on any one occasion.

(4) Upon receipt of the application, the Registrar shall give the holder a written notice of his decision within 10 working days.

(5) If the Registrar decides to grant an extension of the implementation period, he shall amend the registry accordingly.

#### 9 Fees

The fees payable under the Act and these Regulations are provided in Schedule 3.
