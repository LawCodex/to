# Ombudsman Order 2018

BYAUTHORITY:

SiosifaPomana, GeneralManager,TongaPost Ltd. 2018.

LordFakafanua Speakerofthe Legislative Assembly 'EikiFakafanua 'Eiki Sea 'o eFale Alea Na'efakahoko 'iNuku'alofa 'ihe 'aho22ni 'oMa'asi2018.

Made atNuku'alofa this 22"ddayofMarch 2018.

1. ShortTitle

(1) These orders shall be cited asthe Ombudsman Order 2018.

(2) The Scheduleofthe OmbudsmanAct, asamended,isreferred to in these orders as the Principal Schedule.

2. ScheduleAmended ThePrincipalScheduleisamendedbyinsertingafterparagraph(xviii) the following-

"(xix) TongaHome Gas;

(xx) Tonga GasLimited;

(xxi) Electoral Commission;

(xxii) National Identity Card Office;

(xxiii) Electricity Commission;

(xxiv) National Reserve Bank of Tonga;

(xxv) Any subsidiary of a public enterprise, which Government holds majority shares." In exercise ofthepowers vested bysection 11 (8) ofthe OmbudsmanAct, asamended, theSpeaker oftheLegislativeAssembly makes thefollowing orders: 'Ihono ngaue 'aki 'a e ngaahi mafai kuofoaki 'i he kupu 11 (8) 'o e Lao ki he 'Omipatimeni, 'i honofakatonutonu, 'okufa 'u ai 'e he 'Eiki Sea 'o e Fale Alea 'a e Ngaahi Tu'utu 'uni ko 'eni:

1. Hingoanounou

(1) Kuo pau ke ui 'a e Ngaahi Tu'utu'uni ni ko e Tu'utu'uni ki he 'Omipatimeni2018.

(2) Koe Tepile 'o e Lao kihe 'Omipatimeni, 'i hono fakatonutonu, 'oku ui 'i he ngaahi tu'utu'unini ko e Tefito'i Tepile.

2. Fakatonutonu 'aeTepile 'Oku fakatonutonu 'a e Tefito'i Tepile 'aki hono fakahuatu 'i he hili 'a epalakalafi (xviii) 'ae-

"(xix) TongaHome Gas;

(xx) Tonga GasLimited;

(xxi) Komisoni Fili;

(xxii) 'Ofisi 'o e Kaati Faka'ilonga'i Fakafonua;

(xxiii) Komisoni 'Uhila;

(xxiv) Pangike Pule Fakafonua 'a Tonga;

(xxv) Pisinisisi'i 'o hapisinisi 'aepule'anga, 'aia 'okuma'u 'inasi lahi taha ai 'a e Pule' anga."
