# Magistrate’s Courts (Allowances to Witnesses) Regulations

Magistrate’s Court (Allowances to Witnesses) Regulations CAP. 3.12.01 Regulation 1 to 2020 Revised Edition Page 3 C T MAGISTRATE’S COURT (ALLOWANCES TO WITNESSES) REGULATIONS1 Made under section 68A of the Magistrate’s Court Act Commencement [28th March 1990]

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Magistrate’s Courts (Allowances to Witnesses) Regulations.

2. When a witness is called by the Crown or the Government to attend trial before a Magistrate’s Court in either a criminal or a civil case (whether or not he gives evidence) he shall be paid —

(a) if he incurs travelling expenses to and from the Court, his actual expenses reasonably incurred for that purpose; and

(b) a maintenance allowance being the greater of —

(i) $10 per day; or

(ii) any reasonable loss of earnings which he has suffered by reason of attending Court.
