# Customs Duty (Amendment) Order 2018

Customs Duty (Amendment) Order 2018 Order to Page 3 C T CUSTOMS DUTY (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2018 CUSTOMS ACT 2007 IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Customs Act 2007, the Minister responsible for revenue and customs, with the approval of Cabinet, makes the following Order - Commencement [Date]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Customs Duty (Amendment) Order 2018.

(2) The Customs Duty Order 2008, as amended, imposing customs duty on goods effective from1 February2008 and publishedin the Government Gazette Supplement Extraordinary on 21 January 2008 shall be referred to as the Principal Order.

(3) The commencement date of this Order shall be the 9th day of July 2018.
