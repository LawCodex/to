# Customs Duty (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2020

Customs Duty (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2020 Order to Page 3 C T CUSTOMS DUTY (AMENDMENT) (NO.2) ORDER 2020 CUSTOMS ACT 2007 IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Customs Act 2007, the Minister responsible for revenue and customs, with the approval of Cabinet, makes the following Order -

(1) This Order may be cited as the Customs Duty (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2020.

(2) The Customs Duty Order 2008, as amended, imposing customs duty on goods effective from1 February2008 and publishedin the Government Gazette Supplement Extraordinary on 21 January 2008 shall be referred to as the “Principal Order”.

(3) This Order shall come into force on 1 July 2020.

(4) Chapter 2 of the Principal Order is amended by –

(a) replacing the words “Free but see excise schedule” with the word

“Free” in the Import Duty Rate columns for the following tariff items - 0204.22.10 0204.22.20 0204.42.10 0204.42.20

(b) inserting the following new tariff items, description, Unit Qty, Import Duty Rate and Export Duty in their appropriate section and numerical order – Order Customs Duty (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2020 Page 4 to Tariff Item Description Unit Qty Import Duty Rate Export Duty Rate 0204.22.30 ---Mutton breast kg Free but see excise schedule Free 0204.42.30 ---Mutton breast kg Free but see excise schedule Free 0204.22.40 ---Mutton flaps kg Free but see excise schedule Free 0204.42.40 ---Mutton flaps kg Free but see excise schedule Free

(5) Chapter 24 of the Principal Order is amended by –

(a) inserting the following new tariff items in their appropriate section and numerical order – Tariff Item Description Unit Qty Import Duty Rate Export Duty Rate 2402.20.11 ----Cigarettes manufactured locally from locally grown tobacco (Tapaka Tonga) kg Free but see excise schedule Free 2402.20.19 ----Cigarettes manufactured locally from imported tobacco kg Free but see excise schedule Free

(b) deleting the details in the following Tariff item to cater for the new tariff subheading national splits - 2402.20.10 Made at Nuku’alofa this 30th day of June 2020.

Hon. Tevita Lavemaau Minister responsible for Revenue and Customs
