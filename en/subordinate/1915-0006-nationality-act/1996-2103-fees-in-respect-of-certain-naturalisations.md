# Fees in respect of certain naturalisations

NATURALISATIONS1 Made under section 8B of the Nationality Act- Commencement [26th August 1996]

#### 1 Administration fee

US$200.

#### 2 Naturalisation fee to the holder of a Tongan Protected Person Passport

or Tongan National Passport and the spouse US$10,000 each.

#### 3 Naturalisation of children under 21 years of age persons under

regulation 2 above US$1,000 each.

#### 4 Naturalisation of a person on humanitarian grounds

Single person - US$20,000 each Married couple - US$15,000 each Children - US$1,000 each
