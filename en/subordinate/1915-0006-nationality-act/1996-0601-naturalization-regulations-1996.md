# Naturalization Regulations 1996

Made under section 15 of the NATIONALITY ACT (CAP 59)1 Commencement [6th September, 1996]

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Naturalization Regulations.

(2) These Regulations apply to aliens and/or persons who are desirous of applying for naturalization, or resumption of Tongan nationality.

2. In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires —

“Act” means the Nationality Act;2

“Alien” means and includes all persons other than Tongan subjects;

“Minister” means the Minister for Foreign Affairs.3

3. (1) All applications under these Regulations shall be filed in the office of the Minister for Foreign Affairs.4

(2) Every application shall be made in the applicable form prescribed in Schedule 1 – 6 hereto.

(3) Every application for naturalization or resumption of Tongan nationality shall be submitted together with —

(a) 2 passport sized photographs of applicant;

(b) birth certificate of applicant;

(c) passport of applicant;

(d) any certificate of citizenship in the case of application for resumption of Tongan nationality;

(e) the appropriate fee for such application as prescribed in Schedule 7 hereto;

(f) medical certificate;

(g) such other documents as may be required to complete such application.

(4) Any person whose citizenship is in doubt may apply in writing to the King for determination of his citizenship and such application shall be accompanied by —

(i) 2 passport sized photographs;

(ii) the appropriate birth certificate of applicant;

(iii) parent’s marriage certificate;

(iv) parent’s birth certificate;

(v) the appropriate fee as prescribed by Schedule 7 hereto; and

(vi) such other documents as may be required to complete such application.

4. (1) Every applicant shall pay the appropriate fee on application for naturalization, resumption of Tongan nationality and the certificates of naturalization as prescribed in Schedule 7 hereto.

(2) Notwithstanding the requirements of regulation 4(1), every applicant for naturalization under section 8B of the Act, shall pay the appropriate fee as prescribed in a separate regulation for certain naturalizations.

5. (1) There shall be kept and maintained in the office of the Prime Minister registers of —

(a) persons who have been granted certificates of naturalization on residential grounds;

(b) persons who have been granted special certificates of naturalization;

(c) persons who have resumed Tongan nationality;

(d) persons who have been granted certificates of re-admission to Tongan nationality;

(e) persons born in Tonga whose nationality has been confirmed;

(f) persons who have been granted certificates of naturalization as Tongan Protected Persons Passport holders or Tongan National Passport holders or on humanitarian grounds.

(2) The registers specified in subregulation (1) hereof shall, pursuant to section 16(2) of the Act, be open to public inspection upon payment of the appropriate fee set out in Schedule 7 hereto.

6. Every applicant for naturalization, after lodging his application shall be interviewed by the Minister or any officer or officers duly authorised by the Minister to conduct such interview.

7. (1) Every applicant for naturalization on residential grounds shall satisfy the following requirements —

(a) that he has resided in Tonga for a period of not less than 5 years;

(b) that he is of good character;

(bb) that he has adequate knowledge of the Tongan language;5

(c) that he intends if his application be granted to reside in the Kingdom;

(d) that he is of good health;

(e) such other conditions as may be stipulated in writing by the Prime Minister.

(2) Notwithstanding the requirements stated in subregulation (1) hereof all applications on other grounds shall satisfy the requirements specified in the application form set out in Schedule 6.

8. (1) Where a certificate of naturalization is granted by His Majesty in Council to any alien, such person shall take the oath of allegiance as prescribed by section 14 of the Act.

(2) Such oath shall be taken and signed in the presence of the Prime Minister or if authorised in writing by the Prime Minister, a Minister or a diplomatic or consular officer or senior officer of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.6

(3) The certificates of naturalization issued pursuant to clause 29 of the Constitution and section 8 of the Act, shall be in the form prescribed in Schedule 8.
