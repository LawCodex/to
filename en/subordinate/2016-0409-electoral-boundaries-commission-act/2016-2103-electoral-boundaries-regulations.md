#### 1 Short Title

This Regulation may be cited as the Electoral Boundaries Regulations.

#### 2 Electoral Boundaries for next general election

(1) The boundaries of the 17 electoral constituencies for the 2017 general election of the representatives of the people shall be the same as for the 2014 general election, as set out in the Schedules to these regulations.

(2) The boundaries hereby established shall continue in force until altered by other regulations made under section 20 of the Electoral Boundaries Commission Act.

#### 3 Electoral Boundaries Regulations 2014 repealed

The Electoral Boundaries Regulations 2014 are hereby repealed.
