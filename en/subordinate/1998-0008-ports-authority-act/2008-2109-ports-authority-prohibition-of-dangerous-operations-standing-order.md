# Ports Authority (Prohibition of Dangerous Operations) Standing Order

EndnotesCAP. 22.07.03 Ports Authority (Prohibition of Dangerous Operations) Standing Order Page 2 2020 Revised Edition to C T PORTS AUTHORITY (PROHIBITION OF DANGEROUS OPERATIONS) STANDING ORDER 1 Made under section 79(1) of the Ports Authority Act 1998 Commencement [26th August, 2008]

#### 1 This Standing Order may be cited as the Ports Authority (Prohibition of Dangerous

Operations) Standing Order 2008.

#### 2 The Port Master may, by notice in writing served on any person, prohibit with

immediate effect any operation which, in the opinion of the Port Master, is dangerous or likely to pose an undue risk to the safety of the port or other port users, or is otherwise undesirable.

#### 3 This Standing Order also applies to the operation of any vessel(s) wishing to use the

port regardless of whether that vessel is registered in Tonga or some other country, and regardless of whether such vessel(s) holds certificates declaring that it is safe.

#### 4 The Port Masters powers under this Standing Order must be exercised on reasonable

grounds and any decision to prohibit any operation or vessel from operating in or using the port may be subject to judicial review in the Supreme Court.
