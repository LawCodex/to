# Civil Aviation (Airport Charges) Regulations

REGULATIONS1 Made under section 7 of the Civil Aviation Act 1990, and continued in force under section 237 of the Civil Aviation Act 2014 —

Commencement [24th June, 2008]

#### 1 Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Airport Charges) Regulations.

#### 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires —

“airport charge” means only aeronautical charges that an airport operator charges users of airport services;

“airport operator” means any person or corporate body who is delegated power under section 5(1) of the Civil Aviation Act to operate airports in the Kingdom, or, is issued a licence under regulation 126(1) of the Civil Aviation Regulations to operate an airport in the Kingdom.

#### 3 Airport charges

(1) The Ministry shall approve all airport charges before they may be imposed or varied by an airport operator.

(2) An airport operator shall apply in writing to the Ministry for approval at least two calendar months prior to imposing or varying an airport charge.

(3) The airport operator shall submit to the Ministry, in relation to each proposed airport charge —

(a) a description of the service related to the proposed airport charge;

(b) the details of the proposed charge for the service;

(c) a report on any public consultation;

(d) any responses from relevant parties from the aviation industry; and

(e) a submission on whether the proposed airport charge is consistent with the relevant ICAO guidelines.

#### 4 Minister to consider

The Ministry shall give due regard to the following matters when the Ministry is considering the decision —

(a) the degree of consultation with relevant parties in the aviation industry;

(b) the level of support from the relevant parties in the aviation industry;

(c) reasonable financial justification;

(d) consistency with ICAO guidelines; and

(e) any other factor the Ministry deems reasonable to be considered.

#### 5 Notice of decision

(1) The Ministry shall advise the airport operator its decision on the application as soon as practicable, but it shall not be longer than one month from the date of receipt of the application.

(2) An airport operator shall cause to be published in a newspaper widely circulated in the Kingdom and broadcasted a public notice advising the public of the approval by the Ministry of an airport charge, and that such decision shall come into effect after one month from the date of the public notice.

#### 6 Services to comply with approved airport charge

An airport operator shall provide all its services in accordance with the airport charges which are filed and approved by the Ministry.

#### 7 Refusal of airport

Where the Ministry decides to refuse an application by an airport operator, the Ministry shall, within one month after receipt of the application, provide to the airport operator a written notice stating that the Ministry is disallowing the proposed airport charge and setting out the reasons for its decision.

#### 8 Appeal

Any person who —

(a) does not agree with a decision of the Ministry regarding a proposed airport charge; and

(b) proves to have a sufficient interest in the matter, may lodge an appeal to the Minister, and such appeal shall be dealt with by arbitration under these Regulations.

#### 9 Arbitration

(1) The Minister shall appoint one or more independent aviation experts to be the arbitrators where an appeal under regulation 8 involves a dispute between an airport operator and a member of the public, including any operator in the aviation industry.

(2) The Ministry and the airport operator shall appoint one or more independent aviation experts to be the arbitrators where an appeal under regulation 8 involves a dispute between the Ministry and the airport operator.

(3) Any arbitrator appointed under sub-regulations (1) and (2) shall have the power to —

(a) set the arbitration rules and procedures;

(b) summon and receive evidence from witnesses, including expert witnesses; and

(c) make any ruling as to costs of the arbitration.

(4) The decision of the arbitrator shall be final.

#### 10 Register of airport charges

The Ministry shall maintain and make available to the public a Register of airport charges.
