# Nuku'alofa Harbour Regulations

Made under the Harbours Act1 Commencement [1st January 1929]

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Nuku'alofa Harbour Regulations.

2. A registered inter-island vessel of not more than 20 registered tons burden, and any locally owned vessel of not more than 45 feet in length overall may, between the hours of sunset and sunrise, lie at anchor or moor to a buoy in that part of Nuku'alofa harbour within the limits specified in regulation 3, hereinafter called the boat harbour, without carrying a riding light.

3. The boat harbour is all that part of Nuku'alofa harbour lying to southward of a line 105 degrees true between the iron tripod beacon with white top situated on the foreshore opposite to the Wireless Station and the iron beacon situated on the foreshore opposite to the Roman Catholic Church, Ma'ufanga
