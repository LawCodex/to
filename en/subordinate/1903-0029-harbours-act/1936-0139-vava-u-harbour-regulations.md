# Vava'u Harbour Regulations

Made under the Harbours Act1 Commencement [1st January 1936]

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Vava‘u Harbour Regulations.

2. A registered inter-island vessel of not more than 20 registered tons burden, and any locally owned vessel of not more than 45 feet in length overall may, between the hours of sunset and sunrise, lie at anchor or moor to a buoy in that part of Vava'u harbour within the limits specified in regulation 3, hereinafter called the boat harbour, without carrying a riding light.

3. The boat harbour is all that part of Neiafu Harbour to the eastward of a line drawn south 27 degrees east magnetic from the Government flagstaff to a white conical beacon on the eastern foreshore of the harbour
