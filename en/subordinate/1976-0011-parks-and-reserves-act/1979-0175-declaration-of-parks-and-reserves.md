# Declaration of Parks and Reserves

DECLARATION OF PARKS AND RESERVES Made by the Parks and Reserves Authority1 Commencement [29th June, 1979]

1. This declaration may be cited as the Parks and Reserves Declaration.

2. The following areas are hereby declared to be Parks and Reserves —

(a) HAKAUMAMA’O REEF RESERVE: which area is bound by the following points —

Lat. 20° 59' 30.6” S— Long. 175° 12' 57.3” W.

Lat. 20° 59' 30.0” S— Long. 175° 12' 04.4” W.

Lat. 21° 00' 13.2” S— Long. 175° 12' 03.8” W.

Lat. 21° 00' 13.7” S— Long. 175° 12' 56.8” W. and which is demarcated in the plan in Schedule 1;

(b) PANGAIMOTU REEF RESERVE: which area is bound by the following points —

Lat. 21° 07' 09.8” S— Long. 175° 09' 54.1” W.

Lat. 21° 06' 56.7” S— Long. 175° 09' 64.2” W.

Lat. 21o 06' 56.4” S— Long. 175° 09' 29.1” W.

Lat. 21° 07' 09.5” S— Long. 175° 09' 29.0” W. and which is demarcated in the plan in Schedule 2;

(c) MONUAFE ISLAND PARK AND REEF RESERVE: which is bound by the following points —

Lat. 21° 06' 44.7” S— Long. 175° 08' 37.0” W.

Lat. 21° 06' 12.7” S— Long. 175° 08' 37.4” W.

Lat. 21° 06' 12.5” S— Long. 175° 08' 20.0” W.

Lat. 21° 06' 44.5” S— Long. 175° 08' 19.6” W. and which is demarcated in the plan in Schedule 3;

(d) HA’ATAFU BEACH RESERVE: which is bound by the following points —

Lat. 21° 04' 11.6” S— Long. 175° 20' 00.3” W.

Lat. 21° 04' 05.9” S— Long. 175° 20' 09.6” W.

Lat. 21° 04' 00.1” S— Long. 175° 20' 04.3” W.

Lat. 21° 04' 05.2” S— Long. 175° 09' 56.1” W. and which is demarcated in the plan in Schedule 4;

(e) MALINOA ISLAND PARK AND REEF RESERVE: which is bound by the following points —

Lat. 21° 02' 24.0” S— Long. 175° 07' 59.1” W.

Lat. 21° 01' 48.7” S— Long. 175° 07' 59.6” W.

Lat. 21° 01' 48.2” S— Long. 175° 07' 21.3” W.

Lat. 21° 02' 23.6” S— Long. 175° 07' 20.8” W. and which is demarcated in the plan in Schedule 5;

(f) ‘EUA NATIONAL PARK: situated on the Government Estate on the eastern side of the Island of ‘Eua, Kingdom of Tonga, containing an area of 449.4 Hectares more or less and bounded as follows —

Commencing at the intersection of the Tofi‘a boundary between the King’s Estate and Government Estate or the eastern shore line of ‘Eua, thence follows this eastern shore line in a southerly direction to the point of intersection of shoreline with a bearing of 135° 45' 35" from the north eastern boundary ,of Hon. Ve‘ehala’s Estate, thence follows this bearing in a north westerly direction to the ridge top, thence follows the ridge top to the point of intersection with the Tofi‘a boundary between the King’s Estate and Government Estate, thence follows this Tofi‘a Line to the point of commencement, and which is demarcated in the plan in Schedule 6.
