# Court Fees Act

AN ACT TO REGULATE THE FEES PAYABLE IN THE COURTS OF THE KINGDOM1 Commencement [1st September, 1927]

1. Short title This Act may be cited as the Court Fees Act.

#### 2 Collection

(1) The fees specified in the Schedule hereto shall be paid as therein prescribed in respect of the several matters to which they are applicable:

Provided that no fee shall be charged or payable in any case in which such fee charged would have to be paid by any Government Department.2 Allocation

(2) All moneys received as fees shall be paid by the officer receiving the same into the Treasury for the benefit of the general revenue.
