# Bicycle Registration Act

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE REGISTRATION OF BICYCLES1 Commencement [3rd August, 1944]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Bicycle Registration Act.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires —

“bicycle” means any pedal vehicle propelled by human volition;

“public way” means any road, street, place, bridge, wharf, or other way lawfully used by the public.

#### 3 Registration of bicycle

(1) Every owner of a bicycle shall before using the same on any public way make application for registration in the form set out in Schedule 1 and register such bicycle in the police office of the district in which the owner resides and pay the annual registration in respect thereof as laid down in Schedule 2 hereto:

Provided always that nothing in this subsection shall be so construed as to prevent the owner, or any person authorised by him from taking such bicycle along a public way to the police office for the purpose of registration.2 Number plate

(2) When application is made by an owner for registration of a bicycle such bicycle shall be brought to the police office for inspection and upon registration being granted the police officer shall affix to the bicycle a number plate as directed by the police officer in charge and the chief police officer of the district shall issue to the owner a certificate of registration in the form prescribed in Schedule 3 hereto.3

(3) Any person failing to comply with the provisions of this section shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $20.4

(4) Any person who —

(a) makes any false statement in his application;

(b) unlawfully alters any particular in a certificate of registration; or

(c) uses a number plate on a bicycle other than that to which it has been given, shall be guilty of an offence and be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $30.5

#### 4 Fine

Every person who shall let out for hire a bicycle without having first registered the bicycle as a bicycle for hire shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $40.6
