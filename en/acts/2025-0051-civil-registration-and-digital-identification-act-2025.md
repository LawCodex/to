#### 3 Office of Registrar General

(1) The Prime Minister with the consent of Cabinet may appoint a Registrar General of Civil Registration and Digital Identification.

(2) There shall be such sub-registrarsas may be appointed by the Registrar General following consultation with the Chief Magistrate and with the consent of the Prime Minister; and a sub-registrar may hold that post in conjunction with any other public office.

(3) Where a sub-registrar is, by reason of unforeseen circumstances temporarily unable to perform the duties of the office, a Governor or Government Representative may, with the consent of the Prime Minister, be appointed by the Registrar General to act in the place of the sub-registrar.

#### 69 Limitation of action

No prosecution shall be instituted against any person for an offence under section 65, 66 or 67 of this Act unless the same shall be commenced within 5 years from the time of the commission of such offence.

## PART VI – GENERAL

#### 70 Sub-registries

The Registrar General shall establish service centres and a sub-registry office with necessary facilities at suitable locations for the purpose of its activities under this Act, including for -

(a) receiving and disseminating information to the general public about the Digital Identification Database and UINs;

(b) receiving, updating and disseminating information for the purpose of registration, notification of UINs and other aspects of administration of the identification system;

(c) receiving notifications and updated information, and delivering certificates, under Part I of this Act;

(d) paying and receiving any fees as prescribed;

(e) answering questions from the general public; and

(f) any other purpose required for the Registrar General’s functions under this Act.

#### 71 Fees

The fees chargeable under this Act shall be prescribed by Regulations.

#### 72 Power to make regulations

The Registrar General with the consent of Cabinet may make regulations prescribing all matters which by this Act are required or permitted to be prescribed, or which are necessary or convenient to be prescribed for carrying out or giving effect to this Act.
