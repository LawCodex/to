# Raffles and Lotteries Act

RAFFLES AND LOTTERIES ACT1 AN ACT TO REGULATE THE HOLDING OF RAFFLES AND OTHER LOTTERIES2 Commencement [24th November, 1919]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Raffles and Lotteries Act.3

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act —

“article” includes money;4

“lottery” means a scheme or device in respect of which direct or indirect consideration is paid to participate and by which prizes of money or money’s worth are distributed according to a draw; and includes lotto, bingo, a sweepstake and every other similar scheme or device;5

“Minister of Police” includes the chief officer of the police in any district;

“raffle” means any mode of disposing of any article by chance.

#### 3 Raffles to be licensed

Every person desiring to hold a raffle shall apply to the Minister of Police for a licence and shall on making such application state the description of the article or articles to be raffled together with the amount intended to be charged for each chance in the raffle and the total number of chances proposed to be sold.

#### 4 Duties of Minister

The Minister of Police before issuing a licence for a raffle shall wherever practicable make an inspection for the article or articles to be raffled and if the sum proposed to be raised by the raffle appears to him to be excessive as compared with the fair value of the article to be raffled he may either refuse to issue a licence or may issue a licence subject to such conditions as may from time to time be imposed by the Cabinet in that behalf.6

#### 4A Lotteries to be licensed

Every person desiring to hold a lottery shall apply to the Minister of Police for a licence and shall on making such application state the description of the scheme or device to be used with the amount intended to be charged for each chance and the total number of chances proposed to be sold.7

#### 5 Fees for licence

The fees specified in the Schedule shall be paid in advance to the Minister of Police.

#### 6 Offences and penalties

Every person who shall —

(a) hold or attempt to hold any raffle or lottery without having first obtained a licence from the Minister of Police;

(b) sell or attempt to sell any chance in a raffle or lottery in excess of the total number of chances authorised by the licence; or

(c) conduct or attempt to conduct a raffle or lottery contrary to the conditions therein imposed,8 shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $1,000 and in default of payment to a period of imprisonment not exceeding 2 months.9

#### 7 Regulations

(1) The Minister of Police with the consent of Cabinet may make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act.10

(2) The Raffles Regulations11 made by the Minister of Police on 14 November 1930 shall be deemed to have been made under this section.12 THE SCHEDULE (Section 5)13 FEES FOR LICENCES FOR RAFFLES AND LOTTERIES14 Where the total value of the chances authorised to be sold does not exceed $100 the fee shall be 10 per cent of the value.

Where the total value of the chances authorised to be sold exceeds $100 the fee shall be 10 per cent on the first $100 and 5 per cent on the remainder.
