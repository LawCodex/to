# National Anthem of Tonga Act

AN ACT TO CONFIRM THE NATIONAL ANTHEM OF THE KINGDOM OF TONGA1 Commencement [29October 1974]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the National Anthem of Tonga Act.

#### 2 National Anthem

The National Anthem of Tonga shall —

(a) have the words set out in Schedule 1 hereto;

(b) have the music and arrangement set out in Schedule 2 hereto, provided that the key of the music may be changed; and

(c) have the words accompanying the music in the manner set out in Schedule 3 hereto.

## SCHEDULE 1

(WORDS)

‘E ‘Otua Mafimafi, Ko ho mau ‘Eiki Koe, Ko Koe ko e falala‘anga, Mo e ‘ofa ki Tonga:

‘Afio hifo ‘emau lotu,

‘A ia ‘oku mau fai ni, Mo Ke tali homau loto,

‘O malu’i ‘a Tupou.
