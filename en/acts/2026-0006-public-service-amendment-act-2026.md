Public Service (Amendment) Act 2026 Section 1 to Act 1 of 2026 Page 5 C T PUBLIC SERVICE (AMENDMENT) ACT 2026 Act 1 of 2026 AN ACT TO AMEND THE PUBLIC SERVICE ACT [CAP 2.11] I assent, TUPOU VI, 25th June 2026.

BE IT ENACTED by the King and Legislative Assembly of Tonga in the Legislature of the Kingdom as follows: 1 Short Title and Interpretation

(1) This Act may be cited as the Public Service (Amendment) Act 2026.

(2) In this Act, the Public Service Act [Cap 2.11], as amended, shall be referred to as the “Principal Act”. 2 Schedule I – Part 1 amended The Principal Act is amended by repealing the current Schedule I - Part 1 and replacing it with the following new Schedule I - Part 1 –

“1. Prime Minister’s Office;

2. Ministry of Infrastructure;

3. Ministry of Tourism;

4. MinistryofLands,Survey,SpatialPlanningandNatural Resources;

5. Ministry of Education and Training;

6. Ministry of Health;
