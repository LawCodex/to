## PART I – PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short Title and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the Privacy Act 2025.

(2) This Act shall come into force on a date proclaimed by Cabinet.

#### 2 Objectives

The objectives of this Act is to –

(a) ensure that the processing of personal information complies with principles of personal information protection, including digital privacy and personal information security;

(b) provide individuals with rights with respect to the processing of personal information relating to them;

(c) set standards for the transmission of personal information outside of the Kingdom;

(d) establish an institutional mechanism to promote and enforce the principles, rights and obligations provided for in this Act; and

(e) provide a legal foundation to promote the digital economy of the Kingdom and its participation in the regional and global economies through the beneficial uses of personal information.

#### 5 Application to data controllers and data processors

(1) Subject only to the exceptions provided in this Act or other written law, this Act shall apply to a data controller or data processor in respect of any personal information if –

(a) the data controller or data processor is established in the Kingdom;

(b) the processing occurs within the Kingdom, provided that the mere transiting of personal information through the Kingdom shall not constitute processing occurring in the Kingdom; or Section 6 Privacy Act 2025 Page 12 Act 34 of 2025 to

(c) the processing relates to the targeted offering of goods or services to the data subject in the Kingdom, or the monitoring of the behaviour of the data subject as far as his behaviour takes place within the Kingdom.

(2) A data controller or data processor is established in the Kingdom if it is –

(a) ordinarily resident in the Kingdom;

(b) incorporated in the Kingdom; or

(c) has a branch office in the Kingdom.

#### 6 Data subject’s representative

(1) Nothing in this Act shall prevent a data subject from authorising another person to make a decision or receive information about that data subject’s personal information.

(2) Where a data subject has authorised a person to make a decision about that data subject’s personal information that authorised person’s decision shall be considered the decision of the data subject.

(3) Where a data subject has authorised a person to receive information about that data subject’s personal information, provision of such information to that authorised person shall be considered a provision of such information to the data subject.

### DIVISION 1 – PROCESSING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

#### 27 Lawful basis for processing personal information

(1) A data controller shall not process personal information, or permit a data processor to process personal information on its behalf, except where -

(a) the data subject has given and not withdrawn his consent to the processing for the specific purpose or purposes for which it will be processed; or

(b) such processing is necessary –

(i) for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party;

(ii) to complete steps that the data controller is required to complete prior to entering a contract with the data subject;

(iii) in ordertoprotectthevitalinterestsof the datasubject or any other individual in a case where the data controller or data processor cannot reasonably be expected to obtain the consent of the data subject or such other individual;

(iv) in order to comply with a legal obligation to which the data controller or a data processor processing such personal information on its behalf, is subject to;

(v) the processing is authorised by law and carried out by a competent public authority in furtherance of its legal mandate;

Privacy Act 2025 Section 28 to Act 34 of 2025 Page 23

(vi) the processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the data controller or a data processor processing such personal information on its behalf;

(vii) the processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the data controller, or a data processor processing personal information on its behalf, or by a third party to whom the personal information is disclosed, except where such interests are overridden by the interests of fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject;

(viii) the processing is necessary for archiving purposes in the public interest, or for the purpose of historical, statistical or scientific research; or

(ix) to comply with a written law of the Kingdom.

#### 28 Lawful basis for processing sensitive personal information

(1) A data controller shall not process sensitive personal information, or permit a data processor to process sensitive personal information on its behalf, except where one of the conditions of Section 27 has first been met and –

(a) the data subject has given and not withdrawn his consent to the processing for the specific purpose or purposes for which it will be processed; or

(i) to protect the vital interests of a data subject in a case where the data subject is physically or legally incapable of giving consent;

#### 67 Service of notices
