# Transport Services Act

Transport Services Act CAP 24.04 Section 1 to 2020 Revised Edition Page 5 C T TRANSPORT SERVICES ACT AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE MINISTRY OF TRANSPORT AND THE INTEGRATION AND REFORM OF THE TRANSPORT SECTORS IN THE KINGDOM INTO A SINGLE ADMINISTRATION UNDER THE MINISTRY OF TRANSPORT1 Commencement [19th February 2009]

## PART 1 - PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Transport Services Act.

#### 2 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires —

“Chief Executive Officer” means the Government chief executive officer responsible for transport;2

“integrated transport sector” means the integration of the land, sea and air transport sectors in the Kingdom to be administered by the Ministry;

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for transport;3

“Ministry” means the Ministry responsible for transport.4 Section 3 CAP 24.04 Transport Services Act Page 6 2020 Revised Edition to

## PART 2 - GENERAL ADMINISTRATION

#### 3 Minister responsible for transport5

The Minister responsible for transport shall be responsible for the administration of this Act.

#### 4 Ministry responsible for transport

There shall be a Ministry responsible for transport under the direction and control of the Minister, consisting of the Chief Executive Officer and such other officers and employees that may be appointed in accordance with section 8.

#### 5 Functions of the Ministry

The functions of the Ministry shall be to —

(a) administer the integration of the land, sea and air transport sectors in the Kingdom;

(b) ensure that the services relating to the integrated transport sector in the Kingdom are maintained;

(c) regulate the integrated transport sector in the Kingdom; and

(d) undertake any other function prescribed under any other Act relating to land, sea or air transportation.

#### 6 Annual report

The Minister shall present an annual report in accordance with clause 51 of the Constitution.

#### 7 Chief Executive Officer

(1) There shall be a Chief Executive Officer in the Ministry, appointed in accordance with the Public Service Act and the relevant policies of Government.

(2) The Minister may give policy directions not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, as to the duties and responsibilities of the Chief Executive Officer and any other officer of the Ministry.

(3) It shall be the duty and function of the Chief Executive Officer to act in accordance with policy direction given to him by the Minister within the provisions of this Act, and generally to assist the Minister and to advise him, as he may deem appropriate, on any transport matter.

#### 8 Appointment of staff

There may be appointed in accordance with the Public Service Act and the relevant policies of Government such officers or employees in the Ministry to assist the Minister in the performance of his functions.

## PART 3 - ASPECTS OF TRANSPORT SERVICES

#### 9 Powers

(1) The Ministry is empowered to —

(a) do any action that may be required to be done in the process of integrating the land, sea and air transport sectors;

(b) facilitate the provision of transport services within the limits of available finance and resources and according to the needs of the people of the Kingdom;

(c) provide facilities for the provision of a comprehensive transport sector;

(d) provide such training facilities as may be required to maintain the personnel of the transport sector at the highest possible level of professional and technical expertise and performance;

(e) request, conduct, or assist research in transport matters by employees of the Ministry or by such other persons or bodies as he may decide;

(f) establish a statistics and transport information service;

(g) establish any facility that is conducive to the enhancement of transport services in the Kingdom;

(h) ensure that the provision of transport services are conducted in a safe manner; or

(i) collaborate with national, international, and nongovernmental organizations.

(2) In exercise of his powers under subsection (1), the Ministry shall act in consultation with relevant Ministries and stakeholders and shall refer any important matters to Cabinet.

#### 10 Delegation of powers

The Minister may delegate any of his powers under this Act, other than powers under section 11 in writing to the Chief Executive Officer or to any other officer.

#### 11 Regulations

The Minister may, with the consent of Cabinet, make regulations for carrying out of the objects of this Act.

## PART 4 - MISCELLANEOUS

#### 12 Savings

Any existing orders, appointments, certificates, licences or notices made or granted under the Civil Aviation Act6 , the Shipping Act7 or the Traffic Act8 , before this Act comes into effect shall remain in force until revoked according to law.

#### 13 Act to prevail

Where there is a conflict between the provisions of this Act and any other Act, the provisions of this Act shall prevail.
