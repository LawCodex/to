# Birds Preservation Act

AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE PRESERVATION OF WILD BIRDS1 Commencement [20th November, 1915]

## PART I—PROTECTION OF BIRDS2

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Birds Preservation Act.3

#### 2 Definition4

In this Act —

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for environment;5

“protected area” means any area comprising land, or water, or land and water, as is specified in the Second Schedule hereto;6

“protected bird” includes all such birds whether imported into or indigenous to the Kingdom and the eggs and progeny of any such birds as are mentioned in the First Schedule hereto;

#### 3 Penalty for taking protected birds

Any person who shall wilfully kill, shoot, capture, take or destroy any protected bird at any time within the period set opposite their respective names in the said First Schedule or shall use any means whatever within the said respective periods for the purpose of killing, shooting, capturing, taking or destroying any protected bird shall be liable for every such offence to a penalty not exceeding $20 or in default of payment to imprisonment with hard labour for a period not exceeding 2 months:7 Provided always that the Minister responsible for forests and may at any time with the consent of the Cabinet permit any person to collect specimens subject to such conditions as he may impose of any or all protected birds.8

#### 4 Power to amend the Schedules

The Minister may by Order with the consent of the Cabinet amend the First Schedule.9

#### 5 Offences under Part I

If any person within the periods mentioned in the said First Schedule shall catch or buy or sell or offer or expose for sale or knowingly have in his possession or under his control any protected bird he shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $50 and in default of payment to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 6 months and the bird, and any boat, net, gear and equipment used in connection with the catching of the bird, may be forfeited to the Crown and shall be disposed of in such manner as the Minister thinks fit.10

## PART II—PROTECTED AREAS

#### 6 Protected areas

The area specified in the Second Schedule to this Act is hereby declared to be a protected area; and the Minister may by Order with the consent of Cabinet amend the Second Schedule.11

#### 7 Prohibitions within protected areas

(1) No person may, within a protected area, and without the prior consent in writing of the Minister —

(i) discharge or cause to be discharged into the protected area any effluent or noxious or toxic liquid or substance;

(ii) erect any harbour, wharf, pier, jetty or other building works, temporary or permanent;

(iii) cut, damage, remove or destroy any mangrove;

(iv) carry out any boring, drilling or dredging operations.12

(2) Any person contravening the provisions of the last preceding subsection shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction for every such offence to a fine not exceeding $500 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 6 months, or to both such fine and imprisonment, and, in the case of convictions under paragraph (iv) of the immediately preceding subsection any boat, net, trap, trawl, gear and equipment used in connection with the offence may be forfeited to the Crown and shall be disposed of in such manner as the Minister thinks fit.13

## PART III—GENERAL

#### 8 Power of police officers and fisheries officers

(1) Whenever any police officer or fisheries officer (being a person duly authorised in writing by the Minister to act as such) suspects upon reasonable grounds that any person has committed an offence against this Act, he may inspect and search any baggage, package, vessel or vehicle belonging to such person or in his possession or control, and if there is found as a consequence of such search any protected bird appearing to have been obtained in contravention of this Act, the same may be seized and taken before a Court.14

(2) Any police officer may enter upon any land for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this Act or for preventing or detecting offences thereunder.15
