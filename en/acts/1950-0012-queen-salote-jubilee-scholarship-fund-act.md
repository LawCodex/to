# Queen Salote Jubilee Scholarship Fund Act

AN ACT TO ESTABLISH THE QUEEN SALOTE JUBILEE SCHOLARSHIP FUND1 Commencement [19th September, 1950]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Queen Salote Jubilee Scholarship Fund Act.

#### 2 Funds to be appropriated2

The Ministry of Education shall appropriate a sum of Six Thousand Pounds from the Surplus Funds of the Kingdom and shall invest or re-invest such sum in such manner as Minister for Education shall direct.

#### 3 Interest to accumulate3

Interest on such invested sum of Six Thousand Pounds shall be allowed to accumulate, and the annual amount of interest earned by such invested sum of Six Thousand Pounds together with such further sums as may be appropriated from time to time from the annual revenue of the Kingdom and the annual interest thereon shall be added to the said sum each year and further invested in such manner as Minister for Education may direct.

#### 4 Queen Salote Jubilee Scholarship Fund

The said invested sum of Six Thousand Pounds together with the accumulated interest thereon and such other sums as may from time to time be added thereto shall form a fund which shall be known as the Queen Salote Jubilee Scholarship Fund.

#### 5 Purpose of Fund4

The said Queen Salote Jubilee Scholarship Fund shall, when so required, be used exclusively for the purpose of providing scholarships for selected Tongan scholars at approved educational institutions in New Zealand or Australia or elsewhere overseas, and when so required the Minister for Education with the consent of Cabinet may authorize expenditure from such fund for such purpose
