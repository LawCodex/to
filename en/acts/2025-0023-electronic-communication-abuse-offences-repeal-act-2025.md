Electronic Communication Abuse Offences (Repeal) Act 2025 Section 1 to Act 21 of 2025 Page 5 C T ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATION ABUSE OFFENCES (REPEAL) ACT 2025 Act 21 of 2025 AN ACT TO REPEAL THE ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATION ABUSE OFFENCES ACT 2020 I assent, TUPOU VI, 28 August 2025.

BE IT ENACTED by the King and Legislative Assembly of Tonga in the Legislature of the Kingdom as follows: 1 Short Title

(1) This Act may be cited as the Electronic Communication Abuse Offences

(Repeal) Act 2025.

(2) In this Act, the Electronic Communication Abuse Offences Act 2020, shall be referred to as the “Principal Act”. 2 Repeal and saving

(1) The Electronic Communication Abuse Offences Act is hereby repealed.

(2) Under section 15 of the Interpretation Act, the repeal of the Electronic Communication Abuse Offences Act does not affect –

(a) the past operation of or anything done or suffered under that Act;

(b) any offence committed, or any right, liberty, obligation or penalty acquired or incurred under that Act; or

(c) any action, proceeding, or thing pending or incomplete when this Act comes into operation, but every such action, proceeding or thing may be carried on and completed as if that Act had not been repealed.

Passed by the Legislative Assembly on this 14th day of May 2025.
