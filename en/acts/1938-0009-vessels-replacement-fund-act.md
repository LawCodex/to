# Vessels Replacement Fund Act

AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATION FROM THE SURPLUS FUNDS OF THE KINGDOM OF THE SUM OF TEN THOUSAND PA'ANGA TO BE INVESTED AS A VESSELS REPLACEMENT FUND1 Commencement [15th July, 1938]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Vessels Replacement Fund Act.

#### 2 Fund to be appropriated2

The Treasurer of the Government shall upon being so directed by Cabinet appropriate a sum of $10,000 from the Surplus Funds of the Kingdom and invest or re-invest such sum in such manner as Cabinet shall direct.

#### 3 Interest to accumulate

Interest on such invested sum of $10,000 shall be allowed to accumulate and the annual amount of interest earned by such invested sum of $10,000 shall be added to the said invested sum.

#### 4 Purpose of fund3

The said invested sum of $10,000 and accumulated interest shall when so required be used exclusively for the purpose of the replacement of any Government Vessels and when so required the Prime Minister with the consent of the Cabinet may authorize expenditure from such fund for the purposes specified herein.
