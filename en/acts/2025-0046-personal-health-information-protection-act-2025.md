## PART 1 - PRELIMINARY

#### 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Personal Health Information Protection Act 2025.

#### 2 Purposes

The purposes of this Act are – Section 2 Personal Health Information Protection Act 2025 Page 8 Act 31 of 2025 to

(a) toestablishrulesforthe access,collection,useanddisclosureofpersonal health information about persons that protect the confidentiality of that information and the privacy of persons with respect to that information, while facilitating the effective provision of health care;

(b) to provide persons with a right of access to personal health information about themselves, subject to limited and specific exceptions set out in this Act;

(c) to provide persons with a right to require the correction or amendment of personal health information about themselves, subject to limited and specific exceptions set out in this Act;

(d) to provide an appeal process with respect to personal health information; and

(e) to provide remedies for contraventions of this Act.

#### 5 Interpretation, health information custodian

(1) Subject to sub-section (2), a health information custodian who operates more than one facility, program or service described in one of the paragraphs of the definition of “health information custodian” in section 3(1) shall be deemed to be a separate custodian with respect to personal health information of which it has custody or control as a result of or in connection with operating each of the facilities, programs or services that it operates.

(2) Where a health information custodian who operates more than one facility, program or service wishes to share personal health information between those facilities, programs or services in order to provide integrated health care or services at those facilities, programs or services, the custodian may apply in writing to the Minister to act as a single health information custodian on behalf of those facilities, programs or services.

(3) Where the Minister receives a request under this section from a health information custodian to act as a single custodian, the Minister shall approve Personal Health Information Protection Act 2025 Section 6 to Act 31 of 2025 Page 13 the request if the Minister is of the opinion that it is appropriate in the circumstances, having regard to the public interest and –

(a) the applicant's ability to provide persons with reasonable access to their personal health information;

(b) the applicant's ability to comply with the rules for the collection, use and disclosure of personal health information under Part 4; and

(c) whether the sharing of personal health information is necessary in order to effectively provide integrated health care.

(4) The Minister may afford a person an opportunity to be heard before approving or rejecting a request under subsection (3).

(5) Any decision the Minister may make under subsection (4) shall be final.

(6) Subject to subsection (7), a health information custodian does not cease to be a health information custodian with respect to personal health information until custody or control of the information passes to another person who is legally authorized to hold the records.

(7) If a health information custodian dies, the estate trustee of the health information custodian shall be deemed to be the health information custodian with respect to the personal health information held by the deceased health information custodian until custody or control of the information passes to another person who is legally authorized to hold the records.

#### 6 Interpretation, collection, use and disclosure

(1) For the purposes of this Act, the providing of personal health information between a health information custodian and an agent of the custodian is a use by the custodian, and not a disclosure by the person providing the information or a collection by the person to whom the information is provided.

(2) A provision of this Act that applies to the collection, use or disclosure of personal health information about a person by a health information custodian with the consent of the person, whatever the nature of the consent, does not affect the collection, use or disclosure that this Act permits or requires the health information custodian to make of the information without the consent of the person.

(3) A provision of this Act that permits a health information custodian to disclose personal health information about a person without the consent of the person –

(a) does not require the custodian to disclose it unless required to do so by law;

(b) does not relieve the custodian from a legal requirement to disclose the information; and

(c) does not prevent the custodian from obtaining the person’s consent for the disclosure.

#### 9 Information practices

(1) A health information custodian that has custody or control of personal health information shall have in place information practices and policies that comply with the requirements prescribed by Regulations.

(a) the type of information that was viewed, handled, modified or otherwise dealt with;

#### 11 Accuracy

(1) A health information custodian that uses personal health information about a person shall take reasonable steps to ensure that the information is as accurate, complete and up to date as is necessary for the purposes for which it uses the information.

A health information custodian shall take steps that are reasonable in the circumstances to ensure that personal health information is not collected without authority.

### DIVISION 2 – CAPACITY

#### 24 Capacity

(1) A person is capable with respect to giving a consent under this Act if the person –

(a) understands the nature of the personal health information to which the consent relates; and

#### 89 Offences

(1) A person is guilty of an offence if the person –

(a) wilfully accesses, collects, uses or discloses personal health information in contravention of this Act;

(b) makes a request under this Act, under false pretences, for access to or correction of a record of personal health information;

(c) wilfully contravenes section 13;

(d) in connection with the access, collection, use or disclosure of personal health information or access to a record of personal health information, makes an assertion, knowing that it is untrue, to the effect that the person –

(i) is a person who is entitled to consent to the access, collection, use or disclosure of personal health information about another person;

(ii) meets the requirement of section 27(2)(b) and (c);

(iii) holds the beliefs described in section 27(5); or

(iv) is a person entitled to access to a record of personal health information under section 57;

(e) disposes of a record of personal health information in the custody or under the control of the custodian with an intent to evade a request for access to the record that the custodian has received under section 58(1);

(f) wilfully disposes of a record of personal health information in contravention of section 15;

(g) contravenes section 36(2) or (3) or section 51(7) (a), (e) or (f);

(h) wilfully obstructs the CEO or a person known to be acting under the authority of the CEO in the performance of his functions under this Act;

(j) wilfully fails to comply with a direction made by the CEO or a person known to be acting under the authority of the CEO under this Act; or
