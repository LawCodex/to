# Evacuation Act

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE EVACUATION OF PERSONS FROM ONE PART OF THE KINGDOM TO ANOTHER1 Commencement [17th September, 1947]

#### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Evacuation Act.

#### 2 Power to order evacuation2

On any occasion Cabinet may, in the interest and for the welfare of any particular body of persons residing within the Kingdom, by Order, order the complete or partial evacuation of that body of persons from its normal place of residence to any other part of the Kingdom.

#### 3 Competent authority for evacuation3

Whenever Cabinet shall order the complete or partial evacuation of any body of persons, as provided by section 2, Cabinet shall appoint one or more persons to be the Competent Authority for Evacuation.

#### 4 Powers and duties of competent authority4

The Competent Authority shall have the general management and control of the evacuation named in any such Order, and for that purpose may —

(a) make such rules as it thinks necessary for securing the satisfactory, expedient and comfortable evacuation named in the Order;

(b) requisition property, for the purposes of the evacuation, in the district or area to be evacuated, or partially evacuated;

(c) apply any money received by it from the Treasurer in defraying any of the legitimate expenses of the evacuation;

(d) subject to the provisions of this Act do such other things as appear to it to be necessary or expedient for furthering the interests and well-being of the evacuated persons during the course of evacuation and resettlement.

#### 5 Treasurer to advance money for effecting evacuation5

For the purpose of effectively carrying out any such Order the Treasurer shall, on the Authority of the Competent Authority, advance money from Public Funds for the transport, accommodation, maintenance and care of persons so evacuated.

#### 6 Limitation of Order6

Any such Order shall remain effective until it be rescinded by Cabinet.

#### 7 Penalties

Any person who fails to carry out the provisions of any such Order, or any order, rule or instruction given by the Competent Authority thereunder, shall be guilty of an offence, and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $500 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years, or to both such fine and imprisonment.7
