#### 3 Meaning of conviction in relation to a serious offence

For the purposes of this Act, a person shall be taken to be convicted of a serious offence if —

(a) the person is convicted, whether summarily or on indictment, of the offence;

(b) the person is charged with, and found guilty of, the offence but is discharged without any conviction being recorded; or

(c) the court, with the consent of the convicted person, takes the offence of which the person has not been found guilty into account in passing sentence on the person for another serious offence.

#### 4 Meaning of quashing of convictions

For the purposes of this Act, a person’s conviction of a serious offence shall be taken to be quashed —

(a) where section 4(b) applies, if the finding of guilt is quashed or set aside;

(b) where section 4(c) applies, if either —

(i) the person’s conviction of the other offence referred to in that section, is quashed or set aside; or

(ii) the decision of the Supreme Court to take the offence into account in passing sentence for that other offence is quashed or set aside; or

(c) where His Majesty grants the person a pardon in respect of the person’s conviction of the offence.

#### 5 Meaning of deriving a benefit

(1) A person derives a benefit if that person has knowingly, directly or indirectly benefitted from a serious offence or the proceeds of crime, whether or not that person undertook or was involved in that serious offence.

### DIVISION 1 – TRANSACTION REPORTING AUTHORITY

#### 6 Establishment of the Transaction Reporting Authority

(1) The Transaction Reporting Authority is established as an autonomous, independent unit within the Reserve Bank.

(2) The Transaction Reporting Authority shall serve as the central, national agency of Tonga responsible for receiving, analysing and disseminating information concerning money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing, as provided for by this Act.

(3) The Transaction Reporting Authority shall consist of -

(a) a DirectorappointedbytheGovernor onsuchterms asthe Governormay determine and in accordance with this Act;

(b) such other persons as may be appointed by the Governor; and

(c) any official with relevant skills from a government agency seconded to the Transaction Reporting Authority on the approval of the Governor in consultation with the Director and relevant head of the government agency.

(4) The Director shall exercise all of the powers, duties and functions of the Transaction Reporting Authority under this Act.

(5) The Director may authorise any person employed in the Transaction Reporting Authority, subject to any terms and conditions that the Director may specify, to carry out any power, duty or function conferred on the Director under this Act.

(6) The Governor may direct the Transaction Reporting Authority on any matter that, in the Governor’s opinion, materially affects public policy or the strategic direction of the Transaction Reporting Authority.

(7) The Reserve Bank shall provide sufficient resources to the Transaction Reporting Authority to enable it to undertake its functions and responsibilities under this Act.

### DIVISION 3 – MONEY LAUNDERING OFFENCES

#### 20 Intentional money laundering

(1) A person commits the offence of money laundering if he engages in a dealing with property knowing or believing it to be the proceeds of crime.

(2) A person shall be liable upon conviction to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 25 years or a fine up to 100 times the sum laundered, or both, and in the case of a legal person by a fine of 1000 times the sum laundered or $10,000,000, whichever is greater.

#### 21 Reckless money laundering

(1) A person commits an offence if he engages in a dealing with property and is reckless to the possibility that it is the proceeds of crime.

(2) A person shall be liable upon conviction to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 10 years or a fine up to 50 times the sum laundered, or both, and in the case of a legal person by a fine of 500 times the sum laundered or $5,000,000, whichever is greater.

(3) Where particulars of a restraining order are registered under the Land Act, a person who subsequently deals with the property shall, for the purposes of section 81 be deemed to have notice of the order at the time of the dealing.

#### 140 Contravention of restraining order

A person who knowingly contravenes or attempts to contravene a restraining order by disposing of or otherwise dealing with property that is subject to the restraining order commits an offence and is liable upon conviction to —

(a) in the case of a natural person, a fine of up to $100,000 or imprisonment for a period of up to 5 years or both; or

(b) in the case of a legal person, a fine of up to $1,000,000.

## PART VIII – MISCELLANEOUS

#### 141 Penalties

#### 142 Regulations

The Attorney General may, with the consent of Cabinet make Regulations for the effective carrying out of the provisions of this Act.

#### 143 Repeal, Transitional and Savings

(1) The Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act [Cap 4.07] is hereby repealed.

(2) All appointments made under the Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act [Cap 4.07] and subsisting on the date this Act comes into force shall be treated as appointments made under this Act for the term of the original appointment.

(3) Any appeal or prosecution commenced before this Act came into force shall be continued and disposed of as if this Act had not come into force.

(4) When the period for any application, appeal, or prosecution had expired before this Act came into force, nothing in this Act shall be construed as enabling the application, appeal or prosecution to be made under this Act by reason only of the fact that a longer period is specified in this Act.
